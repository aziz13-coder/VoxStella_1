Analyzing Career/Job According to Morin's System
Step-by-Step Process
STEP 1: Identify the Primary House - The 10th House
According to Morin's Action Triplicity (Houses 10, 6, 2), the 10th house is THE primary house for career and profession.
From the text:
"First in the order of dignity and nobility it is immaterial, as are arts, rulerships, dignities, and honors, to which man is raised up because of his well-deserved merit, famous actions, and capability... Therefore, of this triplicity, the angle of the MC lays claim to be the principal house."
What to analyze in the 10th:
•	Sign on the cusp - Shows the nature/style of the career
•	Planetary ruler(s) of the 10th - Primary career significator
•	Planets in the 10th - Direct career influences
•	Aspects to the 10th cusp - Modifying influences
•	State of the ruler (dignity, aspects, house placement)

STEP 2: Examine the Supporting Houses of the Action Triplicity
The 6th House - Type of Work Environment
"The second is [the house of] animate material things, which are subordinates, servants, and animals"
What this means for career:
•	Working WITH people (employees, subordinates, colleagues)
•	Service professions
•	Work involving animals
•	The daily work environment and tasks
•	Health-related professions
Analyze:
•	Planets in 6th
•	Ruler of 6th and its condition
•	Whether career involves managing people/animals
The 2nd House - Income from Work
"The third is [the house of] inanimate material things, such as gold, silver, equipment, and the other goods that are also immobile, acquired by his own labor"
What this means for career:
•	Wealth gained through profession
•	Material resources needed for work
•	Financial success of career choice
•	Physical tools/equipment used in profession
Analyze:
•	Planets in 2nd
•	Ruler of 2nd
•	Material rewards from the career

Morin's key principle:
"Any house signifies the same thing but weaker as that to which it is opposed... the seventh will by accident be a significator of illnesses and death because it is opposed to the first"
The 4th House (opposite the 10th):
•	Has secondary significations about career
•	Especially relevant for: 
o	Family businesses
o	Working from home
o	Career foundations/endings
o	Parental influence on career
Analyze:

Analyzing Marriage in Morin's System: Step-by-Step Guide
Primary House: The 7th House
According to Morin, the 7th house is the PRIMARY significator of marriage.
Here's his exact reasoning:
The Triplicity of Marriage and Love
The 7th house belongs to what Morin calls the "Triplicity of Union/Marriage", which includes three types of human connection:
1.	7th House (DSC angle) - Matrimony (bodily/sexual union)
o	"The primary cause of attaining perpetuity"
o	The most important house for marriage
2.	3rd House - Brothers and blood relatives
3.	11th House - Friends (benevolent union)
Step-by-Step Analysis for Marriage
Step 1: Examine the 7th House Cusp
•	What sign is on the cusp? This shows the nature/quality of marriage
•	Find the ruler of that sign - This is your primary marriage significator
Step 2: Analyze the Ruler of the 7th
•	Which house is it located in? (shows where/how you meet spouse, circumstances)
•	What is its zodiacal state? (dignity, debility)
•	What aspects does it receive? 
o	Benefics (Jupiter, Venus) = favorable
o	Malefics (Saturn, Mars) = challenges
Step 3: Planets IN the 7th House
•	Any planets bodily present in the 7th?
•	These are powerful marriage significators by location
•	Morin emphasized: "presence of a planet can do more than the rulership of one that is absent"
Step 4: Check the 1st House (Secondary Indicator)
•	Why? Morin's key principle: "any house signifies the same thing but weaker as that to which it is opposed"
•	The 1st house (opposite the 7th) has secondary marriage significance
•	Check its ruler and any planets there
Step 5: Consider Venus (Natural Significator)
•	Venus is the natural significator of love/marriage
•	Check: 
o	Which house Venus occupies
o	Which house(s) Venus rules
o	Venus's aspects to the 7th house ruler
Step 6: Examine the Moon (for Women) / Sun (for Men)
•	For a woman's chart: Moon's placement can indicate husband
•	For a man's chart: Sun/Venus for wife
•	Though Morin emphasizes the 7th house system over planetary significators
Example from Morin's Own Chart
In Morin's analysis of his nativity:
•	He had Saturn and the Moon in the 12th house (house of afflictions)
•	Saturn ruled his 7th house (Capricorn on the cusp)
•	Moon also in the 12th
•	Result: He experienced "hatred, and loss of fortune from my mother" and "many evils, losses, and dangers to my life from women"
This shows how he read the 7th house ruler's placement (Saturn in 12th) as indicating difficulties with marriage/partnerships.
Important Morin Principles to Apply
1. Determination is Key
The planet's house rulership determines WHAT it signifies. A planet ruling the 7th becomes a marriage significator regardless of its natural nature.
2. Bodily Presence > Rulership
A planet actually IN the 7th house is stronger than just ruling it from elsewhere.
3. Multiple Factors
Don't rely on one indicator:
•	7th house sign
•	7th house ruler's state and position
•	Planets in the 7th
•	Opposite house (1st) secondary indications
•	Natural significators (Venus, Moon/Sun)
4. Aspects to the 7th Cusp
Planets aspecting the actual degree of the 7th house cusp affect marriage matters.
Summary Checklist
✅ 7th house cusp sign → nature of marriage
✅ 7th house ruler → primary marriage significator
✅ 7th house ruler's location → circumstances
✅ 7th house ruler's aspects → fortune/challenges
✅ Planets IN 7th house → strongest indicators
✅ 1st house (opposite) → secondary indications
✅ Venus → natural love/marriage indicator
✅ Integrate all factors → comprehensive judgment
This systematic approach reflects Morin's emphasis on house rulership and determination as the foundation of horoscopic interpretation.
Analyzing Career/Profession in Morin's System: Step-by-Step Guide
Primary House: The 10th House (MC)
According to Morin, the 10th house is the PRIMARY significator of career, profession, and public actions.
Here's his exact reasoning:
The Triplicity of Action
The 10th house belongs to what Morin calls the "Triplicity of Action and Worldly Good", which includes three types of profit from one's work:
1.	10th House (MC angle) - Actions, honors, dignities, profession
o	"Immaterial goods, as are arts, rulerships, dignities, and honors"
o	Pertains to youth (the active working phase of life)
o	The most important house for career
2.	6th House - Servants and animals (animate material goods/subordinates)
3.	2nd House - Money, riches, possessions (inanimate material goods)
Morin writes: "man progresses from boyhood to youth, and from being or life to acting"
Step-by-Step Analysis for Career
Step 1: Examine the 10th House Cusp (MC)
•	What sign is on the MC? This shows the nature/type of career
•	Find the ruler of that sign - This is your primary career significator
•	Note the actual degree - This is the most powerful point for career
Step 2: Analyze the Ruler of the 10th
•	Which house is it located in? 
o	Shows the source/area of career success
o	Example: 10th ruler in 9th = career through teaching, travel, religion
o	Example: 10th ruler in 2nd = career focused on wealth-building
•	What is its zodiacal state? 
o	Strong (dignity, exaltation) = career success, honors
o	Weak (detriment, fall, peregrine) = career difficulties
•	What aspects does it receive? 
o	Jupiter aspects = expansion, honors, good fortune in career
o	Venus aspects = pleasant work, artistic career
o	Saturn aspects = hard work, delays, but potential authority
o	Mars aspects = energy, conflicts, military/athletic careers
o	Malefic squares/oppositions = career obstacles
Step 3: Planets IN the 10th House
•	Any planets bodily present in the 10th?
•	These are extremely powerful career indicators
•	Morin's principle: "the presence of a planet can do more than the rulership of one that is absent"
Interpretation by planet:
•	Sun in 10th = Fame, authority, leadership positions
•	Moon in 10th = Public roles, fluctuating career, popular appeal
•	Mercury in 10th = Communication, writing, teaching, commerce
•	Venus in 10th = Arts, beauty, diplomacy, pleasant work
•	Mars in 10th = Military, sports, surgery, aggressive professions
•	Jupiter in 10th = Honors, religious/legal careers, expansion
•	Saturn in 10th = Administration, discipline, slow but steady rise
Step 4: Check the 4th House (Secondary Indicator)
•	Why? Morin's key principle: "any house signifies the same thing but weaker as that to which it is opposed"
•	The 4th house (opposite the 10th) has secondary career significance
•	Especially relevant for: 
o	The end of one's career
o	Private vs. public work
o	Foundation of career
Step 5: Examine the 2nd House (Money from Career)
•	The 2nd house shows financial rewards from work
•	Check: 
o	2nd house ruler's condition = earning capacity
o	Planets in 2nd = how you acquire wealth
o	Connection between 2nd and 10th rulers = if career brings wealth
Step 6: Consider the 6th House (Work Environment)
•	Shows daily work, service, subordinates
•	Relevant for: 
o	Working conditions
o	Relationship with employees/employers
o	Health in relation to work
Step 7: Check the Sun (Natural Significator)
•	The Sun naturally signifies authority, reputation, public standing
•	Check: 
o	Which house the Sun occupies
o	Which house(s) the Sun rules
o	Sun's aspects to the 10th house ruler or MC

Analyzing Disease/Illness in Morin's System: Step-by-Step Guide
MAJOR INNOVATION: The 12th House (NOT the 6th!)
This is Morin's most revolutionary teaching about houses. He writes:
"Almost all the astrologers, following Ptolemy, have falsely thought up till now that illnesses pertain to the 6th house per se, although per se they pertain to the 12th house, which is [the house] of bodily afflictions and especially the essential house of illnesses."
Why the Confusion?
Morin explains the traditional error:
•	The 6th house is opposite the 12th house
•	His principle: "any house signifies the same thing but weaker as that to which it is opposed"
•	Therefore: 6th house = secondary illness indicator (by opposition)
•	But 12th house = primary illness indicator (essential)
The Triplicity of Suffering
The 12th house belongs to what Morin calls the "Triplicity of Suffering":
1.	4th House - Suffering at the beginning of life (from parents, birth trauma)
2.	12th House - Suffering during life (illnesses, imprisonment, exile, enemies, afflictions)
o	"The valley of miseries"
o	Called the "House of Enemies of Life"
3.	8th House - Suffering at the end of life (death)
Morin writes: "The Native's suffering consists of all of those afflictions of his body—namely, illness, imprisonment, exile, servitude, and the other things that he suffers during his life; and therefore, all these things and their causes, as enemies of life, are attributed to the twelfth house."
Step-by-Step Analysis for Illness
Step 1: Examine the 12th House Cusp
•	What sign is on the 12th house cusp?
•	This shows the nature and type of afflictions/illnesses
•	Find the ruler of that sign - This is your primary illness significator
Step 2: Analyze the Ruler of the 12th
•	Which house is it located in? 
o	Shows where/how illnesses manifest or originate
o	Example: 12th ruler in 1st = chronic constitutional weakness
o	Example: 12th ruler in 6th = illness from work environment
•	What is its zodiacal state? 
o	Strong (dignity, exaltation) = ability to overcome illness, milder afflictions
o	Weak (detriment, fall) = more severe illnesses, harder to cure
•	What aspects does it receive? 
o	Benefics (Jupiter, Venus) = protection, easier recovery
o	Malefics (Saturn, Mars) = severe, chronic, or acute illnesses
o	Saturn aspects = chronic, cold diseases, delays in healing
o	Mars aspects = acute, inflammatory, fevers, injuries
Step 3: Planets IN the 12th House (MOST IMPORTANT)
This is the strongest indicator of illness potential.

         ↓

         ↓

         ↓

         ↓

4.	Test experience - Does it match reality?
5.	Synthesize - Combine into coherent narrative
