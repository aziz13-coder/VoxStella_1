# Astro Clock Blog Keyword Research

Date: 2026-03-30
Scope: marketing/showcase/guide post for Astro Clock trait profile and career/personality output

## Core finding

For search traffic, the stronger phrasing is:

- `personality` and `traits`, not `character`
- `career` and `vocation`, not just `job`
- `birth chart` / `natal chart` as the anchor term

The current SERP language leans much harder toward `birth chart personality traits`, `career astrology`, and `natal chart career` than toward `character astrology` or `job astrology`.

## Recommended primary keyword cluster

- `birth chart personality traits`
- `career astrology`
- `natal chart career`
- `what career suits me astrology`
- `astrology career report`

## Recommended secondary keyword cluster

- `astrology personality report`
- `birth chart strengths and weaknesses`
- `career based on birth chart`
- `how to read your birth chart for career`
- `career and vocation astrology`
- `natal chart for career and purpose`

## Long-tail blog targets

- `how to use your birth chart to understand personality and career`
- `what career suits me based on my birth chart`
- `how to read personality traits in a natal chart`
- `birth chart career and personality guide`
- `how astrology can help with career choice`

## Phrasing to de-prioritize

- `character astrology`
- `job astrology`
- `astrology software for character`
- `character analysis astrology`

These phrases are understandable, but they are not the dominant search language on the pages currently ranking for this topic.

## Recommended blog angle

Best positioning for one high-value post:

- `How to Use Your Birth Chart to Understand Personality and Career`

This angle fits both:

- informational search intent
- product-demo / conversion intent

It also gives room to show:

- trait profile output
- vocation/profession output
- known-chart comparisons
- practical product workflow

## Recommended title candidates

- `How to Use Your Birth Chart to Understand Personality and Career`
- `Birth Chart Personality Traits and Career: A Practical Guide`
- `What Career Suits You? How to Read Personality and Vocation in a Natal Chart`

## Recommended metadata direction

Use one primary keyword plus two secondary terms in the first paragraph and in one subheading:

- primary: `birth chart personality traits`
- secondary: `career astrology`
- secondary: `natal chart career`

## Decision

For the planned Astro Clock blog, the best keyword mix is:

- primary: `birth chart personality traits`
- secondary: `career astrology`
- secondary: `what career suits me astrology`
- secondary: `natal chart career`

That mix is the best balance between:

- traffic potential
- user intent
- and direct relevance to the software's trait-profile + vocation workflow
