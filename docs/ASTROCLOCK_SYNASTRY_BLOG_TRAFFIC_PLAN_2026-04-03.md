## What This Means For Vox Stella

The strongest traffic path is not one content type. It is a mix of three lanes:

1. evergreen education
2. celebrity case studies
3. timely pop-culture/news hooks

If we rely only on celebrity names, we get volatile traffic with weak conversion.
If we rely only on evergreen education, we miss discoverability spikes.
The best structure is:

1. `50%` evergreen intent capture
2. `35%` celebrity case studies
3. `15%` timely/reactive content

## Recommended Content Lanes

### 1. Evergreen Intent Capture

These should target the most durable search demand.

### 3. Timely / Reactive Posts

This lane should only be used when a couple has current media heat.

Examples:

1. new engagement
2. wedding rumors
3. breakup news
4. a couple starring together in a current show or film
5. a reality-TV couple currently dominating entertainment coverage

This content can win fast impressions, but it has shorter shelf life and should not be the core of the strategy.

## Plausibility Framework

Every candidate topic should be scored before writing.

Use this rubric:

1. `Search intent match` out of 10
   - does the phrase align with synastry, compatibility, or chart-comparison intent?
2. `Current attention` out of 10
   - is there recent media coverage, engagement speculation, a new show, or a viral relationship arc?
3. `Data usability` out of 10
   - do we have enough public chart data to responsibly run the engine?
4. `Conversion fit` out of 10
   - can this article naturally lead a reader toward trying Vox Stella's synastry feature?
5. `Shelf life` out of 10
   - will the article still be useful in 3-6 months?

Suggested weighting:

1. intent match: `35%`
2. current attention: `20%`
3. data usability: `20%`
4. conversion fit: `15%`
5. shelf life: `10%`

## Important Product Rule

Do **not** let marketing pressure dictate the chart lane.

Use this editorial gate:

1. if data quality is strong enough, the pair can become a full synastry case study
2. if data quality is mixed, it can become a blog/demo article with clear disclaimers
3. if data quality is weak, use the celebrity only as a trend hook for an educational article, not as a formal engine output

That protects both product credibility and SEO usefulness.
