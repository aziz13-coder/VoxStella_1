## The Crash True Story: Reading the Mackenzie Shirilla Case Through Vox Stella's Forensic Astrology Engine

Netflix's `The Crash` revisits one of the most discussed vehicle-death cases of the past few years: the July 31, 2022 Strongsville, Ohio crash involving Mackenzie F. Shirilla, Dominic "Dom" Russo, and Davion Flanagan.

The public facts are now well documented. Shirilla was 17 at the time. Russo, 20, was her boyfriend. Flanagan, 19, was a friend and passenger. In the early morning hours after a graduation gathering, Shirilla drove a Toyota Camry with Russo and Flanagan inside. The car struck the PLIDCO building near Progress Drive and Alameda Drive in Strongsville. Russo and Flanagan died. Shirilla survived.

What first appeared to be a tragic crash became a murder case. The Cuyahoga County Prosecutor's Office reported the crash at approximately 5:30 a.m. and said the vehicle accelerated to about 100 mph before impact. The court record and later reporting describe a case built around speed, lack of braking, relationship conflict, prior threats, and the question of intent. Shirilla was convicted after a bench trial and sentenced to life in prison with first parole eligibility after 15 years.

For Vox Stella, this case is valuable because it tests several forensic astrology questions at once:

- Can the engine separate a vehicle crash from a generic accident?
- Can it recognize fatal pressure even when the driver survived?
- Can it see that the case was not random?
- Can it avoid overclaiming a specific relationship type when the chart supports "known person" but not every legal or emotional detail?

This is the kind of case that makes a forensic engine earn its structure.
