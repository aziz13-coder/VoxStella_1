## FUNDAMENTAL PRINCIPLES {#fundamental-principles}

### The Nature of Electional Astrology

Electional astrology is the art of choosing the most auspicious moment to begin an undertaking. However, it operates under critical limitations that must be understood from the outset.

### Three Core Tenets

**1. Elections Cannot Override Natal Promises**
- An election can only facilitate what is already indicated in the natal chart
- If a fortunate marriage is not signified in the nativity, no election can produce one
- Elections work WITH fate, not against it
- They can improve timing and reduce obstacles within what destiny allows

**2. Elections Must Align with Current Directions & Revolutions**
- The natal chart sets the fundamental possibilities
- Primary directions show WHEN certain events are likely
- Solar and lunar revolutions provide the annual and monthly context
- Elections choose the optimal DAY and HOUR within favorable periods
- Without favorable directions/revolutions, even a "perfect" election will fail

**3. The Celestial Constitution Must Match the Intended Action**
- The action must begin AT THE EXACT ELECTED MOMENT
- Everything must be prepared beforehand
- The virtue of an election perishes quickly as planets move through houses
- Use precise timing methods (sundial, stars) not unreliable clocks
- What begins fortunately in the 9th house becomes dangerous as it rotates into the 8th

## THE NECESSITY OF THE NATAL CHART {#natal-chart-requirement}

### Why the Natal Chart is Essential

**Morin's Primary Rule:**
"Every legitimate Election should be made by inspecting the nativity, its revolutions, directions, and the transits of the planets through the places of the nativity and its revolutions."

### Working Without a Natal Chart

If the natal chart is unavailable:
- The election becomes extremely unreliable
- One is working blindly, ignoring whether the native has:
  - Favorable or unfavorable significators for the matter
  - Current directions supporting or opposing the action
  - Natural affinity or antagonism to the undertaking
  
**DO NOT** attempt serious elections without the natal chart unless absolutely necessary. Even then, results are uncertain at best.

### The Hierarchy of Considerations

1. **Natal Chart** - The foundation (essential)
2. **Primary Directions** - When major events are activated (critical)
3. **Solar Revolution** - The year's themes and opportunities (very important)
4. **Lunar Revolution** - The month's specific conditions (important)
5. **Transits** - Daily triggers and conditions (important)
6. **Election Chart** - The specific moment chosen (final refinement)

## GENERAL RULES FOR ALL ELECTIONS {#general-rules}

### Rule 1: Identify the Relevant House

Determine which house governs the matter:
- **1st House**: Personal health, appearance, new beginnings
- **2nd House**: Financial matters, acquisitions, resources
- **3rd House**: Short journeys, siblings, communications, contracts
- **4th House**: Real estate, home, foundation projects, endings
- **5th House**: Creative projects, entertainment, speculation, children
- **6th House**: Employment, service, health maintenance, small animals
- **7th House**: Marriage, partnerships, open enemies, contracts
- **8th House**: Inheritance, surgery, transformation, other's resources
- **9th House**: Long journeys, higher education, publishing, legal matters
- **10th House**: Career, dignity, public actions, authority matters
- **11th House**: Friendships, organizations, hopes, social causes
- **12th House**: Retreat, spiritual work, hidden matters, hospitals

### Rule 2: Examine Natal Determinations

Check the natal chart for:
- The condition of the house ruler related to your matter
- Planets in that house
- Aspects to the house cusp
- The natural significator (e.g., Venus for marriage, Mars for surgery)

**Example**: For a marriage election:
- Examine the 7th house and its ruler in the natal chart
- Check Venus (natural marriage significator)
- Review any planets in the 7th
- Note aspects to the 7th house cusp

### Rule 3: Verify Current Directions

**The Action Should Not Be Taken Unless:**
- There is a favorable direction operating (e.g., ASC or MC to benefics)
- The directions support the specific type of action
- The directed significators are aspecting the house or planet related to your goal

**For seeking dignity/promotion:**
Elect when the ASC, MC, Sun, or Moon direct to:
- The 10th house of the radix
- A benefic planet in the 10th
- The ruler of the 10th
- Favorable aspects of these

### Rule 4: Consult the Annual Revolution (Solar Return)

The solar revolution must show:
- Favorable conditions for the type of action contemplated
- Appropriate planets angular or well-placed
- The significators of the matter unafflicted
- Supportive aspects between relevant houses

**Without a favorable solar revolution, even with good directions, success is limited.**

### Rule 5: Check the Lunar Revolution (Lunar Return)

Examine the lunar revolution preceding the action:
- The Moon's condition and aspects
- The rulers of relevant houses
- Angular planets and their nature
- Aspects between significators

### Rule 6: The Ascendant of the Election

**Critical Considerations:**

a) **Match to the Action's Nature:**
- **Quick/Temporary actions** → Cardinal/Mutable signs
- **Lasting/Permanent matters** → Fixed signs (marriage, building, foundations)
- **Journeys** → Avoid fixed signs, prefer mutable
- **Buildings/Marriage** → Prefer fixed signs, avoid mutable

b) **The Ascendant's Ruler Must:**
- Not be slow, retrograde, or afflicted by malefics
- Not be applying to malefics
- Ideally be the same planet that rules the ASC of the natal chart (very fortunate)
- Be well-disposed both in the election chart and the natal chart

c) **Diurnal/Nocturnal Considerations:**
- By day: Use diurnal signs (Aries, Gemini, Leo, Libra, Sagittarius, Aquarius)
- By night: Use nocturnal signs (Taurus, Cancer, Virgo, Scorpio, Capricorn, Pisces)
- Match the Sun (diurnal sign by day) and Moon (nocturnal sign by night)

### Rule 7: The Midheaven (MC)

The MC represents:
- The action itself
- Undertakings and their outcome
- Authority figures involved
- Public reputation connected to the action

**Ensure the MC is:**
- In a sign appropriate to the matter
- Well-aspected by benefics
- Its ruler is strong and favorably placed
- Not afflicted by malefics from cadent houses

### Rule 8: Angular Planets

**Benefics in Angles** (especially ASC and MC):
- Provide strength and fortune to the undertaking
- Should aspect the ASC or its ruler favorably
- Jupiter or Venus angular is highly beneficial

**Malefics in Angles**:
- Generally avoid, unless they are:
  - Well-dignified in the natal chart
  - Determined to fortunate matters natally
  - Appropriate to the action (Mars for battle, Saturn for building)

### Rule 9: Planetary Hours

While Morin dismissed planetary hours as "Arabian foolishness," traditional practice includes them:
- Elect the hour ruled by a planet favorable to your purpose
- Match the day ruler to the planetary nature of the action
- This is secondary to the chart's overall structure

### Rule 10: Avoid Afflictions

**Never elect when:**
- Malefics are in the 1st, 8th, or 12th houses (danger, death, illness)
- The ASC or its ruler is severely afflicted
- The significator of the matter is badly placed
- Multiple malefic aspects converge on relevant points

## THE MOON IN ELECTIONS {#moon-considerations}

### The Moon's Primary Importance

**Morin's View:**
"All the old astrologers give as much force to the Moon in Elections, as if they would have it pertain if not primarily, at least secondarily to the Election."

The Moon is secondary only to the Ascendant in importance for elections.

### Rule 11: The Moon's Natal Determination

**CRITICAL**: Check what the Moon signifies in the natal chart!
- If the Moon is the significator of death in the nativity, it cannot be made a significator of life in the election
- The Moon's natal house position and aspects constrain its use
- Never use the Moon for purposes contrary to its natal determination

### Rule 12: Avoid the Moon in Bad Natal Positions

**The native should not begin anything new when the Moon is:**
- In the radical place of Saturn or Mars
- In bad aspects to natal Saturn or Mars
- In a sign badly afflicted in the nativity
- In the 6th, 8th, or 12th house of the natal chart

### Rule 13: The Moon's Position in the Election Chart

**Avoid when the Moon is:**
- Afflicted by Saturn or Mars (conjunction, square, opposition)
- In the 6th, 8th, or 12th houses of the election chart
- Possibly the 1st house (due to its coldness - though Morin questions this)
- Combust (within 15° of the Sun)
- In Via Combusta (15° Libra to 15° Scorpio)

### Rule 14: Dangerous Moon Applications

**Avoid these specific applications:**
- Moon applying to Mars from the domiciles of Venus (Taurus/Libra)
- Moon applying to Jupiter from the domiciles of Mercury (Gemini/Virgo)  
- Moon applying to the Sun from the domiciles of Saturn (Capricorn/Aquarius)

### Rule 15: Moon-Benefic Conjunctions

**Moon conjunct Saturn or Jupiter:**
- Good if the Moon is increasing in light (waxing) and swift in motion
- Bad if the Moon has little light (waning) and is slow

**Moon conjunct Mars or Venus:**
- The opposite applies: better when waning/slow
- Worse when waxing/swift

### Rule 16: Moon's Aspects to Natal Planets

The Moon's aspects to natal planets show who and what will help or hinder:

**Favorable aspects (trine, sextile) to natal planets:**
- Indicates it's safe to act with persons that planet represents
- Example: Moon trine natal Mars (if Mars rules or is in the 7th) = favorable for marriage
- Consider both the planet's natural significations and its natal house rulership

**Adverse aspects (square, opposition) to natal planets:**
- Indicates difficulties with persons/matters that planet represents
- Acts in a contrary manner to the trine/sextile interpretation

### Rule 17: The Moon in the Relevant House

**Ideal placement:**
- The Moon in the house related to the election's purpose
- In a place that is fortunate in the natal chart
- Where it also signifies the matter at hand natally
- Aspecting the ASC or ruling it

**Example**: For a journey, Moon in the 9th house of the election, in a position that was fortunate in the natal 9th.

### Rule 18: Moon and Retrograde Planets

**Never** have the Moon:
- Conjunct a retrograde planet
- Applying to a retrograde planet

**Result**: The undertaking will be destroyed or fail completely.

### Rule 19: Priority - ASC or Moon?

When you cannot fortify both:
- **By day**: Prioritize the ASC over the Moon
- **By night**: Prioritize the Moon over the ASC

**Additional considerations:**
- If the Moon is under the Earth by day, it's better for the ASC
- If the Moon is above the Earth by night, it's better for the ASC

### Rule 20: The Lunation Preceding the Election

**Examine the New Moon or Full Moon before the election:**
- Its position in the election chart should be fortunate
- It should fall in an appropriate house
- Its aspects should be harmonious
- This sets the "seed" conditions for the action

### Rule 21: Moon Separating and Applying

**Dangerous separation:**
When the Moon separates from Mars or opposition to the Sun, AND applies to a malefic (especially one determined to evil in the nativity):
- This is a very evil time
- Avoid starting anything important
- Worse if applying by square or opposition

### Rule 22: Judging Outcomes from the Moon

**From the lunation + Moon's next application:**

| Last Lunation Place | Moon Applies to | Result |
|-------------------|-----------------|---------|
| Good | Benefic | Fortunate beginning to fortunate end |
| Evil | Malefic | Unfortunate beginning to unfortunate end |
| Good | Malefic | Good beginning, evil ending |
| Evil | Benefic | Evil beginning, good ending |

## SPECIFIC ELECTIONS BY TYPE {#specific-elections}

### MARRIAGE ELECTIONS

**Prerequisites (Non-negotiable):**

1. **The natal chart must promise a fortunate marriage**
   - Well-placed 7th house ruler
   - Venus (for men) or Mars (for women) favorably placed
   - Few afflictions to the 7th house or its significators
   
2. **Current directions must support marriage**
   - ASC, MC, Sun, or Moon directing to the 7th house
   - Directions to Venus, or to the 7th house ruler
   - Favorable aspects from directed planets to marriage significators

3. **The solar revolution must indicate marriage potential**
   - 7th house activated
   - Venus or 7th ruler prominent and well-aspected
   - No severe afflictions to marriage significators

**Election Chart Requirements:**

**The Ascendant:**
- **Must be in a FIXED sign** (Taurus, Leo, Scorpio, Aquarius)
  - For permanence and stability
  - Avoid mutable signs which indicate impermanence
- Should be the same sign as the natal ASC if possible (very fortunate)
- Or match the 7th house cusp of the natal chart
- The ASC ruler should be:
  - Dignified and strong
  - Aspecting Venus or the 7th ruler favorably
  - Not retrograde or afflicted

**The 7th House:**
- Place benefics (Jupiter or Venus) in the 7th house if possible
- The 7th house ruler must be strong, direct, and well-aspected
- Match the 7th cusp to favorable points in the natal chart
- Avoid malefics in or ruling the 7th

**Venus (Primary Marriage Significator):**
- Strong by sign and house
- Preferably angular (1st, 4th, 7th, or 10th house)
- Aspecting the ASC, Moon, or their rulers favorably
- Not retrograde, combust, or afflicted
- In favorable aspect to Jupiter
- Dignified (in Taurus, Libra, or Pisces is ideal)

**The Moon:**
- Check its natal determination first - ensure it's not a death significator
- In the 7th house, or in favorable aspect to it
- Applying to Venus or Jupiter by trine or sextile
- Not afflicted by Saturn or Mars
- Waxing (increasing in light) for growth
- Not in the 6th, 8th, or 12th houses
- Not applying to retrograde planets

**Jupiter:**
- Should be prominent (angular or aspecting ASC/7th)
- In favorable aspect to Venus and the Moon
- Not afflicted or in detriment/fall

**Malefics (Saturn and Mars):**
- Keep OUT of angles, especially the 1st and 7th
- Avoid aspects to Venus, the 7th ruler, or the ASC
- Best placed in succedent or cadent houses (2nd, 3rd, 5th, 6th, 9th, 11th, 12th)

**Timing:**
- Day ruled by Venus (Friday) is traditional
- Hour ruled by Venus or Jupiter
- Waxing Moon phase (New Moon to Full Moon)
- Avoid Mercury retrograde if documents are being signed
- Avoid Venus retrograde at all costs

**Additional Considerations:**
- The 11th house should be well-placed (friends supporting the marriage)
- The 4th house fortified (foundation of the union)
- The 2nd and 8th houses should be favorable (financial harmony)
- Check both partners' charts if available

**Absolutely Avoid:**
- Malefics in the 1st or 7th houses
- Saturn or Mars afflicting Venus
- Moon void of course
- Moon applying to malefics
- Eclipse within 2 weeks of the marriage
- Any planet retrograde in angular houses

### SURGERY AND MEDICAL PROCEDURES

**Understanding Surgical Elections:**

Surgery is a controlled Mars activity - cutting the body with iron. Elections must balance:
- Mars (cutting, action, courage)
- The Moon (the body, fluids, vitality)
- The Ascendant (the physical body)
- The 8th house (surgery, crisis, transformation)
- The afflicted part (via sign and house)

**Prerequisites:**

1. **Check the Natal Chart:**
   - Identify the afflicted body part by house/sign
   - Determine if surgery is indicated by primary directions
   - Check the 6th house (illness), 8th house (surgery), and 12th house (hospitalization)

2. **Current Directions and Revolutions:**
   - Should indicate health concerns but also potential for healing
   - Avoid periods with severe afflictions to the ASC or 6th/8th/12th houses
   - Look for favorable directions to the healing significators

**Election Chart Requirements:**

**Rule 26: The Moon and Body Parts**
**NEVER cut any bodily part when the Moon is in the sign ruling that part:**

Body Part | Avoid Moon in Sign
----------|-------------------
Head, Face | Aries
Neck, Throat | Taurus
Arms, Hands, Lungs | Gemini
Chest, Stomach, Breasts | Cancer
Heart, Spine, Upper Back | Leo
Intestines, Abdomen | Virgo
Kidneys, Lower Back | Libra
Genitals, Reproductive Organs | Scorpio
Hips, Thighs, Liver | Sagittarius
Knees, Bones, Skin | Capricorn
Calves, Ankles, Circulation | Aquarius
Feet, Lymphatic System | Pisces

**The Moon:**
- **Waning** (decreasing in light) for surgery - to remove, reduce
- In Water signs (Cancer, Scorpio, Pisces) for procedures involving fluids
- NOT in the 1st, 6th, 8th, or 12th houses
- Not afflicted by malefics
- Applying to benefics (Jupiter or Venus)
- Not applying to Mars or Saturn
- Not in the sign ruling the part to be operated on (Rule 26)
- Swift in motion

**The Ascendant:**
- Strong and unafflicted
- Ruler in good condition
- Not in a sign ruling the affected body part
- Dignified and supported by benefics
- Ideally in an earth or water sign for grounding/healing

**Mars:**
- Necessary for surgery but must be carefully placed
- Dignified (Aries, Scorpio, or Capricorn) if possible
- NOT in the 1st, 6th, 8th, or 12th houses
- Not afflicting the ASC, its ruler, or the Moon
- Angular but not malefically aspecting key points
- Direct in motion (not retrograde)

**The 6th House (Illness):**
- Should show the condition but not be catastrophically afflicted
- Avoid malefics here unless well-dignified
- The ruler should be in a position to improve

**The 8th House (Surgery/Crisis):**
- Check carefully - this is the house of surgery
- Its ruler should not be in the 8th or 12th
- Avoid malefics here badly placed
- Should indicate survival and recovery, not death

**Jupiter:**
- The Great Benefic and healer
- Place Jupiter in the ASC if possible
- Or aspecting the ASC favorably
- Trine or sextile the Moon
- In a succedent house (2nd, 5th, 8th, 11th) for support

**Saturn:**
- Keep away from the ASC, Moon, and their rulers
- Can be in the 6th if well-dignified (chronic condition improving)
- Should not afflict any vital significators

**The Physician/Surgeon:**
- Check the 10th house (authority, practitioner)
- Should be well-placed and supported
- Ruler of the 10th should aspect the ASC or its ruler favorably

**Timing:**
- Mars day (Tuesday) for courage but be cautious
- Moon hour for general surgery
- Waning Moon phase
- Avoid lunar eclipses within 2 weeks
- Morning rather than evening (vital forces stronger)

**Purgatives and Cleansings (Rule 27):**
- Moon in water signs (Cancer, Scorpio, Pisces)
- Scorpio or Pisces preferred
- Ruler of ASC connected to a planet UNDER the Earth (pulling downward)
- The ruler should be APPLYING to the sub-earth planet
- Avoid Moon conjunct Jupiter (weakens purgative)
- Avoid Moon in Leo or earth signs

**Absolutely Avoid:**
- Moon in the sign of the body part being cut (Rule 26)
- Moon applying to Mars or Saturn
- Malefics in angles
- Eclipses within 2 weeks
- Mars retrograde
- Moon void of course
- Moon in the 8th house
- ASC severely afflicted

### STARTING A BUSINESS

**Prerequisites:**

1. **Natal Chart Must Show Business Success:**
   - Strong 10th house (career, public reputation)
   - Well-placed 2nd house (income, resources)
   - Favorable 11th house (allies, networking, profits)
   - Jupiter and Venus well-disposed for prosperity
   - Mercury strong for commerce and contracts

2. **Current Directions Support Business:**
   - ASC or MC directing to benefics
   - Directions to the rulers of the 2nd, 10th, or 11th houses
   - Part of Fortune well-aspected by direction

3. **Solar Revolution Favorable:**
   - 10th house strong and aspected
   - 2nd and 11th houses favorable
   - Jupiter angular or in the 2nd/10th/11th
   - Mercury well-placed for business matters

**Election Chart Requirements:**

**The Ascendant:**
- **Cardinal sign** for initiative and action (Aries, Cancer, Libra, Capricorn)
  - OR **Fixed sign** if long-term stability is most important (Taurus, Leo, Scorpio, Aquarius)
- Earth sign for material businesses (Taurus, Virgo, Capricorn)
- Fire sign for leadership/innovative businesses (Aries, Leo, Sagittarius)
- The ASC ruler should be:
  - Strong by dignity
  - Angular or in a succedent house
  - Aspecting the 2nd, 10th, or 11th house rulers favorably
  - Not retrograde, combusted, or afflicted

**The 10th House (Career & Reputation):**
- Most important house for business success
- Jupiter or Venus in the 10th is excellent
- Ruler of the 10th strong and well-aspected
- Sun in the 10th gives authority and recognition
- Should aspect the ASC positively

**The 2nd House (Income & Resources):**
- Jupiter or Venus here brings financial blessing
- Ruler of the 2nd in the 1st, 10th, or 11th is favorable
- Should be in good aspect to the ASC and MC
- Part of Fortune in the 2nd is traditional and favorable

**The 11th House (Profits, Allies, Networking):**
- Strong and well-aspected
- Jupiter here is the traditional profit indicator
- Ruler of the 11th should aspect the ASC or 10th
- Venus here can bring helpful partnerships

**Jupiter:**
- Should be ANGULAR (especially 1st, 10th, or 11th house)
- In favorable aspect to the ASC, MC, and their rulers
- Trine or sextile to Venus
- In dignity (Sagittarius or Pisces) or exaltation (Cancer) if possible
- Direct in motion

**Mercury:**
- Essential for commerce, contracts, and communication
- Should be direct (not retrograde!)
- Well-placed (1st, 3rd, 10th, or 11th house)
- In good aspect to Jupiter and the ASC
- Dignified if possible (Gemini or Virgo)

**The Sun:**
- In the 10th house for authority and recognition
- Aspecting the ASC ruler favorably
- Not afflicted by malefics
- In a sign where it has dignity (Leo) or is comfortable

**The Moon:**
- In the 10th, 11th, or 2nd house
- Waxing for growth
- Applying to Jupiter or Venus
- Not afflicted by Saturn or Mars
- Not in the 6th, 8th, or 12th
- In a sign compatible with the business type

**Venus:**
- Favorable for businesses involving beauty, luxury, art, social matters
- Trine or sextile Jupiter (the classic prosperity aspect)
- Not in detriment (Aries or Scorpio) or fall (Virgo)
- Well-aspected to the 2nd and 10th houses

**Saturn:**
- Useful for businesses requiring discipline, structure, real estate, agriculture
- Should be dignified if used prominently
- In the 2nd or 10th only if well-placed and appropriate to business type
- Otherwise, keep in succedent or cadent houses
- Should not afflict Jupiter, Venus, or the ASC

**Mars:**
- Useful for competitive businesses, sports, military, technology
- Should be dignified if prominent
- Avoid in the 2nd house (spends money)
- Can be in the 10th if dignified and business requires Mars energy
- Should not afflict the ASC, Moon, or benefics

**Part of Fortune:**
- Calculate carefully for the day/night chart
- Ideally in the 1st, 2nd, 10th, or 11th house
- Aspected by Jupiter or Venus
- Not afflicted by malefics

**Business Type Considerations:**

**For Retail/Commerce:**
- Emphasize Mercury, the 3rd and 7th houses
- Cardinal signs for customer interaction

**For Manufacturing/Production:**
- Emphasize Saturn, Mars, the 6th and 10th houses
- Fixed signs for stability

**For Professional Services:**
- Emphasize Mercury, the Sun, the 10th house
- Mercury and Sun well-dignified

**For Creative/Entertainment:**
- Emphasize Venus, the Sun, the 5th house
- Leo or Libra rising favorable

**For Technology/Innovation:**
- Emphasize Mercury, Uranus (if using modern planets), 11th house
- Aquarius or Gemini rising favorable

**Timing:**
- Thursday (Jupiter day) is traditional for business
- Wednesday (Mercury day) for commerce
- Sunday (Sun day) for prestige businesses
- Jupiter or Sun hour
- Waxing Moon
- Avoid Mercury retrograde for signing documents or launching
- New Moon to First Quarter phase for growth

**Absolutely Avoid:**
- Mercury retrograde (delays, communication problems)
- Malefics in the 2nd or 10th houses (unless very well-dignified)
- ASC ruler afflicted or retrograde
- Moon void of course
- Saturn or Mars afflicting Jupiter
- 10th house severely afflicted

### SIGNING CONTRACTS

**Understanding Contract Elections:**

Contracts are Mercury activities involving the 3rd house (documents, communication) and often the 7th house (partnerships, agreements). The 9th house may be involved for legal contracts.

**Prerequisites:**

1. **Natal Chart Considerations:**
   - Mercury's condition in the natal chart
   - 3rd house and its ruler
   - 7th house if partnership contract
   - 9th house if legal contract
   - Current directions should not severely afflict these areas

2. **Type of Contract Determines House Focus:**
   - Business partnership → 7th house
   - Purchase/sale → 2nd house
   - Real estate → 4th house
   - Employment → 6th or 10th house
   - Legal agreement → 9th house
   - Loan/debt → 8th house

**Election Chart Requirements:**

**The Ascendant:**
- Mutable sign (Gemini, Virgo, Sagittarius, Pisces) for contracts involving communication
- Fixed sign (Taurus, Leo, Scorpio, Aquarius) for long-term binding agreements
- The ASC ruler should be:
  - Not retrograde (movement forward)
  - Applying to the ruler of the house governing the contract type
  - Well-aspected and dignified
  - In favorable aspect to Mercury

**Mercury (Primary Significator):**
- **MUST NOT BE RETROGRADE** - this is absolutely critical
  - Retrograde Mercury = delays, miscommunications, reversals, regrets
  - Wait for Mercury to go direct if possible
- Strong by house and sign
- In the 3rd house (documents) or aspecting it
- In the 1st, 3rd, 9th, or 10th house ideally
- In Gemini or Virgo (dignity) if possible
- Applying to Jupiter or Venus
- Not afflicted by Saturn or Mars
- Not combusted (within 8° of Sun)
- In favorable aspect to the ASC and its ruler
- Swift in motion

**The 3rd House (Documents & Agreements):**
- Well-aspected and unafflicted
- Ruler of the 3rd should be strong
- Benefics in the 3rd are favorable
- Should aspect the ASC or the relevant house for contract type

**The 7th House (If Partnership Contract):**
- Strong and well-aspected
- Benefics in or ruling the 7th
- Ruler of the 7th in good condition
- In favorable aspect to the ASC
- Venus well-placed for harmonious partnerships

**The 9th House (If Legal Contract):**
- Strong and unafflicted
- Jupiter dignified and well-placed
- Ruler of the 9th favorably aspecting ASC and Mercury
- No malefics afflicting unless they are well-dignified

**The Moon:**
- Not void of course (no more applying aspects before changing signs)
  - Void of course Moon = nothing comes of the matter
- Waxing for growth and increase
- Applying to Mercury favorably
- In the 3rd, 7th, or 9th house depending on contract type
- Not afflicted by malefics
- Not in the 6th, 8th, or 12th houses

**Jupiter:**
- Favorable aspect to Mercury (wisdom in agreements)
- In the 9th house for legal authority
- Aspecting the ASC and relevant house
- Not afflicted or in detriment/fall

**Saturn:**
- Can be useful for binding, long-term contracts
- Should be dignified if prominent
- In the 3rd or 9th only if well-placed
- Should not afflict Mercury, Moon, or ASC
- Retrograde Saturn = delays and reviews

**Venus:**
- Favorable for partnership contracts
- Should aspect Mercury and the 7th house
- Not in detriment (Aries, Scorpio) or fall (Virgo)
- Harmonious aspects to the ASC

**Mars:**
- Generally avoid Mars afflicting Mercury (disputes)
- Can be useful for competitive contracts if well-dignified
- Should not be in the 3rd, 7th, or 9th if afflicted
- Direct motion preferred

**Special Contract Types:**

**Real Estate Contracts (4th House Focus):**
- Fixed sign rising
- 4th house strong and well-aspected

## URGENT ELECTIONS {#urgent-elections}

### When Time is Limited

Sometimes you must act quickly and cannot wait for ideal conditions. Morin provides simplified rules for urgent situations:

**Rule 21: Minimum Requirements for Urgent Elections**

"When an Election is urgent, and there is not time to check that all the significators are well suited, then in fact it must at least be adjusted so that the Ruler of the ASC is well suited to it, and a benefic is in an angle with a fortunate aspect to the ASC."

### Urgent Election Hierarchy

**Priority 1: The Ascendant**
- Choose a sign appropriate to the matter
- Ensure its ruler is as strong as possible
- Have the ruler aspect the house of the matter

**Priority 2: Benefics in Angles**
- Place Jupiter or Venus in an angle (1st, 4th, 7th, or 10th)
- Have them aspect the ASC favorably (trine or sextile)
- This provides general protection and support

**Priority 3: The Moon (If Night) or Sun (If Day)**
- Keep the Moon unafflicted by malefics
- Have the Moon apply to a benefic if possible
- Place in a cadent house if you cannot fortify it

**Rule 22: Hasty Elections with Focus on Speed**

"In every Election that requires haste, if you cannot fortify the Moon, then put Jupiter or Venus in the ASC, or in the MC, but put the Moon in a cadent house. And beware of letting a malefic aspect the ASC, especially by a malign aspect."

**Minimum Action Steps:**

1. **Calculate the ASC for the time you must act**
2. **Check if the ASC is in an appropriate sign**
   - If not, wait a few hours for it to change if possible
3. **Locate the ASC ruler**
   - Is it retrograde? Afflicted? Weak?
   - If yes, wait if at all possible
4. **Find benefics (Jupiter and Venus)**
   - Can you time it so one is angular?
   - At minimum, avoid having them cadent AND afflicted
5. **Check the Moon**
   - Is it void of course? Wait if possible
   - Is it applying to malefics? Try to avoid this window
   - Is it in the house of the matter? Good if so

**Rule 23: Minimum Standard**

"In an urgent Election, with no time given for checking how far all the significators can be appropriately adjusted, at least adjust the ASC and the MC so that they are well adapted; and let there be benefics in the angles favorably aspecting the ASC, or the MC, or their rulers."

### What You Can Sacrifice in Urgency

**Can be relaxed:**
- The Moon's exact position (just avoid severe afflictions)
- Perfect house placement of all planets
- Specific aspects between all significators
- Planetary hours
- Specific days of the week
- Fixed stars

**Cannot be compromised:**
- Malefics in the ASC (especially Mars or Saturn)
- Severely afflicted ASC ruler
- Retrograde ASC ruler (for things requiring forward motion)
- Moon void of course (for initiating new matters)
- Eclipse within days of the action

### Emergency Protocol

If you absolutely must act immediately:

1. **Check the current moment:**
   - What sign is rising?
   - Where is its ruler?
   - Where are malefics?

2. **If the moment is terrible:**
   - Wait 2 hours for ASC to change signs
   - Wait 13 degrees of rising for ASC to move past exact conjunction with a malefic
   - Wait until the Moon finishes a malefic aspect

3. **If you truly cannot wait:**
   - Focus your will and energy
   - Remember: "the cooperation of man with the stars [is needed], and the lazier or the more careless he is, the less can be hoped for from the stars. But the more diligent, and stronger, and wiser he is, the more can be hoped for."
   - Your personal effort can compensate for imperfect timing

4. **Morin's Wisdom (Rule 23):**
   "Remember that the cooperation of man with the stars [is needed], and the lazier or the more careless he is, the less can be hoped for from the stars. But the more diligent, and stronger, and wiser he is, the more can be hoped for."

### The Bare Minimum

If you can achieve ONLY these, you have a workable (if imperfect) election:

1. ASC in an appropriate sign
2. ASC ruler not retrograde
3. ASC ruler not in the 8th or 12th house
4. One benefic in an angle
5. Moon not void of course

These five points give you a foundation that won't actively harm your endeavor.

## PRACTICAL TIMING CONSIDERATIONS {#practical-timing}

### Rule 4: The Critical Importance of Exact Timing

**Morin's Fourth Rule (Most Important):**

"The Fourth and principal rule of this doctrine in practice consists of this—that those things that are going to be done are started at the moment of the figure or of the elected celestial constitution."

**Why Exact Timing Matters:**

The virtue of an election exists ONLY at the moment the chart is calculated for:
- Planets move continuously through houses
- What is in the fortunate 9th house will soon be in the dangerous 8th
- What is fortunate in the 1st house crosses into the unfortunate 12th
- The ASC changes by 1 degree every 4 minutes
- A delay of 30 minutes can completely change the rising sign

**Practical Implementation:**

1. **Prepare Everything Beforehand:**
   - Have all documents ready
   - Have all persons present
   - Have all materials assembled
   - Have all arrangements made
   - Remove all obstacles to immediate action

2. **Wait for the Exact Moment:**
   - Do not act 5 minutes early "just in case"
   - Do not act 10 minutes late because someone was delayed
   - The moment is THE MOMENT

3. **Use Accurate Time Methods:**
   - **DO NOT use common clocks** (often wrong by 30+ minutes in Morin's time, and even now can be off)
   - Use the Sun's position (sundial)
   - Use star positions (sidereal time)
   - In modern times: use accurate atomic clock time
   - Use astronomical software that gives exact planetary positions

4. **The Action Must Begin at the Moment:**
   - For a journey: step outside, begin walking, mount the horse/enter the car
   - For a marriage: the vow or binding words are spoken
   - For a business: the door opens, the transaction begins
   - For a contract: the pen touches paper to sign
   - For surgery: the first incision

### The Equation of Time

Morin includes an appendix on the Equation of Time because:
- Sundial time (apparent solar time) differs from mean time
- The difference varies from -16 minutes to +14 minutes throughout the year
- Accurate elections require adjusting for this difference

**Modern Application:**
Use astronomical software that calculates exact planetary positions for your location, accounting for:
- Time zone
- Daylight Saving Time
- Longitude of location
- Equation of Time (built into modern calculations)

### House System Considerations

Morin used:
- **Equal houses** in some contexts
- **Regiomontanus** in others
- Often calculated houses from the Midheaven mathematically

**Modern Practice:**
- Use the house system that works best for you
- Be consistent
- Placidus is most common
- Whole Sign has historical precedent
- The principles apply regardless of house system

### Retrogradation Considerations

**Planets going retrograde soon after the election:**
- If the ASC ruler goes retrograde within days, reconsider
- If Mercury goes retrograde (and Mercury is important), reconsider
- If a key significator goes retrograde, reconsider

**Planets stationing (standing still):**
- Very slow, nearly stopped
- Considered weak and unpredictable
- Avoid having ASC ruler or key significators stationing

### Planetary Speed

**Swift planets are generally better:**
- Show things happening quickly
- Indicate vitality and energy
- Especially important for the Moon and ASC ruler

**Slow planets can indicate:**
- Delays
- Sluggishness
- Things taking longer than expected
- For Saturn, this is natural
- For Mars, this is problematic

### The Lunar Void of Course

While Morin doesn't explicitly mention it by name, the principle is embedded in his rules:

**Void of Course Moon:**
- Moon makes no more applying aspects before changing signs
- Traditional interpretation: "nothing comes of the matter"
- Avoid for elections where you want results

**When Void of Course is Acceptable:**
- Matters you want to end with no consequences
- Routine, ongoing activities
- Things you want to "go nowhere"

### Planetary Aspects and Timing

**Applying Aspects:**
- Show what is coming
- More important than separating aspects
- The Moon's applying aspect shows the immediate outcome

**Separating Aspects:**
- Show what is passing away
- Less important for elections
- Can show what led up to this moment

**Exact Aspects:**
- Most powerful
- Within 1 degree is strong
- Partile (same degree and minute) is strongest

## COMMON PITFALLS & LIMITATIONS {#pitfalls}

### The Fundamental Limitation

**Elections Cannot Override Fate:**

This is Morin's most important teaching about elections:

"For any Election, such as for making a fortunate marriage contract, these two things are required: First, that a fortunate marriage was signified in the nativity. Second, that at the time of the contract there are fortunate directions, Revolutions, and transits of the significators. For with one of these lacking, the election will not be produced, however much it may seem to be opportune and favorable."

**What This Means:**

1. **If your natal chart does not promise something, no election can give it to you**
   - Not promised marriage → No election makes it fortunate
   - Not promised wealth → No election makes business successful
   - Not promised long life → No election prevents early death

2. **If current directions and revolutions do not support an action, elections cannot force it**
   - Directions to the 8th house → Surgery may be necessary regardless of election
   - Bad directions to the 10th → Career advancement unlikely even with perfect election
   - Saturn return → Limitations and lessons come regardless of elections

3. **Elections work within the constraints of fate**
   - They choose the BEST possible time within available options
   - They minimize harm and maximize benefit WITHIN LIMITS
   - They cannot create something from nothing

### Degrees of Success

Morin explains that elections can have different degrees of effectiveness:

**Highest Degree:**
- Natal promise is strong
- Current directions are favorable
- Revolution is supportive
- Election is perfect
- Result: "A great and swift effect will result, that was conformable on both sides"

**Middle Degree:**
- Some factors strong, others moderate
- Result: "The effect will be middling"

**Lowest Degree:**
- Natal promise is weak
- Or directions are barely favorable
- Or election is imperfect
- Result: "The effect will be less, or it will only be apparent"

**Morin's Formula:**
"The highest degree on the one side with the lowest degree on the other side, will produce more than the least effect, and with the middle degree more than the middle effect, especially if the highest degree belongs to the natal chart."

**Translation:** The natal chart is most important. A strong natal promise with a weak election is better than a weak natal promise with a perfect election.

### Common Mistakes

**1. Ignoring the Natal Chart**
- "This will be a perfect wedding chart!"
- But: the person's natal chart shows difficult or no marriage
- Result: Wedding may happen, but marriage will be as the natal chart promised

**2. Ignoring Current Directions**
- "I found the perfect business chart!"
- But: the person has directions indicating loss or failure this year
- Result: Business starts but struggles or fails

**3. Perfectionism Leading to Inaction**
- "I can't find the perfect chart!"
- Waiting indefinitely for perfection
- Result: The opportunity passes, or you act anyway at a worse time

**Morin's Advice:** "it rarely happens in Elections that you can adapt all the significators as you would like; but always look to see what can be done that is better and more appropriate for your goal"

**4. Using Elections Without Personal Effort**

Morin emphasizes: "Remember that the cooperation of man with the stars [is needed], and the lazier or the more careless he is, the less can be hoped for from the stars. But the more diligent, and stronger, and wiser he is, the more can be hoped for."

- Elections are not magic
- They do not replace hard work
- They do not compensate for poor planning
- They optimize timing for those who act with wisdom and diligence

**5. Overreliance on Minor Factors**
- Obsessing over planetary hours
- Fretting over minor aspects
- Ignoring major afflictions while perfecting minor details

**Focus on the essentials first:**
1. Natal chart supports action
2. Directions are favorable (or at least not severely adverse)
3. Revolution supports action
4. ASC and its ruler are strong
5. Relevant house and its ruler are strong
6. Moon is well-placed
7. Benefics support, malefics don't harm

Then refine with minor factors if time allows.

**6. Ignoring Practical Constraints**

Morin warns about this:
"in things that are not within our power, as in addressing a King on a selected day and hour, that will be more difficult in many cases, and it can scarcely be done except by the dear mediations of friends, who offer access to the King at that very hour."

**Reality:**
- You may not be able to control when you meet with an authority figure
- Surgery may need to happen when the surgeon is available
- Business opportunities have time constraints
- Marriage requires the other person's cooperation

**Solution:** Do the best you can within practical constraints. Arrange circumstances beforehand through "mediations of friends" (calling in favors, advance planning, flexibility).

**7. Attempting Elections for Actions Not in Your Power**

You cannot elect for:
- What someone else will do independently
- Events controlled by nature or others
- Things already set in motion by earlier causes
- Actions that don't begin at a specific moment you control

**You can elect for:**
- Actions YOU initiate at a specific moment
- Beginnings that are in your control
- Voluntary actions
- Matters where you control the timing

### Recognizing When Not to Act

Sometimes the best "election" is to NOT act:

**Don't act when:**
1. Natal chart strongly contradicts the action
2. Current directions are severely adverse
3. Revolution shows disaster
4. All election attempts show severe afflictions
5. Multiple malefics converge on the action's significators
6. The matter would harm others unethically
7. Your own wisdom says "wait"

**Morin's approach:** He predicted things that would happen to princes and magnates "to the very day," but only when conditions were clear. When conditions were unclear or adverse, he recognized limitations.

### The Problem of Free Will

Morin was concerned about maintaining the Catholic doctrine of free will while practicing astrology.

**His position:**
- Elections do not violate free will
- They work with free will, not against it
- The person still chooses to act
- The stars provide favorable conditions
- The person's effort and wisdom matter greatly

**The balance:**
- Some things are fated (shown in natal chart)
- Within those parameters, you have choice
- Elections help you choose WHEN to exercise free will
- But they cannot override fundamental fate

### The Question of Multiple Elections

**What if many people elect the same moment?**

Morin addresses this critique:
- Each person's natal chart is different
- The same moment affects each person differently
- The election must be adapted to EACH individual's nativity
- What's perfect for one person may be terrible for another

**Example:** A time when Jupiter is in the 10th:
- If Jupiter rules the native's 10th natally → excellent
- If Jupiter rules the native's 8th natally → potentially dangerous
- Same chart, completely different meanings

**This is why you cannot use "universal" elections without checking each person's nativity.**

### Modern Considerations

**Morin didn't have to consider:**
- Time zones (use local time for the location of the action)
- Daylight Saving Time (be very careful with this!)
- International travel (the election is for where the action BEGINS)
- Digital/instant communications (when does an email "begin"?)
- Long-distance relationships (whose chart is primary?)

**Modern applications require judgment:**
- For digital actions, consider when YOU press "send" or "submit"
- For international matters, consider the primary location
- For joint actions, consider both people's charts if possible
- For corporate actions, consider the company chart AND key decision-maker's chart

### The Limits of Astrological Knowledge

**Morin's honest admission:**
"Although this particular prediction may be difficult... yet it is far from being said to be impossible, and I hope that by the Theory—now that it is properly constructed—such predictions will be very frequent among those of us who have understood the doctrine hitherto set forth by us."

Even Morin admitted:
- He couldn't always predict to the day
- He was too busy developing theory to practice extensively
- Perfect elections are difficult to find
- The astrologer needs time and freedom to calculate properly

**For the modern practitioner:**
- Don't expect perfection from yourself
- Do the best you can with available time and tools
- Recognize when you're in over your head
- Refer complex elections to experts when needed
- Keep learning and improving

### A Final Warning

**Morin was concerned about:**
- Astrologers making elections without knowing the natal chart (fraud)
- Astrologers promising things that can't be delivered (fraud)
- People relying entirely on elections without effort (folly)
- Elections being used unethically (harm)

**His standards:**
1. Always require the natal chart
2. Always check directions and revolutions
3. Never promise what the stars don't show
4. Remember that human effort matters as much as timing
5. Use wisdom and prudence, not just technical skill

**The proper use of elections:**
- Is humble about limitations
- Acknowledges fate while optimizing timing
- Requires effort and wisdom from the native
- Works within ethical bounds
- Serves to help, not to control or manipulate

## PRACTICAL EXAMPLES FROM MORIN'S PRACTICE

### Example 1: The Journey to Antibes (1646)

**Context:**
- Count of Chavigny invited Morin on a 500-mile journey
- Morin carefully examined both his own and the Count's charts
- He saw indicators of ill health for himself that year
- He still agreed to go but elected the moment carefully

**Natal Indicators (Count of Chavigny):**
- Sun and Mercury ruled the 9th house
- Moon in the 9th house
- Jupiter ruled ASC and 3rd, conjunct Sun and Mercury, trine 9th cusp
- Mars ruled Sun/Mercury/Jupiter, trine the Moon
- Venus in 3rd, with Saturn (her ruler) as significators of travel
- **Conclusion:** Many journeys and long travels signified

**Primary Direction (1645-1646):**
- Significator of MC directed to sextile Mars (8° Scorpio)
- Presaged labors, travels, and dignity
- Mercury (ruler of 9th) directed to partile sextile of Jupiter (ruler of ASC)
- **Strong indicators of a fortunate long journey**

**Solar Revolution (1646):**
- Venus's radical place in the ASC
- Mercury and Sun (rulers of 9th) in 1st with Mars (ruler of revolution 9th)
- Moon returned to radical place, ruled by Mercury
- Moon trine Venus (ruler of 3rd) and Saturn (ruler of ASC)
- Jupiter (ruler of MC and co-ruler of 9th) in 4th, mutual reception with Mercury
- Jupiter trine the ASC
- **Very strong for long and auspicious journey**

**Lunar Revolution (April 1646, preceding departure):**
- Moon ruled ASC
- Moon trine Sun (ruler of radical 9th) and Saturn (ruler of revolution 9th)
- Moon trine Venus and Mercury (Moon's ruler)
- Mutual reception by exaltation between Moon and Mercury
- Jupiter in ASC, exalted, ruler of MC
- Jupiter partile trine MC
- Jupiter sextile Sun and Saturn (rulers of 9th)
- **"Nothing more fortunate could be selected for a long and auspicious journey"**

**The Election (May 9, 1646, 7:09 AM, Paris):**

Morin's reasoning:
- Departure must be by day
- Didn't want Sun in 1st (would put Saturn and Mars in 1st or 12th, causing illness)
- Didn't want malefics in 10th, 9th, or 8th (would harm actions, journey, or threaten life)
- Chose to put Sun, Saturn, Venus, and Mars in 11th
- Mars strong and oriental, in doryphory to the Sun
- **Jupiter exalted in the ASC** (ruled Count's radical ASC)
- Jupiter trine the MC (which it ruled)
- ASC and MC both sextile Venus (ruled Sun and Saturn)
- **Moon (ruler of ASC) in the 9th house** for the journey
- Moon above radical Venus (though actually in Aries 3°, not Pisces as chart shows)
- Mercury alone well-disposed in 12th (unable to harm)

**Key Choices:**
- Prioritized Jupiter in ASC (primary ruler for the Count)
- Fortified MC for successful undertakings
- Put Moon in 9th house for the journey
- Accepted malefics in 11th where they were less harmful
- Timed it exactly when Count was ready (waited by sundial)

**Results:**
- The journey proceeded
- Morin provides details of the fortunate outcomes in his text
- Demonstrates the utility of proper elections

**Lessons:**
1. All levels (natal, directions, revolutions, election) must align
2. Perfection is impossible - choose the best available
3. Prioritize the most important factors (ASC, MC, house of matter)
4. Work within practical constraints
5. Exact timing matters - they waited for the precise moment
6. Both charts (Morin and the Count) were considered

## GLOSSARY OF TERMS

**Angular Houses:** 1st, 4th, 7th, 10th - The strongest houses, where planets have maximum power

**Applying Aspect:** When a faster planet moves toward an exact aspect with a slower planet

**Ascendant (ASC):** The degree of the zodiac rising on the eastern horizon at a given time

**Benefic:** Jupiter and Venus, planets that generally help and support

**Cadent Houses:** 3rd, 6th, 9th, 12th - The weakest houses, where planets have least power

**Combustion:** A planet within approximately 8° of the Sun, weakened by the Sun's rays

**Detriment:** A planet in the sign opposite its rulership (e.g., Mars in Libra)

**Dignity:** A planet in a sign where it has strength (rulership, exaltation, triplicity, etc.)

**Direction:** A predictive technique moving points in the natal chart forward in time

**Domicile:** The sign a planet rules (e.g., Mars rules Aries and Scorpio)

**Doryphory:** Planets rising before the Sun, acting as bodyguards or attendants

**Election:** Choosing an auspicious time to begin an action

**Exaltation:** A sign where a planet has special honor and strength

**Fall:** A planet in the sign opposite its exaltation, weakened

**Horary:** Answering a question by the chart of the moment it is asked

**Malefic:** Saturn and Mars, planets that generally challenge and obstruct

**Midheaven (MC):** The degree of the zodiac at the highest point in the chart, cusp of the 10th house

**Mutual Reception:** Two planets in each other's signs of rulership

**Partile:** An exact aspect to the same degree and minute

**Primary Direction:** The most important predictive technique, based on the diurnal rotation of the Earth

**Radical/Radix:** The natal chart

**Retrograde:** Apparent backward motion of a planet from Earth's perspective

**Revolution:** A return chart - Solar (birthday) or Lunar (monthly return)

**Separating Aspect:** When a faster planet moves away from an exact aspect with a slower planet

**Succedent Houses:** 2nd, 5th, 8th, 11th - Medium strength houses

**Syzygy:** The New Moon or Full Moon preceding an event

**Transit:** The current position of planets as they move through the zodiac

**Via Combusta:** The "Burned Way," from 15° Libra to 15° Scorpio, considered unfortunate for the Moon

**Void of Course:** The Moon making no more applying aspects before changing signs
