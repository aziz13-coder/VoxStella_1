### CHART 11

In the previous chapter, using Porphyry's method, we found the Predominator to be the Moon.

### DOROTHEUS* METHOD

The Moon is the light of the sect. It is not located in the first, tenth, or eleventh houses. Neither is the Sun, the contrary light. The next candidate is the Lot of Fortune. Assuming our birth time is exact to the minute, Fortune is declining in the twelfth house and is also disqualified. The Prenatal Lunation is 290 Libra (the degree of the New Moon before birth, since this is a waxing Moon). That is also cadent in the third house and thus eliminated. Therefore, we must default to the Ascendant. A valid Predominator must be witnessed by one of its lords, in the following order. The bound lord is the first choice. The requirement for the bound lord is that it witnesses the Predominator from within its own bounds. Jupiter is the bound lord of the O5°38' Leo Ascendant, but Jupiter at 230 Taurus is not within the range of Jupiter's bounds, which extend from 01-06° Leo. To clarify this last point, Jupiter must be witnessing the Ascendant from the first six degrees of Taurus so that he casts his ray into his own bounds, i.e., into the first six degrees of Leo. In this particular instance, Jupiter would have to be in the first six degrees of Taurus (which are actually Venus ' bounds) in order to send his ray into the first six degrees of Leo. Because he is at 230, he does not qualify. Thus, the remaining lords must be examined and for these, witnessing can occur by whole sign. Next in line is the domicile lord of the Leo Ascendant, which is the Sun. The Sun at 02° Scorpio witnesses the Ascendant by whole-sign square, which is sufficient for the Ascendant to be confirmed as Predominator.

Paulus looks first to the light of the sect, which in this case is the Moon. Since this is a night chart, he allows for the Moon to be located in the fifth house; thus it might be deemed the Predominator.
