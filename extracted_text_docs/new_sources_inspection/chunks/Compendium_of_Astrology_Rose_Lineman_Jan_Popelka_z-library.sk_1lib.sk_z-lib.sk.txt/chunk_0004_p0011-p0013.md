Houses and Signs in Natural Order with Planetary Rulers In a horoscope, a planet is said to rule the house whose cusp bears the sign naturally ruled by that planet. If Aries is placed on the cusp of the fifth house in a particular horoscope, Mars, as natural planetary ruler of Aries, rules the fifth house of that horoscope, Regardless of horoscope placement, signs and planets retain their natural rulerships. The affairs of a house are energized by the planets occupying and ruling that house; the activities of a house are expressed in accordance with the sign on the cusp. Aspects, the angular relationships between planets, influence the quality and dispersion of planetary energies. To be in aspect, two planets must be placed a specified number of degrees apart in the zodiac. The energy of a planet is channeled into houses occupied and ruled by other planets if it is in aspect with those planets. Planetary action directed by aspect is modified according to the nature of the aspect and the planets involved. Aspects bring into sharper focus the information provided by the distribution of the signs and planets throughout the houses of the horoscope. The astrologer studies the signs, planets, houses, aspects and interrelationships that exist among the various elements of a horoscope. Each factor is assessed in light of all other relevant factors and interpreted accordingly. The effectiveness of astrology depends not only upon the astrologer's knowledge, but also upon the astrologer's ability to communicate with a client. The rich symbolism of astrology must be interpreted in language that is meaningful to the client, leaving no room for doubt or misunderstanding. Many common astrological terms have no meaning in everyday conversation; technical terms are obscure and often misleading to those who are not well versed in astrology. Archaic terms that sprang from traditional astrological literature and are still in use today do not carry the same connotation as in the past. Words such as "affliction" and "malefic" as currently used in astrology do not have dire meanings An afflicted planet does not indicate calamity; it is one that forms disharmonious aspects with other planets so that planetary energies do not combine compatibly. Afflictions tend to motivate negative expression of planetary energies; positive expression depends upon the effort and direction supplied by the individual. A planet described as malefic is not "evil"; planets do not have the powers of good or evil. Afflictions to malefic planets are, perhaps, more difficult to handle in terms of directing energies productively, but whether energies are helpful or not rests ultimately with the individual who utilizes them. Modem astrology is not fatalistic: it cannot be equated with "fortune telling," It demands a psychological approach, for it deals with the individual's potentials as defined by motivation and need. If the astrologer is to awaken self understanding in the client and acquaint her or him with the potentials for growth and self-realization that are described in the horoscope, the astrologer must be able to communicate on a level that is consistent with the client's needs. Some people identify with material or physical concepts while others relate on an intellectual, inspirational or emotional level. A client can be

The Signs of the Zodiac Each of the twelve signs that make up the zodiac appears in a horoscope in natural sequence as shown in Figures 1 and 2. No matter where Aries is placed in a horoscope, the next sign is Taurus, followed by Gemini, then Cancer and so on to Pisces, which directly precedes Aries. The signs act as twelve distinct mediums through which planetary energies and house affairs are expressed. For example, a person whose horoscope has Leo on the cusp of the second house, which rules personal finances, handles money in a manner characteristic of Leo; financial interests differ from those of one whose horoscope has Virgo, Libra or any sign other than Leo on the second house cusp. If Mercury, which energizes the mind, is in Capricorn, Capricomian qualities influence thought, communication and other mental functions. Signs do not change the natures of planets or the meanings of houses. The influence of a sign on a planet or house is similar to the affect of a colored lens on light. Dispersion of light varies with the color of the lens, but colored lenses do not change the form (nature) of light energy, radiation.
