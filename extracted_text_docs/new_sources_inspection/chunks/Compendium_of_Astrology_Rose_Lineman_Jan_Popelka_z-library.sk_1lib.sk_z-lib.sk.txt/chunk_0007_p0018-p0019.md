### The Line

1^^^^ A straight line without another line intersecting or joining it signifies the power of the Sun. It is called the Scepter of the Sun or the Scepter of Authority. Sign glyphs are pictured and described in the part of this chapter devoted to individual signs. The glyphs are more than a convenient shorthand. Each symbolizes the meaning of the sign it represents. Some glyphs picture parts of the body ruled by corresponding signs, although it may take some imagination to discern this. With each glyph an historical symbol that portrays sign characteristics is shown. Characteristics of the Signs The extent or sphere of a sign's influence on an individual cannot be ascertained until the horoscope is drawn up, showing the relation of signs to planets and houses. Bear in mind that if a sign is a personal factor in a horoscope its astrological qualities will be prominent in that person's nature. For example, the sign occupied by the Sun, which represents the ego and creative self-expression, and the one placed on the Ascendant (the rising sign), which governs appearance and attitudes, describe personal characteristics. Signs associated with houses that concern outside interests such as finances or work are not interpreted in the same manner as those which influence the personality. Nevertheless, the essential qualities of a sign remain the same whether expressed in one's character or applied to less personal areas of activity.

It signifies beginnings and new enterprises; hence, Aries is indicative of thepioneerspirit. A cardinal-fire-positive sign, Aries represents dynamic action, initiative, self-motivation, enthusiastic drive, aggressive effort and impulsiveness. People born when the Sun is in Aries (March 21-April 19) are highly competitive, outgoing, resourceful, confident and decisive. They step into the thick of things boldly, sure of themselves, fearless of adverse consequences, unafraid of failure. Arians are real go-getters. Aries women are especially well-equipped for the career world. They are aggressive, sure in the knowledge that they can do anything a man or another woman can do and more. There is no place for male chauvinism in the life of an Aries woman. Quick-thinking and impetuous, headstrong and audacious, Arians often act without giving adequate consideration to the ultimate results of their actions. Aries men, cocky and brash, may project a "macho" image. They perform daring deeds, but many take reckless and foolhardy chances to prove themselves. Impatient, Arians are not content to work for years toward singular goals. Once they lose enthusiasm for an idea or project, as they quite frequently do, they are quick to tum to something new. Arians needs to develop the habit of thinking things through before acting hastily. In order to realize fulfillment and success, they must leam to direct their boundless energies toward productive and attainable objectives. Arians require the reinforcement of immediate feedback and tangible rewards if they are to stick with their endeavors. What Arians lack in persistence, they make up for in ambition and the will to succeed. They are natural-born achievers who, because of their dauntless courage and drive to be first, are leaders in their peer groups. Conscious of the importance of appearance, Arians dress well and act in a self-assured manner. They talk about their successes but usually neglect to mention their failures. Arians believe that projecting a successful image ensures success. The Aries person projects a confidence that tells the world, "I am ready for anything," and, more importantly, the Arian is. Outspoken, Arians are seldom tactful, often argumentative. An Arian's temper surfaces quickly but just as quickly evaporates. They are warm human beings, spontaneous in friendship and ardent in love. Arians are energetic people who gravitate toward active jobs and hobbies. Both Aries men and women are able executives, capable of making decisions and acting on their own volition. They excel in jobs that require individual skills. Most Arians enjoy outdoor sports such as hunting, tennis and golf. The more daring are often enthusiasts of auto racing and rougher sports such as football, wrestling or boxing. Needlework or carving may appeal to the Arian as an indoor hobby.
