### Taurus

Taurus, ruled by the planet Venus, is the natural sign of the second house. The bull, historical symbol for Taurus, represents the bullheadedness characteristic of this sign. The glyph is composed of a circle (Circle of Spirit, life force) capped by a crescent. The Taurus crescent turns upward to retain, conserve and preserve the life force. The horns of a bull are not hard to picture in the glyph; it is more difficult to imagine the lower jaw, chin and neck (throat)—parts of the body ruled by Taurus. Taurus, a fixed-earth-negative sign, characterizes purposeful action, steady drive, determined effort, endurance, reliability, conservatism and fixed attitudes. Persons bom under a Taurus Sun (April 20-May 20) work long and hard to achieve their objectives. Industrious and persevering, they are not easily discouraged by difficulties; they endure as much hardship as is necessary to pursue their goals. Taureans do not jump into things without a great deal of forethought. Once they choose a course, they stick with it unless unalterable circumstances dictate otherwise. Taureans go about their business quietly. Uncomplaining, they shoulder responsibility with fortitude, wear down obstacles by refusing to yield or turn back, and proceed with relentless determination along their chosen paths. Set in their ways, Taureans resent anyone who interferes or attempts to force them to alter their habits. They try to establish a pattern to their lives. Taureans do not readily adapt to fluctuating trends or inconsistent situations and find it difficult to restructure their plans and actions around changing circumstances. They need to develop a greater degree of flexibility. The singularity of purpose and practical utilization of resources (skills and material assets) that are basic to their natures compensate largely for the Taurean lack of enterprise and versatility. Because of determination and persistence, many Taureans succeed where others fail. Taureans are patient; they can wait a long time, years if need be, to act on their plans. When they do, little can stop them. Taureans make every effort to obtain creature comforts and material security. They are as devoted to their friends and loved ones as they are to the material goals to which they aspire. Although they may be possessive, jealous and stubborn, they are steadfast, sincere and warmhearted as well. Usually gentle and good-natured, it takes a lot to provoke them, but when their anger is aroused they are formidable and unrelenting. Taureans possess an instinctive sense of color and beauty which is reflected in their taste in art, their clothing and their flair for home decor. Taureans are usually practical money managers and have an inborn sense of value. A Taurean will buy one genuine article of real worth instead of two or three cheap imitations at the same price. From the possessions they acquire and the money they accumulate, Taureans derive emotional security and inner satisfaction. Taureans are not essentially initiators or idea people; they are the ones who follow through and get things done. They are creative but not especially innovative. Taureans prefer jobs along established, reliable lines over those which are insecure or risky. Taureans like to do things with their hands, and crocheting, flower arranging, gardening, painting, or sculpting are spare-time interests a Taurean might enjoy.
