£ The crab, historical symbol for Cancer, is descriptive of the protective and defensive qualities inherent in this sign. The glyph is composed of two circles, each joined to a crescent. One is inverted and the other turned upward, indicating that what is given is not lost but retained to nurture the spirit or vital life force. The glyph depicts the human breasts, which Cancer rules along with the stomach and digestive system. Cancer, the natural sign of the fourth house, is ruled by the Moon. A cardinal-water-negative sign. Cancer represents strong emotional drive, great sensitivity, the nurturing instinct, the principle of motherhood, conscientious action, tenacious effort, impressionability and fluid or changeable attitudes. Persons bom when the Sun is in Cancer (June 22-July 22) are imaginative, responsive, sympathetic, gentle and kind—except when they are hurt; then the Cancer "claw" goes into action. When angry, Cancerians are sharp-spoken, momentarily vindictive, soon forgiving but seldom forgetting. Cancerians are somewhat retiring and unassuming, though many develop a sophisticated mask to hide inner insecurity and protect emotional vulnerability. Sensitive, they are deeply hurt by ridicule and derogatory remarks. Cancer people are difficult to understand because their reactions depend upon their moods. When they feel happy and cheerful, they are congenial. When disappointed or depressed, they become irritable, or they withdraw into an impenetrable shell. Cancerians remain aloof from that which hurts them; they won't willingly give anyone or anything a second chance to hurt them further. Cancerians do not*readily reveal themselves, partly because they fear disapproval and partly because they are secretive by nature, a characteristic of all water signs. Cancerians face a need to overcome lack of confidence and a tendency toward self-centeredness; they can do so by tapping the deep well of emotional energy that is theirs and directing it outwardly in the form of creative and useful endeavor. Cancerians seek material as well as emotional security. They need a few dollars to fall back on in case of financial emergency just as they need someone who cares to depend on in an emotional crisis, so they save part of their earnings for the proverbial rainy day and work hard to preserve their homes and families. Noncombative by nature, Cancerians do not like to argue and fight but will do so to protect their interests and the interests of loved ones. Most are more inclined to resist passively than to force a confrontation. They are persistent, though, and can wear down the stiffest opposition in time. Cancerians are warm and affectionate. Many are self-sacrificing in love. They can become overly possessive, smothering those near and dear in their desire to nurture and protect. Loyal and sentimental, they remember the kindnesses others bestow upon them. Still, Cancerians are changeable and their friends and interests shift from time to time. Natural-bom hosts and hostesses, Cancerians have a knack for making others feel at ease, strangers and acquaintances alike. They respond intuitively to the needs of people whom they encounter. Many serve well in public capacities because most people feel comfortable in their presence and instinctively trust them; without effort, they gain the confidence of others. From their jobs, Cancerians need both the reassurance of approval and a paycheck that equals their efforts. They are happiest when taking care of things or people, for both men and women of this sign exhibit strong protective urges. They are well suited to positions in which public trust is important.
