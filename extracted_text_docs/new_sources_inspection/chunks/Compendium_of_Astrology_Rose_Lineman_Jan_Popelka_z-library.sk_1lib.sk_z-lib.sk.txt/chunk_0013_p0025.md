### Libra

The scales is the symbol for Libra, the sign of balance and justice. This theme is further pictured in the glyph, an inverted crescent above the double line of power, which symbolizes the soul of justice ruling the Scepter of Authority. The glyph resembles the setting sun; appropriately, Libra is the natural sign of the seventh house, the cusp of which is the sunset portion of a horoscope. Venus is the planetary ruler of Libra, a cardinal-air-positive sign. The sign rules the kidneys. Libra stands for cooperative action, approvalseeking drive, mentally motivated effort, harmony, teamwork and impartial attitudes. Persons with a Libra Sun (September 23-October 22) are mildmannered and peace-loving. Librans try to resolve their problems by arbitration rather than by force. Violence is repugnant to Librans; they will fight, especially if principles of justice are at stake, but usually only as a last resort. Librans like to argue in the sense of debate, not in the sense of quarreling. They are strategists who outsmart their opponents. Subtly persuasive, they are masters at the art of suggestion. If overt persuasion does not work, a Libran will plant the seed of an idea and nurture it so skillfully that opponents frequently fail to realize they are playing into Libra's hands. The gentle, amiable, courteous manner of most Librans may fool others into thinking they are pushovers, but a Libran is not weak. Instead of Drawn or physical intimidation Librans rely on intelligence and diplomacy to get their way. An instinctive sense of fair play motivates Librans. They weigh all aspects of an issue before forming a final judgment. In so doing, they frequently appear to be vacillating; however, once Librans arrive at decisions they back them up fully, sure that their conclusions are unbiased and just. Librans try hard to please others because the approval of their peers is necessary to their inner security. They need companionship. A Libran is happier and more productive as part of a team or partnership endeavor than when working alone. Socially inclined, they instinctively know how to get along with their associates and acquaintances. Although they seldom display anger, when a Libran s temper does erupt, one can expect a stormy session while it lasts. Librans possess a highly developed sense of aesthetic sensitivity. Everything about these people spells refinement and graciousness. A Libran s choice of friends, clothing and furnishings reflects an aversion for the ugly, the crude, the vulgar, the garish. They shy away from anything or anyone who offends their sense of delicacy and good taste. Because Librans desire a companion with whom to share their life, they are susceptible to romance. The Libran is a romantic, tender, passionate lover who enjoys the intimacy of a candlelight dinner for two. Few Librans go through life without marrying at least once. The lifestyle of most Librans mirrors a yearning for luxury and the finer things of life. They are not extravagant in the strictest sense of the word: few squander their money or buy indiscriminately. They do however, spend as much as they can afford and more on beautiful possessions and expensive social habits. Librans may have little money in the bank, but they derive great pleasure from what their money buys. Librans prefer to work in congenial surroundings that are pleasing to the eye. Discordancies of any kind upset and distract them. Harmony and cooperation are important to a Libran s job performance. Librans enjoy intellectual pastimes such as chess, word games, reading and puzzles. Cultural activities such as the ballet, concerts, the opera and art also appeal to them.
