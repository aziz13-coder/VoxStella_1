### Sagittarius

The centaur-archer, half horse-half man, symbolically describes dual qualities of Sagittarius. The glyph resembles an arrow but is said to depict the upper leg. This sign rules the hips, thighs, liver and hepatic system. Sagittarius, the natural sign of the ninth house, is ruled by the planet Jupiter. A mutable-fire-positive sign, Sagittarius is representative of zealous action, idealistic drive, eager effort, positive thought, adventurous spirit and a philosophical attitude. This sign is linked to religion, spiritual concepts, and moral and ethical standards. A person with a Sagittarius Sun (November 22-December 21) is a high-minded individual who is honest and straightforward. Sagittarians seek the truth in the most profound sense, and speak the truth as they believe it to be. They see things in broad terms, leaving the specifics to somebody else. Sagittarians greet the world with verve and confidence. They aim their arrows boldly toward the stars, confident of success. Frequently Sagittarians bring problems on themselves because they are in too much of a hurry. They are overly optimistic, indifferent to details and prone to making impulsive decisions. Consequently, they trip over their own feet literally and figuratively. Sagittarians take their setbacks philosophically. Failure does not hinder their chances for future success, for they are quick to pick up the pieces and launch a fresh start. Sagittarians are goal-oriented; however, they are not so driven that they stoop to petty tactics in order to reach their objectives. Unwilling to compromise their ideals or principles to get ahead, they forge onward immune to the blandishments of greed and self-serving interests. Sagittarians are not looking for free rides; they refuse to be beholden to anyone for their success. They trust their own abilities to get them to the top. The Sagittarian is a daring free spirit who will not be chained to a confining life style, to freedomthreatening relationships or to narrow ideas. These people want to come and go when they please, eat when they are hungry and say what they think without varnishing the truth. Sagittarians are physically active and intellectually aware. They are explorers at heart who are interested in distant places and profound mental concepts. Whether physical or intellectual in nature, their exploits are motivated by the desire to learn. Sagittarians are outgoing and friendly, though sometimes they come on too strongly as backslappers or practical jokers. Utterlycandid, most Sagittarians are short on tact. They are impatient and quick-tempered. Even so, they are immensely popular; much of their exuberance rubs off on their friends and associates. They are respected for their sincerity and good will, liked for their generosity and sense of humor. Most Sagittarians are flirtatious, but few are seriously interested in romance. They are naturally chary of involvements that might curtail their activities. A desire for freedom causes many Sagittarians to remain single or marry late in life. Sagittarians are extremely versatile They are averse to a rigid routine and a sedentary way of life. A job atmosphere that satisfies the restless Sagittarian temperament is as important to their success as the job itself. Sagittarians enjoy active hobbies and those that are mentally stimulating. Physical characteristics attributed to the sign Sagittarius include an athletic appearance, a high forehead, which is more noticeable with age as the hairline recedes, a rather long face and prominent teeth. The walk is a hurried trot that is not especially graceful. Occupations that deal with foreign affairs, longrange interests, religion, the law, universities and horses are linked to the sign Sagittarius. Advertising agent, archer, attorney, clergyman or evangelist, hunting guide, college teacher or university professor, horse trainer, import/export entrepreneur, jockey, judge or jurist, race track operator and theologian are primarily Sagittarian in nature.
