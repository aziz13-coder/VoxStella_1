The historical symbol for Capricorn, the goat-fish (sea-goat), is alternately pictured as a mountain goat. The long face of the goat-fish expresses the serious, sober nature of Capricorn. The steady rise in the world, characteristic of this sign, parallels the surefooted ascent of a mountain goat. The glyph resembles the knee, which Capricorn rules. This sign also maintains general rulership over the bones and skin. Capricorn is ruled by the planet, Saturn. The natural sign of the tenth house, it is a cardinal-earth-negative sign. Organized action, status-seeking drive, calculated effort, materialism, acquisitive instincts, self-discipline, caution and conservative attitudes are typical characteristics of this sign. A Capricorn Sun (December 22-January 19) increases the desire to better oneself regardless of the conditions into which a person is bom. Capricoms are workers who are conscientious and dedicated to their major objectives; most feel an inward need to accomplish worthwhile achievements that earn them recognition and secure positions in the community. Capricoms waste little time on frivolities. They proceed to make their mark in life a step at a time. Although they encounter frequent roadblocks and obstacles, they suffer few real reversals, for they build a solid foundation along the way. Without financial security and prestige in the eyes of their peers, Capricoms have little peace of mind. The drive for success may bring renown to Capricoms, and they are not averse to fame, but essentially most of them want assurances that what they have today they will have tomorrow, with interest to boot. Capricoms are bom with a mature outlook. They usually know what they want out of life at an early age. Because they possess the common sense to formulate workable plans as a means of achieving their goals and have the practical ability to implement their ideas, Capricoms succeed more often than not. However, the tendency to be overly cautious can bar them from reaching their full potential. Being extremely conservative, many Capricoms fail to set their sights high enough, or they refuse to break with tradition, preferring to remain on the firm ground of known territory rather than risk untried waters. Capricoms are thrifty; they budget their money much as they budget their time and energy. A hefty bank account substitutes for the self-confidence they lack at birth. There is an air of quiet dignity about most Capricoms. They are not the persons a stranger approaches on the beach or at a party—one instinctively knows that forward remarks or actions are unwelcome. Prudent in their choice of friends, Capricoms lean toward persons who are as sensible and conservative as they are. They are somewhat strait-laced and would be uncomfortable in an unstructured or avant-garde lifestyle. Capricoms are restrained in matters of the heart; they seldom get caught up in ill-considered love affairs. Most do not marry until they or their prospective mates are well-established in life. For this reason younger Capricoms tend to choose mature mates; older Capricoms frequently choose younger mates who represent the youth they had no time to enjoy. Whether employed as laborers or performing in an executive capacity, they apply themselves diligently and deliberately, working to achieve ultimately a reasonable degree of security.
