### Pisces

Two fish swimming in opposing directions while fastened together by the silver cord of life symbolize the dual aspects and confusion attributed to Pisces. The glyph supports the same theme. Pisces, the natural sign of the twelfth house, is ruled jointly by Neptune and Jupiter (traditional ruler). This sign governs the feet and is associated with the lymphatic system. A mutable-water-negative sign, Pisces epitomizes subdued action, lethargic drive, disorganized effort, compassion, martyrdom and vacillating attitudes. Persons whose Sun falls in Pisces at birth (February 19-March 20) are gentle, modest, elusive, thoughtful and sympathetic. Pisceans, sensitive to the needs and feelings of fellow human beings, are deeply moved by the tragedies that befall others. Pisceans identify with their surroundings and associates; chameleon-like, many assume the opinions and moods of whomever they are with at a particular moment. They are not necessarily equivocating or intentionally insincere, but they are extremely impressionable and tend to drift with the situations in which they find themselves. Pisceans respond with equal intensity to joy as to sadness. Therefore, their moods range from the heights of ecstacy to the depths of despair. Like all water signs, Pisceans attempt to protect their emotional sensitivity. They may feign indifference in order to shut out people who are capable of inflicting emotional hurts; however, they brood inwardly over harsh treatment and slights, real or fancied. Many Pisceans fall into the trap of self-pity. Some have difficulty controlling and masking their feelings and may burst into tears when they feel sad or rejected. Idealistic and trusting, Pisceans frequently blind themselves to the harsh realities of everyday living. They may have good intentions, but many are unable to deal effectively with practical responsibilities. Pisceans are not motivated by the lure of worldly success. They think in terms of "happy" rather than "dollars" and strive for inner harmony. If they manage to achieve this harmony without losing themselves in a world of escapism and illusion, they become more self-assured, better able to direct their efforts productively. Pisceans possess a natural sense of rhythm. Under suitable circumstances, many are able to convert their dreams into lasting contributions to the arts in the form of music, poetry or the dance. Pisceans are mild-tempered, compliant, yielding and tenderhearted. Easy targets for sob stories, they take their friends' troubles to heart and suffer for others as they do for themselves. They seldom refuse a request for help and may sacrifice their own interests for the welfare of others. Romantic love is essential to Pisceans; without it they feel lonely and unfulfilled. Because they need to know they are loved, appreciated and desirable, Pisceans seek attentive mates who can give them the constant reassurances they crave. A compatible job environment is necessary to Pisceans' productivity. They do not function well when they are ill at ease, unhappy or discontented. In a setting that inspires encouragement, Pisceans can accomplish wonders, for they are adaptable and multi-talented. Sideline interests that appeal to Pisceans are mostly those associated with water, rhythm and the mystic such as scuba diving, fishing, dancing, poetry, spiritualism and Yoga. Some Pisceans tend to be plump, but even so they appear delicate, almost fragile. They have liquid eyes which seem to mirror the depths of the soul. Pursed lips and a small chin make the lovely eyes more prominent. Most Pisceans have dainty hands and tiny feet. They move with the effortless grace of a dancer. Occupations that are in some way related to the sea, fish, oil, beverages, places of confinement (hospitals.
