### Mercury

■ m With the exception of the Moon, Mercury is the fastest moving planet. It orbits the Sun y J in approximately eighty-eight days, but because of retrograde motion it takes about a year to traverse the zodiac. Mercury passes through a sign in less than three weeks when motion is direct; it spends eight to ten weeks in a sign when it is retrograde. The retrograde period averages three and one-half weeks; the stationary period lasts from a few hours up to three days as motion changes from direct to retrograde or from retrograde to direct. Mean daily motion is 1023'. Because Mercury is always within 28° of the Sun's zodiacal position, it must occupy the same sign as the Sun, the preceding sign or the following sign. Mercury is cold, magnetic and androgynous. It assumes the gender of the sign it occupies; it is dry when masculine and moist when feminine. The mind, the mental processes and all forms of communication including travel, touch, use of the telephone, speaking and writing are ruled by Mercury. Mercury takes on the character of the sign in which it is posited and reflects the nature of the planets with which it is configurated (aspected). Its placement in the horoscope has a great deal to do with an individual's innate intellectual and communicative skills. The sign Mercury relates to a person's thinking habits, reasoning ability and the capacity to formulate ideas and to verbalize thoughts. The house in which Mercury is placed describes the things about which a person thinks and talks and where the individual gets ideas. A well-placed Mercury confers dexterity, an agile, perceptive mind, the ability to form judgments based upon logic and sound reasoning, and the capacity to express knowledge and ideas fluently through speech or writing. A lesser-placed Mercury gives the tendency to think in broad terms, to deal intellectually with generalities rather than particulars. Judgment is based on the general picture rather than specific facts. A debilitated Mercury indicates absent-mindedness, a tendency to daydream, and sometimes clumsiness. When Mercury is retrograde in the natal horoscope, the individual is inclined to be introspective, subjective in thinking and hesitant about expressing ideas aloud. A stationary Mercury intensifies the intellectual and communicative expression as described by sign position, house placement and aspects. The planet is associated with ages seven to fifteen, the learning years during which an individual acquires basic learning experiences and essential education. Mercury rules the arms, hands, shoulders, lungs, solar plexus, abdomen, intestines, the thymus gland, the nervous system and the respiratory system. It also governs the intellect and physical (motor) coordination. In the horoscope. Mercury denotes a person's close relatives other than parents, especially brothers and sisters. Mercury has dominion over messages, letters, books, magazines, the signing of documents, neighbors and the immediate neighborhood, commuter travel, transportation facilities, paper, mercury (the metal), Wednesday and the number five. Mercury is the natural ruler of two houses, the third and the sixth. This planet rules two signs, Gemini and Virgo, and is, of course, dignified in both. Mercury is exalted in Aquarius, detrimented in Sagittarius and Pisces and in fall in Leo. The degree of exaltation is 15° Aquarius; the degree of fall is 15° Leo.

Venus never travels farther than 48° zodiacal longitude away from the Sun, thus it is always found within two signs of that of the Sun. Venus is feminine, warm, moist, magnetic and fruitful. Affection, love, beauty, harmony, refinement, aesthetic taste, artistic instinct and sociability are expressed by Venus. This planet rules the principle of attraction and desirability. It describes the receptive sex drive and feminine sexual expression. It governs social behavior on a one-to-one basis and marriage (legal or common-law). Venus influences a person's drive for companionship, the capacity to attract compatible people, one's ability to forge close personal ties and to form business partnerships. In the horoscope this planet may represent a loved one or lover and female relatives. Venus rules the years of youth, ages fifteen to twenty-four, the period when one's romantic and sexual feelings are stirred. The Venus years introduce that first flush of (wo)manhood when a person realizes his or her sexuality and the desire for companionship of the opposite sex is aroused. Venus also partially describes an individual's material prospects, for this planet rules money and possessions. It directs, in part, one's earning capacity and spending habits. Retrograde Venus inhibits the outward expression of social interaction. The individual is reticent in social behavior. Financial development may be slow initially, but it will eventually reach the full potential of growth as otherwise indicated by the horoscope. Stationary Venus focuses and strengthens social expression and financial drive according to horoscope placement by sign and house and the aspects between Venus and the other planets. A dignified Venus lends charm, beauty, refined manners, good taste in clothing, popularity, artistic interest and perhaps artistic talent. A badly placed Venus fosters vanity, laziness, self-indulgence and extravagance; it generates discord in marriage, partnerships and personal relationships. Venus rules the neck, throat, thyroid gland, kidneys, ovaries, veins and circulation of the venous blood. It has part rulership over the female sex organs. Ailments such as goiter, nephritis, and diabetes are attributed in part to an afflicted Venus. Venus is associated with a natural sense of color coordination, a melodious voice, a becoming manner and an attractive appearance. The planet rules dimples, sugar, candy, pastries, flowers, perfume, music, that which is beautiful and pleasing or gratifying, copper, Friday and the number six. The second and seventh are the natural houses of Venus. Venus is dignified in Taurus and Libra, exalted in Pisces, detrimented in Aries and Scorpio and in fall in Virgo. The degree of exaltation is 27'' Pisces; the degree of fall is 27''"Virgo.
