### Earth

As shown in Figure 5, Earth's orbit is farther ©from the Sun than the orbits of Venus and Mercury. Venus and Mercury are called inferior planets because their orbital paths lie closer to the Sun than does that of Earth, not because of their action or quality. The planets whose orbits lie outside Earth's path are termed superior planets, superior referring to the larger orbit. Earth is not entered into the horoscope since it is the observation point. Earth occupies the sign and degree of the zodiac directly opposite that of the Sun at any given time.

### Mars

Mars completes a solar orbit in 687 "t" days. It takes twenty-five to twentysix months for Mars to cross the v V V V zodiac. Mars spends about six and one-half to seven and one-half weeks in a sign when motion is direct and six to six and onehalf months when it retrogrades. The retrograde period lasts for two to two and one-half months; the stationary period lasts for up to five days. Mean daily motion is 31' 27". Mars is masculine, hot, dry, electric and barren. Mars represents an individual's dynamic expression and energy drive. This planet governs all basic physical drives including aggressive sexual drives. It rules the principle of basic desire, desire that motivates action on all physical levels. The action of Mars is initiatory, aggressive, passionate and inflammatory. Mars energy can be constructive or destructive. Properly channeled and directed it lends to stamina, productivity and achievement. Improperly channeled, this energy may be expressed as rage, violence or brutality. Retrograde Mars compels people to work harder in order to utilize their energy productively; because the natural inclination is inward, they must redirect their energy toward gainful outward expression. ### Jupiter

The period of orbit and the zodiacal period I of the outer planets coincide. It takes Jupiter J I about twelve years to travel around the zodiac. Jupiter averages one year in each sign including the retrograde period, which lasts approximately four months each year. The stationary period varies from five to nine days. Mean daily motion is 4'49". Jupiter is masculine, warm, moist, electric and fruitful. Jupiter represents religious, ethical and moral values and is associated with higher education, philosophy, theology and jurisprudence. Jupiter rules the abstract mind whereas Mercury rules the concrete mind. It describes one's intellectual and spiritual interests on the highest and most profound level, the search for truth. Jupiter governs the principle of expansion and preservation on both mental and physical planes. This planet signifies benevolence and prosperity. It denotes an element of good fortune, which safeguards a person's interests and protects one's well-being. Jupiter insures the availability of opportunities and describes one's capacity to utilize these opportunities. The degree of abundance attributable to Jupiter and the form it takes are contingent upon the sign in which it is posited, house placement, and its relative position (aspects) to the other planets in the horoscope. Retrograde Jupiter is indicative of the deep thinker, the philosopher. Persons who have Jupiter retrograde in the horoscope are subjective individuals who keep their convictions to themselves. The stationary position of Jupiter lends to profound beliefs and reinforces the benevolent qualities of the planet. Jupiter rules ages forty-five to fifty-seven, a comparatively prosperous period in a person's life. It is usually during the Jupiter period that one's efforts are fully rewarded. Jupiter holds general rulership over the blood, the arteries and arterial circulation. It rules the hips, thighs, feet (with Neptune), liver and hepatic system, and holds joint rulership of the general glandular system with the Sun. Jupiter is connected with publishing, long-range advertising, foreign interests, legal affairs, attorneys, the courts, churches and universities. It promotes honesty, generosity, joviality and success. Jupiter is associated with all that is ample, plentiful and excessive including obesity, gluttony and extravagance. Customs, conventions, genuineness, validity, horses, horse racing, tin, Thursday and the number three are ruled by Jupiter. The natural houses of Jupiter are the ninth and the twelfth. The planet is dignified in Sagittarius and Pisces, exalted in Cancer, detrimented in Gemini and Virgo and in fall in Capricorn. The degree of exaltation is 15° Cancer; the degree of fall is 15° Capricorn.
