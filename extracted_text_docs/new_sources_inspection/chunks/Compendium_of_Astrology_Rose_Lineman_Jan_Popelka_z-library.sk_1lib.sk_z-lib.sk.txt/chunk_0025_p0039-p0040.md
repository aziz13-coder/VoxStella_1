### Uranus

Uranus takes eighty-four years to traverse the zodiac and seven years to travel through one sign. This planet is retrograde for five months at a time and stationary for up to about two weeks. It moves but 4° each year, averaging 42" (seconds) per day. It is masculine, cold, dry, electric and magnetic, and barren. Uranus was formerly called Herschel after its discoverer. The presence of Uranus in our solar system has been known only since 1781. Much has been learned about this planet in the almost two hundred years since its discovery, but there remains much more to determine. The orbit of Uranus is irregular or eccentric and so is its action. The planet represents the unpredictable, the erratic, the different, the bizarre. Uranian influence is disruptive. It breaks up existing patterns to make room for progress. Uranus enforces change and in so doing destroys in order to create. Uranus is not destructive in the most negative sense of the word, for it replaces what it removes. It breaks with tradition and heralds the future. Uranus embraces the principles of compassion, humanitarianism and altruism; it introduces sodal reform. Uranian action is sudden, unexpected, abrupt, spasmodic, revolutionary and dictatorial. It expresses the unconventional, the radical, the rebellious. Uranus embodies the essence of freedom, originality and inventiveness. Flashes of insight that inspire genius are Uranian in nature. $6

Uranus rules the general circulatory system, the ankles, the shin bones, the pituitary gland and the aura. As the higher octave of Mercury, it has influence over the intuitive intellect and the nervous system, particularly the motor nerves. Spasmodic ailments, convulsions, lack of coordination and accidents involving electricity or lightning are due to Uranian influence. Uranus is associated with the period in life from age seventy to eighty-four. There is little doubt that during these years a person's lifetime routine is changed and a new sense of freedom emerges as one no longer faces the responsibility of earning a living or rearing a family. Uranus rules astrology, positive occultism, inventions, discoveries, discrepancies, contradictions, interruptions, abberations, homosexuality, the strange and strangers, tremors, earthquakes, electricity, magnetism, uranium and the number four. Uranus signifies all that is new, novel, modern, avant garde. The eleventh is the natural house of Uranus, and Aquarius its sign of dignity. Uranus co-rules Aquarius with Saturn, the traditional ruler. As more is learned about Uranus, it becomes increasingly apparent that it is probably the sole ruler of this sign. Uranus is exalted in Scorpio, detrimented in Leo and in fall in Taurus. The degree of exaltation is 7° Scorpio; the degree of fall is 7° Taurus.

### Neptune

Neptune crosses the zodiac once in 165 years and spends about fourteen years in each sign. The mean daily motion is 24"; it moves 2° each year. The retrograde period lasts for five months; it is stationary for approximately two weeks. Neptune is feminine, warm, moist, magnetic and fruitful. Having been discovered in 1846, Neptune is considered one of the "newer" planets. It represents the intangible, the vague, the nebulous and the elusive. Neptunian influence is difficult to pinpoint: its action is obscure, veiled, hidden. Neptune energizes imagination, vision and spiritual powers and governs rhythm. The higher octave of Venus, this planet may inspire artistic genius in music, art, poetry or the dance. It can also bring deception, chaos and confusion. Neptune has dominion over the unconscious. It is the planet of the mystic; it rules escapism in all forms—drug addition, alcoholism and delusion. Retrograde Neptune increases the trend toward inward forms of escapism and self-delusion. Artistic inspiration and mystical tendencies may be enhanced as well. In a stationary position, the Neptunian qualities are strengthened. A well-aspected stationary Neptune can confer the highest level of spiritual attainment and artistic vision; an afflicted stationary Neptune can induce complete loss of touch with reality according to how its influence is channeled. Neptune's horoscope placement indicates the illusions to which a person clings. This area represents a blind spot in the individual's view of life. Neptune rules the feet, the pineal gland and the astral body. It is associated with hard-to-diagnose ailments, hypochondria, catalepsy, drowning mishaps and illnesses due to the introduction of toxic substances into the body or the inhalation of noxious fumes. Neptune is said to rule the years eighty-four to ninety-nine, the so-called age of weakness when physical strength is at low ebb. The planet is associated with clairvoyants, mediums, seances, yogis, hypnotism, instability, bisexuality, solvents, synthetics, transparent materials, camouflage, falsehoods, swindlers and places of confinement such as hospitals, prisons and retirement homes. Neptune governs salt water and the oceans (in conjunction with the Moon), especially the deep seas. Sleep, narcotics, photography, rubber, neptunium, the number seven and all that is secret, concealed, unrevealed, indistinct, indefinite, cloudy or hallucinatory are in Neptune's domain. The twelfth is the natural house of Neptune. Dignified in Pisces, it shares rulership of Pisces with Jupiter, the traditional ruler; however, in view of ongoing studies, it seems likely that Neptune is the sole ruler of Pisces.
