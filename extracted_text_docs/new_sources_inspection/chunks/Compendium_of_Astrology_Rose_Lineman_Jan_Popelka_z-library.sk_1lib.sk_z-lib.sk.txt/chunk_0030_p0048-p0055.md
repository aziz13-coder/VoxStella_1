Planetary Expression in the Signs As a planet travels through the signs of the zodiac, its energy is expressed in a manner that is consistent with the characteristics of the sign it occupies at a particular time. The signs do not alter the basic energies or fundamental principles of the planets; rather each provides a unique means of expressing planetary energies. The Sun in the Signs The Sun represents the ego. The sign in which the Sun falls describes the manifestation of the drive for selfexpression. The Sun sign influences one's approach to goals in life and the method one chooses to accomplish objectives. Some physical characteristics of the Sun sign may be observed in the individual, but usually one's appearance is determined largely by the sign on the Ascendant, especially if the Ascending sign is the stronger of the two or if the planetary ruler of the Ascendant is stronger in the horoscope than the Sun. For basic modes of Sun-sign expression, review the signs in Chapter 2. Pay particular attention to the personal characteristics which describe the individual's drives and motivation. The Moon in the Signs The sign in which the Moon is placed colors emotional responses and instinctive actions. The Moon sign influences one's reaction to events and people encountered throughout life. It describes the attitudes that have been acquired as a result of early childhood training, the maternal relationship, and home and family attitudes. The new Moon denotes inspiration, initiation, the beginning of a cycle. The full Moon signifies maturation, fruition, the peak of a cycle. If the Moon is increasing in light (in the waxing phase from new to full) in the natal horoscope, the individual reacts quickly and in an active manner within the limits of sign characteristics. If the natal Moon is decreasing in light (in the waning phase from full to new), response is more passive and introverted according to the dictates of the sign. A lunation Moon (new Moon) or full Moon in the horoscope can be critical depending upon other astrological factors since it marks a turning point or transition from one phase to another. The lunation cycle is discussed in greater detail in Chapter 10. The Moon in Aries stimulates the emotions. The result is a quick temper, impetuous responses and headstrong attitudes that prompt instinctive rejection of discipline and interference. This placement calls for developing patience and safeguarding against mishaps due to haste and rash actions. The individual whose Moon is in Aries notices Arian traits in his or her mother whether she is Arian or not. This person thinks of the mother as outgoing, aggressive and perhaps bossy. Family relationships and home life are active and competitive. The Moon in its sign of exaltation, Taurus, steadies the emotions and fosters consistent attitudes and stable reactions. Domestic and financial security are important to emotional well-being. This placement contributes to an affectionate, though somewhat possessive, nature. The mother is considered by the individual to be caring and reliable, someone who is there when needed. The individual values the home and family life and works hard to preserve and protect these interests. The Moon in Gemini intensifies natural lunar changeability and generates fluctuating emotions, shallowness, restlessness and loquacity. This placement is indicative of a flighty emotional nature. Feelings are intellectualized and verbalized. The maternal relationship is friendly, more sisterly than parental. Outside activities are often of more concern than home and family interests. The Moon in its sign of dignity. Cancer, adds depth to the emotions and gives rise to strong family ties. A pronounced need to nurture and cherish and, in turn, be approved of and loved is evident. This placement generates a tendency to brood and withdraw when hurt: it also increases psychic sensitivity. The mother is pictured as a protector and homemaker who cares deeply tor her child. Throughout life, the individual maintains an ongoing interest in home and family. Mercury in the Signs The sign in which Mercury is placed describes thinking and study habits, mental and conversational expression, the formulation and communication of ideas. Mercury in Aries quickens thought processes and urges immediate and direct expression of ideas. This placement encourages competitive thinking, a love for debate, argumentiveness, impatience, impulsive decisions and ill-considered plans. Mercury in Taurus inspires practical thinking and common sense. Financial and material considerations are an important part of decision making. This placement restricts mental ingenuity and flexibility, but gives good powers of concentration and prudent judgment.
