Uranus in Aries asserts independence and prompts change. There is danger of impetuous action that severs ties and causes estrangement, action that is motivated by impatience and a desire for freedom at any price. Uranus in Taurus, the sign of fall, induces practical innovations. Changes designed to serve utilitarian purposes and produce concrete results are associated with the use of material resources. Uranus in Gemini enhances ingenuity and inventiveness. However, action is erratic and novel ideas may be left to flounder due to the individual's lack of perseverance. Change may be promoted merely for the sake of change. Uranus in Cancer contributes to emotional unpredictability and a certain amount of emotional instability. Emotional considerations underlie the breaks initiated and the changes effected. Uranus in Leo, the sign of detriment, produces changes of the kind that increase power potential and leadership capacity. The individual may overstep the bounds of propriety and common sense in order to satisfy the urge for prominence and change. Uranus in Virgo promotes systematic research, especially in job methodology and health. On a personal level, the individual may tend to adopt food fads and unconventional methods of medical treatment. Uranus in Libra generates instability in relationships. The desire for freedom and change can disrupt cooperative endeavors, partnerships and marriage. Uranus in Scorpio, the sign of exaltation, gives rise to massive changes of a profound nature. Death and destruction may herald the need for change. Rebellion and self-will are strong motivating factors. Uranus in Sagittarius magnifies .the urge for intellectual freedom. Far-ranging views, advanced abstract concepts and unorthodox ideas are all part of the progressive intellectualism indicated by this placement; however, there may be lack of consistency in opinions. Uranus in Capricorn gives a more conventional expression to the Uranian demand for change. Tradition is reordered and modernized rather than dispensed with entirely. Future security is the prime motivation behind change. Uranus in Aquarius, the sign of dignity, produces humanitarian inspired changes that have far-reaching implications. Innovations are inventive, original and often much ahead of the times. Future trends are bom here. Uranus in Pisces awakens the intuitive intellect; it may prompt fundamental spiritual revelations. All too often the results from subsequent changes are disappointing because Utopian hopes are founded on illusory ideals. Neptune in the Signs Since Neptune spends about fourteen years in each sign, the sign placement has little effect on the individual unless other indications in the horoscope elevate the importance of this planet. The sign in which Neptune falls is indicative of the imagination, the spiritual and intuitive faculties, and the mystical leanings: all those intangible qualities in human nature. Neptune's influence in world trends and generational developments during the twentieth century as it traverses the zodiac from Gemini into Aquarius is discussed in Chapter 28. Neptune in Aries (1861/62-1874/75; circa 2024-2038) fires the imagination. It induces active expression of imaginative impulses and psychic promptings. Perhaps Neptune's entry into Aries in the twenty-first century will herald daring underwater exploits, a new approach to psychic phenomena and a world-wide awakening on a spiritual level. Neptune in Taurus (1874/75-1887/88; circa 2038-2053) lends concrete expression to the imagination. It encourages practical application of spiritual inspiration. This position reflects a generation that channels Neptunian energies into avenues of material concern.

Neptune in Pisces (circa 2012-2024), the sign of dignity, signifies a vivid imagination, strong ascetic tendencies and the potential for almost perfect spiritual attunement. If Neptune is afflicted in this sign, there is danger that the individual may become separated from reality and drift into a world of illusion. Neptune's last appearance in this sign during the midnineteenth century may have contributed to the growth of transcendentalism that took place in the United States at the time. The Moon's Nodes in the Signs The Moon's North and South Nodes always fall in opposing signs. The sign in which the North Node appears shows how a person reaches out to others in order to overcome inertia and to outgrow habits which block progress. The North Node's sign denotes the manner in which one approaches the new experiences that are essential to development. The sign the South Node occupies describes the way a person expresses habitual tendencies that are responsible for stagnation, those tendencies that retard potential development. The North Node in Aries and the South Node in Libra require initiative and self-assertion to overcome the tendency to rely on others. Indecision and the failure to strike out on one's own inhibit growth. The North Node in Taurus and the South Node in Scorpio call for practical and determined action as the means of releasing deep-seated emotional frustrations which limit development. In some instances there may be sexual connotations with these sign positions. If so, the individual can find meaningful expression in sexual love only after self-centered passions are conquered. The North Node in Gemini and the South Node in Sagittarius emphasize the need for a logical, objective approach that shows concern for central facts. It is necessary to correct the habit of acting on generalities without giving sufficient consideration to essential details. The North Node in Cancer and the South Node in Capricorn indicate that one must reach out with feeling and display sensitivity and protective concern toward others as the means of overcoming selfish ambition, emotional coolness and indifference. The North Node in Leo and the South Node in Aquarius signify the need to extend creativity and personal warmth in order to open doors that have remained closed because of impersonal attitudes or eccentric habits. The North Node in Virgo and the South Node in Pisces stress the importance of discrimination and attention to detail. These sign placements emphasize the need to overcome laziness, indecision and aimlessness. The North Node in Libra and the South Node in Aries show that growth comes through cooperation and association with others. Willfullness and argumentativeness that interfere with establishing productive relationships must be stifled. The North Node in Scorpio and the South Node in Tourus specify the need for regenerating the spirit and mastering materialistic tendencies. So positioned, the Moon's Nodes also have sexual implications, which attach significance to healthy sexual expression and require control of licentious tendencies. The North Node in Sagittarius and the South Node in Gemini point out ethical action based on highminded principles as the key to growth. Clever or cunning habits that stand in the way of honesty must be overcome. The North Node in Capricorn and the South Node in Cancer call for a well-organized approach and constructive action. It is imperative that the individual deal with emotional obstacles that create the tendency to withdraw from others. The North Node in Aquarius and the South Node in Leo require open expression of friendliness founded on humanitarian concepts. One must master egotism and play down self-importance in order to grow. The North Node in Pisces and the South Node in Virgo present self-sacrifice and a deep spiritual concern for the suffering of others as the way to outgrow faultfinding and overly critical tendencies.
