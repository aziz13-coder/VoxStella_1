### Functional Houses

Functional houses are grouped according to complementary function; that is, each house in a group represents a particular branch of the same general function described by the group to which it belongs. This important classification is composed of four groups: individual houses (one, five and nine), all ruled naturally by fire signs, temporal houses (two, six and ten), ruled naturally by earth signs, relative houses (three, seven and eleven), ruled naturally by air signs and terminal houses (four, eight and twelve), ruled by water signs. Individual houses are associated with the person, the self, life. In this context, the first house represents the body, the fifth house represents the creative spirit, and the ninth house represents the abstract intellect. This group is also called the Trinity of Life. Temporal houses have bearing on the material aspects of life. Money and possessions are associated with the second house, work and service are associated with the sixth house, and prestige and career interests are associated with the tenth. These houses are known as houses of substance or possessive houses and form the Trinity of Wealth. Relative houses are concerned with human relationships. The Significance of Each of the Houses By studying the signs and planets that influence a house, the astrologer determines conditions and activity relevant to affairs governed by that house. The sign that appears on a house cusp is the ruling sign of that house: the planetary ruler of the cusp sign is the ruler of the house. Planets occupying a house are co-rulers of the house. In an unequal house (quadrant) system of house division, it is possible for all 30° of a sign different from that on the cusp to fall within a house. A sign that does not appear on a house cusp but is contained entirely within a house is called an intercepted sign. Though its expression is somewhat subdued and obscured by the cusp sign, this sign is significant in house affairs and its planetary ruler is also co-ruler of the house. The natural sign and planetary ruler associated with a house bear some influence in the affairs of that house, for these three, though they function differently, share common rulerships.

### The First House

The first house is the most personal of houses. It represents the individual, the appearance, facial characteristics, mannerisms, disposition, attitudes, the body and the condition of the body, and is thus associated with general health. The first house also signifies personal affairs and beginnings, including the beginning of life. This is the house of self. The natural sign of the first house is Aries: the natural ruler is Mars. The sign on the Ascendant (the cusp of the first house), called the rising sign, essentially influences personal characteristics, temperament, personality and appearance. Along with the Sun and Moon, the Ascendant describes the individual. The Sun represents the ego and defines creative self-expression; the Moon is associated with the responsive side of the personality; the Ascendant portrays the outward expression of personal identity, the projection of the personality. The planetary ruler of the Ascending sign is designated the horoscope ruler or chart ruler. The planetary ruler of a sign that is intercepted in the first house is not the horoscope ruler: however, it is considered a co-ruler of the first house.

### The Second House

The second house represents personal material resources. Money, tangible assets, personal belongings, movable possessions, personal income and expenditures all come under the dominion of the second house. This house describes a person's earning capacity, how personal money is spent, how financial obligations are met, how one budgets, saves and invests. This is the house of personal finance. Taurus is the natural sign of the second house, and Venus is the natural ruler.

### The Third House

The third house rules the intellect and communication. A person's thinking capacity, mental expression, conversational tendencies, literary interests, reading preferences and study habits are described in the third house. Speech, letters, writings, documents, books, magazines, telephones, and primary and secondary education are ascribed to this house. Short trips and commuter travel belong to the third house as does walking, hiking, bike riding and visiting. The third house represents brothers, sisters, relatives in general, neighbors and the neighborhood environment. This is the house of learning. The natural sign of the third house is Gemini; Mercury is the natural ruler.

### The Fourth House

The fourth house deals with the home, family life and domestic affairs. Fixed possessions, houses and property are governed by the fourth house. It represents one s mother and describes the maternal relationship in particular, the parental relationship in general. The fourth house reflects one's inherited tendencies as well as early childhood environment and experiences. The conclusion of undertakings rests tn the fourth house and so do the closing years of life. This is the house of the home. Cancer is the natural sign of the fourth house, and the Moon is the natural ruler
