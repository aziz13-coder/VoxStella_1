### The Twelfth House

The twelfth house represents sorrows, troubles, bondage, limitations, anxieties, worries, confinement, seclusion and all that is secret, hidden or clandestine. Confidential matters, private affairs, secret societies, hidden enemies, treachery and deceit are twelfth house concerns. Charitable organizations, welfare offices and relief agencies are part of this house. Hospitals, sanitariums, rest homes, jails, prisons, reformatories and detention camps are represented by the twelfth house. This house is referred to as the house of selfundoing or karma because it is associated with karmic responsibilities. Pisces is the natural sign of the twelfth house; the natural rulers are Neptune and Jupiter. Developmental Focus of Quadrant Houses The quadrant that contains the largest number of planets indicates the stage of development on which an individual naturally focuses his or her attention according to general properties shared by quadrant houses. The first quadrant (houses one through three) is the "me" or "mine" quadrant and the focus is on self-needs. From this point of view, the first house relates to "me," the second to "my" possessions and the third to "my" environment. The third house also describes learning to communicate in the manner that enables one to fulfill self-desires. This quadrant represents the first stage of development in which a person discovers his or her needs, desires and abilities. If the first is the most heavily populated quadrant in the horoscope, the individual focuses initially on self-development as the first or beginning step toward total development. This does not mean that one remains in the first stage of growth throughout life, but future development is heavily influenced by self-interests. The second quadrant (houses four through six) emphasizes the second stage of development, that of merging self-desires with the needs of others in the home and family (fourth house), through children and creativity (fifth house) and in work and service (sixth house). Although the second quadrant is below the horizon, as is the first quadrant, it is in the western hemisphere and therefore indicates less initiative and greater dependency on others in the developmental process. Charts with second quadrant emphasis stress integrating intellectual, emotional and physical needs on an inner level to prepare for outward development. This is difficult to achieve without first learning the lessons of the previous quadrant. The individual must either combine both stages of development or begin at the beginning and learn self-development before concentrating on second quadrant growth. To begin development in the second quadrant is comparable to starting a book in the second chapter; one must eventually read the first chapter in order to get the most out of what follows. The third stage of development, which takes place in the third quadrant (houses seven through nine), begins with developing the self through relationships and social interaction. Third quadrant emphasis concentrates attention on relationships through social expansion (seventh house), regeneration of energies (eighth house) and intellectual or spiritual growth (ninth house). If third quadrant development is to be fully realized, the first and second developmental stages must first be completed. The fourth quadrant (houses ten through twelve) indicates the final stage in total development, the development of responsible action in life through career goals (tenth house), friends and social awareness (eleventh house) and spiritual progress (twelfth house). Fourth quadrant emphasis is the most difficult with which to deal, for the individual's natural tendency is to begin at the end of the developmental process. One cannot achieve the full potential for growth without mastering each stage of development.

The Influence of the Signs in the Houses Each of the twelve signs influences the horoscope. The sign on the cusp of a house indicates the mode of action applied to the affairs of that house; it shows how one deals with the conditions described therein. An intercepted sign also influences the house it occupies and must be considered along with the sign that appears on the cusp. Influence of the Signs in the First House Signs that influence the first house have bearing on the total person: appearance, temperament, attitudes and personal affairs. The sign on the Ascendant (the cusp of the first house) not only influences the first house, it also colors the entire horoscope because it largely describes the personality. The Ascending sign is reflected in part in everything a person does; it is the astrological signature of the individual.
