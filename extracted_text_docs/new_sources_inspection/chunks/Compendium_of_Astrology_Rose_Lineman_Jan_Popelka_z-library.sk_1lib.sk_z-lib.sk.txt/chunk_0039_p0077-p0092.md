Planets and Moon s Nodes in the Houses The energy of a planet is primarily directed toward the affairs of the house it occupies and the house(s) it rules in the horoscope. A planet has some influence in its natural house, but this influence is secondary to that of the planets posited in that house and those ruling the sign on the cusp or a sign intercepted within the house. Planets also influence houses ruled and occupied by other planets with which they form aspects. The strength and flavor of planetary action channeled through aspects depends upon the planets involved and the type of aspects formed. Aspects are defined in Chapter 14 and discussed in detail in Chapters 16 through 26. A void house, one which contains no planets, does not necessarily indicate lack of activity in related affairs. Aspects to its ruling planet energize an empty house. If few aspects are formed with its ruler, the individual tends to be more objective and less personally concerned about its affairs. Greater interest occurs during periods when transits or progressed planets occupy the house or activate it by aspecting its ruler.
