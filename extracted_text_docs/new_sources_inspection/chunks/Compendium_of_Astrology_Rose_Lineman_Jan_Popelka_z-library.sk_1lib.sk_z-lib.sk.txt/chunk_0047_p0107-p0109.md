124 Compendium of Astrology Each of the six large columns shown in Figure 13 contains five subcolumns headed 11, 12, 1, 2 and 3. The subcolumn headed "1" lists the degrees and minutes of the Ascendant, which is the cusp of the first house. The other subcolumns list the degrees and tenths of a degree of the house cusp that corresponds to the heading number. The numerical value of the digit representing tenths of a degree is multiplied by 6 to obtain minutes of arc (ignore the decimal point). The degrees and decimal point are not repeated for each column entry. If no degree is listed one must look up the column to the previous degree entry. Do not omit degrees; if a house cusp is placed at 0°, a zero will be indicated in the column. The appropriate sign for each house cusp is entered directly below th^ heading number. Sign changes that occur at various latitudes are listed throughout the column. In Figure 13 find the sidereal time 20:12:54 at the top of the page in the fifth column. The MC is listed beside the sidereal time. At this time the MC is 1° Aquarius. Find 41° in the latitude column; go across the row to the large column headed 20:12:54. Look under the corresponding subcolumn headed "1" in the 41° row to find the zodiacal longitude of the Ascendant for a birth that took place at 41° north latitude and occurred at 20:12:54 Local Sidereal Time. The sign of the Ascendant is at the top of that subcolumn directly beneath the heading number "1." The Ascendant for 41° north latitude at 20:12:54 Local Sidereal Time is 22026' Taurus. This is the zodiacal position of the cusp of the first house. The cusp of the seventh house (Descendant) lies directly opposite the Ascendant in the horoscope. In this example, the zodiacal position of the Descendant is22026' Scorpio, the point opposite 22026' Taurus (the Ascendant) in the zodiac. To determine the remaining Placidean house cusps for this same sidereal time and latitude, find the cusps for 2, 3, 11 and 12 in the 41° row under the large column headed 20:12:54 in Figure 13. These differences alter the interpretation of the horoscopes. The student should practice with both types until he or she can choose the one that produces the most accurate picture of the individual it represents. If the Local Sidereal Time at birth falls between two times listed in the Table of Houses, an interpolation is made to obtain the correct house cusps at the exact Local Sidereal Time. A similar correction is made to account for the difference between birth latitude and the listed latitude for all localities that do not lie exactly on a parallel of latitude (zero minutes). These calculations are given in Chapter 12.
