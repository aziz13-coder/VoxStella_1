To Correct the Intermediate House Cusps for Time The intermediate house cusps are corrected for time in the same manner as the Ascendant. Always note whether a house cusp moves forward or backward in the zodiac as the sidereal time increases from the earlier reference ST to the later ST. The cusp correction is subtracted from the earlier position if the cusp moves backward (decreases).

### Latitude Correction_for House Cusps

All house cusps except the MC must be corrected for latitude unless the exact latitude of the birthplace is listed in the Table of Houses. In order to calculate the correction for latitude, the latitude increment is divided by the latitude interval, and the result is multiplied by the distance in arc that the time corrected cusp moves during the latitude interval. For most Tables of Houses the latitude interval is 1° (the difference between the degree of birth latitude and the next higher degree of latitude listed); the latitude increment equals the minutes of latitude of the birthplace. The mathematical operations that yield the cusp correction for latitude are similar to those used to determine the time correction for house cusps. The
