### Charting Placidean Houses

The houses form the skeleton of the horoscope. After the house cusps are calculated, they are entered on a chart form. There are two methods of charting Placidean houses. In one method the Ascendant is entered in the eastern section of the horoscope and labeled according to its zodiacal position. The remaining house cusps are drawn in at the exact degree they occupy in the zodiac relative to the Ascendant. Ecliptic chart forms, which denote degree increments on the outer circle, are available; the face of the horoscope is blank.
