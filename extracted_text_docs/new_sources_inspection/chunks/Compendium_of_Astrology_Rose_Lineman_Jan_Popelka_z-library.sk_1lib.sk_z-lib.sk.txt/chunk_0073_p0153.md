### Counseling

Astrology reveals a person's strengths, weaknesses and potentials. It points out the mode of approach or focus best suited to the individual as the means of overcoming obstacles and making the best possible use of his or her potentials in each area of life. It neither condemns nor sanctions a particular way of life. In translating the symbolism of the horoscope into language meaningful to the client, the astrologer deals with astrological facts. It is not the function of the astrologer to make moral judgments; personal views and standards are not acceptable in delineation. Many clients tend to develop close ties with their astrologers, whom they regard as confidants and friends. It is difficult at times for an astrologer not to exert personal influence, unwittingly perhaps, when a troubled client inquires about a specific course of action. However, an astrologer's advice is correctly limited to pointing out all ramifications evident in the horoscope that pertain to the inquiry. The choice to act or not and in what direction rests with the client. The astrologer is ethically bound to protect confidences revealed in consultations, to honor the client's trust and to counsel as objectively and honestly as humanly possible.

### Sun Aspects

The interpretation of a particular aspect depends upon the basic significance of the aspect, the nature of the planets that form the aspect, the signs that these planets occupy, and the houses and affairs that come under the influence of the planets in aspect. The interpretations given in the text must be modified according to sign expression and adapted to the affairs governed by the planets in aspect. An aspect's influence may be qualified by other aspects to the same planet; however, one aspect does not cancel out another aspect. For example, a trine or sextile to an afflicted planet may ease the discomfort associated with difficulties or enable one to cope more readily with difficulties, but it will not prevent difficulties from arising.
