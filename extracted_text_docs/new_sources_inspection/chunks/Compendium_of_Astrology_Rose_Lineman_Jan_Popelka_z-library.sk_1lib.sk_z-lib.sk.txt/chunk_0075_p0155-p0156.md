### Sun Conjunct North Node Opposite South Node: The

Sun in conjunction with the North Node simultaneously forms an opposition with the South Node; both aspects are considered in the interpretation. This configuration suggests that a solar or lunar eclipse occurred near the date of birth. If this eclipse is a natal or prenatal eclipse, these aspects are emphasized accordingly. These aspects direct one's attention toward avenues that permit extensive self-development in accordance with the influence of the conjunction. Willpower is the key with which to overcome restrictive habit patterns as indicated by the South Node.

### Sun Conjunct Ascendant Opposite Descendant: The

interpretation for a Sun/Ascendant conjunction automatically includes the Sun/Descendant opposition. These aspects enhance vitality, willpower and selfconfidence. One is extremely conscious of his or her individuality, abilities and ambitions. This person strives to achieve distinction through personal efforts rather than rely on circumstances or the help of other people. If the conjunction is afflicted, the individual is inclined to be conceited and given to self-aggrandizement. Sun Conjunct MC Opposite IC: These aspects rivet one's attention on career and professional status and urge one to seek public acclaim; they are indicative of the individual who sacrifices family interests for the sake of worldly attainments unless other factors in the horoscope emphasize family affairs. Afflictions to the conjunction suggest misdirected or futile career endeavors, the pursuit of vocational goals for which the individual is unsuited and perhaps family obligations that interfere with career ambitions.

### Sun Sextiles

Sun sextiles indicate opportune avenues of selfexpression; they point out those lines of endeavor to which one's abilities can be advantageously applied. They enable one to bypass, overcome or find the means to deal with adversities that are generated by afflictions to the Sun. Sun Sextile Moon: This aspect fosters success because the drive for significance is in harmony with emotional drives. These people feel confident and secure about themselves. Self-expression is not hindered by the need to justify personal actions. This sextile enables one to establish a comfortable rapport with the public. It also lends to domestic accord because the individual wants to create a home life that is similar to the harmonious family environment remembered as a major influence in childhood. Sun Sextile Mars: This sextile strengthens the vital life forces and adds to natural incentive and enthusiasm. The individual is confident and decisive within the dictates of sign characteristics. Fruitful opportunities for constructive self-expression and productive use of physical energies are indicated in the affairs influenced by the Sun and Mars. Sun Sextile Jupiter: With this aspect the fortunate properties of Jupiter manifest as profuse and rewarding opportunities forself-expression in the areas of horoscope influence. Jupiter tends to bring out benevolence, integrity and the more positive Sun sign attributes. The individual has high ideals and unselfish motives. Sun Sextile Saturn: This sextile enables the individual to structure endeavors around concrete objectives in such a manner that position and success can be attained through personal efforts. Progress is made in a gradual, steady climb to the top. This aspect denotes industriousness, perseverance and prudence. The serious, conservative side of one's nature is emphasized. Sun Sextile Uranus: This aspect enhances personal charisma. The individual is forceful, magnetic and intuitive. Leadership ability is supplemented by a dramatic style of expression and an accurate sense of timing. Occult leanings, which frequently manifest as an interest in astrology, are indicated. Sun Sextile Neptune: This sextile inspires creative selfexpression. Hopes and dreams make up a large part of life and one has the drive to transform those dreams into reality. This aspect stirs spiritual and psychic tendencies, aesthetic leanings, sensitivity and compassion. If artistic talent is shown elsewhere in the horoscope, this aspect generates creative inspiration and confirms such ability. Sun Sextile Pluto: This aspect regenerates willpower and the vital life forces. The individual is able to renew self-confidence, determination and physical stamina through the restorative powers of inner resources. This sextile signifies development of creative abilities and evolvement through self-expression. It indicates opportunities that permit the individual to master the affairs ruled by the Sun and Pluto.

### Sun Sextile Ascendant Trine Descendant; These

aspects insure opportunities for self-expression on a personal and individual level. They also encourage cooperation and harmonious relations with society. This person is able to function equally well as an independent individual or as a member of a partnership or team. Sun Sextile MC Trine IC: These aspects promote opportunities for career and professional advancement; they open up areas of business and political activity if such interests are confirmed by other factors in the horoscope. Domestic affairs run smoothly for the individual. Home-related responsibilities do not interfere with career aspirations unless conflicting indications are present in the horoscope because this person tries to maintain a family life that supports worldly ambitions.
