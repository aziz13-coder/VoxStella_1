### Sun Squares

Sun squares block creative self-expression in the areas of horoscope influence and create obstacles that hinder the individual's drive to achieve. They indicate egofrustrating experiences and produce stress, which can be harmful to one's health. Sun Square Moon: This square generates much inner tension. The individual derives little emotional satisfaction from personal achievements. Discontentment and emotional frustration hinder natural self-expression. Self-consciousness creates strained relations with the public in general and with members of the opposite sex in particular. Parental conflicts during childhood color the individual's adult attitudes toward home and family. Sun Square Mars: This aspect unleashes the passion of Mars and the vitality of the Sun. Much hardship is created through impulsive actions, many of which are motivated by arrogance or anger. A favorable aspect to Satum from either the Sun or Mars will do much to stabilize the unrestrained energy and ambition that is generated by this square. This aspect denotes a hot temper, pugnacity, argumentativeness, extreme competitiveness and, if other aspects confirm, an inclination toward cruelty and violence. Sun Square Jupiter: This square produces conceit, selfindulgence, overbearing self-pride, an exaggerated selfimage and excessive optimism. These characteristics create problems for the individual, which stand in the way of acceptance and success. Tendencies toward pomposity, extravagance and ostentation block natural self-expression. Immoderation and intemperance may present a threat to health. Sun Square Satum: This aspect severely restricts the warmth and vitality of the Sun. The individual tends to be selfish, cold, aloof and extremely reserved. Selfexpression is thwarted by the failure to reach out. Trying circumstances and heavy responsibilities represent handicaps to success; with this square, one can achieve ambitions only through hard work and dedicated effort. The father or a child may become a burden to the individual. Recuperative powers and resistance to disease are impaired. Sun Square Uranus: This aspect brings out rebellious tendencies. Willful actions and erratic behavior generate disruptive conditions, which create obstacles to one's ambitions. Self-expression is awkward. Sporadic endeavors that lack sustaining force hinder progress in the affairs ruled by the Sun and Uranus. Lack of caution and disregard for standard procedures can cause mishaps that endanger the health and safety of the individual and imperil the physical well-being of colleagues. Sun Square Neptune: In this square, Neptune weakens basic Sun-sign characteristics and saps the vitality. This aspect produces self-delusion and an unrealistic selfimage. The individual is a dreamer; pronounced escapist tendencies exist. The possibility of being deceived or of intentionally deceiving others is indicated. The affairs ruled by the Sun and Neptune

### Sun Square North Node Square South Node: These

aspects form a T-square configuration. They indicate that the inability to adjust to current trends causes frustrations and creates stumbling blocks. The individual tends to cling to outdated methods or restrictive attitudes (as indicated by the South Node) that restrain self-expression; this person fails to keep pace with the times. In order to overcome these blocks, the individual must direct attention to the areas influenced by the Sun and try to develop positive Sunsign attributes. Sun Square Part of Fortune: This square describes obstacles that, although they may not frustrate selfexpression, hamper the attainment of related objectives. Self-directed activities bear less fruit than might otherwise be expected in the affairs influenced by the Part of Fortune. Sun Square Ascendant Square Descendant: The mutual squares formed by these aspects indicate that the individual does not project a favorable image. This person is at odds with him- or herself and, therefore, at odds with society. In this configuration, the Sun represents the focal point of a T-square: thus it is in those directions, which come under the Sun's influence, that the individual must apply constructive efforts. Sun Square MC Square IC: These aspects denote conflicts between worldly ambitions and family interests. The individual must pursue the avenues indicated by the Sun in order to surmount barriers that interfere with career success and domestic harmony.
