### Sun Tvines

Sun trines supplement the leadership potential signified by the Sun. They facilitate self-expression and imply ease and good fortune connected with the affairs governed by the Sun and the planets in trine with the Sun. Sun Trine Moon: This trine produces inner harmony, which in turn enables the individual to function smoothly. Because the individual relates well to other people, he or she can gain acceptance and achieve success in the affairs influenced by the Sun and Moon. This aspect mitigates the effects of adverse aspects to either the Sun or Moon and reduces the emotional frustration that might otherwise accompany afflictions to the luminaries. This trine signifies a happy childhood and promotes a harmonious family life in adulthood. Sun Trine Mars: This aspect produces much vitality, ambition and daring. The individual is enthusiastic, energetic, confident, decisive and competitive. This trine generates spontaneous self-expression and sparks self-motivation. Natural leadership qualities are brought to the fore. It fosters interest and/or ability in sports and promotes physical fitness. Sun Trine Jupiter: This trine gives abundant selfconfidence without conceit; it grants self-satisfaction without complacence. A great deal of personal pride and integrity are possessed. This person strives to perfect natural abilities and to utilize talent in a worthy manner. This aspect promotes ease of self-expression and good luck in the affairs governed by the Sun and Jupiter. It encourages interest in religion or philosophy. The vital life forces are safeguarded and one is protected against the debilitating effects of disease. Sun Trine Saturn: This aspect is indicative of success and status. It induces realistic goals and constructive use of abilities. There is respect for authority, convention and tradition. The individual is patient, conservative, diligent and prudent. Self-expression is serious and practical. This aspect indicates longevity if contrary factors are not present in the horoscope.

### Sun Trine North Node Sextile South Node: These

aspects denote the opportunity and capacity to overcome personal limitations. They reduce the restrictions imposed by the South Node and enable the individual to develop through creative self-expression. Sun Trine Part of Fortune: This trine indicates that the self can be successfully expressed in the affairs influenced by the Part of Fortune.

### Sun Trine Ascendant Sextile Descendant: These

aspects tend to bring out positive personality characteristics. The individual projects a confident, optimistic image. One is able to obtain cooperation and support and to establish harmonious relationships. Sun Trine MC Sextile IC: These aspects describe occupational advantages and family support. They generate a productive and happy home life in combination with career success and status.
