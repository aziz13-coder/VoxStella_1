### Sun Parallels

Parallels that occur between planets that also form a zodiacal aspect assume the tenor of the zodiacal aspect; these parallels strengthen the influence of the zodiacal aspect. For example, a parallel acts in a manner similar to that of a conjunction and when formed between two planets in trine gives added force to the trine. If a parallel occurs between two planets in square, the stress induced by the square is greatly magnified by the parallel. A parallel that occurs between two planets that simultaneously form a minor zodiacal aspect elevates the minor aspect to the status of a major aspect. Parallels that are unaccompanied by a zodiacal aspect are given the same general meaning as that of a conjunction. However, because parallels can occur between planets that are widely separated by sign and house, the influence of a parallel that stands alone is not as focused as that of a conjunction. An unaccompanied parallel exerts less impact and is interpreted more broadly than a conjunction. The meaning of a conjunction may be modified, as it applies to an unaccompanied parallel, by the signs that the planets in parallel occupy. Planets that are mutually friendly form a harmonious parallel regardless of sign position. Planets in parallel that are naturally neutral or antagonistic toward each other operate smoothly when they occupy different signs of the same triplicity. The effect is discordant when neutral or antagonistic planets, which occupy different signs of the same quadruplicity, form a parallel. If planets in parallel occupy the same sign but are not within orb of a conjunction, the parallel takes on the full character and influence of a conjunction. Sun parallels unify the creative life force of the Sun with the drives that are basic to the planet(s) in parallel. If a zodiacal aspect exists between the Sun and a planet in parallel with the Sun, strong willpower and ambition are expressed according to the influence of the zodiacal aspect. The interpretation for this parallel corresponds to the meaning of the zodiacal aspect; the combined aspects are given greater weight in the horoscope than single aspects of the same type. If no zodiacal aspect is formed between the Sun and a planet in parallel with the Sun, the parallel is interpreted as a Sun conjunction with some modifications. Except for parallels with Mercury or Venus, the effect of a Sun parallel is milder than that of any major zodiacal aspect the Sun forms. As with all aspects, the influence extends to the houses and affairs governed by both planets. The natural emphasis of a corresponding Sun conjunction may be qualified by the relationship (harmonious or discordant) that exists between the Sun sign and the sign occupied by a planet in parallel. In addition, the following ideas are to be considered when adapting the interpretation of a Sun conjunction to a Sun parallel that stands alone. Sun Parallel Moon: This parallel permits emotional drives to constructively support creative self-expression. It does not give the emotional harmony and selfsatisfaction that a Sun/Moon conjunction describes unless the luminaries occupy compatible signs. Sun Parallel Mercury: This aspect merges willpower and mental energy; it bears equal strength with that of a Sun/Mercury conjunction regardless of sign positions. Sun Parallel Venus: This parallel is given the same meaning and power as a Sun/Venus conjunction except that it does not carry the connotation of possible male effeminacy. Sun Parallel Mars: This aspect takes on the emphasis of an unafflicted conjunction when the Sun and Mars are in harmonious signs. If neither planet is afflicted, the parallel is interpreted as a mildly favorable conjunction even if the planets occupy uncongenial signs. If either the Sun or Mars forms major adverse aspects with other planets, and they are in discordant signs, the parallel is interpreted as an afflicted conjunction. Sun Parallel Jupiter: This aspect has the same significance as a favorable Sun/Jupiter conjunction except the effect is milder. Sun Parallel Saturn: The emphasis of this parallel depends upon whether or not the signs occupied by the Sun and Saturn are harmonious. Sun Parallel Uranus: In this aspect the energies of the Sun and Uranus do not blend compatibly unless the planets occupy harmonious signs. Sun Parallel Neptune: This parallel carries a positive or negative influence according to sign harmony or discord. Sun Parallel Pluto: This aspect is not as potent as a Sun/Pluto conjunction; nevertheless, it increases the power potential of the individual, and, if sign placements agree, gives greater capacity to control that power. Sun Parallel North Node: This parallel has a slightly different orientation than a Sun/North Node conjunction. Although the individual's attention is directed toward channels of self-development as with the conjunction, this person may not recognize or want to improve restrictive habits, which hinder growth. Sun Parallel Part of Fortune; This aspect is as favorable as a Sun/Part of Fortune conjunction. Sign relationships have no bearing on the emphasis of this parallel. Sun Parallel Ascendant: This aspect has a very personal connotation. It is harmonious and constructive regardless of the signs involved.
