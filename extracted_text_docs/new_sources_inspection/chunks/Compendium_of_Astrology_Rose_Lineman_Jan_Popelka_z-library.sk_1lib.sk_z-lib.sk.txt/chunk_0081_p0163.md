### Moon Conjunction

Moon conjunctions have a strong impact on the emotions. Emotional considerations have a pronounced influence in the affairs governed by the Moon and a planet it conjuncts. Moon Conjunct Mercury: This conjunction unites two receptive planets; neither acts without the other. Feelings color reason, and logic influences the emotions. If the conjunction is in a water sign, one's moods and emotions tend to override intellectual considerations; if the conjunction is in an air sign and not severely afflicted, the individual retains objectivity; a fire sign indicates impulsive emotions and impetuous decisions; an earth sign gives more stability to emotional and intellectual expression. A mixed conjunction permits a broader scope of emotional and mental expression because each sign introduces a different mode of expression. The conjunction describes a person who is extremely sensitive because the subconscious and conscious minds function as one. If unafflicted, it indicates a good memory and strong powers of recall. If the conjunction is afflicted, the individual is restless. overly sensitive to criticism, tends to talk excessively and to gossip. Moon Conjunct Venus: This aspect, if unafflicted, brings out the pleasing qualities in one's personality; it denotes charm, grace and sociability. This person is an affectionate individual who feels emotionally unfulfilled without love. He or she gets along well with other people and especially enjoys the company of women. A love of beauty and material comforts is fostered by this conjunction. It indicates refined tastes and artistic instincts. Memories of childhood and the parental home are pleasant; family ties are affectionate. If the conjunction is afflicted, narcissism, sensuality, voluptuousness and eroticism are implied. Moon Conjunct Mars: This conjunction energizes the emotions. Emotional expression is characterized by Martian qualities. This aspect produces aggressiveness, masculinity, courage and intense feelings. Much energy is directed toward the home and family and the affairs influenced by the conjunction. If the conjunction is afflicted, an uncontrollable temper, vindictive anger and explosive emotions are indicated. Quarrels and strife disrupt domestic life and the affairs governed by the Moon and Mars. Moon Conjunct Jupiter: This aspect gives positive emotional expression as signified by sign position. The individual is good-natured, jocular, sympathetic and generous. One benefits from contacts with the public and is protected from public censure. Women are helpful to the individual and vice versa. Domestic interests are safeguarded as are the other interests

### Moon Conjunct North Node Opposite South Node

These aspects show that a lunar or solar eclipse occurred near the birthdate. If the eclipse is a natal or prenatal eclipse, the interpretation is emphasized accordingly. These aspects enable one to instinctively adapt to trends associated with the affairs influenced by the Moon and North Node. Even if the Moon and North Node occupy a fixed sign, this person is subconsciously aware of prevailing conditions and has the capacity to utilize those conditions advantageously; it is through such action that the individual can outgrow the limitations signified by the South Node. Moon Conjunct Part of Fortune: This conjunction describes an individual who derives much emotional satisfaction from the successes achieved in the affairs influenced by the conjunction.

### Moon Conjunct Ascendant Opposite Descendant

These aspects give the ability to project the personality in a manner that is pleasing to the public if the Moon/Ascendant conjunction is not afflicted. Unless contrary factors indicate otherwise, the individual is receptive and responsive. This person is changeable and moody. Appearance and mannerisms reflect lunar characteristics. Moon Conjunct MC Opposite IC: These aspects indicate an aptitude for public life. The individual instinctively senses public opinion and has the ability to use this gift. Women may be helpful in advancing the career. This person attracts favorable publicity unless the Moon is badly afflicted.
