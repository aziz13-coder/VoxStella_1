### Moon Sextiles

Moon sextiles point out those areas that offer the greatest opportunity for constructive emotional expression. One's subconscious is active in the affairs ruled by the Moon and a planet in sextile. The individual is sensitive to the conditions affected by the sextile and responsive to associated opportunities. Moon sextiles also describe a pleasant childhood and a happy relationship with the mother, a relationship

Moon Sextile Mercury: Thissextileproduces intelligence, a good memory, common sense, and intellectual perceptivity. Because it fosters fluent communicative skills, it is an asset to the linguist, the lecturer and the writer. The affairs influenced by the Moon and Mercury are handled in an intelligent manner. This aspect helps prevent psychological problems because the emotions and mind are in harmony. These people are usually happy with the decisions they make. Moon Sextile Venus: This aspect indicates personal charm and popularity. These people are kind, gracious, amiable and loving. Because they instinctively know how to put others at-ease, they are in demand socially. Most are artistically inclined. This aspect produces a happy marriage and a fulfilling home life unless strong contradictions are present in the horoscope. In a woman's horoscope, this sextile is one indication of fertility if neither planet is in a barren sign. The affairs ruled by the Moon and Venus usually prosper financially unless these planets are heavily afflicted. With afflictions, prosperous developments are possible, but attendant expenses can reduce the overall benefit. Moon Sextile Mars: This sextile gives much emotional force with the control necessary to direct this energy productively. These people are resolute, vigorous and energetic. They take an active interest in the affairs governed by the Moon and Mars. Because action is quick and direct, these people lose few of the opportunities generated by this sextile. Moon Sextile Jupiter: This is a good luck aspect. These people possess sound instincts about the affairs ruled by the Moon and Jupiter; they frequently profit from action based on a lucky hunch. This sextile indicates a happy, healthy emotional nature. It fosters optimism, magnanimity, benevolence and high principles. Moon Sextile Saturn: Any aspect between the Moon and Saturn restrains emotional expression to some degree. This aspect produces good emotional control and efficient use of emotional energies. The individual is patient, prudent and provident. The affairs influenced by the Moon and Saturn are handled in a responsible fashion. This sextile induces introversion and subdued emotional reactions. Moon Sextile Uranus: This sextile adds sparkle to the personality. These people come across as vivacious and exciting. They are intuitive, progressive and individualistic. They respond swiftly to the opportunities associated with this sextile. They are able to take advantage of changing conditions that influence the affairs governed by the Moon and Uranus; they benefit from the original and timely manner in which they conduct these affairs. Moon Sextile Neptune: This aspect fosters spiritual inspiration. The subconscious and unconscious minds are mutually supportive. Highly intuitive and extremely psychic, these people are drawn toward the mystic arts. They are imaginative; many possess a photographic memory. These people are sensitive to conditions influenced by the Moon and Neptune and are responsive to the opportunities produced by this aspect. Moon Sextile Pluto: This aspect permits regeneration of emotional energies; these people are never completely drained emotionally. This intense emotional strength generates a powerful sense of purpose. In addition, these people are able to transform emotional energy into physical energy. They exert tremendous energy into the affairs ruled by the Moon and Pluto. This aspect also promises opportunities that can lead to Plutonian evolvement on the highest level.

### Moon Sextile North Node Trine South Node: These

aspects enable the individual to advance in the areas described by the Moon and North Node because this person is aware of public opinion and helpful trends. One is able to shed outmoded ways as indicated by the South Node, ways that might otherwise restrain progress and hinder development. Moon Sextile Part of Fortune: This sextile alerts one to advantages that arise in the areas influenced by the Moon and Part of Fortune. One's instincts play a large part in related successes.

### Moon Sextile Ascendant Trine Descendant: These

aspects denote harmonious relationships with the general public and with close associates. This person is able to create a receptive emotional climate under most conditions unless the Moon is badly atflicted or in its detriment.
