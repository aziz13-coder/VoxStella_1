### Moon Trines

A trine formed by the Moon enables one to express emotional energy in a positive way. The individual is sensitive to the feelings of others. This person instinctively strikes a harmonious rapport with the people encountered in the course of affairs ruled by the Moon and a planet in trine. The individual derives much emotional fulfillment and happiness from the success attained in the areas influenced by a Moon trine. A Moon trine also indicates by sign placement, house position and rulership that part of childhood that makes a favorable impression and is reflected in relations with women and in adult family life. Moon Trine Mercury: This trine gives intellectual expression to the emotions and adds emotional coloring to the mental processes. These people express their ideas and feelings fluently and tactfully, without embarrassment or misunderstanding. They instinctively sense another person's response and thus are able to phrase ideas in a way that is acceptable. Energy flows freely between the subconscious and conscious minds. These people possess more than adequate learning ability; they readily assimilate and retain the knowledge to which they are exposed. A good memory, common sense and eloquent expression of ideas are characteristic of this trine. These people are witty, mentally alert and intellectually versatile. They treat domestic affairs and those interests governed by the Moon and Mercury with perceptivity, feeling and good sense. They communicate well with their families. Moon Trine Venus: This aspect denotes good taste, gentility and charm. These people are kind, sympathetic, affectionate and sociable. Both sexes get along well with women, and most enjoy a happy, fulfilling domestic life. Because of their sweet and thoughtful ways, they encounter much good fortune in the areas influenced by the Moon and Venus. This trine attracts material benefits and prosperity. It generates interest in the arts and a desire for the finer things in life. Moon Trine Mars: This trine activates emotional energy, which can be channeled constructively into the affairs governed by the Moon and Mars. These people feel strongly about what they do; therefore, action is backed up by emotional force. They achieve success because they are ambitious, purposeful and aggressive. They are active in family life and in the affairs influenced by the trine; in related matters, they take the initiative. If a family inheritance is suggested elsewhere in the horoscope, this trine confirms a substantial legacy. Moon Trine jupiter: This trine indicates good fortune in the affairs ruled by the Moon and Jupiter. It provides a natural outlet for emotional frustrations, which are caused by afflictions to the Moon if present. These people hold high ideals and are devoted to the causes or principles that they support. Although spiritual rewards are part of the promise of this trine, material abundance also manifests in the areas influenced by the trine. Family life is pleasant and usually financially beneficial. Moon Trine Saturn: This aspect stabilizes the emotional nature. It inspires caution, conservatism and efficiency. Steady, well-planned efforts represent the key to success in the affairs governed by the Moon and Saturn, Because these people are realistic and

Moon Trine Uranus: This aspect permits free emotional expression. Emotional reactions may be unpredictable, but these people cannot be termed erratic or eccentric. Rather, they are colorful and interesting. They look forward to and frequently initiate changes in the affairs ruled by the Moon and Uranus. They are not bound by tradition or subconscious ties. They possess unusually keen instincts about public opinion and current trends; action is seldom too early or too late to be effective. Friendly, they derive much emotional satisfaction from companionship and group associations. Most of these people are interested in metaphysics and positive occultism; many are drawn to astrology. Moon Trine Neptune: This aspect brings out the spiritual qualities in one's nature. Many of these people are divinely inspired; some attain a state of sublime ecstacy. The subconscious and unconscious minds function in harmony. This trine offers a constructive outlet for repressed desires and subconscious drives. Gentle, sympathetic, imaginative, impressionable, intuitive, psychic and emotionally responsive, these people benefit spiritually through the affairs governed by the Moon and Neptune. If talent is indicated elsewhere in the horoscope, this trine confirms the ability to express the emotions through music, dance or other art forms. Moon Trine Pluto: This trine gives great emotional strength with the power to control and direct this energy constructively. These people exert vast influence in the affairs ruled by the Moon and Pluto. They do not have to resort to coercion in order to achieve their aims; the powerful emotional appeal they possess attracts the benefits promised by the trine. This trine adds emotional depth even if the signs involved or other aspects indicate shallowness. It produces sharp insight into the inner nature of things and does much to counteract any factors that suggest a lack of perceptivity. This aspect also paves the way to complete Plutonian evolvemnt.

### Moon Trine North Node Sextile South Node: This

combination of aspects indicates that one's instincts are in tune with the times. This person is able to discard subconscious attitudes that are restrictive in nature. The capacity to overcome the limitations described by the South Node leaves one free to achieve emotionally satisfying experiences. Moon Trine Part of Fortune: This trine promises benefits and success in the area indicated by the placement of the Part of Fortune. This success is achieved through utilization of lunar drives; resultant benefits are emotionally gratifying.

### Moon Trine Ascendant Sextile Descendant: These

aspects promote harmony in most relationships unless the Moon or the horizontal angles are badly afflicted. These people are sensitive to the feelings and needs of others; they respond instinctively in a manner designed to please. Moon Trine MC Sextile IC; This aspect combination is one indication of professional success unless the Moon or MC is severely afflicted. These people know the value of maintaining good relations with superiors and are able to do so. Home life reflects the happiness and success that is achieved in worldly pursuits.
