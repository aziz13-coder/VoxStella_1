### Moon Parallels

One's emotional expression and natural instincts are linked to the energies and drives represented by a planet with which the Moon forms a parallel. A Moon parallel can produce a harmonious or a discordant merging of planetary energies. The presence or absence of a zodiacal aspect between the Moon and a planet in parallel, the natural relationship between the two planets and the signs the two planets occupy, all have a bearing on the influence of a parallel. A parallel that occurs between the Moon and another planet with which the Moon also forms a zodiacal aspect reinforces the zodiacal aspect. This kind of parallel is interpreted according to the meaning of the zodiacal aspect it supplements. Such a parallel plays a supportive role to a major zodiacal aspect; it gives the strength of a major aspect to a minor zodiacal aspect. Moon parallels that are formed with planets that do not form a zodiacal aspect with the Moon are interpreted as Moon conjunctions. A Moon parallel is not as powerful as the corresponding conjunction unless the Moon and the planet in parallel occupy the same sign. The Moon forms naturally harmonious parallels with Venus and Jupiter. Any other Moon parallel that stands alone takes on the positive characteristics of the corresponding Moon conjunction if the Moon and the planet in parallel occupy compatible signs. A Moon parallel formed with any planet other than Venus or Jupiter is discordant if the Moon and the planet in parallel occupy different signs of the same quadruplicity. A Moon parallel is adversely affected by afflictions to either the Moon or the planet it parallels. When interpreting a Moon/Satum parallel, disregard the reference to the Moon/Satum cycle given in the interpretation for a Moon/Saturn conjunction. This cycle is based upon zodiacal motion; it does not affect parallels or contraparallels.

### Moon Contraparallels

Moon contraparallels create emotional conflicts. The houses and affairs ruled by the Moon and a planet in contraparallel are sources of emotional distress. Related activities provide little emotional satisfaction. A trine or sextile between the Moon and a planet in contraparallel permits a large measure of satisfaction, but neither of these helpful aspects can give complete emotional fulfillment in combination with a contraparallel. A contraparallel that the Moon forms with the Ascendant or MC is treated as an afflicted conjunction. All other Moon contraparallels are interpreted as corresponding Moon oppositions. These aspects are given less weight than oppositions except in those instances when the Moon and a planet forming a contraparallel occupy opposing signs in the zodiac.

Minor Moon aspects describe something about one's emotional nature and subconscious drives. To interpret a minor Moon aspect, consider the principles of the Moon and the planet it aspects, the nature of the signs involved, and the houses ruled and occupied by the two planets. Minor Easy Aspects Formed by the Moon: The semisextile is the most important minor easy aspect the Moon forms. This aspect allows a harmonious flow of energy between the Moon and the planet in semisextile with the Moon. Moon semi-sextiles also point out secondary avenues that can be utilized as constructive outlets for frustrations created by afflictions to the Moon. All minor easy aspects formed by the Moon permit favorable emotional expression through the activities and affairs ruled by the planet that the Moon aspects. The effect is mild but, nevertheless, beneficial. Although these aspects (including the semi-sextile) are not strong enough to overcome afflictions to the Moon, they help offset adverse effects by indicating areas that provide some measure of emotional satisfaction. Minor Hard Aspects Formed by the Moon: Minor hard aspects formed by the Moon signify mild emotional stress and negative subconscious drives. The emotions are disturbed by the affairs and interests associated with a planet that forms a minor hard aspect with the Moon. These aspects can create a subconscious dislike or distaste for the activities and people represented by the Moon and the aspected planet.
