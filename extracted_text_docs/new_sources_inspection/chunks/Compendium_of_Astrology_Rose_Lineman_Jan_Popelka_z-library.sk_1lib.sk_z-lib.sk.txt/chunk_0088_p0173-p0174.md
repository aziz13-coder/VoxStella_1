### Mercury Sextiles

Mercury sextiles are associated with opportunities for intellectual development. One's mental prowess and communicative skills play a major part in the advancement of the affairs influenced by Mercury and a planet in sextile. The signs and houses involved in a Mercury sextile describe helpful mental interests and point out areas in which effective planning attracts opportune conditions. Mercury sextiles enhance literary expression and conversational style; these aspects are extremely helpful to the professional writer or speaker. Mercury Sextile Venus: This sextile produces charming wit and tactful speech. These people are genial and diplomatic; they know how to phrase ideas in a manner that will please others. The voice is wellmodulated and expressive. Because they are gracious and refined, these people are popular. This aspect

This aspect is especially helpful to those who depend upon the voice, language or writing as a vehicle for creative expression—vocalists, composers, diplomats, novelists. Mercury Sextile Mars: This aspect supplies forceful mental energy. These people voice their convictions firmly and without hesitation. They are observant, perceptive, decisive and adroit. Conversation is animated and enthusiastic; language is clear and direct. Many are excellent sales people. They can enthusiastically promote a product and demonstrate its use as well. This sextile enhances motor coordination and manual dexterity. Most of these people combine intellectual and physical interests; they may map out exercise programs or plan strategy in sports or the military. The capacity to formulate constructive plans and to successfully execute them is reflected in the affairs governed by Mercury and Mars. Mercury Sextile Jupiter: This sextile broadens one's intellectual horizons. It indicates abundant opportunities to develop the mind through advanced studies connected with the affairs ruled by Mercury and Jupiter. These people harbor neither intellectual bias nor religious prejudice. They are interested in truth, intellectual honesty and philosophical purity. Most of them are witty and jovial, yet very sincere. This aspect is of particular benefit to persons associated with writing, publishing, universities or religious institutions. Mercury Sextile Saturn: This aspect fosters mental discipline and well-structured thought patterns. It introduces opportunities that can be advanced most successfully by careful planning and systematic followup procedures. These people organize the affairs influenced by the sextile around practical plans of actions. Studious and introspective, they seldom engage in light repartee or small talk. Intellectual inclinations are mainly those associated with professional and business interests. Mercury Sextile Uranus: This aspect denotes all the positive qualities of a Mercury/Uranus conjunction and more. If neither Mercury nor Uranus is afflicted, this sextile indicates intellectual brilliance. The areas governed by Mercury and Uranus present opportunities for the advancement of timely ideas and the utilization of progressive methods. Success comes to these people because they are able to adapt futuristic ideas to the needs of the present. Moreover, they manage to obtain support for innovative ideas. They can convince others of the validity of their plans, for they are magnetic speakers who are masters at elocution and oratory. Mercury Sextile Neptune: This sextile blends intuitive perception with intellectual sensitivity. These people are imaginative and often psychic. Many possess photographic memories. They are spiritually and mentally attuned to the affairs and conditions influenced by the sextile. They are dreamers and idealists, but this aspect fosters practical dreams and gives the opportunity to realize them. Enchanting conversationalists and fascinating speakers, these people are not aggressive. Instead they captivate an audience with the magic of words. Most of them display interest in esoteric subjects. Ability in the mystic arts is indicated if Neptune is unafflicted. Mercury Sextile Pluto: This aspect denotes tremendous intellectual resources. Words are powerful tools to these people. Skilled in the use of language, they know how to choose words for impact. Speech is forceful and compelling. As orators, these people attract massive audiences. This sextile gives penetrating insight into the affairs governed by Mercury and Pluto. Associated activities are well planned and purposeful. These people are capable of regenerating the affairs influenced by the sextile and are able to transform negative conditions into positive factors.
