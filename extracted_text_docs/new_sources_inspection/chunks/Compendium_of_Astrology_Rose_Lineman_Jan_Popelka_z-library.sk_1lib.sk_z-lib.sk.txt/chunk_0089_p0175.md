### Mercury Squares

Mercury squares create nervous tension and learning difficulties. Intellectual expression and communication are inadequate in the areas governed by Mercury and a planet squaring it. Mental blocks and poor planning hinder the proper development of the affairs influenced by a Mercury square. These aspects do not indicate lack of intelligence; they show by sign position, house placement and rulership how and in which areas the individual does not use natural mental abilities to the best advantage. Mercury Square Mars; This square energizes the intellect, but mental energy is difficult to control and channel productively. Through impulsiveness and insufficient planning, these people create problems for themselves in the areas influenced by the square. Brusque to the point of rudeness, they frequently antagonize the very persons whose support would be helpful in developing the affairs governed by Mercury and Mars. Impatience, argumentativeness and anger are characteristic of this square. Coarseness of speech is common. One may risk physical safety performing dangerous, daredevil feats. Health difficulties can also occur as a result of the extreme nervous tension this aspect generates. Mercury Square Jupiter; This aspect promotes improvident schemes and excessive talk. Although these people have fertile minds, they think in terms that are too grandiose to be practical. They are absentminded about details and exercise poor judgment in the affairs ruled by Mercury and Jupiter. Overly optimistic about these matters, they frequently overlook negative potentialities. Tendencies to exaggerate and boast are common. Many of these people are conversational bores who do not know how to listen. This square can indicate an aversion or mental block toward organized religion. Mercury Square Saturn: This square indicates a serious mind and conservative mental attitudes. Often intellectual development is hindered by narrow ideas and prejudicial views. These people cling to traditional thought and the refusal to break with established methods is a major obstacle to the advancement of the affairs affected by the aspect. They hold negative outlooks toward the interests ruled by Mercury and Satum; dwelling on related problems may cause much unnecessary mental anguish. Introspective and reticent to express their opinions, many find casual conversation awkward. Mercury Square Uranus: This aspect denotes a rebellious mind and radical ideas. These people are intellectual nonconformists who frequently disagree with current thought just to be different. They may come up with unprecedented ideas, but few of these ideas have any practical value. Because of the bizarre manner in which they conduct the affairs governed by Mercury and Uranus, many create awkward predicaments for themselves. In attempting to resolve one difficulty, they usually stir up even greater problems. They tend to talk in disjointed phrases and conversation is generally erratic. If Mercury is badly afflicted, this aspect can produce stuttering. Mercury Square Neptune: This square induces forgetfulness, mental confusion and illogical thinking. Impressionable, these people tend to believe everything they read and most of what they hear. They are bewildered by conflicting ideas and controversial theories. Indecisiveness and the failure to form practical plans retard the development of the affairs influenced by the square. During conversation these people wander from one topic to another without making a point. They give evasive answers to pointed questions; many lie outright if backed into a comer. ### Mercury Square North Node Square South Node

These aspects place the individual at an intellectual disadvantage in the areas activated by Mercury, the North Node and the South Node. Ideas are either ahead of or behind the times. Misconceptions arise because of the inability to correctly phrase thoughts. Mercury Square Part of Fortune: This square shows that insufficient planning can reduce the advantages associated with the Part of Fortune. Lack of understanding about the affairs influenced by the square stands in the way of success in these areas.

### Mercury Square Ascendant Square Descendant: This

combination of aspects indicates problems in expressing ideas verbally or in writing, These people are unable to communicate easily with the public or with close associates. Marital relations and partnerships may suffer as a result of breakdowns in communication. Mercury Square MC Square IC: This combination points to inadequate communication and poor planning in relation to fourth and tenth house affairs. Consequently, the home atmosphere is uncomfortable, serious family misunderstandings arise, community standing is jeopardized and career ambitions falter.
