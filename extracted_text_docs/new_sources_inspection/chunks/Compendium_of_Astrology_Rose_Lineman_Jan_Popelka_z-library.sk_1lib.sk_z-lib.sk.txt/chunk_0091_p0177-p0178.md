### Mercury Oppositions

Mercury oppositions produce sharp disagreements. The individual may not understand the affairs influenced by a Mercury opposition, or this person may harbor mental attitudes which give rise to conflict. An inability to fully develop the affairs governed by Mercury and a planet in opposition is a result of the individual's failure to become well informed about these matters. Insufficient planning and inadequate communications in these areas are also indicated. Mercury Opposite Mars: This opposition brings out mental aggressiveness and contrariness. These people argue over anything and everything. If they are unable to win arguments with words they try to outshout opponents. Disrespectful of ideas that are not their own, they are harsh, insolent and loud in speech, and often evoke much hostility. The affairs ruled by Mercury and Mars reflect the consequences of illconsidered actions based on snap judgments and impulsiveness. Mercury Opposite Jupiter: This aspect generates exaggerated plans and inflates the expression of ideas. These people tend to talk good ideas to death. They overestimate their intellectual abilities and misjudge the soundness of their plans. Rather than admit to poor planning, they frequently blame their failures on others. The affairs governed by Mercury and Jupiter are adversely affected by the unrealistic decisions they act on in connection with these matters. Many of these people are at odds with the religious and philosophical views held by the majority of their contemporaries. Mercury Opposite Saturn: This opposition fosters negative mental attitudes and restricts the spontaneous flow of ideas. Intellectual responses and nervous reflexes are slow. These people may be highly intelligent; however, they express ideas in such a prosaic manner that others may think them dull. They have somber, pessimistic mental outlooks and tend to

Mercury Opposite Pluto: Acute awareness and deep comprehension of conflicting ideas are produced by this aspect. These people understand opposing views and the premises on which these views are based. They formulate secret plans, which they execute carefully. Intent about the affairs governed by Mercury and Pluto, they seldom lose sight of these objectives. They are powerful speakers. They may antagonize others with their use of obscene language and overly explicit gestures. At times their language and gestures may become especially offensive.

### Mercury Opposite North Node Conjunct South Node

This combination of aspects induces mental laziness. These people are content with the knowlede they acquire in the natural course of events and often fail to explore timely ideas that could promote intellectual growth and enable them to conquer South Node limitations. Mercury Opposite Part of Fortune: This opposition indicates intellectual disinterest in the affairs influenced by the Part of Fortune. The individual is inclined to ignore the mental challenges presented by this aspect.

### Mercury Opposite Ascendant Conjunct Descendant

These aspects enable one to establish a good intellectual rapport with the general public and with partners. Common mental interests provide the basis for marriage and other close associations. Many of these people attract younger mates. Mercury Opposite MC Conjunct IC: This combination denotes intelligent conversational interchange with family members. The home reflects one's mental interests and intellectual tastes; it is well stocked with books, magazines and educational material.

### Mercury Parallels

A planet that forms a parallel with Mercury influences the mental processes and communicative ability. The signs that Mercury and a planet in parallel occupy color one's intellectual expression. The houses that the two planets occupy and rule give direction to mental interests and communication. A Mercury parallel acts to reinforce any zodiacal aspect that exists between the same two planets. If Mercury and a planet in parallel do not also form a zodiacal aspect, the parallel is interpreted as a corresponding conjunction. A parallel that occurs between Mercury and a planet that occupies the same sign as Mercury carries as much weight in the horoscope as a conjunction. If Mercury and a planet in parallel are placed in different signs, the parallel is given less strength than a conjunction. Mercury forms harmonious parallels with all benefic planets, the Ascendant and the Midheaven (MO. The harmony or disharmony exhibited by a Mercury parallel that is formed with a malefic planet or the North Node depends upon the nature of the signs involved. A discordant parallel is formed if Mercury and the aspected malefic or node occupy different signs of the same quadruplicity.
