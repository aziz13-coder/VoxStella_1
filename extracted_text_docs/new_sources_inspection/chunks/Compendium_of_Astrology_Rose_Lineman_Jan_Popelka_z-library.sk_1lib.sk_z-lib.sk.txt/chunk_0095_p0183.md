### Venus Squares

Planets that form squares with Venus block the graceful, refined qualities that are natural to Venus and interfere with romantic and aesthetic expression. Social interaction in the areas influenced by Venus squares is difficult. These aspects represent obstacles to happiness in marriage; they disrupt harmony in partnerships and generate financial problems. Venus Square Mars: This square creates strong attraction between the sexes. These people are passionate and demanding in love but may be insensitive to the sexual needs and romantic desires of their partners. Romance, sexual involvements and marriage are turbulent; intimate relationships are fraught with arguments. Because of extravagance or mismanagement of funds, the affairs ruled by Venus and Mars are largely unprofitable. Social behavior is often crude; aesthetic taste, undeveloped; partnerships, difficult. Venus Square Jupiter: This aspect overemphasizes social amenities and induces an elaborate display of good manners and gentility. These people are excessive in their expression of affection, sometimes becoming flattering and ingratiating. Both men and women with this aspect tend to be vain about their looks; they value physical beauty in themselves and in their companions. Men see themselves as Adonis-like figures and have delusions about their popularity. Extravagant and careless with money, they are concerned with the comforts, luxuries and pleasures of life. The affairs governed by Venus and Jupiter usually fail to prosper because of excessive expenditures and distorted values. If other factors agree, these people do not like to be alone; however, the companionship they seek through social interaction, marriage or partnership lacks depth and meaning. Venus Square Saturn: This square inhibits social and romantic expression. These people are shy and embarrassed by overt displays of affection. They are not physically demonstrative people, at least not in public. More impressed with utilitarian values than with those of aesthetics or sentiment, they tend to view social interests, marriage and partnerships from a pragmatic standpoint. Intimate relationships produce little happiness or pleasure and often lack the sense

### Venus Square North Node Square South Node: These

aspects indicate lack of social poise and a tendency to ignore social customs. Lack of economic awareness leads them to spend money when they should save and vice versa. It is important that they learn to observe social niceties and to recognize financial trends in order to adequately develop their resources. Venus Square Part of Fortune: Depending upon the signs and houses involved, this square points to setbacks caused by romantic indiscretions or financial mismanagement. Romantic interests and pleasurable pursuits can interfere with the progress of affairs influenced by the Part of Fortune.
