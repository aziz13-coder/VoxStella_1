### Venus Square Ascendant Square Descendant: This

combination of aspects describes a dissatisfied person who, as a result of personal conflicts, is not pleased with the marriage partner and other close associates. These people have difficulty in understanding themselves and in relating to society in general. Venus Square MC Square IC: This aspect combination suggests the inability to establish a profitable social rapport with superiors. It is difficult for these people to sustain family ties or maintain domestic harmony. They tend to resent parents and those professional associates who are more successful than they.

The trines that Venus forms giveeasy expression to social inclinations, artistic interests, romantic tendencies and material desires. A Venus trine promotes beneficial contacts in the affairs influenced by the aspect; related activities are both enjoyable and profitable. Venus rules

Venus Trine Mars: An easy-going and comfortable relationship between the sexes is fostered by this trine. These people enjoy the company of the opposite sex. They are not embarrassed by their sexuality and accept sex as a natural part of life. This aspect is indicative of marital harmony. The affairs ruled by Venus and Mars prosper under the direction of these people. However, they like to spend money, and unless balancing factors are present, save very little for a rainy day. Much personal initiative is directed into social activities, partnerships and other associations. Venus Trine Jupiter: This aspect does much to create prosperity. Most of these people seem to attract wealth and the good things of life. The affairs governed by Venus and Jupiter flow smoothly and profitably with little help from the individual. This is a "good luck" aspect, which promotes good fortune in connection with related interests. These people derive benefits through marriage and partnerships. Artistic ability may be present, but the trine gives little ambition to pursue these inclinations for reasons other than enjoyment. However, a trine or sextile from Saturn or any other stabilizing factor can crystalize talent. If other factors give direction and purpose to artistic ability, the trine promises success and recognition. Venus Trine Saturn: This trine produces enduring marriages and stable partnerships. These people do not rush into marriage or other close relationships. When they make commitments, however, they take their obligations seriously. They are loyal to those they love, although not particularly demonstrative. They are practical with money and other resources and realistic about the affairs governed by Venus and Saturn. Because of prudent management, these interests develop successfully. If artistic ability is indicated in the horoscope, this aspect gives structure and direction to creative inspiration; these people focus their talents constructively. Venus Trine Uranus: This aspect generates lively romances and spontaneous friendships. Vivacious, fun-loving and stimulating, these people are recognized as the trend-setters in social circles and fraternal societies. Most of them form partnerships and friendships that are unusual by popular standards. They are romantically appealing and very attractive to the opposite sex. Marriage is never dull for these people or their spouses. Unexpected financial windfalls frequently manifest in connection with the affairs ruled by Venus and Uranus. This trine produces a distinctive artistic style that is unique to the individual. Venus Trine Neptune: Gentleness, compassion and refined aesthetic taste are characteristic of this trine. These people arouse sympathy in others and attract friends who are kind and thoughtful. They are usually more interested in the spiritual qualities of their relationships than in material prospects. Although they look for spiritual beauty in their companions, the kind of beauty they possess can be best described as alluring, glamorous, enchanting, seductive, fascinating and enticing. This aspect carries a karmic connotation in regard to marriage and the affairs governed by Venus and Neptune. Despite the individual's somewhat lackadaisical attitude toward financial matters, these interests usually prosper. Venus Trine Pluto: This aspect gives strong sexual drives, but it also elevates love to the highest form of expression. These people enjoy love to the fullest. Love relationships are meaningful and frequently inspirational. Spiritual evolvement is linked to marriage and to the associations these people form in the course of affairs influenced by the trine. Constructive social relationships and material benefits are also associated with this trine. These people enrich themselves spiritually and financially; they better the lives of their friends and loved ones. This aspect is one indication of a generous inheritance. Venus Trine North Node Sextile South Node: This aspect combination shows awareness of social and economic trends. These people relate comfortably on a social level. They benefit from timely financial action. Opportunities that enable them to overcome restrictive attitudes are made available through social contacts. Venus Trine Part of Fortune: This trine assures material rewards as a result of the development of affairs influenced by Venus and the Part of Fortune. These interests prosper and bring to the individual some measure of financial success and social acceptance.
