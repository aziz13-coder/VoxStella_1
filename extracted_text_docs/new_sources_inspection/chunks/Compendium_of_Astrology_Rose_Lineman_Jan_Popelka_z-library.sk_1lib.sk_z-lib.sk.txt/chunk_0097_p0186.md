### Venus Oppositions

Venus oppositions indicate romantic and social conflicts. The affairs ruled by Venus and a planet in opposition are mutually antagonistic. These aspects create rifts in marriage, partnerships and other close associations in accordance with the planets, signs and houses involved. They also detract from the harmony and artistic expression that is associated with Venus. A Venus opposition may produce financial discrepancies and economic failures in relation to the interests influenced by the aspect. Venus Opposite Mars: This aspect arouses forceful sexual desires. Sexual conquests are more important than romantic love or feelings of affection, and sex can become a goal in itself. If Venus, which represents the receptive side of sexuality, is the stronger of the two planets by sign position and/or aspects, the individual may be subject to sexual abuse. If Mars, which expresses aggressive sexual urges, is more powerful in the horoscope, the individual may be obsessed with the desire for sexual gratification. Despite strong sexual passions, these people do not relate well to the opposite sex. Intimate relationships are marred by jealousy. Sexual attraction is often the primary basis for marriage. Contention and hostility disrupt the affairs ruled by Venus and Mars. These people tend to quarrel with friends, spouses and partners. Extravagance is likely unless other factors indicate financial prudence. Favorable aspects to Venus or Mars are helpful in reducing the selfish qualities of the opposition and in softening the harshness of sexual expression. Venus Opposite Jupiter: This opposition fosters exaggerated social mannerisms and flowery romantic expression. It also induces vanity, laziness and selfindulgence. These people adopt extravagant social affectations. They greet comparative strangers with effusive familiarity and shower loved ones with flattering words and sentimental gifts. However, much of this attention is insincere and their relationships are largely superficial. Spiritual and material values are in conflict and frequently distorted. Many of these people are spendthrifts who live far beyond their means. The affairs influenced by this opposition lack balance; it is difficult for one to establish proper priorities in relation to the interests ruled by Venus and Jupiter. Venus Opposite Saturn: This aspect places the sociability and affection of Venus in conflict with the aloofness and severity of Satum. It is hard for these people to reach out in love; inhibited and shy with the opposite sex, they suffer romantic disappointments. Marriage promises little emotional fulfillment; the marriage partner may seem cold and uncaring. Many of these people are extremely antisocial; some develop strong reclusive tendencies if other factors concur. Economic setbacks and hardships afflict the affairs governed by Venus and Satum, causing unhappiness and displeasure. Because materialistic considerations outweigh sentiment, this aspect can induce miserliness, greed and avarice. Venus Opposite Uranus: This opposition denotes inconsistancy in friendship, romance and marriage. These people fall in and out of love with alarming abruptness. They are attracted to exotic types and persons who can be classed as "different." Few derive lasting satisfaction from their relationships, and there is a possibility of marital separation or divorce. Some of these people marry several times in search of an exciting partner. Uncooperative and independent, they do not like to be bound by commitments of any sort. The desire for companionship is in direct conflict with the urge for freedom. If other factors in the horoscope support these findings, this aspect is linked to sexual abberations and homosexuality. The affairs ruled by Venus and Uranus are unstable; these interests seldom develop fully unless both Venus and Uranus are otherwise well aspected. This opposition also fosters erratic spending habits and sudden financial reversals.

206 Compendium of Astrology Venus Opposite Neptune: This aspect blurs aesthetic, social, romantic and sexual perceptions. These people idealize lovers and marriage partners. They are vulnerable to romantic disillusionment and sexual seduction. Many have confused or unrealistic conceptions of their sexual identities. Some associate sex with spiritual sublimation. This opposition produces erotic sensitivity. If other astrological factors agree, it may be linked to drug-related sex cults. Bisexual tendencies or confused sexual behavior may also be indicated. Social relationships are subtly undermined and economic structures weakened by this aspect. These people are gullible in marriage, partnerships, finances and the affairs in the horoscope ruled by Venus and Neptune. They are secretive about Venus/Neptune interests and involvement in clandestine relationships is possible. Those who engage in fraudulent activities attract scandal and may be ostracized. Venus Opposite Pluto: This opposition produces overwhelming passions and sexually-rooted conflicts. Aesthetic instincts may be warped by physical urges and material drives. Elements of coercion and cruelty can enter into social, romantic and sexual relationships. This opposition is one indication of a stormy marriage and perhaps separation, divorce, or the abrupt departure of the marriage partner. Underworld involvements can be part of this aspect if other factors in the horoscope reveal similar indications. If illicit activities are suggested, they center around the affairs governed by Venus and Pluto. These people are challenged by this opposition to enlarge the scope of Venus/Pluto activity and influence; these interests represent a power base to be expanded and exploited by any available means.

### Venus Opposite Node Conjunct South Node: These

aspects denote social, romantic and economic disadvantages. The negative tendencies described by the South Node are difficult to overcome because the individual derives pleasure and comfort from the practice of these habits. Venus Opposite Part of Fortune: This opposition interferes with the material and social benefits associated with Venus and the Part of Fortune. Related interests are distasteful to the individual and frequently are unpleasant.

### Venus Opposite Ascendant Conjunct Descendant: This

combination of aspects contributes to happiness in marriage and cooperation in partnerships unless Venus is heavily afflicted. These people relate compatibly to society in general and form alliances that are socially and economically advantageous. Venus Opposite MC Conjunct IC: This aspect combination indicates loving family ties and a harmonious home life. These people are generous with their families. Unless otherfactors elevate the importance of the tenth house, they derive more happiness through family interests than from worldly success. Designed for easy living and entertaining guests, their homes are models of beauty and comfort.
