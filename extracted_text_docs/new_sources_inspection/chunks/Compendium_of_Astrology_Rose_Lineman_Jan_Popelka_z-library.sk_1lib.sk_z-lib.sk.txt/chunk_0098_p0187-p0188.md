### Venus Parallels

Venus parallels influence artistic inclinations, social interaction, romance, marriage, partnerships and financial interests in accordance with the nature of the planet in parallel and the signs and houses occupied and ruled by Venus and the aspected planet. Venusian qualities combine with the energies of a planet in parallel in a manner similar to that characterized by a Venus conjunction. The effect of a Venus parallel is not as powerful as that of a Venus conjunction except in those instances when Venus and the planet in parallel occupy the same sign of the zodiac. If Venus and a planet with which Venus forms a zodiacal aspect also form a parallel, the parallel strengthens the zodiacal aspect; this parallel is given the same meaning as the zodiacal aspect it supports. A Venus parallel that is not accompanied by a zodiacal aspect between Venus and the paralleled planet is interpreted as a modified conjunction when the two planets occupy different signs of the zodiac. An unaccompanied parallel that occurs between Venus and a planet that occupies the same sign as Venus takes on the full character of a corresponding Venus conjunction. A Venus parallel that is formed with Mars, Satum or Pluto assumes the negative characteristics of the corresponding conjunction if the planet in parallel occupies a sign that is incompatible with the sign in which Venus is posited. All other unaccompanied Venus parallels are naturally harmonious and are interpreted as favorable conjunctions.

### Venus Contraparallels

A Venus contraparallel generates discord, disharmony and stress in connection with affairs governed by Venus and the aspected planet. A Venus contraparallel formed with the Ascendant or MC is treated as an afflicted conjunction: other Venus contraparallels are interpreted as mild oppositions. In adapting the meaning of an opposition to the interpretation of a corresponding contraparallel, the antagonistic and separative qualities of the opposition are modified. Dislike is created by a contraparallel instead of the animosity produced by an opposition; distaste is denoted by a contraparallel rather than hostility; annoying social mannerisms replace socially offensive practices; disagreements are indicated but not separations. The negative sexual implications that are characteristic of some Venus oppositions do not apply to Venus contraparallels.

### Minor Venus Aspects

Minor Venus aspects channel artistic, social and romantic interests through the energies of the planets in aspect with Venus according to the signs and houses involved. The affairs influenced by a minor Venus aspect create enjoyment or displeasure, depending upon the nature of the aspect. Minor Venus aspects bear weak influence on the marriage relationship and finances. Minor Easy Aspects Formed by Venus: Minor easy aspects formed by Venus describe secondary interests that are enjoyable, and in the case of the semi-sextile, slightly profitable. They reduce discord and create some measure of harmony in the affairs and relationships that are influenced by Venus and the planet in aspect. These aspects also modify negative sexual tendencies shown by Venus afflictions by providing weak but constructive outlets for sexual energies. Minor Hard Aspects Formed by Venus: The minor hard aspects that Venus forms detract from artistic taste, social expression and marital harmony. These aspects are more annoying than actually troublesome. They point out irritating social mannerisms or lack of refinement and suggest little extravagances or miserly tendencies depending upon the natures of the planets and signs involved. These are limited to areas influenced by the aspects.

### Mars Aspects

Mars aspects give direction to physical energy and sex drive. They influence stamina, aggressiveness, temper, initiative and enthusiasm. Favorable aspects to Mars produce constructive action in the houses ruled and occupied by Mars and the aspecting planets. These aspects enable one to properly utilize strength so that energy is conserved and vigor is maintained. Difficult Mars aspects produce dissipation of energy and destructive action; they bring out the rash, inconsiderate, angry, harsh and sometimes cruel qualities of Mars. As natural ruler of the first house and Aries, Mars influences the personality; as co-ruler (with Pluto) of the eighth house and Scorpio, it also has some bearing on joint financial interests, marital resources, partnership finances, tax concerns and other eighth house affairs.
