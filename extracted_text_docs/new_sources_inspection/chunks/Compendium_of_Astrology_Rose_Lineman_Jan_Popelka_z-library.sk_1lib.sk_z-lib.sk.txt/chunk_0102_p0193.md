### Mars Trines

Mars trines permit natural expression and uninterrupted flow of Martian energy; they point out potentially fruitful areas of development. Constructive action is directed into the affairs governed by Mars and a planet in trine. A Mars trine influences productivity according to the signs and houses ruled and occupied by Mars and the trined planet. Because Mars trines give positive direction to aggressive sexual drives, these aspects counteract negative sexual connotations found elsewhere in the horoscope. Mars Trine Jupiter: This trine produces abundant energy, ambition and enthusiasm and gives positive direction to actions. It combines motivation, initiative and action with integrity, wisdom and good fortune, thus promoting success in the affairs ruled by Mars and Jupiter. Unless strong contradictory factors are present in the horoscope, these people are highprincipled. honest and forthright. This aspect indicates physical strength and muscularity; it also increases sexual vigor and gives positive expression to sexual drives. The protective qualities of Jupiter act as safeguards against bodily harm. Mars Trine Saturn: This aspect is extremely helpful, for it stabilizes Martian energy and gives structure to the activities influenced by the trine. It offsets careless and reckless tendencies if such inclinations appear in the horoscope and fosters action that is orderly, efficient, practical and conservative. These people demonstrate courage, determination, responsibility and drive; they willingly undertake hard work. They cope ably with emergencies; few fall apart in stressful situations. Sex drive is controlled; sexual expression is somewhat restrained but not prudish. Mars Trine Uranus: This aspect indicates decisive, timely actions. The affairs governed by Mars and Uranus succeed as a result of resourceful, innovative treatment. These people do not hesitate to try advanced or unconventional methods to improve or hasten the operation of activities influenced by the trine. They are inventive, daring, enterprising and venturesome. Active, animated and lively, they seem to have endless supplies of energy. Most enjoy the thrills associated with racing fast cars, stunt flying and other exciting or dangerous sports. They are easily aroused sexually; sexual expression is spontaneous, uninhibited. Mars Trine Neptune: This trine strengthens spiritual resources and generates constructive psychic energy. Intuitive and imaginative, these people are inspired by the dreams they visualize. Their inspirations stimulate action to give their dreams form and expression. These people handle the affairs ruled by Mars and Neptune with sensitivity and as much delicacy as is possible within the context of sign expression. They value privacy and tend to shield those activities affected by the trine from outside scrutiny. A fascinating quality that is sensed rather than observed and that produces sexual allure is associated with this aspect. Sex drive is sometimes diverted into spiritual channels. Mars Trine Pluto: This aspect produces physical energy, strength, boldness, courage and constructive activity. These people do not know the meaning of defeat. They neither back away from danger nor bend to threats. Forceful and dynamic, they are active and aggressive in the affairs influenced by the trine. These interests may hold the key to Plutonian evolvement if conflicting factors are not present. This aspect also generates a charismatic quality that attracts power and sexual interest.

### Mars Trine North Node Sextile South Node: This

combination of aspects encourages action that is acceptable to and merits the approval of society. Energy is constructively utilized. The desire and capacity to overcome South Node limitations is present; therefore, inhibiting factors represented by the South Node are largely ineffective. Mars Trine Part of Fortune: This trine denotes success and affirmative action in the affairs influenced by Mars and the Part of Fortune. Physical benefits and worthwhile legacies are part of the promise of this aspect. Mars Trine Ascendant Sextile Descendant: This aspect combination increases assertiveness and strength. These people come across as forceful, direct and decisive within the context of sign expression. Active in personal affairs and in society, refusing to be bystanders, they form part of the mainstream of life. Most benefit from opportunities that stem from marriage or partnerships.
