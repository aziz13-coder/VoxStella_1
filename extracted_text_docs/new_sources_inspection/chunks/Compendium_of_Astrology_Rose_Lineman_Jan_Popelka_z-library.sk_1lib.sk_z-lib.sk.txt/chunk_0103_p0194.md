### Mars Oppositions

Mars oppositions generate competitiveness, aggressiveness and anger. They are associated with verbal and/or physical confrontations as indicated by the nature of the opposing planets, the signs involved, and other factors that bear upon the oppositions. A Mars opposition gives rise to destructive belligerence, hostility and strife if neither Mars nor the opposing planet forms easy or stabilizing aspects with other planets to restrain the violence that is associated with Mars oppositions. The individual is quick to rise to the challenges evoked by a Mars opposition, but action is impulsive, rash, and often foolhardy, until this person learns to channel Mars energy productively. Without constructive direction, energy is misspent in fruitless activity, pointless arguments or senseless physical combat. The affairs influenced by the signs and houses ruled and occupied by Mars and a planet in opposition produce conflict and antagonism. Mars oppositions indicate willfulness and foolish daring; they are associated with risky acts that cause accidents and endanger physical safety. Mars Opposite Jupiter: This aspect dissipates energy through excessive physical activity. These people cannot pace themselves. They tend to take on more than they can possibly accomplish and push themselves to the point of physical exhaustion. Extravagant and reckless, they misjudge the effectiveness of their actions in the affairs governed by Mars and Jupiter. This aspect creates foolhardy daring, many of these people risk physical safety to prove fearlessness. It encourages gambling tendencies, and these people may speculate with others' funds as well as their own. In sexual matters they are usually the aggressors. Mars Opposite Saturn: This opposition pits the impatience, rashness and haste of Mars against the patience, caution and deliberateness characterized by Saturn. The individual is torn between the impulse to act and fear of consequences if the action is carried out. Resultant frustrations breed resentment and hostility. This aspect fosters bravado, but courage and defiance break down when the individual is backed into a comer. At times, aggressive and decisive action is taken in the affairs governed by Mars and Saturn; at other times, inhibitions and fears retard progress. Sexual drives alternate between intense passion and frigidity. If other factors in the horoscope confirm, this aspect can indicate impotence. Mars Opposite Uranus: This aspect prompts precipitous actions; it engenders impetuousness, recklessness, insolence, rebelliousness and radicalism. In the activities influenced by the opposition these people demand complete freedom of action. They institute drastic changes without considering the full implications of their actions. External circumstances that affect the affairs ruled by Mars and Uranus cause instability and turbulence. This aspect is associated with violence, accidents, riots, death-defying exploits and military dangers. Sexual expression is erratic and unconventional. Some of these people engage in promiscuous sexual relations or bizarre forms of sexual expression if confirming factors are present in the horoscope. Mars Opposite Neptune: This aspect weakens physical energy and reduces muscularity. Unless the Sun is strong by sign and aspect, vitality and recuperative powers are undermined. The affairs influenced by this opposition denote areas of ambiguity, ineptitude, delusion and gullibility. This aspect can foster fraud and scandal in the affected areas and these people may engage in clandestine activities in connection with the affairs ruled by Mars and Neptune. Conflict between physical urges and spiritual drives is created by this opposition. It is associated with sexual beguilement, seduction, sexual intrigue and drug-related sex cults. Mars Opposite Pluto: This opposition fosters violence, brutality, sexual abuse and criminal tendencies. Favorable aspects to either Mars or Pluto help counteract negative tendencies generated by the opposition and provide constructive outlets for the powerful physical drives. This aspect produces enormous physical strength. These people seem to attract antagonism and conflict in the affairs governed by Mars and Pluto and tend to rely on physical force as the means of dealing with opposition. Some of these people resort to psychic control to obtain power in

### Mars Opposite North Node Conjunct South Node

These aspects motivate willful actions that defy socially accepted standards. These people deliberately flaunt the rules of society; in so doing they stir resistance and animosity that hinders development in the areas influenced by Mars and the Nodes. Mars Opposite Part of Fortune: This opposition describes challenges with which the individual must deal if the success promised by the Part of Fortune is to be achieved. It also confirms other indications of energy dissipation and accidents.

### Mars Opposite Ascendant Conjunct Descendant: This

combination of aspects focuses initiative and activity in marital relations and partnerships. These people are assertive and attract aggressive, dynamic partners unless sign indications and other factors show otherwise. The harmony of relationships depends upon the aspects Mars forms with other planets. Mars Opposite MC Conjunct /C. This aspect combination implies aggressiveness in home and family matters. If Mars is afflicted, violent domestic quarrels and a strife-ridden family life are indicated. These people want to run the household. Family responsibilities or parental objections often interfere with career interests.
