### Mars Parallels

Initiative and aggressiveness are activated and energy is directed into the affairs ruled by the signs and houses governed and occupied by Mars and the planet(s) that form parallels with Mars. A parallel that occurs between Mars and a planet with which Mars also forms a zodiacal aspect is given the same meaning as that aspect and, therefore, supplements and strengthens it. A parallel that is formed by Mars and a planet that does not aspect Mars zodiacally is interpreted as a mild conjunction with certain modifications. A parallel that occurs between Mars and Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune or Pluto is interpreted as an afflicted conjunction if the two planets in aspect occupy different signs of the same quadruplicity or if either planet is heavily afflicted. However, these parallels do not carry the negative sexual implications associated with corresponding conjunctions. An unaccompanied parallel, which Mars forms with the North Node, Part of Fortune, Ascendant or Midheaven, takes on the positive attributes of the corresponding conjunction regardless of the signs or other aspects involved.

### Mars Contraparallels

A Mars contraparallel generates conflict in the areas influenced by the aspect. Discord is evident in the affairs described by the signs and houses occupied and ruled by Mars and a planet in contraparallel. Mars contraparallels indicate areas in which action is impulsive, headstrong and rash. A contraparallel that occurs between Mars and the Ascendant or MC acts as an afflicted conjunction. All other Mars contraparallels are interpreted as corresponding oppositions, omitting negative sexual tendencies if such are shown by the opposition. Mars contraparallels do not distort sexual drives.

### Minor Mars Aspects

The minor aspects that Mars forms exert mild influence in accordance with the significance of the aspects and the natures of the aspecting planets. This influence manifests in the affairs ruled by Mars and the planets in aspect. Minor Easy Aspects Formed by Mars: Minor easy aspects formed by Mars indicate constructive channels for Mars action. They point out secondary avenues for release of frustrations induced by adverse aspects to Mars. Minor Hard Aspects Formed by Mars: Minor hard aspects formed by Mars indicate impatience, aggravation and anger. Mild agitation is evident in the affairs governed by Mars and the aspected planets. These aspects do not encourage violence, sexual aberrations or other strongly negative Martian tendencies.

### Jupiter Aspects

Jupiter aspects describe religious, ethical, moral and philosophical values. They influence wisdom, judgment, abstract thought and higher education. Favorable Jupiter aspects give direction to the expansive and protective properties of this planet; adverse aspects detract from the beneficial qualities of Jupiter and often encourage excesses. The aspects Jupiter forms with Saturn, Uranus, Neptune and Pluto are interpreted more broadly than aspects that occur between Jupiter and the personal planets. Aspects between Jupiter and heavy planets remain in orb for much longer periods of time than aspects formed with the faster-moving planets. Heavy Jupiter aspects can be in effect for several months when motion is slow of course or retrograde; therefore, the same aspect can appear in the horoscopes of many people in the same age group. House placement and other aspects that influence a common Jupiter aspect will differ in horoscopes that contain the same heavy aspect. The houses occupied and ruled by Jupiter and a heavy planet in aspect and the other aspects these two planets form show how the heavy Jupiter aspect affects the individual.
