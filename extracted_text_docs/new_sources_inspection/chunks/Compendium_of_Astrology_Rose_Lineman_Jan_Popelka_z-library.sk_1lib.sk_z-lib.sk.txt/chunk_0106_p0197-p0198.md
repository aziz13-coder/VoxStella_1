### Jupiter Sextiles

Jupiter sextiles describe opportunities that manifest in the houses occupied and ruled by Jupiter and planets in sextile. They imply success and prosperity in related affairs and foster sound judgment, integrity and ethical action in the areas of horoscope influence. Jupiter Sextile Saturn: This sextile promotes professional and business success; it produces prosperity in the affairs governed by Jupiter and Satum and indicates integrity, high principles and practical interest in advanced education. Most of these people respect the law; many engage in professions related to the courts of jurisprudence. Unless contrary factors indicate otherwise, these people gravitate toward traditional religions and conservative philosophies. ### Jupiter Sextile North Node Trine South Node: This

combination of aspects indicates that associated opportunities can be utilized successfully because the individual is in agreement with and observes prevailing legal, moral and religious customs. lupiter Sextile Part of Fortune: This sextile promises opportunity, success and material rewards associated with the signs and houses involved. Spiritual benefits are also indicated if sign and house placement agree or if other factors in the horoscope support positive spiritual inclinations.

### Jupiter Squares

Jupiter squares obstruct Jupiterian good fortune and thwart the protective influence of Jupiter. They create obstacles to higher eduation and can indicate legal difficulties in the areas of horoscope activation. They also interfere with sound judgment. Depending upon the natures of the afflicting planets, Jupiter squares can imply dishonesty, questionable ethics or faulty religious concepts. lupiter Square Saturn: This aspect does not deny success, but it inflicts heavy responsibilities and demands hard work if success is to be achieved in the activities influenced by Jupiter and Saturn. Some of these people are discouraged by the setbacks and disappointments generated by this square and, therefore, fail to succeed; others overcome the obstacles they encounter and forge ahead despite difficulties. The individual's reaction to this aspect depends upon personal characteristics and other factors in the horoscope that strengthen or weaken the capacity to deal with hardships and problems in a positive manner. In this aspect Saturn represses the natural optimism attributed to Jupiter and restricts Jupiterian expansiveness. Morally upright and lawabiding, most of these people embrace very narrow religious views and strict ethical standards. lupiter Square Uranus: This square prompts hasty, illconsidered decisions in connection with the affairs governed by Jupiter and Uranus; as a result, heavy financial losses can be incurred. Speculation is risky for anyone in whose horoscope this aspect appears; yet, this square encourages gambling tendencies. Enticed by the lure of adventure in strange lands, these people may encounter dangerous situations during their travels if Jupiter is additionally afflicted. They promote unsound humanitarian causes that may be worthy in principle but not in fact. Many of them are attracted by exotic religions, and some engage in bizarre religious practices. The difficulties indicated by this square are almost entirely rooted in impulsive judgment and distorted logic. lupiter Square Neptune: This aspect clouds judgment
