### Uranus Conjunct Ascendant Opposite Descendant

These aspects have an animating effect. Much personal magnetism is possessed by these people. They are independent, rebellious and somewhat dictatorial. They do not willingly take orders or obey the rules set forth by society. They advocate total freedom for themselves. If Uranus or the Ascendant is badly afflicted, these people are extremely unpredictable and eccentric; some are irrational in their insistence on personal liberty. They can be virtual despots. Sweeping changes frequently shatter personal interests. Uranus Conjunct MC Opposite IC: This combination of aspects indicates extremes regarding worldly fortunes. Overnight success is possible as are sudden downfalls, especially if Uranus or the MC is afflicted. Many of these people go into unique or unusual professions; those that do not add something original to the professions they enter. These people are known for their ingenuity, originality, unpredictability and humanitarianism.

### Uranus Sextiles

Uranus sextiles present unusual opportunities, which arise unexpectedly. The individual who is unprepared cannot fully utilize these opportunities, for they appear suddenly and are withdrawn abruptly. The individual who prepares in expectation of the chance to get ahead has a distinct advantage when Uranian opportunities manifest; this person is able to act quickly before desirable opportunities are snatched away. Uranus Sextile Neptune (1966-68): This aspect appears in the horoscopes of multitudes of people since it stays in orb for more than three years each time it occurs. It signals an opportune period for psychic, spiritual, scientific and social development. Many of these people are bom during times when humanitarian causes and social reform are key issues. As growing youngsters they are conscious of social needs and ideals; they carry these attitudes into adult life. Only if Uranus or Neptune bears personal significance in the horoscope, however, are these attitudes prime motivating factors in the life of the individual. If one of these planets is prominent in the horoscope, the individual centers efforts directed toward social progress around activities connected with the houses ruled and occupied by Uranus and Neptune and affairs governed by other planets that aspect these two. Uranus Sextile Pluto (1943/44; 1995-97): This sextile awakens interest in metaphysics and science. It is closely associated with atomic, nuclear and space physics. It affects generations of people on a broad level and few people on a personal level. If this aspect is of particular significance in a natal horoscope, it indicates that opportunities for spiritual and intellectual development arise in connection with the affairs the two planets govern.

### Uranus Sextile North Node Trine South Node: These

aspects provide opportunities that enable the individual to influence society's attitudes. This person is aware of the need for change and may affect progressive reforms. These aspects also make it easy for the individual to shed restrictive habits described by the South Node. Uranus Sextile Part of Fortune: This sextile permits free access to opportunities associated with the Part of Fortune. Progressive ideas and innovative methods play large parts in developing the affairs influenced by this aspect.

### Uranus Sextile Ascendant Trine Descendant: This

combination of aspects fosters uninhibited personal expression. Magnetic and animated, these people act without pretense. They choose unusual marriage partners. For most, marriage is exciting and in many ways a unique experience. Uranus Sextile MC Trine IC: This aspect combination is indicative of unique career opportunities, which occur when least expected. These people advance rapidly in their professions. They have unusual homes that are always open to their friends. Family life is never dull.

### Uranus Squares

Uranus squares provoke rebellion, sudden disasters and unpredictable events. Development of affairs governed by Uranus and planets in square is blocked by external misfortunes, abrupt changes and eccentric treatment. Uranus Square Neptune (1954/55): This square is associated with major disasters such as those caused by earthquakes, tornados, severe lightning storms, flash floods, airplane crashes and ship sinkings. Nations may be misled by false social doctrines and unrealistic Utopian causes while under the influence of this aspect. On a personal level, this aspect creates peculiar conditions, chaos and panic in affairs ruled by Uranus and Neptune. Development of related interests is often interruped by tragic circumstances over which the individual has no control. These interests may fail to progress properly because the individual is unable to cope with the confusion and instability that the square generates. Uranus Square Pluto (1932-34): This aspect indicates extremism, fanaticism and terrorism on a world-wide scale. It is connected with massive destruction, particularly that brought about by volcanic eruption, major earthquakes, widespread lawlessness, or the misuse of atomic or nuclear power. This aspect is thought to foster political upheaval, anarchy and revolt. If it has personal significance in the horoscope, tumultuous and often violent conditions tend to disrupt the affairs governed by Uranus and Pluto. Lifeshattering experiences may occur as a result of natural disasters, political insurrections, military atrocities or global wars. The individual may be swept into radical causes or become involved in terrorist activities. Sexual abuse is possible if other factors in the horoscope agree.

### Uranus Square North Node Square South Node: This

combination of aspects indicates disrespect for traditions of society. Many of these people deliberately scom accepted social customs in order to assert individualism and independence. In so doing, they attract the radical elements of society and sudden reversals of fortune. Uranus Square Part of Fortune: This square describes sporadic action and unfortunate circumstances that interrupt progress. Lack of sustained endeavor and unstable conditions thwart the development of affairs influenced by Uranus and the Part of Fortune.

### Uranus Square Ascendant Square Descendant: This

aspect combination generates disobedience, rebelliousness, unpredictable behavior, radical attitudes and personal eccentricities. Marital separations, divorce and serious rifts with partners are attributed to these aspects. Most of these people are uncooperative and demanding. Uranus Square MC Square IC: These aspects create career disruptions and family instability. Inconsistency is evident in professional and domestic affairs. Frequent job changes are probable. Some of these people lose positions because they refuse to conform to company rules and procedures. Many quit jobs and uproot their families because of the compelling need for change and freedom.
