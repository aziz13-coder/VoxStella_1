### Other Pluto Aspects

Pluto Parallels: Parallels that Pluto forms with the North Node, Part of Fortune, Ascendant and MC are treated as corresponding conjunctions. The strength of these parallels depends upon Pluto's strength in the horoscope. Pluto parallels do not carry as much weight as conjunctions, but if Pluto is influential in the natal horoscope these aspects exert moderate influence in the houses involved according to sign expression. Pluto Contraparallels: A contraparallel that occurs between Pluto and the Ascendant or MC is interpreted as an afflicted conjunction. A contraparallel formed between Pluto and the North Node or Part of Fortune corresponds to a weak opposition. Minor Pluto Aspects: Minor aspects that occur between Pluto and the Nodes, Part of Fortune and the angles are of no significance in the natal horoscope. These aspects are not usually calculated or listed in the aspectarian.

Aspects of the Moon's Nodes and Part of Fortune Aspects formed between the Moon's Nodes and Part of Fortune, and aspects that the Nodes and Part of Fortune form with the Ascendant and MC are unimportant unless a planet aspects the Nodes or Part of Fortune. Planets energize the horoscope; the Nodes and Part of Fortune do not. Conjunctions and oppositions that occur between these points are more significant than other mutual aspects. An opposition is automatically formed by the opposite Node or angle if a Node or angle forms a mutual conjunction or conjuncts the Part of Fortune. If a planet aspects the conjunction, energy is focused in the house in which the conjunction occurs. Other major aspects that occur between the Nodes, Part of Fortune and angles link the interests of the houses involved. Interpretation depends upon the natures of planets that aspect these points and the type of planetary aspects formed. Minor aspects are meaningless. Mutual aspects between the Ascendant and MC are not interpreted as such. The orientation between the angles as it varies from the norm (square) distorts the size of the houses and quadrants, placing emphasis on the larger portions.
