Astrological Guidance in Planning for the Future Various techniques are available by which one can obtain greater insight into the potentials indicated in the natal chart and developments that take place during a lifetime. They help the individual achieve greater self-understanding, improve relationships and know the time of life when certain courses of action are most apt to be beneficial. More importantly, through these studies one becomes increasingly aware that the direction a natal potential takes is in his or her hands. When personal decisions and actions are based on the proper utilization of astrological influences, a person gains greater control over the cause and effect of life. Progressions and Transits A progressed horoscope updates a natal horoscope: it reveals developmental patterns and emerging trends as they unfold according to the promise of the natal chart. A natal horoscope never loses its initial character, but it is no more static than life itself, for it describes an ongoing growth process. For example, when a person's natal Sun progresses into the next sign, that person does not lose original Sun-sign characteristics; he or she gains added perspective through new Sun-sign qualities. One can most successfully fulfill certain potentials and realize particular goals when the flow ol creative energies shown by the progressed horoscope harmonizes with and centers on associated areas. For instance, when planets progress to the tenth house or aspect the MC or its planetary ruler, career development is emphasized. The most common method of progressing a horoscope, the "day for a year" method, relates each successive day after birth to one year of life. Beginning with the GMT birthdate and counting it zero, the number of days that corresponds to one's age in years is counted forward in the ephemeris to obtain the progressed ephemeris date (FED). The progressed horoscope is calculated in the same manner as the natal chart (Chapters 12 and 13) using the time and location of birth but substituting astrological data (sidereal time, planetary positions, etc.) listed in the ephemeris on the FED for that given on the birthdate.
