### Converse Directions

Converse directions progress a horoscope "backwards." Procedure to erect a converse chart is the same as the "day for a year" method of progressing a horoscope except that one counts backward in time from the birthdate the number of years of interest to find the converse ephemeris date (CED) for which the converse horoscope is cast. A converse horoscope gives insight into innate behavior patterns and karma. To more fully understand the potentials that appear in a horoscope progressed to a given year after birth, one studies a converse chart erected for the same number of years before birth. Astrological factors that link the natal, progressed and converse charts give clues to karmic problems as described by natal and converse ties and point out the means by which karmic relationships can be best worked out as shown by corresponding progressed indications. Converse charts reveal hidden aspects of the personality such as latent talent and deep-seated psychological motivations. They are helpful in uncovering the root causes of emotional or psychological conflicts and in resolving problems. Solar and Lunar Returns Solar and lunar returns provide information that elaborates on that found in the natal and progressed horoscopes. A solar return, erected according to the date and time in a given year that the Sun returns to the zodiacal position it occupies in the natal horoscope and the location in which the individual resides on that date, indicates by the Sun's house position the main emphasis of interest for that year. Influences operative during that year based on the solar return as a whole (planets, signs, houses and aspects) corroborate information found in the progressed horoscope and give additional details concerning progressed general trends. For example, a progressed Uranus aspect is in force for several years; a solar return may contain supplementary information that clarifies the progressed Uranian influence and further defines potential Uranian events. A lunar return, based on the date and time each month that the Moon returns to its natal zodiacal position and the individual's place of residence on that date, gives a view of developments that occur from the time of the chart to the time of the Moon's next return the following month. The Moon's position and aspects describe activities and interests that influence the individual on a personal level and often affect the emotions. By providing a monthly outlook a lunar return pinpoints more closely events and situations described in the solar return and progressed horoscope.

### Synastry

No one person reacts to any two other people in the same way. Lack of understanding can undermine relationships that might otherwise be productive. Through synastry techniques, valuable insights into the marital or love relationships, the parent/child relationship, friendships, business affiliations and other associations can be gained, insights that enable people to recognize and understand each other's needs and to identify those factors upon which the success of a relationship depends. Comparison and composite charts are the two most useful tools available to the astrologer for determining strengths and weaknesses inherent in relationships.

### Comparison Charts

The basis for a relationship, potential problem areas and strong points can be determined by comparing the natal potentials of the persons involved, noting similarities and differences in the charts and aspects between the planets and angles of one chart and those of the other. Findings are assessed on the basis of compatibility and mutual ties (aspects). Differences are not necessarily incompatible; they may be complementary. For instance, what one person lacks the other may contribute to balance relationship needs. To analyze a relationship involving two people, two comparison charts, often referred to as compatibility charts, are erected. To do this, the planets and angles from one partner's horoscope are inserted into the natal chart of the other and vice versa. A balance of elements and modes in the two charts is desirable although not essential to sustaining the relationship. Imbalance adds a narrow perspective. The house that a partner's planets occupy in one's natal chart describe the role the partner plays. The main concept in role playing is indicated by the Sun, for the position of a partner's Sun in one's natal chart shows how the individual relates to the partner's personality. For example, if a woman's Sun occupies the fourth house in a man's natal chart, he sees her as someone to look after his needs much as his mother did when he was a child; if a man's Sun appears in a woman's fourth house, she relates to him as a fatherfigure. The position of the Moon describes the emotional response, Mercury the intellectual, Venus the affectional and Mars the sexual. Jupiter indicates areas in which one partner benefits the other. Saturn points out areas of restriction, indicating one's responsibility to the partner within the framework of the relationship. Aspects (use one-half natal orb) indicate specific potentials and qualities of the relationship. Afflictions indicate potential problems; easy aspects describe helpful bonds based on common interests or characteristics according to the natures of the planets forming the aspects. A relationship may be fruitful for a limited time period only. A conjunction to one of the angles in a natal horoscope by a partner's natal planet yields clues to the durability of the association; progressed indications describe its future and probable duration. If several persons are involved, which is often the case in business associations, each natal chart is compared with every other. Utilization of a composite chart, which incorporates all relationship potentials into a single horoscope, reduces to one the number of charts needed to assess a relationship.
