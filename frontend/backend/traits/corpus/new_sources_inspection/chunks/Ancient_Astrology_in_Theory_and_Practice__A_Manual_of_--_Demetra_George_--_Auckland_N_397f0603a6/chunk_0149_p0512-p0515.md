### EVALUATION OF THE MASTER AND JOINT-MASTER OF THE NATIVITY

Porphyry does not require that the Oikodespotes witness the Predominator, as do many other astrologers. However, he is concerned that this Master and Joint-Master witness each other to ensure their cooperation and that they wit ness the Ascendant or the Moon (regardless of the sect of the chart). Because both the Moon and the Ascendant signify the physical body, the planet that des ignates the number of years of life should ideally be connected to the physical body itself.
