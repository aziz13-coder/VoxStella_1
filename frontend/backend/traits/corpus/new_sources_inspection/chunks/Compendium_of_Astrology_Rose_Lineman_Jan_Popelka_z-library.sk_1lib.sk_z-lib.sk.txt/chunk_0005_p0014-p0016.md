In some instances signs within a classification exhibit conflicting qualities, but each category emphasizes common factors inherent in the signs listed therein. Triplicities (Elements) One major classification is called triplicities or elements. There are four triplicities: fire, earth, air and water. Each triplicity contains three signs; each sign is separated from the other two belonging to the same triplicity by 120° zodiacal longitude (see Figure 3). Signs that belong to the same triplicity are compatible. That is, the mode of expression of any one sign in a given triplicity harmonizes with the mode of expression of the other two members even though sign characteristics differ in some respects. Characteristics common to each triplicity are summarized below. An individual whose horoscope contains a predominating number of planets in a particular triplicity exhibits traits associated with that triplicity. For example, the fiery individual probably has many planets in fire signs; heavily populated earth signs are indicative of the "solid citizen"; an abundance of air signs signifies the intellectual; water signs, the sensitive.

### Characteristics

Action, drive initiative, motivation. Determination, persistence, stability, self-reliance. Adaptability, changeability, flexibility, versatility. The predominating quadruplicity in a horoscope describes in part that person's attitudes and approach to circumstances. Cardinal people act promptly; fixed people resist change; mutable people adapt easily to changing conditions. Positive and Negative Signs All fire and air signs are classified as positive (masculine); earth and water signs are classed as negative (feminine). Positive signs indicate dynamic, outgoing expression; negative signs denote receptivity and passive expression. Articulate and Human Signs Gemini, Virgo, Libra, Sagittarius and Aquarius are called articulate signs. These signs are also classed as human by some authorities. Others who do not combine the two classifications omit Libra and the last 15s of Sagittarius from the human category but include them in the articulate group. Articulate and human signs are associated with human qualities such as speech and sparse body hair. With the exception of Libra and Sagittarius, these signs are symbolically represented by human figures. The scales is the pictorial symbol for libra; the centaur-archer is the symbol for Sagittarius. Inarticulate Signs (Quadrupedian, Bestial) The inarticulate signs are Aries, Taurus, Leo and Capricorn. These signs are also called quadrupedian and bestial signs. They are symbolized by four-footed animals that have voices but lack power of speech. Capricorn is pictured either as the goat-fish (twofooted) or a mountain goat. The last 15° of Sagittarius is classed as quadrupedian or bestial by authorities who do not classify it as human, but it is not included in the inarticulate category.

### Mute Signs

The water signs. Cancer, Scorpio and Pisces, are termed mute signs. They are characterized by the crab, the scorpion and the fish respectively, all voiceless creatures. These signs are often associated with an inability to communicate freely.

### Fruitful Signs

This classification describes another attribute of the water signs. Cancer, Scorpio and Pisces. These signs are associated with fertility.

### Barren Signs

Gemini, Leo and Virgo are considered barren signs and are associated with infertility.

### Double Signs

The double signs, Gemini, Sagittarius and Pisces, also called double-bodied or bicorporeal signs, are characterized by dual qualities. Double signs imply dualism in the area of activity described by their position in a horoscope.

### Equinoctial Signs

The Sun's ingress (entry) into the equinoctial signs, Aries and Libra, ushers in two seasons of the year. In the northern hemisphere, spring commences at the time of the vernal equinox when the Sun enters 0° Aries. The Sun's entrance into 0° Libra coincides with the autumnal equinox and the beginning of autumn. These signs are associated with beginnings and announcements. Solstice Signs (Tropical Signs) Cancer and Capricorn are solstice signs. The Sun's entrance into 0° Cancer marks the summer solstice and the official start of summer in the northern hemisphere. As the Sun enters 0° Capricorn, the winter solstice begins the winter season. These signs are also called tropical signs because they occupy the parts of the ecliptic where the Sun reaches its farthest point north (the Tropic of Cancer) and south (Tropic of Capricorn)
