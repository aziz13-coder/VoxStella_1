### Ingress Signs

The equinoctial and solstice signs, Aries, Cancer, Libra and Capricorn, all cardinal signs, are classed together as ingress signs. As a group, these signs represent beginnings.

### Spring Signs

Aries, Taurus and Gemini, the spring signs, are the signs that the Sun traverses during the spring each year in the northern hemisphere. These signs are associated with the planting of seed and the budding stage of new growth.

### Summer Signs

Cancer, Leo and Virgo are the summer signs in the northern hemisphere. The Sun transits these signs during the summer months. These signs are indicative of growth and development.

### Autumn Signs

Libra, Scorpio and Sagittarius are the autumn signs in the northern hemisphere. The Sun crosses these signs in autumn. The maturing phase of growth and the harvest are associated with these signs.

### Winter Signs

Capricorn, Aquarius and Pisces, the signs through which the Sun travels during winter in the northern hemisphere, are the winter signs. They are associated with the dormant period of growth. Note that the seasons and the growth cycle are reversed in the southern hemisphere. Signs of Long Ascension Cancer, Leo, Virgo, Libra, Scorpio and Sagittarius, the summer and autumn signs, are signs of long ascension in the northern hemisphere. Since there are twelve signs and twenty-four hours in a day, it would seem logical that a different sign would rise over the eastern horizon every two hours. This is true only at the equator. These slow-rising signs take more than two hours to pass over the eastern horizon and represent the longer phase of an uneven cycle. Signs of Short Ascension Aries, Taurus, Gemini, Capricorn, Aquarius and Pisces, the spring and winter signs, are signs of short ascension in the northern hemisphere. These fast-rising signs take less than two hours to rise over the eastern horizon and represent the shorter phase of an uneven cycle. Signs of long and short ascension are reversed in the southern hemisphere. Northern Signs (Nocturnal Signs) Aries, Taurus, Gemini, Cancer, Leo and Virgo are called northern signs because they occupy the northern half (lower section) of a chart with Aries on the cusp of the first house. Southern Signs (Day Signs) The southern signs, also called day signs, are Libra, Scorpio, Sagittarius, Capricorn, Aquarius and Pisces. In a natural chart these signs are placed in the southern (upper) half, which represents that part of the sky above the horizon. Southern signs are associated with objective needs and external interests. Although each group of signs is based on common properties of its members, the diversity of signs is apparent from the number of different categories to which each sign belongs. Signs of the same group do not express like qualities in an identical manner. For example, cardinal signs Aries and Cancer both express initiative, a cardinal quality. However, Arian initiative is spontaneous (fire) and dynamic (positive). Cancerian initiative reflects sensitivity (water) and receptivity (negative). Characteristics common to the triplicity, quadruplicity and positive or negative classifications of a sign are among more prominent sign qualities. These categories are noted in the sections of this chapter that describe individual sign attributes.
