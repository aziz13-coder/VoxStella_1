### Musical Tone: A

The duality of Gemini is reflected in its historical symbol, the twins, and in its glyph. The glyph pictures two Crosses of Matter, which are symbolic of the pairs found in nature. Gemini rules the hands, arms, and lungs and has general rulership over the nervous system, which is composed of two major parts, the central and the peripheral. The natural sign of the third house, it is ruled by the planet Mercury. The mutable-air-positive sign, Gemini, typifies diversified action, intellectual drive, diffuse effort, dexterity, fluency and attitudes that are both flexible and skeptical. A Gemini Sun (May 21-June 21) gives a person a lifelong thirst for knowledge without the persistence to concentrate on one particular field or topic for a long period of time. Geminis leam easily, remember what they leam and are ever curious to know more. They accept little at face value or on the basis of popular opinion. Geminis must find out and prove for themselves, for only in fact and logic is their skepticism satisfied. Geminis' interests are broad, but most need to develop continuity of thought and action. Usually they have too many overlapping interests or conflicting objectives, which siphon energy away from main concerns. However, Geminis are able to handle two or more separate concepts at the same time. They can mull over several ideas and work on various endeavors concurrently. They may splinter their energies and talents, but Geminis have the capacity to unite the "splinters" into a cohesive effort toward an overall goal, and this they must do if they are to reach their full potentials. Restless in body and mind, most Geminis have nervous dispositions. If routine becomes monotonous, a Gemini is apt to grow fidgity; pencil twirling, toe tapping, finger drumming or excessive chattering are attempts at alleviating boredom. Geminis are non-conformists who resist cumbersome regulations that constrain their individuality. They do not function at best in a highly disciplined or regimented atmosphere. The Gemini personality gives the impression of being perpetually lighthearted and carefree. These people are good company, witty, charming and entertaining. However, they can be overly clever and adroit: smooth talkers who are a bit too glib. Intellectual and conversational skills are two of Gemini's strongest assets. A variety of interests, the aptitude to learn and retain knowledge, the capacity to reason and express ideas, and the ability to think and act quickly enable Geminis to cope with almost any circumstances that arise. Their success lies in part in a versatile approach to life and the ease with which they are able to adapt to ever-changing situations. If they cannot adjust (and Geminis are wise enough to know when they cannot), they deftly sidestep obstacles that impede progress. For Gemini, there is always another, perhaps more interesting, course ahead. The Gemini temperament is best suited to an occupation that offers intellectual stimulation and permits diversified activity, though not necessarily of a physically strenuous nature. Fencing, jogging, reading, traveling, writing or just visiting and chatting with friends appeal to most Geminis during free hours. Typically, a Gemini has an ever-youthful appearance which is emphasized by refined, clear-cut features, alert, twinkling eyes, a slightly turned up nose, quick movements, an agile step and a slender build.
