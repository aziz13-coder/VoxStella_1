### Musical Tone: F

Leo, whose natural house is the fifth, is ruled by the Sun. The sign is associated with royalty. Fittingly, its historical symbol is the lion, king of the jungle. The curved lines of the glyph smoothly link life force (spirit) with the soul. Leo rules the back, spinal cord and heart, which the glyph resembles. Authoritative action, power-seeking drive, noble effort, love of life, vitality, creativity and the principle of fatherhood are embodied in this fixed-fire-positive sign. Those persons whose natal Sun is placed in Leo (July 23-August 22) are warmhearted, outgoing, proud and courageous. They possess a deep-rooted desire to get to the top. Leos like to be in the mainstream of life. They tend to gravitate toward center stage where they can exercise their natural leadership abilities. If circumstances frustrate their drives, Leos may develop less desirable qualities such as arrogance, conceit and pomposity. Whatever Leos do, they do with dramatic flair. It is almost impossible for them not to attract attention. Leos harbor much pride in themselves; they try to live up to a reputation of honesty, integrity, reliability and generosity. Because self-esteem is important to Leos, they seldom act in a manner that is demeaning or might give them cause for regret. Leos stand firm in their ideas and beliefs. They can be opinionated, stubborn, bossy and egotistical despite the fact that they are basically broad-minded, just and kind. Many tend to get all wrapped up in their own self-importance, so they need to periodically evaluate their perspective. As a rule, Leos are popular. They work hard, and play hard. Warm, gregarious and fun-loving, they are convivial social mixers. Aware of the value of friendship, they form many lasting associations. Leos' flamboyant style and personal magnetism appeal to the opposite sex. In romance Leos are ardent, affectionate, sentimental and extravagant. Love is important to them; they need someone to adore, admire and flatter them, for in love, as in everything else, Leos must play the leading role. If they do not receive the affection and attention they need, their vanity and pride suffer as well as their hearts. Leos have optimistic, cheerful, good-natured dispositions. At times they are hot-headed, but do not harbor resentment or thoughts of revenge. There is too much in life for Leos to do and enjoy for them to waste energy in self-defeating efforts. Although Leo is a barren sign, it rules children. Leo parents are not noted for large families, but most are genuinely fond of youngsters. They demand obedience and respect from their offspring (no one questions the authority of a Leo mother or father): however, Leos are demonstrative parents who express their love in ways that children can understand. The ability to take charge, to exert authority and to execute responsibility combined with a natural aptitude for the spotlight qualify the Leo individual for a leader's role. Proud and ambitious they are ill at ease in subordinate positions. Women of this sign are as discontented in secondary posts as are the men. Whatever course Leos choose, they will probably be found at the helm eventually. Number: 2 Leo
