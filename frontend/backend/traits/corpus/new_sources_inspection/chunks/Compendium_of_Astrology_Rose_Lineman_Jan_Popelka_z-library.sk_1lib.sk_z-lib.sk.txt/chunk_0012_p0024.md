Virgo is symbolized by the virgin (purity of body) holding a hyacinth (purity of mind) in one hand and a stalk of grain in the other. The grain represents the potential for fertility, but Virgo is a so-called barren sign. This sign is more apt to produce a child of the mind than a child of the body, for it is ruled by the mental planet. Mercury. The concept of purity is further exemplified in the glyph, which depicts the female generative organs in chastity. Virgo rules the abdomen and intestines and exercises part rulership over the female reproductive system. A mutable-earth-negative sign, Virgo is the natural sign of the sixth house. Methodical action, dedicated effort, exacting drive, a sensible attitude, a knack for detail and a discriminating mind are attributes of Virgo. Persons whose Sun is in Virgo (August 23-September 22) are predominantly motivated by a sense of duty; they spare no effort to fulfill their obligations. Virgos abhor laziness, procrastination and tardiness in themselves as well as in others. Although career-oriented, Virgos are more interested in doing an efficient job than in taking over as boss. Most do not resent the detailed work that others consider tedious. They are perceptive and logical. To a Virgo, facts should fall into an brderly sequence; if they do not, something is amiss. Unfortunately, Virgos can focus so much on trivia that they "can't see the forest for the trees." Virgo's strongest point is the ability to analyze data and reach reasonable and accurate conclusions though they need to leam to distinguish between the relevant and the extraneous. Because they are basically insecure, Virgos are often indecisive. The drive toward perfection that most of them exhibit is a means of bolstering self-confidence. Meticulous in everything they do, Virgos can be real "nit-pickers" who expect their associates to be as fastidious and efficient as they are. Most Virgos are worriers who fret over anything and everything. Diet and hygiene are important to Virgos. Many are vegetarians; some make a fetish of cleanliness, bathing several times a day. Virgos are modest, gentle, somewhat reticent individuals. They seldom fritter away precious time in pointless socializing and have little regard for purely hedonistic activities. Most are drawn to persons who are intellectually stimulating or who share similar job interests. Thoughtful of friends in practical ways, Virgos are more apt to help needy acquaintances help themselves than to extend outright handouts. Undemonstrative and inhibited, most Virgos appear indifferent to romance. A Virgo's standards in a mate are high, perhaps more idealistic than realistic, and many choose to remain single rather than lower their expectations. Virgos are happiest in jobs that serve a useful purpose. To achieve fulfillment, they must feel that their work is of practical value to society. Herb gardens, weaving, pets, volunteer service, walking and writing provide healthy spare time outlets for Virgos' nervous tension.
