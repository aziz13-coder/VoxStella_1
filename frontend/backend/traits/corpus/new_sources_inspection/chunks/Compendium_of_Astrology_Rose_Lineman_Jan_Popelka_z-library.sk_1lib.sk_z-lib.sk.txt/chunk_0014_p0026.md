### Scorpio

Historically, the symbol for Scorpio is the scorpion, whose venomous sting characterizes the biting sarcasm attributed to this sign. Some authorities also ascribe the eagle and/or the phoenix to Scorpio. The eagle expresses the potential for greatness inherent in this sign; the phoenix represents the resurrectional or regenerative powers associated with Scorpio. The glyph represents female genitalia; it resembles the glyph for Virgo, but the last leg of the Scorpio glyph is open, indicating sexual activity. The Scorpio glyph is sometimes written with an arrowhead to better distinguish it from that of Virgo. Scorpio rules the sex organs and rectum and has part rulership over the human reproductive system. Traditionally Mars was given rulership of this sign and more recently, Pluto. Scorpio is the natural sign of the eighth house. It is associated with regeneration, sexual expression, death, metamorphosis, transformation and life after death. Intense action, passionate drive, resolute effort, endurance, unflinching courage, unswerving purpose and inscrutable attitudes are qualities basic to this fixed-water-negative sign. The innate power in Scorpio can be likened to that of a seething volcano, impossible to contain. A typical Scorpio (October 23-November 21) is strong-willed, masterful, obdurate, stout-hearted. Scorpiosfear nothing. They plowinto anything that suits their purposes, overriding opposition and pushing aside obstacles much as a steam roller clears a path. The capacity to overcome almost insurmountable barriers is largely responsible for the success Scorpios achieve. Their weakness, if any exists, is in the tendency to drive themselves too hard. Scorpios do not admit to fatigue or illness until they reach the point of exhaustion where they can no longer continue on will power alone. The same characteristics that enable Scorpios to accomplish great deeds can lead to nithlessness, violence and cruelty if energy is misdirected. Scorpios are reserved and taciturn yet argumentative and capable of explosive rage. They never pretend to agree with anyone in the interests of peace or harmony and can be brusque and sarcastic. Most are able to get across in a few words more than other people can express in a paragraph or two. Often, only a piercing look is all that is needed for a Scorpio to make a point. Despite the emotional depth and passion in their nature, Scorpios seldom reveal their inner feelings by facial expresion or mannerism except in anger. A stony mask hides their secrets well. Selective in their choice of companions and intensely loyal to the chosen few, Scorpios can be vindictive and vengeful toward anyone who hurts their friends or loved ones. There is no in-between for Scorpios. They approve or disapprove, agree or disagree, like or dislike, love or hate—they are never indifferent. Scorpios are passionate lovers who are inclined to be jealous and possessive. These people possess a natural curiosity and suspicion that is not satisfied with pat answers and superficialities. They probe until they ferret out any concealed facts. Naturally shrewd, Scorpios are seldom fooled or victimized by swindlers and con-artists. Most Scorpios have the physical strength to carry out strenuous tasks as well as the intelligence to handle intricate mental problems. They are industrious and thorough as employees, masterful and domineering as employers. Many Scorpios are sports enthusiasts who prefer demolition derbies, football and hunting over less violent sports. Other sideline activities might include herpetology, secret societies and the occult.
