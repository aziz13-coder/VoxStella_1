### Aquarius

Aquarius, the sign of compassion, is aptly symbolized by the waterbearer generously pouring the waters of humanitarianism. Curiously, the glyph looks very much like a graphic representation of electro-magnetic waves, which come under the dominion of Uranus, one of the planetary rulers of this sign, although the glyph originated long before the phenomenon of electromagnetic radiation was discovered. Uranus shares rulership of this sign with Saturn, the traditional ruler. Aquarius is the natural sign of the eleventh house and governs the ankles, shins and circulatory system. A fixed-air-positive sign, Aquarius signifies progressive action, altruistic drive, community effort, inventive genius, unpredictability and unconventional attitudes. Aquarians (January 20-February 18), never quite satisfied with the status quo, are the vanguards of social reform and community betterment programs. Humanity at large is their concern. Aquarians are far ahead of the times in thought and action, A farsighted approach to the problems that beset humankind lends to the potential to affect great advances in the quality of life. Traditionalists frequently resist Aquarianinspired changes but Aquarians do not give up easily. With determined effort, they often overthrow firmly entrenched conventions. Aquarians are a paradox in human nature. They are stubborn, inconsistent, unselfish, conceited, tolerant, impatient, rebellious, peace-loving. Erratic attitudes and behavior make them difficult to understand. Aquarians value their freedom and privacy, yet they are the "joiners" of the zodiac. The satisfaction they derive from the friendship and companionship provided by group activities is essential to their inner confidence and sense of well-being. Aquarians have a large assortment of friends, male and female, from all walks of life. They choose their friends on the basis of common concerns and intellectual rapport, not status or background. Neither bigoted nor snobbish, Aquarians care little about the trappings of society. Instead they look for the redeeming values in a person and seldom make moral judgments about another person's character or lifestyle. Most Aquarians accept people as they are. Aquarian objectives for reform lie on a broader plane, devoted to furthering the general welfare. Although their interest in people and the human condition is genuine, Aquarians do not get emotionally involved with the majority of their associates or the causes they promote. They view their relationships and activities from an impersonal, intellectual standpoint. Aquarians must maintain their individuality in all relationships, including marriage, if the relationship is to survive. Aquarians are fascinated by that which is different. They tend to choose jobs that allow freedom of thought and originality of action. In their spare time Aquarians like to experiment with novel ideas. Many devise useful gadgets. Some Aquarians like to tinker with motorcycles and sports cars; others create original designs for clothing and jewelry. Other Aquarian pastimes include citizen's band or ham radio, fraternal clubs or sororities and flying. A classic profile distinguished by strangely fascinating, deep-set eyes is typically Aquarian. Although they are usually well-proportioned, incongruous movements and an uncoordinated walk give the impression they are not.
