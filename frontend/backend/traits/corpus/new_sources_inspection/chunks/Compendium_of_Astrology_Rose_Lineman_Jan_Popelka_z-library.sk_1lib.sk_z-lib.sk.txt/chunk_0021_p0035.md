### The Sun

The Sun crosses the zodiac once each year and spends about a month in each sign. The Sun's mean daily motion is 59' 8" (fifty-nine minutes and eight seconds). Its motion is always direct, never retrograde or stationary. The Sun, referred to as a planet in astrological terminology, is also called a luminary or the Greater Light. It is masculine, hot, dry, electric and essentially barren, but it may be moderately fruitful if placed in a fruitful sign. An intensely hot star that emits life-sustaining energy and heat, the Sun is essential to life on Earth. In a horoscope, it represents a person's vital life force, the ego, the individuality, the creative powers, the drive for self-expression. A person's urge to achieve is expressed through the Sun in accordance with the sign it occupies and is directed toward areas defined by the Sun's house placement. This most important planet acts as a channel in the horoscope for all planetary energies to the extent indicated by the aspects between the Sun and the other planets. The Sun embodies the principle of fatherhood; it signifies a person's natural father and a woman's husband in the horoscope. It rules children in general and one's first offspring in particular. The Sun symbolizes authority in the sense of sovereignty and power as opposed to bureaucracy. A well-placed Sun adds leadership ability; it reinforces a person's capacity to take charge of affairs and to retain command of the situations that arise. A person whose Sun falls in a debilitating sign or is out of harmony with other planets finds it difficult to express basic drives and utilize talents. Natural vitality may be weakened. The Sun has dominion from age twenty-four to thirty-four, the years when most people begin their families and try to establish themselves in life. Developmental stages of life are more fully discussed in Chapter 28. The heart, back, spine, spinal cord and the right side of the body are ruled by the Sun. It shares rulership over the general glandular system with the planet Jupiter. The Sun governs heads of state, gambling, gold metal, daylight; Sunday and the number one. It is associated with entertainment, speculation and all that is showy, ornate or ostentatious. The fifth house is the natural house of the Sun. It rules the sign of Leo, which is the natural sign of the fifth house. The Sun is dignified in Leo, exalted in Aries, detrimented in Aquarius and in fall in Libra. The degree of exaltation for the Sun is 19° Aries; the degree of fall is 19° Libra.

### The Moon

The Moon, Earth's satellite and the swiftest moving "planet," completes one circuit of the zodiac in a little less than twenty-eight days. It averages two and one-half days in each sign at a time. The mean daily motion is 13° 10' (thirteen degrees and ten minutes) and is always direct. The Moon may be called a luminary or the Lesser Light. It is feminine, cold, moist, magnetic and fruitful. Emotions are governed by the Moon. It indicates the way people react emotionally to the situations and people they encounter, and particularly how they relate to women. The Moon describes the mother-child relationship; in the horoscope it represents a person's mother and a man's wife. The Moon reflects the way people think of and feel toward their motheiis but does not necessarily describe the mother's true nature. The formative years from birth to age seven are ruled by the Moon. The planet is associated with one's inherited tendencies, early background and childhood development. The Moon is linked to the subconscious; it has much to do with the memory, although it does not rule either the concrete or abstract mind. The Moon is passive, changeable, more fluctuating than any other planet. It is the receiver of energy. The Moon takes on the character of the sign it is in and of the planets with which it is aspected in the horoscope.
