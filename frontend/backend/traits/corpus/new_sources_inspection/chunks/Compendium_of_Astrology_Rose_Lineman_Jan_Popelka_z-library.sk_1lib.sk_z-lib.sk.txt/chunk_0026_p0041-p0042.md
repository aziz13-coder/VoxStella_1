### Pluto

Pluto, the slowest moving planet, takes 248 years to complete one circuit of the zodiac, averaging about twenty-one years in each sign. Due to the eccentricity of its orbit, it speeds through Scorpio in twelve years and slows to thirty-one years while crossing Taurus. The retrograde period is five months; the stationary period lasts up to seventeen days. In 1980, the average yearly motion was 1° in the sign Libra. Since Pluto's recent discovery in 1930, its influence has been observed in four signs: Cancer, Leo, Virgo and Libra. Its transit of Scorpio (1983/84-1955) will give opportunity for further study. Much that is said about this planet is theoretical. Pluto appears to be a powerful planet whose action influences mass destiny. It represents transformation and regeneration at the highest level, destruction and annihilation at the lowest. Mob action lies at Pluto's doorstep; so does the evolution of some of the greatest movements in history. Plutonian influence is operative for a long time before it manifests overtly; when it does, there is no restraining the force. Plutonian energy is potent, never weak or mediocre. Pluto's action can be purging, purifying and spiritually uplifting, or it can be obscene, degrading and criminal. For good or ill, Pluto-evolved change always has an irrevocable impact. The effect this planet has in the personal horoscope is debatable. Unless Pluto is the horoscope ruler or occupies a strong position in the horoscope, many people seem to react only mildly to the Plutonian influence. Perhaps response is on an inward or spiritual level and therefore less readily observed, or the metamorphosis may be so slow and subtle, unfolding over a period of years, that the individual is unaware of the fundamental changes taking place. Pluto acts to eliminate the dross, to cleanse, purify and rejuvenate on both the spiritual and physical planes. The horoscope placement of Pluto points out the means of regeneration and evolvement. Pluto coerces and urges development, but the individual makes the final choice. A person may strive for the heights of greatness or join the dregs of humanity, or one may resist its influence and stagnate. Plutonian power is at the individual's disposal; if one misuses it, there is no turning back. Retrograde Pluto is indicative of inner changes. The individual faces a choice between purification of the soul or spiritual bankruptcy. Stationary Pluto stresses the potency of this planet for greatness or for degradation, depending upon the direction of its power. Pluto rules death, afterlife and rebirth, that cycle of the soul that is essentially unknown to humankind. It is associated with age one hundred and over. Pluto rules the excretory system, the sex organs and gonads, and the astral cord. Pluto is the higher octave of Mars. Aswith all higheroctave planets, there is an occult significance attached. Pluto governs invisible forces, witchcraft, black magic and levitation. It is associated with corruption, the underground. Hades, reincarnation and resurrection. Reptiles, bats, vampires, plutonium, tungsten and the digit zero are ruled by Pluto. It has dominion over atomic fission and all that implies the unleashing of power and the transformation or recycling of energy. The eighth is the natural house of Pluto. Pluto rules the sign of Scorpio, jointly with the traditional ruler. Mars. Some authorities designate Pluto as co-ruler of Aries; however, it seems unlikely that the lengthy gestation of Pluto-induced action is compatible with the impulsive action of Aries. Pluto may be exalted in Leo; it is in detriment in Taurus; it may be in fall in Aquarius. The degree of exaltation is probably 17° Leo; the degree of fall, 17° Aquarius. ### The Moon's Nodes

The points at which a planet's orbit intersects the ecliptic are called planetary nodes. Other than the Moon's Nodes, the significance of nodes to astrology is as yet not fully understood. Lunar nodes are meaningful: they are entered in the horoscope along with the planets. The nodes of other planets are usually omitted. Planetary nodes are discussed in Appendix A. The position of the Moon's Nodes moves an average of 3' retrograde each day, although the true motion is both direct and retrograde. They take about nineteen years to complete one reverse cycle of the zodiac. The Moon's Nodes neither possess nor express planetary energy. They are sensitive points in the horoscope that indicate an individual's capacity for growth within the framework of the society in which he or she lives.

### The North Node

The position of the ascending or North Node, also called the Dragon's Head, is the point of intersection of the lunar orbit and the plane of the ecliptic as the Moon travels from south to north relative to the ecliptic. The North Node signifies those areas in which the individual's attitudes are in harmony with current trends. By reaching out into the realms of new experience as indicated by the horoscope placement of the North Node, one can develop more fully and abundantly.

### The South Node

The descending or South Node (Dragon's Tail) marks the orbital/ecliptic intersection point as the Moon travels from north to south relative to the ecliptic. The South Node lies directly opposite the North node in the zodiac. The imaginary line that links the North and South Nodes is the Nodal Axis or Dragon. The South Node represents deeply imbedded habit patterns that hinder a person's efforts to reach out. The individual may be out of step with the times and thereby at a disadvantage in the area of horoscope activation.

Planets and Moon's Nodes in the Signs The sign a planet occupies describes the manner in which the energy of the planet is manifested. The planetary ruler of a sign has a major influence on the sign and on the planets in that sign. Each sign is subdivided and planetary sub-rulers are assigned to various degrees within the sign. The ruler of a sign may also act as a sub-ruler. In those degrees that are both ruled and sub-ruled by the same planet, the characteristics of a sign are unmodified. A sub-ruler other than the planetary ruler of the sign acts in conjunction with the ruler; its action is subordinate. Because of the influence of sub-rulers, a planet passing through a particular sign does not operate in precisely the same way in all degrees of that sign, although the basic mode of sign expression remains unchanged.
