54 Compendium of Astrology A decanate sub-ruler shades or modifies the qualities of a sign in its designated decan according to its natural planetary influence. A planet that occupies its own decan of a sign in which it has little standing operates more freely than in other degrees of that sign. For example, in Scorpio, its sign of fall, the Moon is weakened; however, in the third decan (sub-ruled by the Moon) lunar energies are strengthened, and the individual is better able to cope with the emotional passion associated with this sign position. In order to distinguish the decans, each decanate is known by the name of its sub-ruler. The name of the sign ruled by the sub-ruler is also applied to the second and third decans. Decanates of Aries The first decan of Aries, a fire sign, is ruled and subruled by Mars, the planetary ruler of Aries. The dynamic, initiatory and impetuous qualities of Aries are emphasized in this decan. The first decan is called the Mars decanate of Aries. The second decan of Aries is ruled by Mars and subruled by the Sun, the ruler of Leo which is the next sign in the fire triplicity. This is the Leo or Sun decanate of Aries. The influence of the Sun in the second decan lends sustaining power to the natural initiative that is characteristic of Aries. The third decan of Aries is ruled by Mars and subruled by Jupiter because Jupiter is the ruler of Sagittarius, the third sign in the fire triplicity. In this decan, Jupiter supplements and expands the natural Aries expression. The third is the Sagittarius or Jupiter decanate of Aries. Decanates of Taurus The first decan of Taurus, ruled and sub-ruled by Venus, brings out the endurance, obstinacy and sensuality inherent in this earth sign. The first is called the Venus decanate of Taurus. The second decan of Taurus is sub-ruled by Mercury, the ruler of Virgo that follows Taurus in the earth triplicity. Mercury's influence here lends greater flexibility and discrimination than is found in the first decan. This is the Virgo or Mercury decanate of Taurus. The third decan of Taurus is sub-ruled by Saturn, which is the ruler of Capricorn, the third sign in the earth triplicity. In this decan, Saturn intensifies the materialistic qualities of Taurus. This is the Capricorn or Saturn decanate of Taurus. Decanates of Gemini The first decan of Gemini, an air sign, is ruled and sub-ruled by Mercury. Diffusion, restlessness and superficiality are predominant characteristics of this decan. The first decan is called the Mercury decanate of Gemini. The second decan of Gemini is sub-ruled by Venus as the planetary ruler of Libra, the next air sign. Venus enhances the Gemini charm; the style of expression is perhaps more pleasing and attractive than in the first decanate. The second decan is the Libra or Venus decanate of Gemini. The third decan of Gemini is sub-ruled by Uranus and Saturn, rulers of the remaining air sign, Aquarius. Since Uranus is the higher octave of Mercury, Uranus probably has greater significance in this decan than does Saturn. Uranus supplements the mental expression of Gemini and adds spontaneity and unpredictability. If theSatum influence manifests itself in this decan, it should increase persistence and decrease diffusiveness without stifling versatility or impeding flexibility. The third decan is the Aquarius or Uranus-Saturn decanate of Gemini. Decanates of Cancer The first decan of Cancer, awatersign, is ruled and subruled by the Moon. Thisdecan emphasizes the nurturing, protective, emotional and fluctuating attributes of the sign. This is the Moon decanate of Cancer. The second decan of Cancer is sub-ruled by Pluto and Mars, rulers of Scorpio. This decan strengthens the Cancer tenacity but also increases turbulence. Plutonian influence in this decan can help transform Cancer sensitivity into productive creative expression. The second is the Scorpio or Pluto-Mars decanate of Cancer. The third decan of Cancer, sub-ruled by Neptune and Jupiter as rulers of Pisces, reinforces the psychic nature of Cancer and strengthens compassion. This decan also heightens secretive tendencies and mystical inclinations. This is the Pisces or Neptune-Jupiter decanate of Cancer.

Planets and Moon's Nodes in the Signs 55 Decanates of Leo The first decan of Leo. the second fire sign, is ruled and sub-ruled by the Sun. This decan produces great vitality and power in the expression of the Leo qualities. This is the Sun decanate of Leo. The second decan of Leo is sub-ruled by Jupiter, ruler of Sagittarius, the fire sign which follows Leo in the zodiac. The Sagittarius or Jupiter decanate of Leo exhibits a freer and more versatile expression of the Leo characteristics. The third decan of Leo is sub-ruled by Mars as ruler of Aries, the remaining fire sign. The Aries or Mars decanate of Leo expresses greater assertiveness, independence and courage. Decanates of Virgo The first decan of Virgo, the second earth sign, is ruled and sub-ruled by Mercury. The Mercury decanate of Virgo emphasizes perception and discrimination, sensitivity to detail and fastidiousness. The second decan of Virgo is sub-ruled bySaturn, ruler of Capricorn. The Capricorn or Satum decanate of Virgo promotes a businesslike approach and reinforces the organized methodical expression of Virgo. The third decan of Virgo is sub-ruled by Venus as ruler of Taurus. The Taurus or Venus decanate of Virgo strengthens the practical qualities of the sign and increases stability. Decanates of Libra The first decan of Libra, ruled and co-ruled by Venus, stresses harmony and companionship. The Venus decanate of Libra emphasizes expression through combined efforts on a partnership basis. The second decan of Libra is sub-ruled by Uranus and Saturn, rulers of Aquarius. Uranus injects greater freedom and independence into Libra expression, but it does not override the unity and cooperation that is basic to Libra. If the Saturn influence predominates in the Aquarius or Uranus-Saturn decanate of Libra, Saturn stabilizes and thus reinforces Libra qualities. The third decan of Libra is sub-ruled by Mercury as ruler of Gemini. Mercury sharpens intellectual expression in the Gemini or Mercury decanate of Libra; it fosters flexibility and versatility, but it also adds restlessness. Decanates of Scorpio The first decan of Scorpio is ruled and sub-ruled by Pluto and Mars. The Pluto-Mars decanate is the most powerful decanate of this sign and perhaps the harshest, for the intense Scorpio characteristics are essentially unmodified. The second decan of Scorpio is sub-ruled by Neptune and Jupiter, rulers of Pisces. The Pisces or Neptune- Jupiter decanate reflects a more subtle manifestation of Scorpio attributes. Neptunian influence may, in some instances, subvert the higher motives of Scorpio expression. The third decan of Scorpio is sub-ruled by the Moon, ruler of Cancer. In the Cancer or Moon decanate, the Moon softens the harshness of the sign and lends to sympathy and kindness in a rough sort of way, for there is no real gentling or taming of Scorpio expression. Decanates of Sagittarius The first decan of Sagittarius is ruled and sub-ruled by Jupiter. The Jupiter decanate emphasizes the optimistic and idealistic qualities of the sign, but exaggerates Sagittarian restlessness and wanderlust as well. The second decan of Sagittarius is sub-ruled by Mars, ruler of Aries. The Aries or Mars decanate shows increased daring, initiative and immoderation. Martian influence promotes fool hardiness, improvidence and opportunism. The third decan of Sagittarius is sub-ruled by the Sun, ruler of Leo. In the Leo or Sun decanate, the Sun enhances Sagittarian vitality. Leonine influence adds staying power and modifies the restless attributes of Sagittarius so that expression is more stable than in the first and second decans. Decanates of Capricorn The first decan of Capricorn is ruled and sub-ruled by Saturn. The Saturn decanate of Capricorn is predominantly goal-oriented. This decan stresses the diligence, perseverance and status-consciousness that is associated with this sign.

56 Compendium of Astrology The second decan of Capricorn is sub-ruled by Venus as ruler of Taurus. Venus augments the materialistic and acquisitive characteristics of Capricorn in the Taurus or Venus decanate. The third decan of Capricorn is sub-ruled by Mercury as ruler of Virgo. Mercury mellows the dogmatism of the sign. Common sense and greater flexibility underscore Capricorn expression in the Virgo or Mercury decanate.. Decanates of Aquarius The first decan of Aquarius is ruled and sub-ruled by Uranus and Satum. The Uranus-Satum decanate is progressive, unpredictable and indicative of sudden change. The second decan of Aquarius is sub-ruled by Mercury as ruler of Gemini. The Gemini or Mercury decanate of Aquarius emphasizes the intellectual expression of the sign. This decan is associated with unprecedented ideas and inventive genius. The second decan does not modify the natural Aquarian tendencies; its action is to supplement. The third decan of Aquarius is sub-ruled by Venus as ruler of Libra. Venus lends warmth to the impersonal Aquarian expression. There is greater scope for acceptance of the Aquarian movement in the Libra or Venus decanate. Decanates of Pisces The first decan of Pisces is ruled and sub-ruled by Neptune and Jupiter. The Neptune-Jupiter decanate is given a highly spiritual connotcition. It is said to bring out the inevitable. In this decan the self-sacrificing elements of Pisces expression are emphasized. The second decan of Pisces is sub-ruled by the Moon, ruler of Cancer. The Moon heightens impressionability and emotional vulnerability. The Cancer or Moon decanate exhibits a morbid, self-pitying or maudlin mode of expression? The third decan of Pisces is sub-ruled by Pluto and Mars, rulers of Scorpio. Pisces, a comparatively weak sign, is strengthened by Pluto and Mars. Piscean expression may manifest as either spiritual regeneration or degradation because of the Plutonian influence in this decan. The Scorpio or Pluto-Mars decanate is representative of the power within the self to overcome inner weaknesses or bring about self-destruction. Duads (Dwadasamas) A further subdivision divides the signs into twelve duads, sometimes called dwadasamas, of IVz" each. The first duad is ruled by the planetary ruler of the sign; the second duad is ruled by the ruler of the sign that immediately follows in the zodiac; the third duad is ruled by the ruler of the next sign in the zodiac and so on. The final duad is ruled by the ruler of the sign that directly precedes the sign of interest. The duad ruler is subordinate to both the planetary ruler of the sign and the decanate sub-ruler.
