### Navamsas

A ninefold division of signs based on Hindu astrology describes the sub-influence of signs within a sign. Each sign is divided into nine equal parts called navamsas, of S'A0 each. Beginning with the first navamsa of Aries, a sign is assigned to each navamsa in order according to natural zodiacal sequence, repeating the sequence as necessary to the ninth navamsa of Pisces. The navamsa sign colors natural sign expression thus creating slight variations within a sign.

### Special Degrees Critical Degrees

Critical degrees, which stem from the Hindu concept of the twenty-eight Lunar Mansions (subdivisions of the zodiac based upon the Moon's average daily motion), are, unlike navamsas, widely accepted by western astrologers. Critical degrees roughly separate the Lunar Mansions, each of which spans 12-%° of the zodiac. The critical degrees are 0°, 13°, and 26° in cardinal signs (Aries, Cancer, Libra and Capricorn), 9° and 21° in fixed signs (Taurus, Leo, Scorpio and Aquarius), 4° and 17° in mutable signs (Gemini, Virgo, Sagittarius and Pisces). Critical degrees supplement planetary strength, and they can be the deciding factor in determining which of two planets is the more influential. Of two planets that otherwise carry equal weight in a horoscope, the one occupying a critical degree is deemed the stronger of the two.

### Anaretic Degrees

The last degree of a sign, from 290to 30°, is called the anaretic degree. Anaretic degrees signify an urgent need to correctly utilize the planetary energy of the planets placed therein. The individual is directed to deal with fundamental issues as indicated by the horoscope placement of the planets that occupy anaretic degrees. These planets activate conditions which one cannot ignore but must cope with in order to grow in that area. An anaretic degree placed on a house cusp draws attention to the affairs of the house. If the individual neglects these matters, a stalemate develops; the affairs of the house stagnate, and progress in this area is retarded.

### Nodal Degrees

A planet that falls in the same numerical degree (not necessarily in the same sign) as the Moon's Nodes is given special significance. If placed in a nodal degree a planet is said to indicate crisis or catastrophe at some later point in life.
