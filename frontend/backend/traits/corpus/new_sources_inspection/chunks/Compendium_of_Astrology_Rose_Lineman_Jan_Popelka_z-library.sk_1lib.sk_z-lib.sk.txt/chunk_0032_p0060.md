### The Ascendant-Descendant Axis

The cusp of the seventh house, the Descendant, lies directly opposite the cusp of the first house, the Ascendant. The line that connects these two houses, the Ascendant-Descendant axis (horizontal axis), represents the horizon. The section of the horoscope above this axis, the southern hemisphere, charts signs and planets that appear in that part of the sky above the horizon at the time and place of birth. Houses seven through twelve, which lie above the Ascendant-Descendant axis, the axis of intellectual expression and spiritual reaction, are referred to as collective or dispersive houses. A large number of planets placed in the collective houses of the southern hemisphere or day section of the horoscope indicates outgoing, active, intellectual expression and mental objectivity in a general way. Signs and planets that appear below the horizontal axis are those that are below Earth's horizon. Houses one through six, which fall below this axis in the northern hemisphere or night section of the horoscope, are termed personal or focal houses. A majority of planets occupying personal houses signifies receptive, passive intellectual expression and mental subjectivity. The Midheaven (MC)-Imum Coeli (IC) Axis The IC falls directly opposite the MC in the horoscope. The line joining these two angles is defined as the MC- IC axis, or vertical axis. The vertical axis, the axis of physical action and emotional response, separates the eastern and western hemispheres of the horoscope. In a quadrant system of house division (unequal house system), the MC denotes the cusp of the tenth house and the IC marks the cusp of the fourth house.

### In such a system, the houses are not the same size

that is, depending upon the time and place of birth, one house differs from another in the number of zodiacal degrees it contains. An equal house system is one in which each house is comprised of 30°. The cusps of the fourth and tenth houses are dependent upon the degree of the Ascendant (as are the other house cusps). The angles of a horoscope occupy the same sign and degree in all house systems for any particular set of birth data. Regardless of the house system used, the MC and IC always form the vertical axis even if these points differ from the cusps of the tenth and fourth houses. The houses that lie in the eastern section of the horoscope (left of the vertical axis) are collectively termed independent houses. Houses in the western hemisphere of the horoscope (right of the vertical axis) are dependent houses. These are houses four, five, six, seven, eight and nine in a quadrant system. A preponderance of planets in the western hemisphere indicates that freedom of action is limited to some extent by society.
