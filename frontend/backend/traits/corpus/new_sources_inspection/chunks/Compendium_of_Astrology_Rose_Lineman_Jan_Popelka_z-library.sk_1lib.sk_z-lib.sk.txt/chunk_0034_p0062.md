### FIRST QUADRANT

The IC and Descendant bound the second quadrant, which contains houses four, five and six. A combination of northern and western hemisphere emphasis is shown here. When a large number of planets is placed in the second quadrant, tendencies toward intellectual, emotional and physical passiveness are emphasized. The third quadrant is bounded by the Descendant and the MC. A majority of planets occupying this quadrant combines emotional and physical receptivity with mental objectivity and outgoing intellectual expression. The MC and Ascendant bound the fourth quadrant. Heavy planetary distribution in this quadrant indicates outgoing intellectual, emotional and physical activity (see Figure 6). Quadrants are also associated with stages of developing the self in relation to the general focus of the houses contained therein. This aspect of quadrant emphasis is discussed in this chapter in the section entitled "Developmental Focus of Quadrant Houses" following individual house descriptions. The term occidental is used variously to denote all the houses in the western hemisphere (second and third quadrants) or to specify houses one, two, three, seven, eight and nine, which form the first and third quadrants. The term oriental is sometimes applied to all houses in the eastern hemisphere (first and fourth quadrants) and sometimes to those houses in the second and fourth quadrants, namely houses four, five, six, ten, eleven and twelve. These terms are misleading when applied to houses and, if used, should be explicitly defined. It is less confusing and always correct to refer to a planet as being oriental of the Sun when it rises and sets before the Sun or as occidental if it rises and sets after the Sun. Hylegiacal and Anaretic Places Hylegiacal places, also called aphetic places, are portions of the horoscope that are termed life supportive. The hylegiacal places are the last 5° of the twelfth house, the first 25° of the first house, the last 5° of the sixth house, the first 25° of the seventh house the last 5° of the eighth house, all ot the ninth house the entire tenth house and the lirst hall of the eleventh house (see Figure 7). ### MYLGGIACAL PLACES

Figure 7. The Hylegiacal Places (shaded areas) in the Horoscope A benefic planet that is posited in a hylegiacal place and is strong and well aspected is designated the Hyleg or Apheta of the horoscope, the giver of life. In a man's horoscope, the Sun, if qualified, is the Hyleg; if the Sun is not in a hylegiacal place, or is afflicted and weak by sign, the Moon is used; if neither planet qualifies, the planetary ruler of the Ascendant is considered next, then Jupiter, Mercury and Venus in that order. In a woman's horoscope, the Moon is considered first in determining the Hyleg, then the Sun, the Ascendant ruler, Venus, Jupiter and Mercury. If all such planets are afflicted, the hylegiacal benefic that is least afflicted is chosen Hyleg. The Hyleg is associated with longevity and the sustaining of life. The Anareta, the most influential malefic, which occupies or rules the sign on the cusp of the fourth, sixth, eighth or twelfth house, is the destroyer of the horoscope; it is associated with death. Its position is the anaretic place, which describes the termination of life.

### House Classifications

The houses are classified according to similar or complementary association.

### Modal Houses

Modal houses are classed according to the general mode of activity shared by the houses in each group. This threefold division classifies the houses as angular, succedent or cadent. The angular houses are one, four, seven and ten. Each of these houses is ruled naturally by a cardinal sign, and each reflects cardinal qualities in its activities. These houses are indicative of initiatory, dynamic action. Planets placed in angular houses are powerful, for they act as motivators. Succedent houses (two, five, eight and eleven), naturally fixed houses, are associated with reliable, steady action in a general sense. Specific action in any house is determined by the planets and signs involved. Ideas and activities that originate in angular houses are carried out in these houses. The cadent houses, ruled naturally by mutable signs, are three, six, nine and twelve. Adaptability and diversified action are shown here. These houses disperse the results of angular and succedent house activity.
