Influence of the Signs in the Second House Signs that influence the second house describe a person's attitudes toward money and material possessions. These signs indicate in part how a person manages personal financial affairs. Aries on the cusp of the second house indicates motivation and dynamic interest in financial affairs. One's initiative is directed toward moneymaking activities. Taurus, the natural sign of the second house, lends to practical interest in financial affairs when it occupies the cusp of this house (or is intercepted within).
