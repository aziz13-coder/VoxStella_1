### Planetary Dispositors

The planetary ruler(s) of a sign is said to disposit any planet(s) placed in that sign. When a planet occupies a sign other than the one it rules, this planet is disposited by the ruler(s) of the sign in which it is placed. The dispositor of a planet influences the action of the disposited planet and its affairs and is taken into consideration in the interpretation of the planets in the various signs. Interrelationships in the horoscope particularize these meanings. For example, Jupiter, dispositor of Venus in Sagittarius, influences Venusian affairs and expression in the manner consistent with its horoscope standing. If Jupiter is badly afflicted, some negative Jupiterian qualities manifest in Venusian activities to the degree indicated by Venus aspects. An aspect formed between a planet and its dispositor strengthens the influence of the dispositor on the disposited planet. If the planets form no mutual aspect but both are in aspect with the same third planet, their energies are united by the third planet, and the dispositor s influence is greater than if no link is shown. Examination of a dispositor sheds light on motives that are not immediately apparent in the disposited planet. In Chapter 13, Figure 18, Jupiter disposits Venus; they are not in aspect to each other, but both aspect the Sun, also disposited by Jupiter (Sun conjunct Venus, Sun square Jupiter).
