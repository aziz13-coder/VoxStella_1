Method II: The GMT Method of Calculation The GMT method of calculating the Local Sidereal Time has grown in popularity with the increased use of standard time. In this method the birthtime is first converted to Greenwich Mean Time. This step serves a dual purpose; corrections that are applied to planetary positions listed in the ephemeris also require the calculation of the Greenwich Mean Time of birth. The sidereal time listed in the emphemeris on the GMT birthdate is adjusted according to the GMT birthtime. The GMT birthdate is the date on which the GMT birthtime occurs. It is usually the same day as the recorded birthdate, but it may be the day preceding or following the given date. One correction applied to the GMT interval includes the longitude time correction. The sidereal time adjustment and subsequent correction give the sidereal time at Greenwich at the time of birth; the Greenwich sidereal time is then adapted to the longitude of the birthplace to determine the Local Sidreal Time at the place of birth.
