Correcting the House Cusps for a Placidean Horoscope The position of the house cusps for a horoscope may be taken directly from the Table of Houses if the exact Local Sidereal Time and latitude of the birthplace are listed. If the Local Sidereal Time falls between two consecutive sidereal time listings, the positions of the house cusps are interpolated according to the proportional sidereal time difference. Except for the MC (Midheaven), which forms the cusp of the tenth house in the Placidean system of horoscope erection, a similar interpolation is made based on latitude for births that do not occur at an exact degree of latitude listed in the Table of Houses. Time Correction for House Cusps The position of a house cusp moves in proportion to the corresponding time change. The ratio of the sidereal time increment to the sidereal time interval is equal to the ratio of the house cusp increment to the house cusp interval at a specified degree of latitude. The sidereal time increment (ST increment) is the time difference between the calculated Local Sidereal Time (LST) at birth and the closest earlier sidereal time listed in the Table of Houses; the sidereal time interval (ST interval) is the time difference between the closest earlier sidereal time and the following (later) consecutive sidereal time listed (the calculated LST will fall between these two listings); the house cusp increment is the distance in arc a cusp moves during the time span of the ST increment; the house cusp interval is the distance a cusp moves during the ST interval.
