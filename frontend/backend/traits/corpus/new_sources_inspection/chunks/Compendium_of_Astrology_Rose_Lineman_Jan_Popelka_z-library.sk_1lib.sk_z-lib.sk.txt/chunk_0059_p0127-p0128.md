Calculation of House Cusps 143 2S°15' Aquarius (cusp 11 at 20:16:07 for 41°) - 05' (correction for latitude) 28° 10' Aquarius (cusp 11 correct to the birth LST and the birth latitude) Summary of House Cusp Calculation

### 1. Calculate and record the Local Sidereal Time

that corresponds to the clock time of birth at the place of birth. Look for the calculated Local Sidereal Time in a Table of Houses. If the Local Sidereal Time appears in the table, the MC listed for that time may be entered on the chart form without correction. The other house cusps that are listed for this time at the degree of latitude of birth need only be adjusted to the exact latitude of the birthplace according to the procedure outlined in Steps 13-19.

### 2. If the Local Sidereal Time of birth does not

appear in the Table of Houses, it will fall between two consecutive sidereal time listings, one that is earlier in time than the Local Sidereal Time and one that is later. Record these two sidereal times. Label the earlier time "earlier ST"; label the later time "later ST."

### 3. Calculate and record the time difference

between the Local Sidereal Time of birth and the earlier ST. Label the result "ST increment." If you prefer the log method of calculation, find the log of the ST increment in a log table; record and label the log of the ST increment.

### 4. Calculate and record the time difference between

the earlier ST and the later ST. Label the result "ST interval." If calculations are to be completed by the log method, find the log of the ST interval in a log table, record and label.

### 5. Complete this step with either the regular

method of calculation or the log method. Regular method: Divide the ST increment by the ST interval to determine the constant ST factor. Record and label the ST factor. It will be used to correct each house cusp for time. Log method: Subtract the log of the ST interval from the log of the ST increment to determine the log of the ST factor. Record and label the result; it is a constant that is used to correct each house cusp for time. Follow Steps 6-8 to correct the MC. Proceed with Steps 9-19 to correct all other house cusps for time and latitude.

### 6. Record the zodiacal position of the MC that

is listed for the earlier ST.

### 7. Calculate the cusp increment (correction) for

the MC by either the regular or log method. Regular method: Multiply the ST factor calculated in Step 5 by 1 ° (or 60') to determine the cusp increment. ### 9. Record the position of the house cusp of

The result is the cusp increment. Log method: Add the log of the cusp interval (determined in Step 10) to the log of the ST factor (found in Step 5). The anti-log of the result is the cusp increment.
