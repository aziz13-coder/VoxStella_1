### 4. Calculate the daily motion for the planet (or

node) of interest. If a noon ephemeris is used, calculate the difference between the planet's (node's) positions listed on the GMT birthdate and the preceding date if the GMT birthtime is a.m.; find the difference between the planet's (node's) positions listed on the GMT birthdate and the following date if the GMT birthtime is p.m. If a zero hour ephemeris is used, calculate the difference between the planet's (node's) positions listed on the birthdate and the following date for both a.m. and p.m. birthtimes. The result is the daily motion for that planet (node) on the birthdate. Note whether the planet's motion is direct or retrograde. The Moon's Node's average motion is retrograde. Some ephemerides list the position of the True Node, whose position may differ from that of the Mean Node, which most ephemerides list, and whose motion may be direct or retrograde. Use the True Node's position and motion if it is listed.

### 6. Correct the planet's (node's) position that is

listed on the GMT birthdate. To correct the position

### of a planet that is in direct motion, proceed as follows

If a noon ephemeris is used, subtract the correction derived in Step 5 from the planet's position listed on the GMT birthdate if the GMTbirthtime is a.m.; add the correction to the listed position if the GMT birthtime is p.m. If a zero hour ephemeris is used, add the correction to the position listed on the GMT birthdate. To correct the position of a planet in retrograde motion, add the correction for an a.m. birth when using a noon ephemeris; subtract the correction for a p.m. birth when using a noon ephemeris; subtract the correction for all births when using a zero hour ephemeris. The result gives the correct zodiacal position of the planet (node) at the time of birth.
