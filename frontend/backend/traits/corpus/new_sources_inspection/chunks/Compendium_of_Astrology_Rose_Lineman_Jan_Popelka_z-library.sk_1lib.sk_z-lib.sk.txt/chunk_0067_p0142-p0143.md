Venus' aspects are considered next and so on until all aspects are entered in the aspectarian. Basic Significance of the Aspects Aspects are classed as hard (difficult) or easy (soft). Hard aspects stimulate action and induce motivation. Except for the conjunction and parallel, all hard aspects are disharmonious to some extent depending upon the strength of the aspect and the natures of the planets involved. These aspects create tension, friction or difficulties, which in tum produce activity. The conjunction and parallel may be harmonious or disharmonious; either way, they stimulate planetary energies as do all hard aspects. Easy aspects generate harmony; they blend planetary energies smoothly and create conditions of ease and accord. They partially mitigate difficulties or hardships imposed by inharmonious aspects and contribute to "good luck." Planetary action in a horoscope depends largely upon the aspects formed. The same aspect will not have the same effect in one horoscope as in another. All relevant factors, including other aspects, are weighed in determining the influence a given aspect exerts in a particular horoscope. In any case, the essential characteristics of an aspect remain unchanged although its significance may be modified by other factors. Conjunction: A major hard aspect, the conjunction is the most powerful of all aspects. It merges planetary energies and focuses planetary drives. If the natures of the two planets in conjunction are in harmony (as with two benefics), the conjunction unifies and strengthens planetary influence in a horoscope. If the planets are naturally discordant (as with a benefic and a malefic or with two malefics), the conjunction is disruptive; the stronger planet will overshadow or distort the weaker planet's influence. Vigintile: A minor easy aspect, the vigintile is mildly harmonious but has little effect in a natal horoscope. Quindecile: A minor easy aspect, the quindecile has a weak but favorable influence. Semi-sextile: A minor easy aspect, the semi-sextile is of some importance in a natal horoscope. The energies of planets in semi-sextile adapt harmoniously; if these planets are otherwise afflicted, the semi-sextile may partially mitigate adverse effects. Decile: A minor easy aspect, the decile, also called a semi-quintile, has a harmonious effect although its influence in the natal horoscope is weak. Semi-square: A minor hard aspect, the semi-square ranks as the most influential of the minor aspects. The semi-square is irritating and creates friction between the planets in aspect. Mild stress accompanies the dispersion of planetary energies. Septile: Probably a hard aspect, the septile is seldom used. This minor aspect is said to produce an intangible effect that may have to do with karma or destiny. Sextile: A major easy aspect, the sextile is considered helpful and beneficial; it is associated with opportunities and advantages. Quintile: A minor easy aspect, the quintile is favorable although moderately weak.

Aspects Defined 159 Square: A major hard aspect, the square is the most dynamic aspect. The square generates tension and galvanizes action. It produces difficulties and obstacles that thwart planetary drives, but it also spurs ambition to overcome such barriers. Tredecile: A minor easy aspect, the tredecile is harmonious but carries little weight in a natal horoscope. Trine: A major easy aspect, the trine is the most propitious and influential easy aspect. The trine is associated with ease and good fortune. A trine to an otherwise afflicted planet is extremely helpful in alleviating the difficulties imposed by adverse aspects. Sesquiquadrate: A minor hard aspect, the sesquiquadrate ranks only slightly lower than the semisquare in influence. This aspect creates agitation between planetary energies and gives rise to aggravation and discord. Bi-quintile: A minor easy aspect, the bi-quintile has a moderately harmonious effect; it is of slight significance in a natal horoscope. Quincunx: A minor hard aspect, the quincunx emphasizes the need for adjustment; planetary energies do not combine harmoniously and their expression is strained, uncomfortable. Opposition: A major hard aspect, the opposition is separative in nature. Planets in polarity present a challenge as well as an attraction and antagonism between opposing energies. The opposition is second only to the conjunction in strength. Parallel: A major hard aspect, the parallel has a unifying effect for good or ill depending upon the nature of the planets involved. A harmonious parallel reinforces planetary influence; a disharmonious parallel induces discord and conflict. Contraparallel: A major hard aspect, the contraparallel is disruptive; planetary energies are at cross-purposes.
