### 4. As a reminder when determining planetary

influence in the horoscope, note on the chart form all planetary dignities, debilities and mutual receptions. Also note the chart ruler (the planetary ruler of the sign on the Ascendant).

### 5. In the interpretational notes, enter a general

observation based on the final dispositor or a circle of dispositors if either exists. 6. Note any special aspect configurations. Interpret.

### 7. From the aspectarian, determine if about half of

all major zodiacal aspects are of one particular kind (conjunction, sextile, etc.). Do not include the parallel or contraparallel in the count. These are not zodiacal aspects; they are based on declination. Note the general influence of the type of aspect that is in the majority. Conjunction emphasis lends the power of unification to planetary drives; conditions that permit the focusing of energies and encourage the merging of interests are indicated; activity is expressed with and through other people. A majority of sextiles signifies opportune circumstances that foster productive use of energies and enable one to handle the affairs of life to best advantage. Square emphasis stimulates ambition; the individual must rise to the challenge of obstacles and trying conditions or be defeated by adversities. Trine emphasis denotes harmonious conditions that allow one's affairs to flow smoothly; the rewards of life come easily and abundantly to the individual. A predominance of oppositions gives the individual a sharp awareness of the polarities and contrasts in life; conflicting conditions and opposing interests force the individual to adequately balance internal and external affairs.

### 8. Note any planet that has no major or minor

aspect. An unaspected planet has no effective outlet. Its influence is limited and weakened because its energy is not integrated in the horoscope and, therefore, corresponding drives are not integrated in the totality of the person. 9. Note the position of the prenatal eclipse.

### 10. Note the event if a solar or lunar eclipse occurred

on the birthdate.

### 11. Interpret and record the meaning of the sign on

the cusp of the first house (the Ascendant) and any sign intercepted within the house. Interpret according to sign and house position the planet(s) that occupies the first house, the planet(s) that rules the sign on the cusp and the planet(s) that rules a sign intercepted within the house. Interpret the aspects that these planets form. Study the natural ruler(s) of the house and any planets that occupy the natural sign of the house to determine the influence these planets exert in this house. Repeat the same procedure for each house in the horoscope. Include interpretation of the Nodes and Part of Fortune, and evaluation of the Vertex, East Point and eclipse effects.
