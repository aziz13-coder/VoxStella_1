These aspects suggest that Susan may have difficulty relating emotionally on both personal and public levels of social interaction. The trines (applying, see Chapter 14) mitigate potentially severe effects of the contraparallels. The only other major Venus aspect, a contraparallel with Pluto in combination with the Pluto/Ascendant square, coarsens the natural physical beauty and refined taste associated with Venus as Ascendant ruler and indicates discord in seventh house relationships (Pluto rules Scorpio, which appears on the cusp). As each house, planet and sign is studied in detail, some modifications to deductions based on first house factors may be required. It is impossible to obtain a well-rounded view from one or even several factors of a horoscope. However, upon examination and evaluation of all factors, a clearer picture of Susan's nature and potentials should emerge. In her thirties at the time of this writing, Susan, after experiencing career disappointments, an unhappy marriage and subsequent divorce, is a well-adjusted young woman who, through self-understanding, recognizes her weaknesses and capitalizes on her strengths. At present, she holds a responsible and wellpaying position (Taurus and Capricorn) with an advertising agency (Sagittarius), which produces commercials for television and radio (Aquarius). Spare time outlets include working with children (Leo and fifth house) and handcrafts (Venus). Final analysis reveals that although Susan tends to look on the brighter side of things (she is a very positive personality), she is realistic about herself and practical in her attitudes toward life. For this reason, a practical, realistic approach was taken in discussing astrological potentials with her, an approach that she understood and appreciated. An astrologer who establishes a good rapport with clients and earns their trust will find that clients return again and again for consultations, for astrology does not stop with the natal chart; that is merely the beginning.
