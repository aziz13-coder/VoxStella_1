### Sun Conjunctions

A person's life force and the drive for self-expression are brought into focus by the energies of planets in conjunction with the Sun. In the final analysis, the effect of a conjunction depends largely upon the aspects made to the conjunction by other planets. A conjunction which has an orb of 30' or less is termed cazimi. The energy of a planet which lies so close to the Sun is thought to be "burnt up" by the heat of the Sun. A planet which forms a cazimi conjunction with the Sun has no identity of its own; its principles-are engulfed by the ego. A conjunction whose orb is between 31' and 3° is called combust. A combusted planet does not operate independently of the Sun; its energy is integrated with that of the Sun. Except when it forms a cazimi or combust conjunction, the Sun vitalizes any planet it aspects. A cazimi or combust conjunction weakens a planet, but it gives greater force to the Sun's drives. Sun Conjunct Moon: This aspect unites the ego and the emotions. The drive for achievement is closely allied with instinctual urges. Self-satisfaction is increased because the drive for self-expression is in harmony with the emotions. Whether or not one's actions are correct, the individual suffers little guilt or remorse unless other factors in the horoscope indicate differently; this person feels that what he or she does is right. The new Moon (Sun/Moon conjunction) strengthens self-motivation directed toward the affairs ruled by the Sun and Moon within the context of sign expression. The individual identifies with the home and views the parents as a unit. If the conjunction is afflicted, tendencies toward selfcenteredness and lack of conscience are intensified. Emotional disturbances, if they occur, deplete vitality and can, therefore, adversely affect physical well-being. Sun Conjunct Mercury: Since Mercury is never more than 28° apart from the Sun, the conjunction is the only major zodiacal aspect that can occur between these two planets. This conjunction strengthens the will to act on ideas. The individual does not merely think over or talk about plans, but gives concrete expression to ideas through action. This aspect restricts self-objectivity; one fails to recognize shortcomings and character flaws because the ego influences thought patterns. If the conjunction is afflicted, willfulness and egotism are increased. The individual is inclined to be nervous and may lack good motor coordination.

It denotes charm, good looks and popularity. The individual is affectionate, companionable and socially active. In a male horoscope this aspect can indicate effeminacy unless it is in a masculine (positive) sign or Mars is strong in the chart. This conjunction fosters aesthetic leanings and even if actual talent is not indicated elsewhere in the horoscope, appreciation of the arts, beauty and culture is signified. Financial drives are strong motivating factors. If the conjunction is afflicted, laziness, self-indulgence, procrastination, hedonism, vanity, and/or materialism are possible depending upon the nature of the afflicting planets and the sign the conjunction occupies. Sun Conjunct Mars: This aspect signifies boldness and self-assertiveness within the limits of sign expression. Initiative is combined with willpower to produce forceful and courageous action. This aspect fosters heroism and valor. Much physical activity is directed toward the affairs these planets influence in the horoscope. If the conjunction is afflicted, aggressive tendencies manifest as combativeness and belligerence; vital energies are bumed up through physical overexertion. If Uranus is the afflicting planet, the individual is prone to accidental mishaps. Sun Conjunct Jupiter: This conjunction protects the vital life forces and enriches creative self-expression. Jupiter increases the willpower and confidence of the Sun; the individual is high-spirited, optimistic and selfassured. Jupiter introduces an element of good fortune into the affairs ruled by the Sun and Jupiter. Afflictions to the conjunction generate improvidence, wastefulness, extravagance and too much reliance on luck. One tends to overestimate personal abilities and to inflate self-importance. The inclination to indulge in excesses, which cause obesity or are otherwise detrimental to health, is present. Sun Conjunct Saturn: In this aspect Saturn restricts the vitality and disciplines the self-expression of the Sun. A strong sense of duty is instilled. The individual must work diligently to achieve objectives. If the conjunction is well aspected, constructive selfrestraint, focus, and responsible action are signified; solar energies can be channeled productively, though painstaking effort is required. If the conjunction is afflicted, continual frustrations and setbacks may induce discouragement and despair. Self-confidence is reduced; natural self-expression is inhibited by an inferior self-opinion. Sun Conjunct Uranus; This conjunction indicates sparkling vitality and adds to personal magnetism. Creative self-expression is original, individualistic, innovative, inventive, uninhibited and unconventional. The individual is highly intuitive and may possess occult abilities. If the conjunction is afflicted, the individual is prone to actions that are contradictory, precipitous, radical, rebellious, eccentric, erratic and unpredictable. Sun Conjunct Neptune: This aspect generates strong spiritual and inspirational tendencies. If other factors in the horoscope confirm, the ego is expressed through the intangible; this is the aspect of the mystic and the visionary. If the conjunction is afflicted, self-delusion, escapism, confusion and self-destructive leanings are indicated. Physical vitality is low unless a favorable aspect from Mars increases energy. Sun Conjunct Pluto: This conjunction unites the power of Pluto with the vital life force of the Sun, a potent combination. The Plutonian potential for good or ill cannot be ignored, for it is linked irrevocably to the ego, the self. This aspect gives vast powers to the individual, powers that can be used constructively or destructively. Here lies the opportunity for selfregeneration and massive influence of the highest order in the area of chart activation; a power-hungry, ruthless drive for domination is also possible. The choice is one the individual must face. In this aspect Pluto demands a commitment from the Sun.
