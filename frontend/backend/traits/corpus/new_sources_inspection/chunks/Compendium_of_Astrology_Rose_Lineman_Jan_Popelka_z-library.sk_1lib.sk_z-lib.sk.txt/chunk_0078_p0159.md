### Sun Oppositions

Sun oppositions bring conflicts of will into play. Interests that are influenced by a Sun opposition are thrown into prominence. The individual is challenged to take action in the affairs and conditions that are ruled by the Sun and the opposing planet. Sun Opposite Moon: This opposition creates emotional stress and dissatisfaction, which drain vitality. The affairs governed by the Sun and Moon are intensified; they demand the personal attention of the individual. This opposition places stress on the individual's relationships; this person fails to identify with people in a general sense. One tends to categorize men and women separately and is tense around members of the opposite sex. The individual views the mother and father as separate entities, not as a parental unit; because of this, it is difficult for this person to maintain family unity in adult life. The signs of the opposition describe contrasting characteristics in one's nature; inner responses do not coincide with the conscious will. The individual derives little ego satisfaction or emotional pride from creative self-expression in the areas influenced by the opposition. A trine or sextile to the luminaries can provide an avenue for self-expression through which one can constructively channel the energy generated by the opposition. Sun Opposite Mars: This aspect generates animosity and hostility. The affairs that are influenced by the opposition produce sharp disagreements and much strife. These people are aggressive and antagonistic toward those who oppose them or compete against them. This aspect signifies physical strength and indicates a tendency to use physical force as a means of self-expression, especially in a male horoscope. It is characteristic of a bully who browbeats rivals or one who resorts to physical coercion. Sun Opposite Jupiter: This aspect fosters self-aggrandizement and personal extravagance. Self-expression reflects an inflated ego and grandiose ideas. The individual tends to overestimate personal influence or ability and to underestimate opposing forces or conditions. Ostentatious displays of religion or philosophy and presumptuous or pretentious behavior often accompany this opposition unless strong conservative tendencies appear elsewhere in the horoscope. Much vital energy is wasted through excessive practices. Obesity or liver damage can stem from over-indulgence. Sun Opposite Saturn: This opposition stirs conflict between the individual and those who have influence over the pursuit of worldly ambitions. The affairs affected by the opposition produce unwanted obligations

Many of these people are deluded by a false sense of well-being and misplaced trust; they may be the victims or the perpetrators of subterfuge and deceit. Self-expression is unrealistic and muddled. This aspect generates negative psychic energy and warns against contact with the spiritual world as well as misuse of drugs or alcohol. Sun Opposite Pluto: This aspect produces an unrelentless drive for power. The individual strives for absolute domination and control in the affairs influenced by the Sun and Pluto. Self-expression is forceful, coercive and demanding. The tension generated by this aspect creates an invisible aura of formidable force, an aura that inexplicably frightens or intimidates many of the people who come into contact with the individual. This aspect is associated with the autocrat or despot.

### Sun Opposite North Node Conjunct South Node

These aspects emphasize the need to overcome selfish tendencies and egocentric attitudes, which inhibit growth. Self-development depends largely upon the individual's capacity to reach out and cooperate with others in areas indicated by the North Node. Eclipse action near the birthdate is suggested by this configuration: such action should be verified and interpreted if applicable to the horoscope. Sun Opposite Part of fortune: This opposition indicates self-interests, which hinder the success promised by the Part of Fortune. It places the individual at a disadvantage in the affairs influenced by the opposition.

### Sun Opposite Ascendant Conjunct Descendant: These

aspects emphasize marriage, partnerships and coordinated activities over those endeavors that are primarily self-oriented. These people exert leadership and influence in their associations. Sun Opposite MC Conjunct IC. These aspects imply that a harmonious and productive family life is essential to the individual's progress in the world. Without a satisfying home life, this person does not function at full capability. The extent of worldly attainments hinges upon one's capacity to establish the kind of home life that fulfills individual needs.
