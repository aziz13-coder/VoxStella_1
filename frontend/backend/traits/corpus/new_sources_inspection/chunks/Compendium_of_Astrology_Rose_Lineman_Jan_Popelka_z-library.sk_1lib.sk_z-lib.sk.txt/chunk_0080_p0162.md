### Sun Contraparallels

Except for contraparallels formed with Mercury or Venus, Sun contraparallels carry less weight in the horoscope than the major zodiacal aspects; they are equal to Sun parallels in strength. A Sun contraparallel generates conflict between the drives of the Sun and the energies of the planet in aspect. The Sun and a contraparalleled planet operate at crosspurposes. The meaning of a Sun contraparallel formed with the Ascendant or MC corresponds to that of an afflicted conjunction. Other Sun contraparallels are treated as Sun oppositions; however, Sun contraparallels are not as challenging as oppositions and create slightly less tension. A Sun contraparallel that occurs with Mercury or Venus is as influential as a Sun/Mercury or Sun/Venus conjunction, but the influence is separative in nature rather than unifying. Sun Contraparallel Mercury: This contraparallel fosters ill-planned actions in relation to the affairs ruled by the Sun and Mercury. Depending upon the nature of the signs that the Sun and Mercury occupy, the individual may implement plans without adequate forethought, or may hesitate so long that action comes too late. Fire and air signs indicate haste; earth and water signs denote slower action. Sun Contraparallel Venus: This aspect encourages selfgratification and preoccupation with creature comforts. The individual is inclined to be lax about the affairs influenced by the Sun and Venus. The desire for luxurious living is in conflict with the urge to achieve. If other astrological factors confirm, pleasureseeking can become a way of life.

### Minor Sun Aspects

It is customary to include the semi-sextile, semi-square, sesquiquadrate and the quincunx in the interpretation of a natal horoscope. The use of other minor aspects is a matter of the astrologer's choice. The semi-square, sesquiquadrate and quincunx, all minor hard aspects, are considered stronger than the semi-sextile, a minor easy aspect. Minor Easy Aspects Formed by the Sun: A minor easy aspect, which is formed between the Sun and another planet, supports positive characteristics of both planets. These qualities blend in a harmonious fashion in accordance with the aspect's basic significance (Chapter

### 14) and supplement the creative self-expression and

vitality of the Sun. Make a determination based on the nature of the Sun and the planet in aspect, the signs these planets occupy and the meaning of the aspect. Apply these findings to the houses and affairs influenced by the Sun and the planet in aspect. Consider the comparatively weak influence the minor aspect bears in relation to other astrological factors in the horoscope. As a guide to interpreting a minor easy Sun aspect, read the meaning for a Sun sextile and a Sun trine. A minoreasy aspect does not confer the easy flow of expression that is associated with a trine nor does it denote the opportunities and advantages signified by a sextile. However, a minor easy aspect does generate other similar favorable characteristics, although the effect is much less pronounced. Minor Hard Aspects Formed by the Sun: A minor hard aspect, which occurs between the Sun and another planet, brings out minor discordancies in the natures of the two planets. Such an aspect indicates friction, which irritates self-expression. To interpret a minor hard Sun aspect, consider discordant characteristics of the two planets in aspect and the basic significance of the aspect. Determine the manner in which friction is produced and decide to what extent irritation or stress is generated. Include the Sun sign and the sign the planet in aspect occupies in the determination. Friction and irritation will manifest in the houses and affairs governed by the Sun and the planet in aspect.

### Moon Aspects

The Moon is receptive: it assumes characteristics of the sign it occupies and of the planets it aspects. Moon aspects have a decided bearing upon one's emotional nature and instinctive responses. How a person reacts to situations and conditions encountered is described by the Moon aspects. They also indicate how one relates to other people, women in particular.
