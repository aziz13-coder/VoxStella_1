### Moon Squares

Squares formed between the Moon and other planets are indicative of subconscious blocks and inner frustrations, which obstruct emotional expression. Subconscious inhibitions or prejudices that are hereditary and environmental in nature can retard the progress of affairs influenced by the Moon and a planet in square. Frustrating experiences stand in the way of emotional fulfillment. The individual must develop a constructive approach in order to overcome the obstacles generated by Moon squares. Moon Square Mercury: This aspect indicates a restless mind and changeable moods. It shows a lack of continuity of thought; the mind is easily distracted. These people are seldom happy with their decisions; they tend to rethink past decisions over and over again. Emotional impulses block clear judgment in matters ruled by the Moon and Mercury. These people are inclined to be irritable, moody, critical and loquacious. This aspect supports any other factors in the horoscope that point to possible psychological problems. Moon Square Venus: This square blocks feelings of affection. Depending upon the quality of the signs involved, romantic expression may be hesitant, fickle, maudlin or too intense. Family interests and relationships with women provide little happiness or emotional satisfaction. Sentimentality and extravagance create setbacks which impede the affairs governed by the Moon and Venus. Moon Square Mars: Hostile feelings and violent emotional reactions are characteristic of this aspect. These people are quick to take offense at petty remarks and trivial criticisms; they resent interference of any kind. They encounter many obstacles of their own making because they have little regard for the consequences of their actions. The affairs influenced by this square are beset by arguments and quarrels; the domestic sphere is discordant. Moon Square Jupiter: This square induces profuse emotional expression. These people are overly indulgent with loved ones, overly generous to the disadvantaged, overly sympathetic with the downtrodden; they are too liberal in their reactions to anything or anyone that appeals to the emotions. Unless good aspects offset this square, these people are vulnerable to a sob story. The affairs governed by the Moon and Jupiter suffer as a result of action that is motivated by misplaced sympathy. Moon Square Saturn: This aspect severely inhibits emotional warmth. It generates despondency, selfpity, gloominess, unhappiness, discouragement, fear and anxiety. These people tend to draw into a shell. It is imperative for their emotional well-being that they learn to be more optimistic and outgoing. Because the approach is narrow and lackluster, affairs ruled by the Moon and Saturn develop slowly. This square creates the tendency to accept hardships as necessary burdens, an attitude that reduces the capacity to overcome obstacles. The home environment is cold and somewhat depressing. Moon Square Uranus: This aspect generates an emotional need for change and excitement. Restless and discontented, these people turn from one interest to another in the search for increasingly greater thrills. They are seldom satisfied with the course of events influenced by this square; their actions in these areas reflect a radical departure from the norm. Home life and family relationships are generally disruptive. Moon Square Neptune: This square creates a chaotic emotional state. These people are confused about their feelings, unable to sort out inner frustrations. They tend to fantasize; many have difficulty in distinguishing between fantasy and reality. They cling to childhood illusions; they delude themselves about family relationships and domestic interests. These people suffer disappointments because of the impractical and muddled way they handle the affairs governed by the Moon and Neptune. Moon Square Pluto: This square signifies forceful and intense emotional expression. These people refuse to be dominated emotionally; they can be abusive. The lust for power is directed toward the affairs influenced by the square. If other factors in the horoscope confirm, these people can gain control of large

### Moon Square North Node Square South Node: This

aspect combination places the Moon at the focal point of a T-square configuration. The individual is insensitive to public opinion and prevailing trends. One must eliminate emotional bias, which retards progress, and channel lunar drives productively in order to develop adequately in areas indicated by the Nodes. Moon Square Part of Fortune: This square produces emotionally frustrating experiences, which detract from achievements associated with the Part of Fortune.

### Moon Square Ascendant Square Descendant: These

aspects suggest the inability to project emotionally in a pleasing manner. The failure to relate on an emotional level can cause family problems, marital difficulties and partnership conflicts. Moon Square MC Square IC: This combination of aspects denotes an emotionally frustrating atmosphere at home and in career. Emotional stress manifests as job dissatisfaction and domestic discontent.
