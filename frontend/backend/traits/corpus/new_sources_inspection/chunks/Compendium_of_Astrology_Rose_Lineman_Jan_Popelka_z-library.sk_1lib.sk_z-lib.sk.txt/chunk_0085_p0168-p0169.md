### Moon Oppositions

Moon oppositions point out contradictions in one's emotional nature. The individual does not relate well on an emotional level to the people and affairs represented by the opposing planet. One is acutely aware of the need to develop in the areas influenced by a Moon opposition. These interests cannot be ignored despite the antagonism, repugnance or distaste generated by the opposition. Moon Opposite Mercury: This opposition hinders the ability to express thoughts and feelings adequately. These people are often misunderstood because they cannot relate to others on an emotional or an intellectual level. They may chatter incessantly about nothing in particular, or they may refuse to volunteer

Most of them show little perceptivity or sensitivity to the affairs governed by the Moon and Mercury; they lack objectivity in these matters. The conflict that exists between emotional desires and intellectual considerations gives rise to indecisiveness and vacillation. These people are unsure about their decisions and uncertain about their feelings. They have difficulty dealing with their frustrations. This aspect is one indication of possible psychological difficulties. A trine or sextile to either planet can provide a constructive outlet for emotional or mental problems. A minor easy aspect here is also of some benefit. Moon Opposite Venus: This aspect denotes a rift in family life and in those affairs ruled by the Moon and Venus. These people feel emotionally separated from their mothers; many feel unloved or unappreciated. Often they are ill at ease with women. Depending upon the signs these two planets occupy and the influence of other related factors in the horoscope, these people may subconsciously withhold affection as a means of protecting emotional vulnerability, or they may flit from one romance to another in search of affection and emotional fulfillment. Many try to buy love with money or expensive gifts. This aspect also brings out extravagance, which causes financial setbacks in the affairs affected by the opposition. Moon Opposite Mars: This aspect produces impulsive emotions and volatile emotional reactions. These people are impatient and quarrelsome. Quick to anger, they can work themselves into violent rages when they are displeased. Many harbor subconscious dislike for the feminine sex in general. Males express this aversion outwardly by treating women as inferiors or chattel; females tend to be pushy and overbearing with other women. These people exercise little restraint in the affairs influenced by the Moon and Mars; they encounter many difficulties as a result of headstrong actions and the antagonism such actions create. Moon Opposite Jupiter: This opposition generates emotional excesses. These people overreact to common occurrences. Religion and morality are of deep emotional concern. They may be rigid and unbending in their beliefs, or they may be too liberal, depending upon the signs and other aspects that influence the opposition. Many of them subconsciously identify with the underdog; they tend to get involved in lost causes and movements that appeal to the emotions, even though these activities may be of little real value in terms of human welfare. The affairs governed by the Moon and Jupiter falter because of the extreme emotionalism these people project into these areas. Moon Opposite Saturn: This aspect indicates rigorous emotional restraint. These people instinctively withdraw from other persons and situations that affect them emotionally. They fail to reach out in the areas influenced by the Moon and Saturn; they lack the imagination to broaden the scope of these activities. Subconscious inhibitions place severe restrictions on the development of Moon/Saturn interests. These people are serious, stem, austere and unemotional. Materialism and worldly ambition are strong motivating factors. Moon Opposite Uranus: This aspect shows a lack of emotional discipline and a subconscious aversion to restrictive ties. The impersonal qualities of Uranus demand emotional freedom in relationships. These people are quick to sever any association in which they find themselves becoming personally involved on an emotional level. The irresponsibility shown by them in the affairs governed by the Moon and Uranus prohibits satisfactory development. These interests reflect sporadic activity, erratic conditions and illtimed changes. The pattern of instability suggested by this aspect is hard to break because it stems from a disruptive and insecure childhood. Moon Opposite Neptune: This aspect obscures reality in the affairs influenced by the Moon and Neptune. These people are very psychic; however, inner promptings are often inaccurate or wrongly interpreted. They should not engage in occult activities for they are extremely vulnerable to negative psychic forces. They are dreamers but most fail to realize their dreams unless other factors in the horoscope provide practical direction. A well-placed Saturn or a favorable Saturn aspect with the Moon or Neptune is helpful. This opposition opens the door to subconscious escapist tendencies. Without easy aspects to either the Moon or Neptune, drugs, alcohol and hallucinations can become a real danger.

The individual must find a suitable release; if one fails to do so, an emotional explosion is inevitable. Dormant tendencies toward corruption, violence and cruelty can erupt without warning. If neither planet forms an easy aspect, look for harmonious aspects to planets that occupy the houses and signs ruled by the Moon and Pluto. The planet that bears an association with the Moon or Pluto through sign, house or aspect and has the largest number of favorable aspects or has the highest standing in the horoscope will provide an acceptable outlet for the Moon/Pluto energy.
