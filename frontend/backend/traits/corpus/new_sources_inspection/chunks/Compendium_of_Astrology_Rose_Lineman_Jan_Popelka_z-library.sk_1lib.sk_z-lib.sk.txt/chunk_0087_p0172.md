### Mercury Conjunctions

Mercury conjunctions energize the mind. A planet with which Mercury forms a conjunction influences the intellectual processes. Mental energy is channeled into the houses and affairs ruled by Mercury and the conjuncted planet according to the modes of expression associated with the sign(s) the two planets occupy. Mercury Conjunct Venus: Mercury and Venusare never separated by more than 76° in the zodiac. The only major zodiacal aspects that occur between these two planets are the conjunction and the sextile. These two planets can also fonn either a parallel or a contraparallel. A Mercury/Venus conjunction gives a pleasing voice that can best be described as melodious, mellow or dulcettoned. People with this aspect are gifted conversationalists. Tactful and persuasive, they express their ideas with grace and chann and are usually amiable and goodnatured. Sociable and friendly, most are popular. Intellectual interests gravitate toward the arts and moneymaking activities. However, if the conjunction is afflicted they tend to put on social airs. The women are coquettish, and the men foppish. Speech is flattering; mannerisms, artificial. Mercury Conjunct Mars: This aspect indicates a lively, aggressive mind. These people are mentally active and quick-witted. Intellectually competitive, they are always ready to accept the challenge of argument or debate. They come straight to the point without apology or excuse regardless of what anyone else thinks. Most of them possess a satirical sense of humor and are quick to comment on human foibles and shortcomings. If the conjunction is afflicted, these people can be viciously sarcastic; they make malicious comments and caustic remarks. Speech is abrupt, abrasive and quarrelsome. Mercury Conjunct Jupiter: This aspect combines the concrete and abstract thought processes: mental capacity and reasoning ability are broadened. These people are as comfortable with theoretical concepts as they are with factual topics. Avid students, most seek some form of higher education. This conjunction indicates intellectual integrity. If the conjunction is afflicted, these people tend to think of themselves as authorities on almost any subject. They insist on promoting their ideas and many get carried away with the sound of their own voices. Mercury Conjunct Saturn: In this conjunction Saturn disciplines the mind and produces strong powers of mental concentration. These people are able to block out extraneous thoughts and focus on a particular idea. Mental interests are of a practical nature. These people tend to study subjects that can be applied to career or business pursuits. Although they may not learn quickly, they remember and utilize what they do leam

Mercury Conjunct Uranus: This conjunction is associated with foresight, mental resourcefulness and intellectual genius. It encourages an original trend of thought and describes a skeptical individual who accepts no idea as valid until he or she proves it or reasons it out independently. Intellectually, these people are progressive, inventive and intuitive. Unorthodox thinkers, they are the intellectual forerunners of tomorrow's society. Science, the humanities, astrology and other occult topics are among the many mental interests they enjoy. Provocative conversationalists full of progressive, new and unusual ideas,- they express their thoughts in a unique style that is free of cliches and time-worn phrases. Afflictions to this conjunction produce intellectual obstinacy, sporadic study habits and eccentric ideas. Mercury Conjunct Neptune: This aspect produces a colorful imagination rich in imagery. These people can create word pictures as vivid and beautiful as the concepts they visualize mentally. They tell picturesque tales, tales that may be far removed from truth or reality. Soft-spoken and subtle, they are poets, romantics, dreamers. This aspect confirms clairvoyant ability or literary talent if similar indications are present elsewhere in the horoscope. If the conjunction is afflicted, reasoning is distorted by the inability to distinguish between truth and fallacy. Severe afflictions are characteristic of the habitual liar if other factors support the tendency. Mercury Conjunct Pluto: This conjunction gives deep intellectual insight and broadens the scope of intellectual awareness. The potential for extensive mental development is strengthened. The mind is shrewd, probing, penetrating and powerful. These people understand fundamentals and underlying truths, yet they can also comprehend broad concepts. They are capable of devising large-scale operations that require strategic planning and secrecy. Afflictions to the conjunction create the tendency to use intellectual abilities as a weapon to achieve power.

### Mercury Conjunct North Node Opposite South Node

These aspects denote one who is intellectually perceptive to modem trends. This person embraces and profits from new ideas. Mental development helps one outgrow South Node habit patterns. Mercury Conjunct Part of Fortune: This conjunction indicates that effective planning and the promotion of good ideas enable one to achieve success in the affairs associated with the Part of Fortune.

### Mercury Conjunct Ascendant Opposite Descendant

This combination of aspects describes an intellectually motivated person. The sign(s) in which the conjunction occurs shows the mode of mental expression; the conjunction enhances this expression and enables one to project ideas through speech and/or writing. The individual is inclined to be nervous, restless, fidgity and talkative; these characteristics are emphasized if the Ascendant or Mercury is severely afflicted. These people make frequent telephone calls to friends and acquaintances. Mercury Conjunct MC Opposite IC: This aspect combination indicates that one's ideas center around career and busines interests; much planning goes into the pursuit of worldly ambitions. The ability to communicate effectively with superiors is an asset unless Mercury is heavily afflicted. If Mercury is adversely aspected, the inability to clearly express ideas presents a serious setback to one's ambitions.
