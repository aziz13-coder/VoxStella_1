### Mercury Trines

Mercury trines show those areas that spark intellectual interest according to the signs involved, the houses occupied by the planets in aspect and the houses ruled by these planets. These aspects foster good study habits and enhance one's learning aptitude. One can acquire a thorough understanding of the affairs governed by Mercury and a planet in trine. The individual is able to converse easily about affairs influenced by a Mercury trine. Mercury Trine Mars: This trine produces an astute, alert mind. These people are quick to comprehend the subtleties of the situations they encounter. They display insight and understanding in the conduct of the affairs influenced by the trine. Their speech is forceful and competitive. Witty conversationalists, they have snappy comebacks for almost any comment. Many develop speaking and writing skills of professional quality. This aspect denotes good physical coordination as well as mental agility and most of these people show interest in physical fitness. Mercury Trine Jupiter: This aspect indicates scholastic interest, intelligence, sagacity and wisdom. These people are often the intellectuals in their circles. Concerned with truth and justice, many are the philosophers and teachers of the times. The affairs ruled by Mercury and Jupiter flourish because of the sound judgment these people exercise in related matters. They are broadminded and benevolent, and seldom make spiteful remarks or derogatory comments. Unless contrary factors indicate otherwise, they are optimistic, even-tempered, good-natured individuals who possess a lively sense of humor. Mercury Trine Saturn: This trine improves logic and mental objectivity. Reasoning is structured around well-organized facts. These people are efficient planners; all details are accounted for and put into proper sequence, leaving no loose ends to chance. The affairs affected by this trine easily fall into place as a result of sensible judgment, sound management and practical direction. These people discipline themselves to sustain deep mental concentration for long periods of time. What they learn, they learn well. Serious-minded individuals, they show little interest in frivolous reading material or idle chatter. Mercury Trine Uranus: This aspect denotes mental ingenuity and resourcefulness. Advanced thinkers, these people are not swayed intellectually by convention or popular thought. They reach conclusions that are original and often brilliant. In the treatment of affairs ruled by Mercury and Uranus they apply innovative methods and are quick to come up with alternate courses of action as circumstances change. Intuitive and witty, they express ideas in a manner designed to whet interest and to gain approval. They think and speak quickly and are never at a loss for words. When excited, many speak in a staccatolike fashion.

### Mercury Trine North Node Sextile South Node: These

aspects ensure a clear conception of the social and cultural trends that influence an individual. Ideas are in harmony with prevailing thought. One recognizes and understands South Node tendencies and is thus able to ovecome restrictive attitudes. Mercury Trine Part of Fortune: This trine enables one to successfully utilize intellectual and communicative skills in the areas influenced by the Part of Fortune.

### Mercury Trine Ascendant Sextile Descendant: This

combination of aspects permits clear expression of ideas. Other people understand and appreciate this person's ideas. The ability to communicate with one's spouse, partners and other associates promotes mental understanding and harmony in relationships. Mercury Trine MC Sextile IC: These aspects emphasize the importance of education to one's career. The individual has the ability to leam the skills needed to advance professional and business ambitions. This person communicates on the same level as job superiors and others who hold prominent positions. The home and family life present opportunities for learning and the individual may regard the home as a place of education. Family communication is active and friendly.
