### Mercury Contraparallels

Mercury contraparallels produce misunderstandings and mental tensions. The individual has little understanding of the affairs that are influenced by the signs and houses ruled and occupied by Mercury and a planet in contraparallel. Misconceptions and faulty communications contribute to problems that arise in areas affected by a Mercury contraparallel. These affairs fail to develop fully because the individual is ill-informed about these matters. This person may have an aversion to learning about related topics. Mercury contraparallels formed with the Ascendant and MC are interpreted as afflicted conjunctions. Except for a Mercury/Venus contraparallel, all other Mercury contraparallels are interpreted as slightly less challenging oppositions. Contraparallels that involve opposite signs of the zodiac bear the same influence as corresponding oppositions; other Mercury contraparallels are accorded less weight than oppositions. Mercury Contraparallel Venus: This aspect is not seriously antagonistic though it brings out negative tendencies of both planets and detracts from the quality of expression described by the signs they occupy. People with this aspect talk effusively about affairs governed by Mercury and Venus. They formulate plans that sound good in theory but are imprudent in practice. Unless other factors in the horoscope indicate differently, communicative skills are inadequate, social aplomb lacking. Conversational comments are frequently out of place or ill-timed. These people do not know when to talk or when to listen.

### Minor Mercury Aspects

The minor aspects formed by Mercury provide weak links between the intellect and the drives associated with planets in aspect. A minor Mercury aspect influences intellectual expression and communication in accordance with the nature of the aspect, the aspected planet and the signs and houses involved. These aspects describe mild mental interests, intellectual disinterest, conversational ease or discomfort, or nervous agitation, depending upon the basic significance of the particular aspect formed. Minor Easy Aspects Formed by Mercury: The minor easy aspects that Mercury forms indicate mental agreement and pleasant conversation in connection with the affairs influenced by these aspects. A minor easy aspect softens the harshness of Mercury afflictions; it serves as a partial outlet for nervous tension and mental stress. The individual is intellectually at ease with the activities influenced by the minor easy aspects formed by Mercury. However, these effects are of lesser importance than the major aspects of the horoscope. Minor Hard Aspects Formed by Mercury. A planet that forms a minor hard aspect with Mercury represents a source of mental aggravation. With the semi-square, irritating conversational habits manifest; these mannerisms are described by the nature of the aspected planet and the signs and houses involved. The sesquiquadrate indicates disagreeable mental attitudes, which are displayed in the affairs affected by the aspect. A Mercury quincunx implies nervous strain induced by the planet in aspect and intellectual disinterest in the affairs influenced by the quincunx. A Mercury quincunx calls for one to adjust negative mental attitudes that hinder mental development and interfere with the progress of interests that are influenced by Mercury and the planet in aspect.

### Venus Aspects

Venus aspects deal with social behavior, companionship, affection, romance and sexual love. They influence one's intimate relationships; they show the kind of people one attracts and how one relates to others on a personal basis. One's aesthetic tastes, artistic inclinations and material preferences are also described by these aspects. The signs in which Venus and a planet in aspect are placed and the houses the two planets occupy and rule indicate how and in what areas of life one expresses love, beauty and harmony. In addition, the stressful aspects indicate difficulties that interfere with social interaction, partnerships, marriage, financial development, and one's capacity to express affection and attract love.
