### Venus Conjunctions

A planet that forms a conjunction with Venus wields strong influence on one's social behavior, romantic inclinations, aesthetic expression and artistic sensitivity. The sign(s) in which a Venus conjunction occurs and the houses occupied and ruled by Venus and the conjuncted planet describe activities one enjoys, the possessions one desires, the people one attracts and the attachments one forms. Afflictions to the conjunction detract from the beauty and harmony that is associated with Venus. Venus Conjunct Mars: This conjunction emphasizes the sexual nature; it unites the receptive and aggressive sexual drives. These people are acutely aware of their sexuality. They are passionate and amorous, highly attractive and attracted to the opposite sex. Sexual expression depends upon the signs involved and aspects made to the conjunction. If the conjunction is afflicted, preoccupation with sex and promiscuity are possible. If other factors agree and the conjunction is heavily afflicted, prostitution or procuring may be indicated. In extreme cases this conjunction may indicate involvement in violent crimes of sexual assault or rape. The negative tendencies of an afflicted conjunction are more pronounced if the conjunction occurs in Taurus or Scorpio. This conjunction also promotes extravagant spending of personal monies and impractical handling of jointly-owned resources. These people are socially aggressive in the affairs influenced by the conjunction and in the activities governed by planets that aspect the conjunction. Venus Conjunct Jupiter: This aspect is associated with personal happiness and some good fortune. These people are gracious, generous and extremely well liked. They do not exercise much restraint in the area of entertainment and social pleasures. They donate to philanthropic and religious causes. Artistic interests reflect exceptionally fine aesthetic taste. Considerations of beauty, pleasure and physical comfort underlie materialistic values. These people like elegant things; they do not hesitate to spend money to obtain the possessions they desire. Most are fortunate in love and lucky in money matters. The affairs ruled by Venus and Jupiter provide enjoyment as well as ample material benefits. If the conjunction is afflicted, laziness, moral weakness and extravagance are indicated. This aspect strengthens marriage, although it also produces the tendency to marry for reasons of financial status or social prestige. If artistic talent is present in the horoscope and the conjunction is well aspected, these people are able to give concrete expression to artistic leanings; they create objects of lasting beauty and value. They manage money prudently. Miserliness, romantic disappointments, frigidity and/or impotence are indicated if the conjunction is afflicted and other factors concur. Venus Conjunct Uranus: This aspect indicates social and romantic behavior that is unconventional and changeable. These people experiment with love; they form spur-of-the-moment attachments and dissolve relationships just as suddenly. Friendly and group oriented, they pursue social interests that offer thrills and excitement. Most prefer modernistic art forms. The signs, houses and affairs influenced by the conjunction reflect changing conditions and unexpected circumstances; however, unless the conjunction is afflicted, changes bring pleasure and financial benefits. These people earn and spend money erratically. An afflicted conjunction indicates irresponsibility with money and love. These people tend to resist partnership ties and many rebel against the institution of marriage. Venus Conjunct Neptune: This conjunction denotes ethereal beauty and delicate charm. These people possess an elusive spiritual quality that is impossible to define. They are more romantic than sensual in love and tend to idealize romantic and marriage partners. Mystical art, music and the ballet hold aesthetic appeal. Because of their gentleness and sensitivity, these people are well liked. Their friends are usually refined and sympathetic. They do not view the affairs ruled by Venus and Neptune from a practical standpoint, but these affairs can prosper if the conjunction is unafflicted. If the conjunction is afflicted, these people may be forced to make heartrending sacrifices in love and in connection with the interests influenced by the conjunction and the aspecting planets. An afflicted conjunction indicates disappointment and disillusionment in romance and marriage; these people frequently get involved with persons who are unreliable or deceitful. Venus Conjunct Pluto: This aspect generates enormous sexual magnetism. The emphasis of this conjunction depends upon the nature of aspects to the conjunction, the signs, other planets and all astrological factors that influence Venus and Pluto. These people are desirable to the opposite sex, not so much because of physical beauty or personal appeal, but because of the tremendous physical magnetism they possess. They are intensely aware of the power of sex. They dominate the social scene and the people with whom they are involved. This conjunction produces lusty women and virile men. If the conjunction is unafflicted and well placed by sign and house, the evolvement associated with Pluto can be advanced through the correct stewardship of financial resources and the transforming of base physical desires to the highest form of spiritual love. If the conjunction is afflicted and other factors agree, women tend to exploit the male sexual appetites for profit; men regard women as objects of sexual gratification. A heavily afflicted Venus/Pluto conjunction may be associated with sadism, debauchery and sexual orgies.

### Venus Conjunct North Node Opposite South Node

These aspects indicate that the affairs influenced by Venus and the North Node are benefited by one's social conduct and friendly behavior. These affairs can be furthered through social contacts. By developing socially-acceptable behavior patterns, the individual can master undesirable habits denoted by the South Node and grow in the areas specified by the North Node. Venus Conjunct Part of Fortune: This conjunction indicates material rewards and pleasure in connection with the affairs influenced by Venus and the Part of Fortune. These interests benefit extensively from the help of the business or marriage partner if the conjunction is unafflicted. Unless contrary factors in the horoscope indicate otherwise, partnerships are advantageous to the individual.
