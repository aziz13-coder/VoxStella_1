### Venus Sextiles

Venus sextiles denote opportunities that are advantageous socially and financially. Because of Venus' natural rulership of Taurus and the second house, a Venus sextile reveals moneymaking avenues. Activities that offer satisfying aesthetic and artistic expression are also described by the signs and houses influenced by Venus and a planet in sextile. Love and companionship play important roles in the development of the affairs affected by a Venus sextile. These aspects are helpful to marital interests. Venus Sextile Mars: This aspect gives a warm and affectionate love nature; it promotes a harmonious sex life and happiness in marriage. These people relate easily to the opposite sex. They take the initiative in pursuing romance but are not necessarily aggressive; this depends upon the signs and other factors involved. An active social life is indicated. Artistic interests might include sculpture, woodcarving and physical art forms created from metal or produced by the use of metal tools. The affairs ruled by Venus and Mars prosper under the individual's direction and with the help of partners or other associates. Venus Sextile Jupiter: This sextile is a most fortunate aspect. It is one indication of prosperity. If creative talent is present in the horoscope, this aspect confirms artistic ability and contributes to the success and recognition of the artist. Immensely popular, these people are admired by their associates, and this admiration is usually well deserved. They are generous in expressing affection, and in return receive abundant affection from others. They may engage in numerous romances; at least, ample opportunities for romance exist. The affairs governed by these two planets benefit from the protection and abundance promised by Jupiter and from the harmony and balance associated with Venus. Often these people attract affluent companions and form advantageous marriages and partnerships. Venus Sextile Saturn: This sextile strengthens and steadies the affections; it promotes stable relationships and a lasting marriage. These people are not outgoing socially unless strong factors indicate otherwise; they tend to engage in social activities that are linked to business or professional interests. Social frivolities hold little appeal. These people attract persons who are serious and sincere; they limit personal relationships to those that are meaningful. They are thrifty but not stingy, acquisitive but not grasping. The affairs influenced by the sextile present opportunities that lead to the accumulation of money or other valuable assets. If artistic ability is indicated elsewhere in the horoscope, this aspect enables one to develop this ability along constructive lines. Venus Sextile Uranus: This aspect creates a refreshing social outlook and a lively romantic lite. Many of these people fall in love at first sight. Love relationships are not lasting unless other factors in the horoscope produce stability and endurance; however, Uranian romances seldom end on a sour note. These people attract persons who are progressive and stimulating. They enjoy the companionship of numerous acquaintances from all walks of life. They benefit emotionally and financially through social and fraternal organizations. Friends are a major influence in the development of the affairs ruled by Venus and Uranus. These people are quick to realize and utilize the opportunities that arise in these affairs and their interests prosper as a result of timely action. Most of them like contemporary music and modern art. Venus Sextile Neptune. This sextile is associated with creative inspiration and artistic expression. These people derive much enjoyment from music, painting and other art forms and may possess talent in these areas. They are compassionate, companionable, amiable, affectionate and loving. Idealistic and sensitive in romance, they attract lovers who are gentle and refined. The affairs governed by Venus and Neptune provide opportunities for spiritual development as well as those directed toward financial

### Venus Sextile North Node Trine South Node: This

combination of aspects produces opportunities to form harmonious and prosperous alliances. These people are aware of economic trends; they adjust easily to social customs and readily discard sociallyunacceptable attitudes. Marriage, partnerships and social involvements aid the individual's growth in the areas influenced by these aspects. Venus Sextile Part of Fortune: This sextile increases opportunities to develop the affairs influenced by Venus and the Part of Fortune. It indicates success and ample remuneration in connection with related endeavors.
