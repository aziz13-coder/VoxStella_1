### Mars Conjunctions

Mars conjunctions induce incentive and generate activity. Initiative and aggressive action are directed toward the interests influenced by a Mars conjunction. A Mars conjunction funnels physical energy into the affairs described by the signs and houses occupied and ruled by Mars and the conjuncted planet. The productivity of endeavors prompted by a Mars conjunction depends upon the type of aspects made to the conjunction and the nature of the planets that aspect the conjunction. Mars Conjunct Jupiter: This conjunction generates energy, enthusiasm, ambition and ample sex drive. These people are physically active, enterprising. courageous and adventurous. They are go-getters who expect to succeed, and many do. They make worthwhile contributions in the areas governed by Mars and Jupiter. Unless the conjunction is badly afflicted, the affairs influenced by this aspect develop fully and prosperously. Adverse aspects to the conjunction encourage impatience, belligerence, argumentativeness, recklessness and extreme action. Mars Conjunct Saturn: This aspect controls the use of energy and strengthens physical endurance. These people are industrious, willing workers. Action is disciplined; sex drive is partially diverted into workrelated activities. The affairs ruled by Mars and Satum are well organized if the conjunction is unafflicted. Afflictions to the conjunction make it difficult for the individual to handle Satum-induced frustrations. Saturn limits the enthusiasm of Mars and restricts Martian initiative. If the conjunction is afflicted, suppressed anger can build into resentment that may be expressed in vindictive acts. Adverse aspects to the conjunction generate fear of failure, cowardice and dislike for competitive activities. Mars Conjunct Uranus: This aspect increases energy, initiative, courage and daring, but unless the conjunction is well aspected, control and constructive direction of energies are lacking. This aspect is indicative of the daredevil, the thrill-seeker, the extremist, the rebel and the revolutionary. These people crave freedom and excitement. Unafraid in the face of danger, many engage in jobs or sports that are hazardous. If nothing in the horoscope indicates stable relationships, sporadic sexual impulses can lead to

If the conjunction is afflicted, these people are accident-prone and foolhardy. Mars Conjunct Neptune: This conjunction unites physical and spiritual energies. The emphasis of this conjunction depends upon the relative strength of the two planets in the horoscope, the signs and houses involved, and the aspects other planets make to the conjunction. If Mars is stronger than Neptune by sign placement and house position, physical desires override spiritual considerations; if Neptune is the stronger of the two planets, physical energy and sex drive contribute to spiritual development. Mystery and secrecy shroud the affairs governed by Mars and Neptune. Physical lethargy and muscle weakness are indicated if the conjunction occurs in a weak sign or debilitating factors affect either planet. If the conjunction is afflicted, these people can be the victims or perpetrators of fraud, deceit and subterfuge as described by the afflicting planets. Mars Conjunct Pluto: This aspect generates driving ambition and almost superhuman strength. Even if the conjunction is placed in a weak sign, physical energy is greatly reinforced by this aspect. These people are fearless, bold and powerful. By governing the affairs ruled by Mars and Pluto with an iron hand, they exert tremendous influence in the areas activated by the conjunction. Magnetic physical attraction and strong sexual energies are indicated by this aspect. If the conjunction is afflicted, the tendency to use physical force or personal influence as a means of intimidating others is evident. Afflictions can indicate uncontrollable passions, violence, cruelty, immorality and/or criminal inclinations, if other factors in the horoscope support such findings.

### Mars Conjunct North Node Opposite South Node

These aspects induce action that is timely and acceptable in light of prevailing trends. The tension generated by the opposition challenges the individual to deal with the limitations described by the South Node, and unless Mars is heavily afflicted, this person has the incentive and desire necessary to overcome these restrictions. Mars Conjunct Part of Fortune: This conjunction signifies that much energy is directed into the affairs influenced by the Part of Fortune. The interests governed by Mars benefit from the advantages associated with the Part of Fortune.

### Mars Conjunct Ascendant Opposite Descendant; This

combination of aspects energizes the personality. The individual is impulsive, impetuous, aggressive, competitive, bold and quick-tempered within the context of sign expression. Physical strength, motivation and leadership are indicated. If Mars is afflicted, anger, impatience, recklessness and boisterousness are produced; the individual is accident-prone and subject to high fevers. Mars Conjunct MC Opposite IC: This aspect combination focuses the energy and passion of Mars on worldly ambition and career achievements. Much action in professional and business affairs is guaranteed, although these aspects do not ensure success unless Mars is well aspected. Good aspects from Jupiter, Satum or the planetary ruler of the sign on the cusp of the tenth house are especially helpful. Unless Mars is heavily afflicted, these people are not easily discouraged by career setbacks. They are quick to act in all matters that pertain to professional interests.
