### Mars Sextiles

Mars sextiles promote positive expression of the dynamism, courage, forcefulness and initiative that is characteristic of Mars. They support constructive development of the affairs governed by Mars and the planets in aspect and point out areas of potential achievement according to the signs and houses involved. These aspects help alleviate adverse effects of difficulties and obstacles imposed by afflictions to Mars; they enable the individual to handle Marsrelated frustrations in a positive manner. Mars Sextile Jupiter: This aspect denotes honesty, generosity, and respect for the laws and ethics of society. Optimistic, energetic and ambitious, these people show enthusiasm for their work. Most of them are athletically inclined; many engage in outdoor sports of some sort. This aspect increases stamina and muscular strength and indicates vigorous but controlled sex drive. Mars Se.xtile Saturn: This sextile curbs the recklessness of Mars; valor replaces audacity; self-discipline restrains brashness. Initiative, ambition and endurance are strengthened. Opportunities to advance the affairs ruled by Mars and Saturn, though not necessarily abundant, are worthwhile. Unless strong contrary factors are present in the horoscope, these people are achievers because they make efficient use of their abilities and are not easily discouraged by life's hardships. The ability to act quickly and effectively in emergency situations is evident. Sexual expression is somewhat subdued. Mars Sextile Uranus: This aspect brings out daring, fearlessness and adventurousness. These people are brave, courageous and individualistic, but they are not foolhardy. They possess amazing foresight about the affairs influenced by the sextile and are able to act quickly and decisively when opportunities arise. Few are satisfied to rest on their achievements; as soon as one goal is reached, most strive for greater attainments. Because they are sure of themselves and unafraid to venture into unknown areas, many of these people open the gates to new and uncharted fields. They are the experimenters, the explorers and the discoverers. These people sparkle with sexual magnetism. Mars Sextile Neptune: This sextile modifies the overtness and abrasiveness of Mars. These people are forceful but not pushy. They are honest though not especially open. They conduct the affairs governed by Mars and Neptune secretively, and jealously guard the privacy of these interests. Many of these people are psychic. Alert to subtle developments, they make shrewd moves based on intuitive awareness. Often they are interested in meditative spiritual exercises and yoga-type bodybuilding practices. This aspect occurs in the horoscopes of good dancers, championship swimmers and able water skiers. Sexual expression is gentle, loving. Mars Sextile Pluto: This aspect reinforces ambition, leadership, muscular strength and sexual energy. The drive for power is channeled into the affairs influenced by the sextile as shown by the signs and houses occupied and ruled by Mars and Pluto. These people have the potential to reach the highest level of Plutonian evolvement and opportunities to do so. They possess a sense of destiny that gives purpose to life. They are vigorous physically and sexually. Most engage in some form of body-building exercises.

### Mars Sextile North Node Trine South Node: This

combination of aspects indicates action that is in tune with prevailing trends and customs. These people direct their energies toward productive ends. They are able to master the hindering attitudes denoted by the South Node and to pursue opportunities for growth designated by the Mars/North Node sextile. Mars Sextile Part of Fortune: This sextile gives forceful direction to the development of affairs influenced by the aspect. It also indicates those advantages derived through muscular strength and physical well-being.

### Mars Sextile Ascendant Trine Descendant: These

aspects indicate outgoing personal expression and forthright action. In marriage and partnerships these people are forceful, but not exacting. They obtain cooperation because they are direct, energetic and willing to work with others. Mars Sextile MC Trine IC: This aspect combination generates professional opportunities and increases the potential for success. Sharply aware of worldly goals, these people energetically pursue their ambitions. They are active in family affairs as well and take great interest in home-related activities.
