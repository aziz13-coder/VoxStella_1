### Mars Squares

Mars squares create obstacles that are caused by rash or inappropriate actions. They also stir ambition and activity. These squares appear frequently in the horoscopes of successful people; the key to success lies in harnessing the energy of Mars and channeling this energy productively. The areas associated with the signs and houses ruled and occupied by Mars and a planet in square represent sources of frustration, trouble and tension. Favorable aspects to Mars or to a planet that squares Mars indicate the means with which to overcome or deal with difficulties produced by Mars squares. Mars Square Jupiter: This square produces excesses in the activities and interests influenced by the signs and houses ruled and occupied by Mars and Jupiter. It indicates wastefulness, recklessness, foolhardiness,

These people tend to overestimate their potentials in the affairs affected by the square and to underestimate the consequences of extreme actions. They are fearless to the point of foolhardiness. To prove their daring, they risk money and safety and eagerly accept any challenge. Most seem to have energy to bum, but dissipate it through excessive activity. Many suffer hernias, injuries or muscular ailments caused by physical strain. The need for sexual gratification is emphasized, but satisfaction rarely gives inner fulfillment. Mars Square Saturn: In this aspect the frustrating action of Saturn places strong restraints on the ardor and courage of Mars. This square produces fear of danger and dislike for war and the military. If other afflictions to Mars and Saturn are present, cowardice is indicated. Anguish and despair undermine selfconfidence. If these people fail to successfully develop the affairs ruled by Mars and Saturn it is because they yield to defeat when setbacks are encountered. They tend to blame failures on adverse conditions and persons who block their efforts. They stoically endure hardships, but they harbor angry resentment against the conditions or persons whom they hold responsible for their problems. Sex drive is limited. If other factors agree, this aspect is one indication of impotence in a man's horoscope or frigidity in a woman's. Mars Square Uranus: This square causes overstimulation of physical energy and sex drive and produces a state of constant agitation. These people possess voracious appetites for action and excitement. They court danger and seek thrills in the activities influenced by the square. They are impatient, impulsive, willful, reckless and resent interference. This square generates hectic conditions in the affairs governed by Mars and Uranus. These people implement hasty changes without regard for the consequences of their actions. The desire for freedom breeds discontent, rebellion and explosive temper. Patience and caution are much needed. Violence and accidents associated with dangerous sports, high-speed driving, guns, knives, explosives and electrical equipment are possible. Unusual sexual tendencies can develop if other factors in the horoscope suggest sexual aberrations. Mars Square Neptune: This aspect stimulates psychic energy and arouses spiritual interest. Caution should be exercised, however, in occult activity. The signs and houses associated with this square show areas of incompetence and illusion. Imaginative but inappropriate actions produce chaos and confusion in the affairs governed by Mars and Neptune; these interests are influenced by hidden motivations and covert activities. The physical drive of Mars is weakened by the escapist tendencies of Neptune. Alcoholism, drug addiction and sexual perversion are possible if other factors in the horoscope agree. If Neptune is stronger in the horoscope than Mars, escapism may be expressed in the form of spiritual delusion or cultism. Mars Square Pluto: This aspect increases aggressiveness and gives added force to physical and sexual drives. These people tend to intimidate others through the use of physical coercion. Indomitable in the affairs ruled by Mars and Pluto, they refuse to bend under difficult circumstances or to yield to pressures generated by this square. If either of these two planets form additional adverse aspects, violence, brutality or criminal tendencies may exist. This square produces a powerful sex drive and can be an indicator of sexual abuse, debauchery, manipulation and exploitation.

### Mars Square North Node Square South Node: This

aspect combination shows disregard for prevailing customs in the affairs influenced by Mars and the North Node; headstrong actions create difficulties and hardships in these areas. Anger and hostility impede the capacity to overcome faults described by the South Node. Mars Square Part of Fortune: This square denotes difficulties that are created by rash actions. This individual must master hasty impulses, which cause setbacks, in order to obtain the benefits associated with the Part of Fortune.

### Mars Square Ascendant Square Descendant: These

aspects indicate personal and marital problems. Friction exists in partnerships because the individual is belligerent, domineering and uncooperative in accordance with the signs involved. Mars Square MC Square IC: This combination of aspects produces hostility, friction and disputes that affect domestic and professional interests. Anger is directed toward job superiors and family members. Some of these people are agitators who deliberately aggravate officials and provoke domestic quarrels.
