### Jupiter Conjunctions

The influence of a conjunction that occurs between Jupiter and a heavy planet is felt primarily in the house in which the conjunction takes place and in the houses of the horoscope that are ruled by Jupiter and the conjuncted planet according to the sign on the cusp. This influence extends to the affairs governed by planets that aspect the conjunction. The influence of the conjunction in the natural houses of Jupiter (ninth and twelfth) and the natural house(s) of the conjuncted planet is general, for it concerns a large number of horoscopes. The emphasis of a Jupiter conjunction depends upon aspects made to the conjunction by other planets. If the conjunction is favorably aspected, the finer qualities of Jupiter manifest. If the conjunction is afflicted, excesses, extravagances and poor judgment are shown in regard to the affairs described by the signs and houses occupied and ruled by Jupiter, the conjuncted planet and the afflicting planet(s). Jupiter Conjunct Saturn: This aspect occurs about every twenty years. It restrains potential excesses in the affairs influenced by Jupiter and Saturn. It fosters reliability, sincerity, rather narrow ethical standards and realistic values. If it is well aspected, this conjunction is a strong indication of success, for it links the opportunities and prosperity associated with Jupiter to the diligence and persistence attributed to Saturn. If the conjunction is afflicted, Saturn imposes severe limitation on Jupiterian expansiveness and good fortune. With an applying conjunction, hopeful opportunities can turn sour as Saturn imposes hard work, heavy responsibilities and slow progress. A separating conjunction indicates that hard work and dedicated effort will be eventually rewarded; the narrower the orb, the longer it lakes to gain success and the greater the effort required to attain the benefits promised by Jupiter.

218 Compendium of Astrology Jupiter Conjunct Uranus: This conjunction occurs about every fourteen years. It awakens the higher mind to advanced trends of thought, which are unhampered by traditional logic or conventional philosophies. The affairs governed by Jupiter and Uranus are furthered through progressive methods and modem techniques. Religious views are unorthodox and frequently originate in the mind of the individual. Many of these people are fascinated by faraway places, but the extent of travel depends largely upon the sign in which the conjunction is placed and other factors in the horoscope. If the conjunction is afflicted, logic may be distorted and ideas too abstract or far-out to be useful; some of these people twist the law to serve selfish purposes. This conjunction, if favorably aspected, is especially helpful to those who are engaged in advertising, publicity and promotion, for these people are quick to adopt fresh outlooks as situations and conditions change. Jupiter Conjunct Neptune: This aspect occurs approximately every thirteen years. It supports spiritual qualities shown elsewhere in the horoscope. Most of these people are idealistic, philanthropic and deeply religious. Mystical forms of religion appeal to many. These people are intuitive to the conditions associated with the affairs ruled by Jupiter and Neptune; they are particularly sensitive to the interests of the house in which the conjunction takes place. If the conjunction is afflicted, the affairs directly influenced by the aspect fail to develop properly because of impractical treatment based upon unrealistic ideals. This conjunction, if favorably aspected, is a good luck aspect, but it often proves disadvantageous because these people tend to rely too much on luck in situations that require common sense and practical action. Jupiter Conjunct Pluto: This aspect occurs about every twelve years. It supports spiritual regeneration and the highest form of Plutonian evolvement as described by the horoscope. This conjunction is a power aspect, the potential of which is concentrated primarily in the houses ruled and occupied by Jupiter and Pluto. If the conjunction is heavily afflicted, philanthropic qualities are overshadowed by intense Plutonian drives. This aspect can then indicate the power-hungry opportunist if other factors in the horoscope concur.

### Jupiter Conjunct North Node Opposite South Node

These aspects indicate that the individual's philosophies and values conform to contemporary standards. These people attract advantages because they are in tune with current trends. The capacity to overcome South Node limitations is expressed in the house occupied by Jupiter and the North Node. Jupiter Conjunct Part of Fortune: This conjunction increases the benefits associated with Jupiter and the Part of Fortune. The affairs influenced by this aspect develop abundantly and profitably; good fortune can be expected in connection with these interests.

### Jupiter Conjunct Ascendant Opposite Descendant

This aspect combination shows optimism and joviality; it brings out the best personality traits as described by the Ascendant unless Jupiter is severely afflicted. If Jupiter or the Ascendant is badly afflicted, personal expression is exaggerated and superfluous. These aspects can indicate obesity. Jupiter Conjunct MC Opposite IC: This combination of aspects denotes career success, high standing in the community, and a reputation for integrity and sincerity. If Jupiter is afflicted, business ethics may be questionable and principles sacrificed in the interests of professional advancement.
