### Jupiter Trines

Jupiter trines afford good luck and prosperity. The affairs influenced by a Jupiter trine flourish and prosper in the natural course of events. A Jupiter trine safeguards the interests associated with the signs and houses occupied and ruled by Jupiter and the aspected planet. It is also extremely helpful in offsetting the effects of afflictions to Jupiter and the trined planet. Jupiter Trine Saturn: This trine enables one to accept the responsibilities described by Saturn and to cope with difficulties imposed by afflictions to either Jupiter or Saturn. The affairs governed by Jupiter and Saturn develop successfully and produce material benefits. These people are blessed with wisdom, integrity and high community standing. They uphold high principles and observe practical values. Idealism is tempered by logic; philanthropic interest is directed toward utilitarian avenues. This aspect is especially helpful to educators, clergymen, jurists and public servants. Jupiter Trine Uranus: This aspect promotes interest in humanitarian causes; the signs and houses occupied and ruled by Jupiter and Uranus indicate the directions in which humanitarian interest can be most successfully expressed. These people understand human needs in terms of contemporary conditions. They have the capacity to effect progressive changes in education, the law, and the affairs governed by Jupiter and Uranus. This trine shows a desire to induce changes that alter and improve the lot of humankind. Most of these people find travel enjoyable, enlightening and beneficial. This aspect is found in the horoscopes of many astrologers, contemporary philosophers, modem religionists, psychics and philanthropists. Jupiter Trine Neptune: This trine permits constructive expression of the highest spiritual ideals. Religious truths are often revealed under the influence of this aspect, for it encourages contemplation and spiritual development. It is the aspect of the evangelist or gum rather than the spiritual recluse, for the expansive properties of Jupiter dictate that spiritual knowledge be shared. ### Jupiter Trine North Node Sextile South Node: These

aspects show that one's values correspond to those accepted by society. Productive growth patterns are indicated in areas influenced by Jupiter and the North Node. The individual is able to recognize and discard the restrictive habits or attitudes described by the South Node. Jupiter Trine Part of Fortune: This trine is a fortunate aspect. It promises progress in the areas of horoscope influence and assures some measure of success and affluence even if Jupiter and the Part of Fortune are afflicted. If afflictions exist, this trine enables one to surmount difficulties. The individual can constructively utilize conditions associated with the trine whether or not these circumstances are entirely favorable.

### Jupiter Trine Ascendant Sextile Descendant: This

aspect combination brings out positive attributes of the Ascendant. These people express goodwill and thereby attract the goodwill of others. These aspects promote happy marriages and profitable partnerships. Jupiter Trine MC Sextile IC: This combination of aspects generates conditions that are conducive to career and business success. These people are considered honest and trustworthy unless Jupiter is heavily afflicted or other factors in the horoscope detract from their reputations. Opportunities for advancement frequently stem from family connections.
