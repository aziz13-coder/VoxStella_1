### Jupiter Oppositions

Jupiter oppositions generate immoderation in the affairs governed by Jupiter and opposing planets. Positive planetary expression is distorted by overemphasis or magnification of the principles represented by a planet that opposes Jupiter. Jupiter oppositions describe areas in which legal, philosophical and religious differences arise. These aspects influence developments that interfere with higher education, Jupiter Opposite Saturn: This aspect creates conflict between the principle of expansion, represented by Jupiter, and the principle of contraction, characterized by Saturn. The individual is forced to take a hard view of conditions generated by the opposition. If the individual is pessimistically inclined or lacks determination, a defeatist attitude is apt to develop. If courage and persistence a re shown in the horoscope, the individual is better able to maintain a balanced, realistic perspective. This aspect does not preclude success, but progress tends to be gradual. Development of affairs governed by Jupiter and Saturn is restrained by heavy responsibilities, hardships and extreme conservatism. Many of these people are strict conformists who are afraid to break with tradition. This aspect fosters religious bias and prejudicial attitudes. Jupiter Opposite Uranus: This opposition forces awareness of the importance of freedom and magnifies the need for it in both thought and action. These people balk at laws and traditions that restrict individual liberties or impinge upon civil rights. Efforts toward reform are directed at the affairs influenced by the houses ruled and occupied by Jupiter and Uranus. The influence of this aspect depends upon the overall tenor of the horoscope. In a strong horoscope that lends to constructive action, progressive changes, which benefit all of humanity, are indicated. If common sense and direction are lacking, the individual may do little more than arouse religious controversy or incite civil disobedience. This aspect is found in horoscopes of rabble-rousing radicals as well as in horoscopes of great humanitarians. Jupiter Opposite Neptune. This aspect produces religious and spiritual conflicts. These people are spiritually idealistic and may blindly follow religious charlatans. They are inept in conducting the affairs governed by Jupiter and Neptune unless other factors in the horoscope provide practical direction in these matters. ### Jupiter Opposite North Node Conjunct South Node

People in whose horoscopes these aspects appear are given to extravagant habits and excesses that hinder growth and development. These people derive some security from their habits and, therefore, hesitate to break them. They are opposed to generally accepted religious beliefs and social standards. Jupiter Opposite Part of Fortune: This opposition indicates that lack of education can interfere with the development of affairs influenced by Jupiter and the Part of Fortune. Failure to conduct these affairs in an ethical manner can arouse active antagonism. This, in tum, creates conditions that stand in the way of success.

### Jupiter Opposite Ascendant Conjunct Descendant

These aspects produce benefits through marriage, partnerships and public interests. These people gain the cooperation of others. They form associations that are both profitable and socially advantageous. Sometimes the marriage partner supports the individual's college education. Jupiter Opposite MC Conjunct 1C: This combination of aspects denotes a fortunate home life and an advantageous heritage. Family background has a strong influence on religious beliefs, education and social values. These people benefit from parental generosity; consequently, they are generous with their own families.

### Jupiter Parallels

Jupiter parallels influence one's values and beliefs. These aspects, if unafflicted, denote good fortune and success. If Jupiter and a planet in parallel also form a zodiacal aspect, the parallel assumes the nature of the zodiacal aspect and thereby strengthens it. A parallel that occurs between Jupiter and a planet not in zodiacal aspect with Jupiter is treated as a moderately forceful conjunction. The effect of an unaccompanied Jupiter parallel is milder than that of a corresponding conjunction, although the tenor is the same. Unaccompanied Jupiter parallels indicate successful development of affairs that come under the influence of the planets, signs and houses involved; associated interests are safeguarded unless afflictions are present.

### Jupiter Contraparallels

Jupiter contraparallels formed with the Ascendant and MC are interpreted as afflicted conjunctions. Other Jupiter contraparallels act as weak oppositions. Contraparallels do not produce the sharp conflict associated with oppositions. The energies of Jupiter and a planet in contraparallel are disparate; action is discordant. Conditions of disharmony manifest in affairs associated with the signs and houses ruled and occupied by Jupiter and planets in contraparallel. With the exception of a contraparallel formed with Satum, Jupiter contraparallels bring excesses and extravagances into play. The properties of a planet that forms a contraparallel with Jupiter are magnified; these energies lack constructive direction and adequate restraint. The prolific action of Jupiter produces overexpansion in the affairs influenced by a contraparallel. A contraparallel between Jupiter and Satum inhibits expansion; interests governed by these two planets develop slowly when under the influence of a contraparallel. A Jupiter/Satum contraparallel contributes to prejudicial beliefs.
