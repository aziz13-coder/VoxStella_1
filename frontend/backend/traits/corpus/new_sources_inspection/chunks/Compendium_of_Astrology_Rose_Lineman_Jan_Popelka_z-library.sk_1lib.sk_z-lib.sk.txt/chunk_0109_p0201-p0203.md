### Saturn Aspects

Saturn aspects point to areas in the horoscope in which responsibility and discipline manifest. The energies of planets that form easy aspects with Saturn can be channeled constructively into the houses ruled and occupied by Saturn and the aspecting planets, for Saturn lends structure to related endeavors. Adverse Saturn aspects discipline harshly; they generate frustrating conditions that restrict the free flow of planetary energies. Aspects that occur between Saturn and Uranus, Neptune or Pluto have general influence on many people because the same aspect appears in numerous horoscopes. They are interpreted broadly unless one of the planets forming the aspect of interest is of particular importance or strength in the individual's horoscope. The significance of a common Saturn aspect is emphasized mainly in the houses occupied and ruled by Saturn and the aspected planet. The influence exerted in the natural houses of the aspecting planets and in the signs these planets occupy and rule is secondary: it depends upon the strength of other aspects of these planets and the overall tenor of the horoscope.

### Satum Conjunctions

A Saturn conjunction forces serious, mature, responsible action in the affairs governed by Satum and the planet it conjuncts. If the conjunction is favorably aspected, discipline takes the form of practical restraints that foster singleness of purpose, conservative action and systematic procedures in the activities influenced by the conjunction. Hard work and perseverance are rewarded eventually. If the conjunction is afflicted, discipline is restrictive; rigid conditions and setbacks generate discouragement and frequently produce failure. Satum Conjunct Uranus: This conjunction occurs about every ninety-one years. This aspect restrains excessive radicalism and modifies extreme conservatism. Inventiveness is directed toward practical ends. Prudence and foresight combine to produce constructive action. These people carefully consider potential risks before they implement changes in affairs governed by Satum and Uranus. They are not overly cautious but they do not take foolish chances either. If the conjunction is afflicted, development of related interests is erratic. The tendency to push too far ahead at times and lag behind at others generates unstable conditions. The lack of consistent effort hinders progress in the areas of horoscope activation. Satum Conjunct Neptune: This aspect forms approximately every thirty-five years. If unafflicted, this conjunction gives concrete expression to creative and artistic abilities. These people differentiate between the real and unreal and are able to give practical application to their dreams. The combination of inspiration and dedicated effort represented by this aspect fosters remarkable development in the affairs ruled by Satum and Neptune. If the conjunction is seriously afflicted, discouragement, anxiety and apprehension obstruct progress. Some of these people cannot face the harshness of life; they become despondent and withdraw from reality. ### Saturn Conjunct North Node Opposite South Node

These aspects indicate that the individual accepts traditional values and conservative social trends. If this person is bom into an unstructured or extremely avant-garde society, he or she may be a misfit. It is difficult for this individual to overcome biased attitudes and to adopt new social concepts. Saturn Conjunct Part of Fortune: This aspect delays benefits associated with the Part of Fortune, but it does not deny these rewards unless the conjunction is severely afflicted. Successful development of the affairs influenced by the conjunction depends upon disciplined action and constructive endeavor.
