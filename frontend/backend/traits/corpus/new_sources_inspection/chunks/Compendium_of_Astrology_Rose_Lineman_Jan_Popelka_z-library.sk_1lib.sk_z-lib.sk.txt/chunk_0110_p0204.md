### Saturn Sextiles

Saturn sextiles are associated with disciplined, responsible endeavors. These aspects bring stability to the activities of the houses and planets involved. Saturn sextiles show how and in what areas the individual's organizing ability is best applied. A Saturn sextile denotes opportunity for development and steady progress in the affairs influenced by the aspect. Saturn Sextile Uranus: This aspect combines the urge for freedom with the need for discipline. These contrasting qualities do not conflict when Saturn and Uranus form a sextile; rather, the urge for freedom is directed constructively into affairs governed by the two planets. These people respect tradition; however, they are not bound by conventions. This sextile produces opportunities that enable one to be innovative within the framework of traditional concepts. Old ways are updated and adapted to modem needs. Changes induced by this aspect are both practical and timely. Saturn Sextile Neptune: This sextile brings visionary powers down to earth. Saturn, the realist, forces Neptune, the dreamer, to be practical and definitive. Saturn permits no ambiguities or illusions. This aspect does, however, shield from public scrutiny the activities ruled by Saturn and Neptune. These people exert considerable background influence in affairs affected by the sextile. Opportunities that permit constructive, behind-the-scenes action are generated by this sextile. Saturn Sextile Pluto: This aspect disciplines the power of Pluto. It gives opportunities for regeneration that can be developed by structured, responsible action. Through hard work and intense effort, the affairs influenced by this sextile can be transformed and upgraded. These people frequently attain positions of authority in areas activated by the sextile, though many remain anonymous to the public despite the enormous power they wield.

### Saturn Sextile North Node Trine South Node: This

aspect combination shows that the individual respects the traditions of society. Experiences induced by the North Node have a maturing effect. This person has the discipline and restraint required to overcome South Node limitations. Saturn Sextile Part of Fortune: Benefits derived from opportunities generated by this sextile have lasting impact upon the individual. Substantial advantages stem from interests that are influenced by Saturn and the Part of Fortune.

### Saturn Sextile Ascendant Trine Descendant: These

aspects contribute to serious attitudes, personal reserve, reticence and reliability. Unless contradictory factors appear in the horoscope, a lasting marriage and stable partnerships are indicated.

### Saturn Squares

Saturn squares give rise to severe difficulties. These aspects teach patience and perseverance but often produce discouragement and pessimism as well. A Saturn square forces the individual to deal with major obstacles and to work diligently in order to successfully develop affairs governed by Saturn and the squared planet. The way a person responds to a Saturn square depends upon personal characteristics and other factors in the horoscope that affect one's capacity to handle frustations and misfortunes. One person may bear Satum-induced burdens in abject resignation; another may surmount handicaps and rise to success. Saturn demands disciplined action. This planet, when squared, punishes sternly if discipline is lacking; the punishment is failure. Saturn Square Uranus: This square poses a dilemma. The individual is at once faced with the need for discipline and the desire for freedom. Situations arise which demand circumspect and cautious action; yet, this person rebels at restraint. Precipitate action creates severe setbacks in the affairs ruled by Saturn and Uranus; ultraconservative treatment impedes progress. In this square, Saturn chastens Uranian waywardness. The tension and frustration produced by this aspect can cause accidental injury or illness. Reinforced by other afflictions, the aspect may attract violence. Saturn Square Neptune: This square generates fear, anxiety and insecurity. Saturn forces the individual to face reality; Neptune deceives and confuses. Attempts to evade difficulties associated with the affairs governed by Saturn and Neptune create greater problems. Frequently, these people fail to recognize the true nature of the problems they encounter; consequently, remedial action is ineffective. Many of these people are lonely; some turn to drugs or alcohol for solace: others become recluses. This square warns against devious practices, for deceit can bring about one's downfall. Saturn Square Pluto: This aspect sometimes restrains the higher evolutionary principles of Pluto. Affairs ruled by Saturn and Pluto are often plagued with calamities, disasters and other misfortunes that retard progress. This aspect encourages greed and avarice; it is associated with brutal power plays and criminal activity.

### Saturn Square North Node Square South Node: These

aspects promote prejudices that inhibit development in areas influenced by Saturn and the Nodes. These people tend to cling to selfish attitudes described by the South Node. Saturn Square Part of Fortune: This square produces barriers to benefits associated with the Part of Fortune. Misfortune rather than success is indicated.

### Saturn Square Ascendant Square Descendant: This

aspect combination fosters negative attitudes. Remote and cynical, these people are distant with marriage partners and mistrustful of business partners. Saturn Square MC Square IC: This combination of aspects describes reversals that hinder one's career. Rigid parental discipline in childhood and heavy family responsibilities in adult life are also indicated.
