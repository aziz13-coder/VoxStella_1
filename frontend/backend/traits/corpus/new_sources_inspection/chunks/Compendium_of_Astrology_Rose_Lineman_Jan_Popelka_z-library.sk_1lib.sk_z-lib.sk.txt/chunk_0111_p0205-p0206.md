### Saturn Trines

A Saturn trine enables one to structure the affairs governed by Satum and the trined planet around realistic goals. Satum trines are associated with practical developments and concrete rewards. Satum, in itself, does not signify abstract benefits. If spiritual benefits are promised by the planet, signs or houses involved in a Satum trine, substantive results are produced in addition to any abstract blessings that manifest. Satum Trine Uranus: Under the influence of this aspect traditional concepts can be expanded and adapted to modern modes of life. These people utilize contemporary practices in developing the affairs ruled by Satum and Uranus without destroying the essence of associated traditions. This trine gives practical expression to inventive abilities. It brings scientific research into the realm of applied technology. This aspect also provides constructive avenues tor humanitarian expression.

### Satum Trine North Node Sextile South Node: This

combination of aspects enables one to reach out to and benefit from experiential areas activated by the North Node. These aspects produce the responsibility and self-discipline to overcome biased attitudes and the maturity to recognize the value of experience. Satum Trine Part of Fortune: This trine brings lasting benefits through developments linked to Satum and the Part of Fortune. Status in affairs influenced by this aspect is included among the advantages promised by the Part of Fortune.

### Satum Trine Ascendant Sextile Descendant: This

combination of aspects fosters personal reticence and quiet dignity. Partnerships are, for the most part, advantageous. These aspects add to the endurance of marriage, although marital opportunities may not appear until late in life. Satum Trine MC Sextile IC: These aspects are conducive to professional success. Career progress is gradual, for Satum cannot be hurried. With hard work and dedication, one can eventually reach the top. Most of these people uphold the traditions passed on by their parents. Home life is sedate and disciplined.

### Saturn Oppositions

Planets that oppose Satum defy tradition, discipline, conservatism and other Satum qualities. Satum oppositions reveal conflicting attitudes that affect the houses ruled and occupied by Satum and the opposing planets. It is up to the individual to balance these conflicts and to utilize positive traits of Satum and the planets in opposition. Satum Opposite Uranus: This opposition pits conservatism against radicalism, discipline against independence. These people tolerate no interference in the activities governed by Satum and Uranus. They demand complete freedom of action in these matters; yet, their behavior toward others concerned is repressive and dictatorial. The interests influenced by this aspect develop erratically and are subject to sudden breaks, unexpected reversals and misfortunes caused by external crises. Satum Opposite Neptune: This aspect seriously undermines one's sense of reality. Constructive action in areas governed by the opposing planets is weakened by inefficiency, unreliability and lack of organized endeavor. These affairs may be breeding grounds for treachery and scandal. Some of these people are untrustworthy. These people seldom openly confront the conflicts produced by this opposition; instead, they employ subterfuge to get around problems. This aspect generates mistrust, suspicion and remorse. Satum Opposite Pluto: This opposition indicates that diligence and discipline can open the door to Plutonian evolution. The outcome of affairs influenced by this aspect depends upon the individual's capacity for hard work and disciplined effort. Action can be constructive or destructive. This aspect fosters cupidity and ruthless ambition. If criminal tendencies are strong in the horoscope, this aspect can motivate lawless activity.

### Satum Opposite North Node Conjunct South Node

This combination of aspects restricts the capacity to develop adequately in areas influenced by either Node. These people are afraid to reach out to new experiences described by the North Node; they find security in tradition. The South Node points to rigid prejudices and selfish practices, which inhibit growth. Satum Opposite Part of Fortune: This opposition alerts one to the need for disciplined action and controlled endeavor if areas influenced by the Part of Fortune are to develop successfully. Heavy afflictions to Satum or the Part of Fortune increase the severity of handicaps that restrain development in affairs affected by the opposition.

### Saturn Opposite Ascendant Conjunct Descendant

These aspects foster enduring marriages and practical partnerships. Some of these people choose older marriage partners; many marry late in life. These people have mature attitudes toward marriage and partnerships: they assume much responsibility in these relationships. If Saturn or the Descendant is afflicted, partners are often cold and calculating. Partnerships may be burdensome; marriage may lack warmth. Saturn Opposite MC Conjunct IC: This aspect combination denotes strict childhood discipline, burdensome parental obligations and weighty family responsibilities. Enduring family ties are forged out of a strong sense of duty, but family relationships are cold unless other factors in the horoscope provide emotional warmth.
