### Saturn Parallels

Saturn parallels imply responsible action and constructive effort in affairs governed by Saturn and planets in parallel. A parallel that occurs between Saturn and a planet in zodiacal aspect with Saturn reinforces the aspect and is interpreted accordingly. Other Saturn parallels compare with Saturn conjunctions, but parallel influence is milder. If Satum or the planet in parallel is heavily afflicted, the parallel assumes negative characteristics of the corresponding conjunction. Parallels formed between Saturn and Uranus, Neptune or Pluto have little personal significance; the influence of these aspects is general unless other factors support narrower interpretation.

### Satum Contraparallels

Satum contraparallels describe hardship and misfortunes that afflict affairs ruled by Satum and the aspected planet(s). These aspects are restrictive and oppressive. A contraparallel that occurs between Satum and the Ascendant or MC is treated as an afflicted conjunction. Other Saturn contraparallels are interpreted as mild oppositions; however, contraparallels do not produce the awareness associated with oppositions. Contraparallels that occur between Satum and Uranus, Neptune or Pluto are interpreted broadly unless other factors indicate personal significance.

### Minor Satum Aspects

Minor Easy Aspects Formed by Saturn: Minor easy aspects that Saturn forms are of secondary importance. These aspects channel planetary energies into the houses and affairs influenced by Satum and the aspected planets. Although these aspects provide for constructive utilization of planetary energies, resultant achievements are less significant than those produced by the major aspects. Minor easy aspects that occur between Saturn and Uranus, Neptune or Pluto carry little weight in a natal horoscope. Minor Hard Aspects Formed by Satum: Minor hard aspects formed by Saturn describe frustrations and aggravations that impede developments in affairs governed by Saturn and the aspected planets. These aspects do not create severe hardships; they produce friction and stress and give rise to situations that hamper progress. Minor hard aspects that Saturn forms with Uranus, Neptune or Pluto exert little influence in a natal horoscope.

### Uranus Aspects

Uranian aspects produce changes in areas influenced by Uranus and the aspected planets. Favorable Uranian aspects bring about progressive changes that facilitate the advancement of affairs. Adverse Uranian aspects encourage radical action: these aspects foster irretrievable breaks, sudden catastrophes and major crises. Aspects that occur between Uranus and Neptune or Pluto have little personal impact unless one of the planets in aspect occupies the first house, rules the horoscope or disposits the horoscope ruler, the Sun or the Moon. The main emphasis of these aspects is directed toward the houses the aspecting planets occupy and the houses these planets rule (according to the signs on the cusps). The signs these heavy planets occupy and the houses they naturally rule represent common factors that influence many people whose horoscopes contain the same aspect. Due to the eccentric orbits of Uranus and Pluto and the lengthy retrograde periods of the three outer planets, the same Uranus/Neptune or Uranus/Pluto aspect may reform during two or more consecutive years. Partile years are given for each aspect: however, its influence, active while the aspect is in allowable orb, extends over a much longer period of time.

### Uranus Conjunctions

Uranus brings originality and progress to the planets it conjuncts and to the affairs governed by these planets. Uranus conjunctions merge humanitarian drives with the energies of the aspected planets. When unafflicted they produce free and uninhibited planetary expression. Afflicted Uranus conjunctions generate resistance, rebellion and revolt. Uranus Conjunct Neptune (1820, 1993): This aspect, which last occurred in 1820, recurs about every one hundred seventy-one years. This conjunction has a generational effect on psychic and spiritual development. If Uranus or Neptune bears personal influence in the individual horoscope, interest is created in spiritualism, mediumship, astrology, science and related fields. It also encourages scientific research. These people are imaginative and innovative, and employ experimental methods in the affairs activated by the conjunction. Many devise ingenious gadgets and labor-saving devices. If the conjunction is afflicted, inventive ideas are impractical; matters controlled by Uranus and Neptune suffer from internal confusion and external misfortunes. Some of these people follow false prophets or support unworthy humanitarian causes. Uranus Conjunct Pluto (1965/66): This conjunction takes place about every one hundred fifteen years. It is the major force behind revolutionary developments that alter the modes of life of the generation it affects. An era of progressive reform is signaled. If Uranus or Pluto has personal significance in the horoscope change, innovation and reform are major factors in the development of affairs influenced by the conjunction. These people show deep interest in the occult; many are talented occultists. If the conjunction is afflicted, magical powers are often diverted toward base and selfish purposes. Sexual deviations are possible if confirming factors exist in the horoscope.
