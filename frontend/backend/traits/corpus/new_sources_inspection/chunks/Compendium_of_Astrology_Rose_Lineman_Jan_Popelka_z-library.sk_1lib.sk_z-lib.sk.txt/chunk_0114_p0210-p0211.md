### Uranus Trines

Uranus trines create fortunate conditions associated with the signs and houses occupied and ruled by Uranus and trined planets. Unusual circumstances arise that benefit the individual and promote extensive progress in affairs influenced by Uranus trines. Uranus Trine Neptune (1921/22; 1939-1943): This aspect creates a climate that is conducive to psychic and occult development; it heightens general interest in the supernatural and the spiritual side of humankind. Generations bom under this aspect are spiritually aware. Occult movements and those that promote spiritual growth develop more fully when this aspect operates. As a generation, these people are idealistic and humanitarian. The few people in whose horoscopes this aspect is personally significant are spiritually gifted. Many are spiritualists, mystics or psychics. These people are sensitive to and idealistic about the affairs influenced by this trine. Unless Uranus or Neptune is seriously afflicted, these interests develop fully and easily. Uranus Trine Pluto (1921/22): This trine produces major changes and reforms that alter the lives of millions of people. It is associated with constructive utilization of atomic and nuclear power. Space exploration and scientific developments that improve the quality of life are attributed in part to this aspect. If influence is exerted in the individual horoscope, this trine increases interest

### Uranus Trine North Node Sextile South Node: This

aspect combination shows that the individual is receptive to progressive trends and responsive to social needs. Some of these people play major roles in changing prevailing attitudes and in modernizing outdated social concepts. These people are able to rid themselves of negative habit patterns associated with the South Node, which might otherwise hinder progress. Uranus Trine Part of Fortune: This trine fosters fortunate conditions and progressive changes, which enable one to fully develop areas influenced by the aspect. Unexpected rewards and benefits of an unusual nature are associated with this trine.

### Uranus Trine Ascendant Sextile Descendant: These

aspects bring out personal magnetism and original behavior patterns. These people are not crowdfollowers; they are trend-setters. Numerous marital and partnership opportunities manifest. Marriage may occur quite suddenly. Unusual circumstances and extraordinary events favorably influence both individual endeavors and partnership efforts. Uranus Trine MC Sextile 1C: This combination of aspects facilitates career progress. Sudden rise to fame is possible. Professional interests are advanced by innovative methods and modem procedures. Frequent opportunities spring from family connections. Home life is harmonious but irregular; these people do not conform to standard family practices.

### Uranus Oppositions

Uranus oppositions are especially difficult to handle. The opposition is basically a separative aspect; Uranus is disruptive by nature and fosters breaks. Affairs governed by Uranus and a planet in opposition can be so shattered by misfortune and disaster that few pieces remain with which to rebuild. Uranus forces change and does not mend the breaches it provokes. After a Uranian break, the individual is presented with an entirely different set of circumstances than existed before, and there is no way to completely restore former conditions. Conditions may be improved or not depending upon how the individual deals with Uranian experiences. UranusOpposite Neptune (1906-1910): This aspect has influence over natural disasters and other misfortunes similar to those associated with the Uranus/Neptune square; however, opposition-induced calamities are more cataclysmic in nature than those generated by the square. This opposition may be involved in major hurricanes, typhoons and tidal waves of the sort that cause widespread destruction and irreparable damage. This aspect indicates a generation that is beset with social upheaval and confused by erratic social conditions. If Uranus or Neptune is strong in the personal horoscope, misfortunes and breaks in the affairs ruled by the two planets often catch the individual completely off guard. Neptune deludes and deceives; it blinds one to impending disaster. This aspect also attracts disturbing, negative psychic experiences that can be dangerous. Uranus Opposite Pluto (1901/02): This aspect incites riots and mob violence. Tremendous and often destructive changes are forced upon people bom during the period that the opposition is in effect. This aspect creates conditions similar to but more drastic and turbulent than those generated by the Uranus/ Pluto square. If one of these two planets is personally significant in the horoscope, threatening conditions and crushing circumstances can cause violent conflicts and serious breaks in the affairs influenced by the opposition. This aspect represents illegitimate activities and criminal action. If confirming factors are present in the horoscope, it sometimes fosters sexual perversion and abuse.

### Uranus Opposite North Node Conjunct South Node

These aspects are indicative of the extreme nonconformist who automatically resists prevailing social trends and insists on personal liberty and social freedom. Erratic habits and eccentric attitudes hinder growth. Uranus Opposite Part of Fortune: This opposition produces extremes of action, unstable conditions and disastrous disruptions that interfere with the development of affairs influenced by the aspect. Progress is sporadic.

### Uranus Opposite Ascendant Conjunct Descendant: This

combination of aspects indicates exciting marriages and novel partnerships. Many of these people rush into impetuous marriages. If Uranus or the Descendant is afflicted, divorce is likely. These people resist confining ties: they break involvements that restrain freedom and individuality. Partnerships are unstable. Uranus Opposite MC Conjunct IC: This aspect combination indicates an unstable home life that is upset by frequent moves and family rifts. These people rebel against parental authority. As adults, they want to be free to come and go as they please in their own homes. Many home activities center around friends and group interests. If Uranus is afflicted, some of these people grow increasingly eccentric in late years.
