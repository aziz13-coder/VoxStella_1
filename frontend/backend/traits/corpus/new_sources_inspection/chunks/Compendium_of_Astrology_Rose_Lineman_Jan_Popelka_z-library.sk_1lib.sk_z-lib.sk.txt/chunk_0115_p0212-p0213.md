### Uranus Parallels

Parallels that Uranus forms with the inner planets are interpreted as weak conjunctions. The emphasis of these Uranus parallels depends upon aspects that other planets form with Uranus and the paralleled planets. Influence extends to the signs and houses ruled and occupied by Uranus and planets in parallel and the affairs governed by planets that aspect the paralleled planets. Parallels that occur between Uranus and the other outer planets are insignificant in the natal horoscope unless Uranus or the paralleled planet is strong in the horoscope. These parallels, if personally significant, indicate erratic conditions and changing situations in accordance with the nature of the paralleled planets. Unpredictable circumstances affect the stability of affairs governed by Uranus and outer planets in parallel with Uranus. If afflicted, these parallels do not exert the destructive impact associated with some afflicted Uranian conjunctions; they do not imply sexual aberrations. Under affliction, these parallels strengthen adversities indicated by the afflicting planets. A parallel that occurs between Uranus and the North Node, Part of Fortune, Ascendant or MC acts as a mild conjunction.

### Uranus Contraparallels

Contraparallels that are formed between Uranus and the inner planets, the North Node and the Part of Fortune assume the nature of corresponding oppositions, but they carry much less impact than oppositions. Contraparallels that Uranus forms with other outer planets are interpreted as mild oppositions. These aspects have general application to the generation bom during the time the aspects remain in orb. They do not connote the major disasters, extreme violence or sexual deviations that are associated with some Uranian oppositions. A contraparallel that occurs between Uranus and the Ascendant or MC bears the influence of an afflicted conjunction. A Uranus/Ascendant contraparallel influences the personality and first house affairs. These people are magnetic, individualistic, extremist, radical, unpredictable, rebellious and eccentric. Personal interests are unstable. Things suddenly go awry for these people. A Uranus/MC contraparallel adversely affects one's career and other tenth house interests. It is indicative of sudden career or business reversals and frequent job changes.

### Minor Uranus Aspects

Minor Easy Aspects Formed by Uranus. Minor easy aspects that Uranus forms indicate minor innovations and refreshing changes in accordance with the nature of the aspects and the influence of the planets, signs and houses involved. These aspects are of little importance in the natal horoscope. Minor Hard Aspects Formed by Uranus: Minor hard aspects formed by Uranus describe inconsistencies that create minor setbacks and stressful conditions in the affairs influenced by the aspects. These aspects do not generate serious rifts or drastic changes. The influence of minor hard aspects that occur between Uranus and other outer planets is negligible in a natal horoscope.

### Neptune Aspects

Neptune aspects bring intangible forces into play. They influence imagination, inspiration, intuition, psychic receptivity and spiritual development. Favorable Neptune aspects foster sympathy, sensitivity, psychism and spiritual idealism. They support artistic ability and produce imagery and creative inspiration. Adverse Neptune aspects induce illusions, delusions, hallucinations, obscurities and fallacies. An afflicted Neptune beguiles and bewilders and thus creates confusion and chaos in the affairs governed by Neptune and the afflicting planet(s). Neptune afflictions generate superstition, escapism, hypocrisy, treachery and deceit. The influence of a Neptune conjunction depends upon the nature of aspects made to the conjunction. The aspects Neptune forms with other outer planets do not occur often; when they do form, they remain in orb for lengthy time periods. These aspects have a generational effect and do not exert much influence on a particular individual unless Neptune or the aspected planet bears personal significance in the horoscope.

### Neptune Conjunctions

Neptune Conjunct Pluto: This aspect last reached partile in 1891 and will not recur until the twentyfourth century. It denotes an era of spiritual revival and rebirth. The influence of this conjunction can be uplifting or degrading. If the conjunction is well aspected, the highest level of spiritual growth and psychic development is indicated. If the conjunction is afflicted, spiritual degeneration and depravity are possible. If Neptune or Pluto is strong in the horoscope and favorably aspected, this conjunction gives inner strength and spiritual power that reinforces action of the aspecting planets. Mystery and secrecy are associated with affairs governed by Neptune and Pluto. Underhandedness and corruption are indicated if the conjunction is afflicted. This aspect, when afflicted and in combination with other factors that support sexual confusion or lack of sexual identity, is sometimes found in the horoscopes of bisexuals and transvestites.

### Neptune Conjunct North Node Opposite South Node

People in whose horoscopes these aspects appear are intuitive to and receptive to prevailing attitudes. They are easily influenced by the opinions and customs of society and tend to drift with popular trends. Neptune Conjunct Part of Fortune: This conjunction misleads and obscures. The individual may be unaware of or overlook advantageous possibilities associated with the Part of Fortune and therefore fail to properly develop these areas.
