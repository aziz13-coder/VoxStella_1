### Neptune Sextiles

Neptune Sextile Pluto: This sextile, the only major aspect formed between these two planets in the twentieth century (partile 1950-56 and 1976-86), concerns the spiritual development of the generation bom while it is in effect. It represents opportunities that, if utilized by society, can produce the highest level of spiritual development. Under the influence of this aspect, highminded concepts transcend base human desires. If Neptune or Pluto occupies the first house, rules the Ascending sign or disposits the chart ruler, the Sun or the Moon, this sextile has personal significance in the natal horoscope. Activities that are influenced by this aspect provide opportunities for personal spiritual development. These people are able to promote idealistic movements through the affairs governed by Neptune and Pluto. If other factors in the horoscope confirm, this aspect generates interest and some ability in science, metaphysics and the occult.

### Neptune Sextile North Node Trine South Node: These

aspects enable one to sense prevailing trends and to utilize these trends advantageously. If Neptune is unafflicted, this individual possesses uncanny instincts with which to effectively guide the affairs influenced by these aspects. Neptune Sextile Part of Fortune: This sextile enables one to recognize obscure opportunities that might otherwise be overlooked. Intuition is a major factor in the successful development of affairs influenced by Neptune and the Part of Fortune.

### Neptune Sextile Ascendant Trine Descendant: This

combination of aspects generates much personal appeal. These people come across as sensitive, sympathetic, elusive and somewhat mysterious. They attract opportunities that provide personal benefits as well as other advantages described by the signs and houses occupied and ruled by Neptune and planets that favorably aspect Neptune. These people are idealistic about marriage. Many feel psychically or emotionally attuned to their marriage partners. Hidden benefits are associated with marriage and partnerships. Neptune Sextile MC Trine IC: This aspect combination indicates that good career opportunities often arise in mysterious ways. These people benefit from behindthe-scenes connections and family affiliations. They possess unerring instincts about professional and business affairs. Most feel emotionally close to their families. Many are spiritually inspired by their parents or vice versa.

### Neptune Squares

Neptune Square Pluto: This aspect occurred early in the nineteenth century and will re-form in the twentyfirst. It indicates an era of spiritual bankruptcy and social corruption. Psychic and spiritual forces may be used for coercive and degrading purposes. The general tenor of the period during which this aspect operates reflects debauchery, decay and dissolution. If Neptune or Pluto is important in a horoscope that contains this square, the affairs governed by the two planets can show tendencies to crime, corruption, treachery and nefarious plots. If other factors in the horoscope agree, pornographic interest, moral depravity or sexual perversion are possible.

### Neptune Square North Node Square South Node: This

aspect combination creates an idealistic and highly unrealistic concept of prevailing attitudes. Many of these people are drifters. They tend to attract the rootless segment of society. Some are drawn into the drug culture. Neptune Square Part of Fortune: This square generates confusion and subterfuge in affairs influenced by Neptune and the Part of Fortune. Muddled conditions, deceptive circumstances and the individual's ineptness in related matters block progress. Obscure developments tend to undermine benefits associated with the Part of Fortune.

### Neptune Trines

Neptune Trine Pluto: This trine, active in the eighteenth century, will recur late in the twenty-first. It creates a climate that attracts positive occult forces. It indicates a generation that is receptive to positive occult and psychic influences. This generation reflects an inborn understanding of the supernatural. If Neptune or Pluto is important in an individual's horoscope, this aspect gives spiritual insight and instinctive understanding of the unknown. It fosters interest in atomic and nuclear science, metaphysics, and the mysteries of life and death. Conditions influenced by this aspect clear the path to Plutonian evolution.

### Neptune Trine North Node Sextile South Node: This

combination of aspects generates spiritually uplifting conditions. This person has the advantage of being sensitive to and in harmony with prevailing trends and popular attitudes. Neptune Trine Part of Fortune: This trine indicates smooth-flowing developments that contribute to the success promised by the Part of Fortune. These people are intuitively aware of obscure conditions that affect interests influenced by Neptune and the Part of Fortune.

### Neptune Trine Ascendant Sextile Descendant. This

aspect combination brings out beguiling personal qualities. These people are bewitching and fascinating. They attract partnership and marital opportunities that are secret, covert or in some way mysterious. Because people with these aspects are sensitive and sympathetic to the needs of their partners, their marriages are usually harmonious. Neptune Trine MC Sextile IC: These aspects create background conditions that aid career development. These people are intuitive to hidden factors that concern their careers. With their winning ways they gain the support of persons who can help them advance professionally. Opportunities frequently stem from family connections. If contradictory factors are not present in the horoscope, these people form close emotional ties with their families.
