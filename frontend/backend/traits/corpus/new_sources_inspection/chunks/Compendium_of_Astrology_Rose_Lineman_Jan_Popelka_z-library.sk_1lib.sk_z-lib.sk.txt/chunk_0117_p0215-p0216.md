### Neptune Oppositions

Neptune Opposite Pluto: This opposition marks an era of conflict between forces of good and evil. It last occurred in the seventeenth century, and will re-form in the twenty-first. If heavy malefics afflict Neptune and Pluto while they remain in opposition, godlessness, moral decay and corruption take over during the period of affliction. A Jupiter sextile or trine to the opposing planets provides temporary respite from corrupt influences while the Jupiter aspect remains in orb and promotes morality and spiritual growth. If Neptune or Pluto is influential in the natal horoscope, the individual is tormented by moral and spiritual conflicts. Affairs governed by the two planets are subject to duplicity, subterfuge, vice and crime. This opposition can encourage suicidal or homicidal tendencies. If confirming factors exist in the horoscope, sexual perversion is possible.

### Neptune Opposite North Node Conjunct South Node

These aspects give rise to insidious habit patterns that are extremely hard to overcome. These habits are so much a part of the individual that he or she frequently fails to recognize them for what they are. If confirming factors exist, these aspects can contribute to psychological escapism, alcohol or drug abuse. Neptune Opposite Part of Fortune: This opposition brings surreptitious activities into play. The individual may be tricked or cheated out of benefits associated with the Part of Fortune.

### Neptune Opposite Ascendant Conjunct Descendant

This combination of aspects introduces intrigue into marriage and partnerships. These people form secret pacts and covert alliances. Ties with the marriage partner may be karmic in nature. If Neptune is afflicted, infidelity in marriage and fraud in partnerships is possible. These aspects are sometimes found in the horoscopes of bigamists. Neptune Opposite MC Conjunct IC: If Neptune is well aspected, this configuration indicates deep spirituality

### Neptune Parallels

Parallels that occur between Neptune and inner planets, the North Node, Part of Fortune, Ascendant or the MC are interpreted as mildly forceful conjunctions. Other planets that aspect Neptune and planets in parallel with Neptune influence the parallels. Parallels that are favorably aspected assume positive characteristics of corresponding conjunctions. Parallels that are adversely aspected act as afflicted conjunctions. Parallels that Neptune forms with other outer planets have litde influence in a natal horoscope unless Neptune or the planet in parallel is influential in the horoscope.

### Neptune Contraparallels

Neptune contraparallels, except for those formed with the Ascendant and MC, are interpreted as weak oppositions. Contraparallels between Neptune and outer planets are insignificant in a natal horoscope unless Neptune or the aspected planet is strong. A contraparallel that occurs between Neptune and the Ascendant or the MC is treated as an afflicted conjunction.

### Minor Neptune Aspects

Minor Easy Aspects Formed by Neptune: Minor easy aspects formed by Neptune indicate psychic and spiritual interests that manifest in affairs associated with the signs and houses ruled and occupied by Neptune and the planets in aspect. These aspects do not give ability in art or occult sciences, although interest is evident. Neptune formed asemi-sextile with Pluto in 1916. Minor Hard Aspects Formed by Neptune: Minor hard aspects that Neptune forms generate reveries and fantasies. They do not foster drug or alcohol abuse or other harmful forms of escapism that are associated with major hard Neptunian aspects. These minor hard aspects create illusions, confusion and vagueness. A Neptune/Pluto semi-square was active in 1931 and 1932.

### Pluto Aspects

Pluto aspects bring about transformation for better or worse. This process is a gradual one that develops slowly throughout the individual's lifetime. Favorable Pluto aspects induce spiritual, mental, emotional, physical or material regeneration in accordance with the planets, signs and houses involved. Adverse Pluto aspects are associated with degeneration. The emphasis of a Pluto conjunction depends upon the nature of aspects made to the conjunction. A favorably aspected Pluto conjunction elevates Plutonian qualities; an afflicted conjunction emphasizes base characteristics of Pluto. In interpreting Pluto aspects, Pluto's strength in the horoscope and its influence on the individual must be carefully considered. Many people do not seem to react individually to the influence of this planet unless Pluto rules the Ascendant, rules a sign (Scorpio) intercepted within the first house, occupies the first house, or disposits or forms major aspects with the chart ruler, planets co-ruling or occupying the first house, the Sun or the Moon. Aspects that Pluto forms with outer planets are indicative of mass movements and worldwide trends that are part of the history of the generation the Pluto aspect influences. These aspects may affect the individual on a personal level, but if so, Pluto must exert strong personal influence in the natal horoscope. Because Pluto is the slowest moving planet, it cannot act as significator in any aspect it forms with another planet unless the motion of the planet in aspect is slow of course or stationary. The interpretations for aspects that occur between Pluto and other planets are listed under the aspects of the planet that normally acts as significator as all planetary aspects are listed except those formed by the Sun.

### Pluto Conjunctions Pluto Conjunct North Node Opposite South Node: This

combination of aspects enables the individual to penetrate basic causes behind prevailing trends and attitudes. If Pluto is favorably aspected, this person may be able to transform negative conditions that exist in the houses ruled and occupied by Pluto into a better way of life. If Pluto is afflicted, experiences associated with interests Pluto governs can have a degrading effect. Pluto Conjunct Part of Fortune: This conjunction centers Plutonian power in the area in which most successful development is promised by the Part of Fortune. The affairs influenced by this aspect bring enormous rewards in one form or another if the conjunction is favorably aspected. If the conjunction is afflicted, turmoil and destruction can ensue.
