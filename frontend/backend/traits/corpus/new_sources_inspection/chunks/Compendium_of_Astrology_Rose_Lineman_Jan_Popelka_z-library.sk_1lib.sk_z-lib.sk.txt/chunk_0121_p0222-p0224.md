These positions are exact on the Adjusted Calculation Date (ACD), a date different from the birthday. To find the ACD, add the GMT interval (Chapter 12) as calculated for the natal horoscope to the sidereal time listed in a noon ephemeris on the GMT birthdate if the GMT birthtime is a.m. (opposite procedure than when calculating the local sidereal time of birth); leaf forward in the ephemeris to find this sidereal time; the corresponding date is the ACD. For an afternoon birth (GMT), subtract the GMT interval from the ST listed on the birthdate (noon ephermis); leaf back in the ephemeris to find the date which corresponds to the calculated ST. If a zero hour ephemeris is used, the GMT interval is always subtracted from the ST listed on the birthdate, and the ACD always occurs on a date prior to birth. The ACD and birthdate coincide only if the GMT birthtime is the same as the ephemeris reference time (noon or midnight). The ACD is a constant date that applies to any given year. The Ascendant and MC, key points in a horoscope, externalize indications shown in a natal horoscope when aspected by progressed planets. Hard aspects, especially the conjunction, formed by progressed planets to natal angles or by progressed angles to natal planets, describe significant events in the life of an individual. Aspects involving the Ascendant are associated with happenings of a personal nature, inner changes, and events that affect the body, Midheaven events deal with external conditions and activities. A progressed horoscope shows the major influences that operate during a given year. Transits, the movements of planets on a particular day identified by planetary positions listed in the ephemeris for that date, describe astrological influences operating on the day of interest. Hard aspects to natal or progressed planets formed by fast-moving transiting planets frequently trigger or time events indicated in the horoscope. Transiting aspects (hard) to the angles also time events as do conjunctions to cusps of houses that rule affairs activated by progressed aspects. The transiting Moon, which moves about 10 every two hours, frequently times events almost to the minute if the birthtime and horoscope are accurate. If the horoscope is inexact, timing of events is unpredictable. The progressed Moon, when it conjuncts a planet, angle or house cusp of interest, indicates the time of an event to the day or even closer if calculations are correct. Easy progressed aspects describe conditions and circumstances that affect related affairs; hard progressed and transiting aspects are associated with specific events such as marriage, surgery, childbirth or a career change. The progressed Mars/Saturn trine (an easy aspect) in Susan's horoscope described conditions that could be helpful to career advancement during 1968; it did not signify a specific event. However, during the same period, progressed Mars conjuncted her natal MC, an aspect associated with external events. A transiting planet cannot time an event that is not indicated in the horoscope.
