### Composite Charts

A composite chart is one that describes a relationship as an entity. It is erected by plotting the midpoints between like planets and angles of one natal chart with that of the partner(s). It reveals the combined potentials of the persons involved in terms of their association, not in terms of individual needs and contributions. Some astrologers prefer composite techniques, others comparison, but most utilize both methods when applicable to gain a clearer picture of a relationship and the role each member plays. To delineate a composite chart, natal interpretations are adapted to relationship symbolism. Signs and planets associated with the houses give a general view of relationship potentials and areas of primary interest; aspects (natal orbs applicable) point to particulars. For example, a well-aspected Jupiter in the tenth house of a composite chart suggests that members of the association can, working together, accomplish goals of the relationship; in the second house, it suggests that financial benefits can accrue as a result of combined activities. Jupiter afflictions bring out extravagances and excesses associated with the planet. The Sun represents the lifeline of a relationship. Well aspected, it indicates a vital, viable association. Sun afflictions drain and endanger a relationship, although accompanying easy solar aspects reinforce Sun energies and revitalize the association. Composite charts are especially helpful in assessing associations that involve large numbers of people such as corporation boards and organizational committees.

### Relocation Charts

Contemplating a move? Erect a relocation chart to determine the area that is most advantageous in relation to your natal potentials. A relocation chart is calculated for the time and date of birth and longitude and latitude of the contemplated site. It the distance between the new location and the place of birth is small, only negligible differences may exist between the relocation and natal charts. ### Election Charts

Election charts are erected to find the best time to implement specific plans. For example, if one is planning to embark on a business venture, a day within the tentative time frame (on which transiting planets, especially those which bear upon the business, are making favorable aspects) is chosen as the beginning date. The time that corresponds to the Midheaven or Ascendant, which most closely relates to the nature of the business and ties in with relevant houses or planets in the natal chart, is selected as a possible opening or beginning time, and a chart is erected for the tentative time, date and site of the opening. The time and house cusps are adjusted to place planets of significance where their influence will be most beneficial, and malefics in houses that are not directly connected with the business, its finances, customers, etc. The application of election charts is almost unlimited; they are helpful in determining when to get married, travel, seek promotion or apply for a job, sign important papers, purchase or sell property, invest, make changes, or to begin activities of any nature.

### Horary Charts

Horary charts, closely allied to election charts, are designed to answer specific questions of interest. They are constructed according to the date and time the question is first proposed (mentally or verbally) and the location of the querent, the person who asks the question. If the initial time is unknown, the time at which the client first approaches the astrologer with the question (by mail, telephone or in person) is used. Information about lost articles, missing persons, contemplated actions or decisions (Is it to my advantage to accept the job offer? Is the partnership opportunity with Mr. Jones beneficial to me? Is it wise to invest in U.S. Steel shares at the present time?) is contained in horary charts. The first house of a horary chart rules the querent (questioner); the house that is naturally associated with the matter of interest rules the query (the matter inquired about). Study of the planetary rulers of the houses of the querent and the query and the Moon, which describes by its aspects the dynamics of the chart (action or lack of it), reveals the answer to the question; irrelevant elements of the horary chart are disregarded. For example, if one wishes to locate a misplaced sum of personal cash, the sign and house positions and aspects of the planetary rules of the Ascendant (querent) and the second house (query, personal money) and the Moon and the interrelationships that exist among these three planets describe if, where and when the money can be found. Astrological factors that have no bearing on the question are not considered.
