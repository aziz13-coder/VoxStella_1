### Medical Astrology

Medical astrology is a highly specialized field that requires knowledge of the functioning of the human body and psyche as well as relationships of planets and signs to minerals, vitamins, enzymes, bacteria and other disease-producing agents, all parts of the body and body systems. It is limited by law to diagnostic procedures unless the astrologer is also licensed to practice medicine. A skilled astrologer can identify from the natal chart deficiences that cause disease and then suggest medical tests to confirm the diagnosis. Few physicians and psychiatrists are willing to work with an astrologer; those who do find the service invaluable, especially for cases in which causative factors are hidden or disguised. The medical astrologer works from the premise that illness is a result of the misuse or misdirection of planetary energies and that the square, quincunx and opposition describe deficiencies in the human body that may leave one vulnerable to disease. An illness indicated in the birth chart manifests when progressed and/or transiting planets trigger the natal potential. Once inherent lacks are identified, treatment is directed toward cause rather than symptoms. The techniques of medical astrology can also be successfully applied to preventive medicine. Helpful immunization or prophylactic procedures can be instituted to overcome body weaknesses described in the natal chart. Also, some herbalists rely upon an astrological assessment of body needs when recommending natural remedies to combat disease or to build up the body. Modern medical astrology, still in its infancy, demands a sophisticated approach. Studies conducted

### Mundane Astrology

Mundane astrology, unlike natal astrology, deals with the world rather than the individual. Matters of political significance to a particular nation or to the world as a whole are part of this branch as are the weather, revolutions, universal philosophical trends and current events. The technical knowledge and advanced techniques required in this highly specialized craft can befound in literature devoted to thesubject. However, something can be learned about national political and philosophical developments by studying a horoscope based on the "birthdate" of a country and adapting natal symbolism to the nation instead of the individual.

### Astrological Development

Microcosm and Macrocosm Astrological factors influence various phases of individual human development, common generational attributes and conditions that prevail over thousands of years. They describe the psychological emphasis of the particular period in life, generation or astrological era with which they are associated. Although personal growth (psychological, emotional, physical and spiritual) varies with each individual, human development follows a general pattern from birth onward that is described by planets, which govern each period of life.
