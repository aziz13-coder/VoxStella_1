^ ^Arabian Parts Other than the Part of Fortune (Chapter 13), Arabian Parts (or Points) are not much used in modern astrology. The system of combining three points of a horoscope to form a particular "part" regarded as a sensitive point was developed in Arabia during the Middle Ages. Since then, additional "Arabian" Parts have been devised. Certain of the parts show by house placement in the natal chart the house a related planet occupies in a solar chart, a chart whose first house cusp is at the Sun's zodiacal position (Chapter 12). Thus, by using these parts, one can compare solar house emphasis with natal house emphasis without erecting a solar chart. For example, if the Part of Fortune, which is calculated by adding the positions of the Ascendant and Moon and subtracting the Sun's position from the sum, is in the ninth house of a natal chart, the Moon occupies the ninth house in that person's solar chart. Other parts that relate similarly to a solar chart are

### calculated as follows

Part of Commerce (Caduceusl: Ascendant plus Mercury minus the Sun. The Caduceus indicates the solar house of Mercury. Part of Love (Heart): Ascendant plus Venus minus the Sun. The heart indicates the solar house of Venus. Part of Passion (Sword): Ascendant plus Mars minus the Sun. The Sword indicates the solar house of Mars. Part of Increase (Pomegranate): Ascendant plus Jupiter minus the Sun. The Pomegranate indicates the solar house of Jupiter. Part of Fate (Hourglass): Ascendant plus Satum minus the Sun. The Hourglass indicates the solar house of Saturn. It is sometimes called the Part of Responsibility. Part of Catastrophe (Lightning Flash): Ascendant plus Uranus minus the Sun. The Lightning Flash indicates the solar house of Uranus, Part of Treachery (Trident): Ascendant plus Neptune minus the Sun. The trident indicates the solar house of Neptune. Part of Organization (Gavel): Ascendant plus Pluto minus the Sun. The Gavel indicates Pluto's solar house position. The names of the parts are symbolic of their meanings. When aspected natally or by progression, the parts illuminate conditions with which they are associated. For instance, aspects to the Heart (Part of Love) describe conditions that affect love and affectional relationships in context with the rest of the horoscope. Parts that do not relate to the solar chart

Part of Male Children: Ascendant plus Jupiter minus the Moon. Part of Death: Ascendant plus cusp of the eighth house minus the Moon. This part is read in conjunction with the Anareta.
