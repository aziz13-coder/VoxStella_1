Alliance Stress Anchor: Sino-Soviet Split, 1960-1961

This local anchor supports a non-Atlantic, non-British `alliance_stress`
benchmark case.

External source:
- Britannica, "The Sino-Soviet split":
  https://www.britannica.com/topic/20th-century-international-relations-2085155/The-Sino-Soviet-split

Anchor summary:
- Britannica describes the Sino-Soviet split as the shattering of the strict
  bipolarity of the Cold War and says the USSR and China became bitter rivals
  for leadership in the Communist and Third worlds.
- The split was already active in the early 1960s and is suitable as an
  ally-network rupture case rather than a generic diplomacy row.
