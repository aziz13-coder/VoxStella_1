Alliance Stress Anchor: United Arab Republic Breakup, 1961

This local anchor supports a non-Atlantic alliance-break case for
`alliance_stress`.

External sources:
- Britannica, "United Arab Republic":
  https://www.britannica.com/place/United-Arab-Republic
- United Nations, "Syrian Arab Republic":
  https://www.un.org/en/node/122913

Anchor summary:
- Britannica states that the United Arab Republic was the political union of
  Egypt and Syria proclaimed on 1 February 1958.
- The United Nations page states that the UAR continued as a single member and
  that, on 13 October 1961, Syria resumed its separate membership after
  returning to independent status.
- This makes the 1961 secession a clear alliance or union rupture benchmark.
