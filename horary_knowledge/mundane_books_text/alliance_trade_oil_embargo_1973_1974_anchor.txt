Alliance / Trade Anchor: 1973-1974 Oil Embargo

This local note preserves the historical anchor used for the Phase 5
`alliance_stress` and `trade_and_commerce` benchmark expansions.

- U.S. Department of State, Office of the Historian:
  https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1969-76v36/preface
  The volume preface says the 1973-1974 embargo strained the U.S.
  relationship with Saudi Arabia and also strained the Atlantic Alliance,
  as U.S. allies needed American help on energy while distancing themselves
  from U.S. Middle East policy.

This file is a local benchmark note so citation validation can stay file-based.
