Epidemic Wave Anchor: U.S. H1N1 Second Wave, 2009-2010

This local anchor supports post-1919 recurrence and second-wave hardening for
`epidemic_wave_pressure`.

External source:
- CDC MMWR, "Influenza Activity — United States, 2009–10 Season":
  https://www.cdc.gov/mmwr/pdf/wk/mm5929.pdf

Anchor summary:
- CDC states that the second wave of 2009 pandemic influenza A (H1N1) occurred
  in the United States during the 2009-10 season.
- CDC also reports two peaks in positive specimens: an initial June wave and a
  second wave peaking in late October 2009.
