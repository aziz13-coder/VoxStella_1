Epidemic Wave Anchor: Influenza Pandemic Surges, 1957 and 1968

This local anchor supports post-1919 `epidemic_wave_pressure` hardening beyond
the 1918-1919 family.

External source:
- CDC, "Influenza Milestones":
  https://stacks.cdc.gov/view/cdc/52910/cdc_52910_DS1.pdf

Anchor summary:
- CDC's influenza timeline says H2N2 emerged to trigger a pandemic in 1957,
  replacing the 1918 H1N1 pandemic virus.
- The same CDC timeline says H3N2 emerged to trigger a pandemic in 1968.
- These are valid later-pandemic surge windows for the epidemic-wave domain.
