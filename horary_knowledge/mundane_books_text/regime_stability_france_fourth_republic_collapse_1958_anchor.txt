France Fourth Republic collapse 1958 anchor

Source summary:
- Britannica's France / Fourth Republic material says cabinet instability defined the regime and that the April-May 1958 cabinet crisis gave conspirators their chance.
- On May 13, 1958 activists in Algiers seized control of the city with army support.
- On June 1, 1958 the National Assembly voted Charles de Gaulle full powers for six months, which Britannica describes as putting a de facto end to the Fourth Republic.

Benchmark use:
- Treat May 13, 1958 through June 1, 1958 as the core constitutional-crisis window.
- Use this as a regime-stability benchmark because the breakdown concerns cabinet failure, parliamentary incapacity, and collapse of the republican structure rather than one personal succession alone.
