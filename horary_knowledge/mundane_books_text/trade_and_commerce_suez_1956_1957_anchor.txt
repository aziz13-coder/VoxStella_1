Trade Anchor: Suez Canal Closure 1956-1957

This local note preserves the historical anchor used for the Phase 5
`trade_and_commerce` benchmark expansion.

- National Army Museum, Suez Crisis:
  https://www.nam.ac.uk/explore/suez-crisis
  The museum summary says Nasser nationalized the Franco-British Suez Canal
  Company in July 1956, Britain's oil supply was damaged by the canal's
  closure during the crisis, and the canal was reopened to shipping in
  April 1957.
