Trade / Commerce Anchor: GATT 1947 and Kennedy Round 1967

This local anchor supports multilateral non-energy `trade_and_commerce`
hardening.

External source:
- WTO, "Fiftieth Anniversary of the Multilateral Trading System":
  https://www.wto.org/english/thewto_e/minist_e/min96_e/chrono.htm

Anchor summary:
- WTO says the General Agreement on Tariffs and Trade was signed by 23 nations
  on 30 October 1947 in Geneva.
- WTO also says the Kennedy Round Final Act was signed in June 1967 by about
  50 countries representing roughly 75 percent of world trade and used
  across-the-board tariff cutting.
