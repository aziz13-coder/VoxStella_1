# Significations

Status: first pass

## Overview

The current source set is concrete about what mundane charts signify. It does not speak vaguely about “collective energy.” It maps public actors, institutions, and conditions onto houses and planets.

This file records the strongest first-pass mappings from the current corpus.

## House Significations

The clearest operational house framework in the current source set comes from Green and Watters.

### First house

Core meaning:

- the nation and its inhabitants as a whole
- the people
- general national condition, prosperity, and health
- in war charts, the side initiating action or the aggressor

Sources:

- Green volume, pages 22-24
- Watters, pages 71-72 and war-chart material later in the book

### Second house

Core meaning:

- national wealth
- treasury / exchequer / revenue
- taxation
- banks, stock exchange, trade
- wealth of the people

Sources:

- Green volume, pages 22-24
- Watters, pages 71-72

### Third house

Core meaning:

- communications
- internal transit and locomotion
- newspapers, post, telephones, rumors
- elementary education and younger children

Sources:

- Green volume, pages 22-24
- Watters, page 72

### Fourth house

Core meaning:

- land
- homes and real estate
- crops, mines, buildings
- the domestic physical base of the nation

Sources:

- Green volume, pages 22-24 and 27
- Watters, page 72

### Fifth house

Core meaning:

- theatres, amusement, pleasure, spectacle
- children and schools
- speculation and betting
- higher social and ceremonial life
- in Watters, also ambassadors and diplomats from foreign countries

Sources:

- Green volume, pages 23 and 27-28
- Watters, page 72

### Sixth house

Core meaning:

- public health or sickness
- workers, labor, employees
- army, navy, civil service, public services
- food reserves and record-keeping in Watters

Sources:

- Green volume, page 23
- Watters, pages 72-73

### Seventh house

Core meaning:

- foreign affairs
- treaties and alliances
- public enemies
- hostile foreign powers
- courts of law
- public opinion
- in war charts, the enemy or defending side

Sources:

- Green volume, page 23
- Watters, page 73 and war-chart material

### Eighth house

Core meaning:

- public mortality
- deaths and dangerous crises
- death duties and taxation on death
- national debt
- resources of allies or foreign counterparts
- peril through war, epidemics, disasters, famine

Sources:

- Green volume, page 23
- Watters, pages 73-74

### Ninth house

Core meaning:

- shipping and long-distance traffic
- religion and churches
- law, judges, supreme courts
- universities and higher learning
- foreign trade, foreign affairs, international law, world assemblies
- publishing, propaganda, censorship

Sources:

- Green volume, page 24
- Watters, page 74

### Tenth house

Core meaning:

- monarch, king, president, head of state
- government and administration
- authority, national reputation, prestige, credit, power

Sources:

- Green volume, page 24 and 28-29
- Watters, pages 74-75

### Eleventh house

Core meaning:

- parliament
- house of commons and legislative bodies
- legislation
- friends of the nation
- government support base

Sources:

- Green volume, page 24 and 29

### Twelfth house

Core meaning:

- prisons, reformatories, hospitals, asylums
- secret enemies, spies
- hidden disorder and institutional confinement
- the charitable or dependent population in some contexts

Sources:

- Green volume, page 24
- Watters assigns state charity and welfare-like dependency more closely to twelfth-house logic

## Planetary Significations

### Moon

Strongest current collective meaning:

- the people
- public opinion
- mass feeling

Watters is explicit that in mundane charts the Moon rules the people, and therefore also has bearing on public opinion.

Sources:

- Watters, pages 51-52
- Green’s Moon chapter, pages 29-37 in the collected volume

### Sun

Strongest current collective meaning:

- king
- president
- dictator
- head of the nation
- sovereign dignity and prominence

Watters is explicit here. Green also places the monarch and government under strong Sun testimony in many house interpretations.

Sources:

- Watters, pages 51-52
- Green volume, pages 26-29

### Mercury

Strongest current collective meaning:

- communication
- documents
- education
- trade and exchange
- diplomacy and discussions with foreign powers

Sources:

- Watters, page 52 onward
- Green volume, pages 37-45

### Venus

Strongest current collective meaning:

- peace
- treaties
- allies
- courts
- public streets
- museums
- resources in the hands of the people through Taurus

Watters is especially concrete here and also notes that affliction can reverse Venus into civil strife, bad alliances, or corruption.

Sources:

- Watters, pages 44-45
- Green volume, pages 45-50

### Mars

Strongest current collective meaning:

- war
- violent crime
- soldiers in combat
- arsenals
- death and carnage
- coercive and intelligence functions of the state

Sources:

- Watters, pages 45-46
- Green volume, Mars chapter

### Jupiter

Strongest current collective meaning:

- foreign service and foreign relations
- highest courts
- universities
- churches and clergy
- counselors to the ruler
- national honor
- prosperity and victory testimony when well placed

Sources:

- Watters, pages 47-48 and 55-56
- Green volume, pages 62-65

### Saturn

Strongest current collective meaning:

- administration
- law and order
- boundaries
- state officials and municipal officers
- discipline, restraint, prudence, hardship

Green is especially strong on Saturn as administration and formal order. Watters adds boundaries of the nation and institutional power.

Sources:

- Green volume, pages 66-72
- Watters, pages 56-60

### Uranus

Strongest current collective meaning:

- legislation and law-making power
- parliament and assemblies
- reform, revolution, strikes, riots, rebellion
- the common man en masse in Watters

Green gives the clearest parliamentary and legislative framing. Watters broadens Uranus toward mass reform, media, and upheaval.

Sources:

- Green volume, pages 73-75
- Watters, pages 60-63

### Neptune

Strongest current collective meaning:

- diffusion, confusion, hidden processes
- navy
- prisons, secret enemies, espionage, propaganda
- masses or submerged classes in some contexts

This planet needs more careful second-pass synthesis because the current corpus mixes symbolic, institutional, and class-based meanings.

Sources:

- Watters, pages 63-65
- Green volume, Neptune chapter

## Bonatti’s Social Actor Framework

Bonatti’s wording is older, but it adds an important structuring layer:

- significator of the king
- Lord of the Year
- common people
- allies
- nobles
- rustics
- religious and secular men

This matters because Bonatti does not merely list house and planet meanings. He frames mundane judgment around ranked social actors inside an annual revolution.

Source:

- Bonatti, pages 5-9 and 31-32

## Strong Cross-Source Agreements

The current source set is already stable on several major assignments:

- Moon = the people
- Sun = ruler / head of state
- second house = national money / treasury
- seventh house = foreign relations and enemies
- tenth house = ruler / government / authority
- eleventh house = parliament / legislative action
- twelfth house = prisons / hidden enemies / institutional confinement

These are strong enough to be treated as doctrine candidates rather than loose notes.

## Product Notes

- The first mundane runtime should probably begin with house-and-actor mapping rather than sophisticated score weights.
- The most defensible first derived entities are:
  - `people`
  - `ruler_government`
  - `foreign_powers`
  - `treasury_finance`
  - `public_health_labor`
  - `legislature`
  - `hidden_institutions`
- Bonatti’s actor hierarchy suggests a future model should separate actor-signification from event-domain scoring.

## Open Questions

- How should the older king-centered language be normalized for modern nation-state use?
- Should parliament map primarily to Uranus, the eleventh house, or both?
- How much of the traditional assignment of foreign diplomacy belongs in the seventh versus the ninth house in the final runtime design?
