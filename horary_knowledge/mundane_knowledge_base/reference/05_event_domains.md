# Event Domains

Status: first pass

## Overview

The current source set does not treat all mundane topics equally. Some domains are deeply developed and operational. Others are clearly present but need more research before product use.

This file groups the strongest current domains and marks which ones are mature enough to become future model families.

## Strongest Domains In The Current Corpus

### 1. War and armed conflict

This is the strongest and most mature domain in the current source set.

Green gives war as a direct reading topic through Mars, the seventh house, the tenth house, and afflictions to rulers and luminaries.

Examples from Green:

- Mars in the first can produce aggressive national spirit, rumors of war, actual war, riots, fires, and violent death
- Mars in the seventh strains foreign relations and can produce war
- Mars in the tenth produces military questions, warlike activity, and, if the luminaries are seriously afflicted, war itself
- Mars badly configured with Saturn or Uranus produces riots, murders, assassinations, cabinet changes, and serious national trouble

Source:

- Green volume, pages 53-59

Watters gives the clearest operational war-chart doctrine:

- the governing chart of a war is the moment hostilities begin
- the first house rules the aggressor
- the seventh rules the defending nation
- if exact outbreak data is unavailable, national charts or the ruler's chart should be used with transits, eclipses, and solar arc work

She also gives a hard retrograde Mars claim:

- when a nation starts a war under retrograde Mars, the aggressor is defeated

Source:

- Watters, pages 110 and 203-205

Bonatti confirms that war is not marginal but core:

- his revolution material includes chapters on the king's soldiers, adversaries, pacification of war, and what follows from war significators

Source:

- Bonatti, pages 5-9 and 44-49

Product readiness:

- strong enough for a first-class model family

### 2. Government stability and leadership crisis

This domain is also very strong.

Green repeatedly ties mundane judgment to the condition of:

- the monarch
- the government
- Parliament
- prominent public figures

He specifically gives:

- eclipses in the midheaven as possible precursors to death of the monarch
- Mars-Saturn affliction as loss of support, autocratic behavior, riots, and assassination risk
- Mars-Uranus affliction as government disturbance, cabinet change, election defeat, and failure of Bills

Source:

- Green volume, pages 17-19 and 58-59

Watters adds:

- retrograde Mars at accession or inauguration can mark defeated or disgraced administrations and even death in office
- eclipses trigger death of public men and assassination of national leaders
- heavy-planet sign changes can end eras of political power and remove public figures from the scene

Source:

- Watters, pages 103-104, 110, and 112-113

Bonatti gives this domain a formal structure through:

- the significator of the king
- the Lord of the Year
- the king's allies
- the king's soldiers
- the condition of nobles and populace

Source:

- Bonatti, pages 40-45

Product readiness:

- strong enough for a first-class model family

### 3. Civil unrest, crime, and internal disorder

This is another strong domain, though more scattered across chapters.

Green repeatedly links afflicted Mars and related configurations to:

- riots
- strikes
- murders
- conspiracies
- lawlessness
- disorder in Parliament
- public excitement and turbulent meetings

Source:

- Green volume, pages 54-59

Watters supports this through eclipse and Uranian doctrine:

- eclipses can correlate with riots and sudden public disorder
- Uranus in mundane work is tied to reforms, mass movements, elections, revolutions, and social experimentation

Source:

- Watters, pages 40-42 and 103-104

Product readiness:

- strong enough for a first-class model family

## Strong But Slightly Broader Domains

### 4. Finance, revenue, and economic stress

This domain is strong, though it is distributed across second-house, Venus, Jupiter, and Saturn material rather than given as a single chapter.

Green is explicit about:

- revenue
- exchequer
- taxation
- trade and commerce
- banks
- stocks and shares
- expenditure, failures, and depreciation

Source:

- Green volume, pages 22-24, 48-52, and 60-61

Watters reinforces this:

- Venus through Taurus governs banks, markets, and resources in the hands of the people
- eclipses can coincide with sudden economic change
- heavy-planet sign changes can end long economic conditions

Source:

- Watters, pages 44-45, 103-104, and 112-113

Product readiness:

- strong enough for a first-class model family

### 5. Diplomacy, treaties, and foreign affairs

This domain is well supported.

Green gives strong diplomacy language through:

- seventh-house foreign affairs
- Venus preserving peace and friendly relations
- Jupiter supporting foreign service and honorable relations
- Mars in the seventh straining relations and producing warlike talk or actual war

Source:

- Green volume, pages 23-24, 49-50, and 55-56

Watters adds:

- Venus as ruler of Libra signifies peace, treaties, allies, and courts
- retrograde Mars can void treaties and damage foreign policy

Source:

- Watters, pages 44-45 and 110

Product readiness:

- strong enough for a first-class model family

### 6. Public health, epidemics, and mortality

This domain is well represented, though not as unified as war.

Green includes:

- health of the nation
- hospitals and charities
- infectious disease
- epidemics
- sickness among workers or the population
- mortality linked to afflictions and house position

Source:

- Green volume, pages 49-61

Watters adds:

- Venus badly afflicted can signify plagues or epidemics
- Mars signifies infectious disease, fevers, and violent mortality
- the eighth house covers public mortality and peril from epidemics, famine, and disaster

Source:

- Watters, pages 45-48 and 73-74

Product readiness:

- strong enough for a model family, but likely after war, government, civil unrest, finance, and diplomacy

## Present But More Research-Gated Domains

### 7. Weather, crops, and earthquakes

This domain is clearly real in the source set, but it needs more careful second-pass study before scoring.

Bonatti's Treatise 10 is entirely devoted to:

- heavy rains
- winds
- clouds
- mutation of the air
- monthly and seasonal rain judgment

Source:

- Bonatti, pages 11-12 and 238-243

Green also supports weather and earthquake reading:

- crops, drought, rain, storms, and weather effects through fourth-house and planetary conditions
- earthquakes under certain afflicted conditions and in earthquake-prone regions
- a full eclipse and earthquake chapter structure in the collected volume

Source:

- Green volume, pages 45, 61, and contents pages 4-5

Product readiness:

- research-gated for now

### 8. Religion, law, ideology, and public morality

This domain is present across Jupiter, Mercury, Mars, and house material.

The sources repeatedly speak about:

- churches and clergy
- judges and courts
- universities
- law and political ideology
- moral order and public philosophy

This domain is probably important, but it may fit better as a supporting layer inside government, diplomacy, and public-mood models rather than as a standalone first model family.

Sources:

- Green volume, pages 37-61
- Watters, pages 47-48 and 74

Product readiness:

- medium, likely secondary

## Product Notes

- The strongest first product surface is not one generic mundane score. It is a set of domain lenses.
- War and government material are the most operationally explicit in the current sources.
- Weather should remain in the knowledge base and benchmark layer before it becomes a public-facing score family.

## Open Questions

- Should diplomacy and war be separate models or opposite poles in one geopolitical model family?
- Is public health strong enough for v1, or should it remain secondary until the source summaries are more complete?
- How much of Bonatti's weather doctrine can be normalized without flattening it into vague modern language?
