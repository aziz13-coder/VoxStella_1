# Alliance Stress Anchor: Sino-Soviet Split, 1960-1961

- Source: Britannica, "The Sino-Soviet split"
- URL: https://www.britannica.com/topic/20th-century-international-relations-2085155/The-Sino-Soviet-split

## Why this anchor is used

- It gives a clean non-Atlantic, non-British allied-bloc rupture.
- It supports treating the early-1960s break between China and the Soviet Union
  as alliance-network collapse rather than only broad foreign-policy drift.

## Historical note

- Britannica says the Sino-Soviet split shattered the strict bipolarity of the
  Cold War and turned the USSR and China into bitter rivals for influence in
  the Communist and Third worlds.
