## Why this anchor is used

- It gives a non-Atlantic alliance / union fracture outside the Britain-France
  and Atlantic cases already in the corpus.
- It supports alliance stress as a separate runtime lens for secession and bloc
  rupture.

## Historical note

- The UAR united Egypt and Syria in 1958.
- Syria resumed separate UN membership on 13 October 1961 after seceding from
  the union.
