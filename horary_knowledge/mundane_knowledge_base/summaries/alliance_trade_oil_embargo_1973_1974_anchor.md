# 1973-1974 Oil Embargo Anchor

Status: benchmark anchor

This summary records the external historical anchor used for the Phase 5
`alliance_stress` and `trade_and_commerce` expansions.

## Event Basis

- The U.S. Department of State's historical volume preface treats the
  1973-1974 oil embargo as both an energy-commercial shock and an alliance
  strain.
- It explicitly says the embargo strained the U.S.-Saudi relationship and
  also strained the Atlantic Alliance as U.S. allies sought energy security
  while distancing themselves from U.S. Middle East policy.

Source:

- [U.S. Department of State, Office of the Historian](https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1969-76v36/preface)

## Benchmark Use

The benchmark treats `1973-10-17` through `1974-03-18` as the active embargo
window for both alliance-network strain and trade/commercial disruption.
