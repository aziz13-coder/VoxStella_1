# Bonatti Summary

Status: first pass

## Role In The Corpus

Bonatti is the strongest current traditional-source anchor. He is less useful for modern examples and more useful for formal judgment structure, conjunction cycles, revolutions, locality extent, and weather doctrine.

## Main Contributions

### 1. He provides the deepest formal structure

Bonatti gives the most explicit classical framework for:

- conjunctions
- revolutions
- Lord of the Year logic
- rulers, soldiers, nobles, clerics, rustics, and populace
- what follows from war, pacification, and adversaries

Primary anchors:

- pages 35-49

### 2. He makes locality more granular than a capital-only point

Bonatti is the strongest current source for the claim that place is not just a city point but includes:

- region
- clime
- horizon
- mountains, valleys, lakes, marshes, and other local terrain

Primary anchors:

- pages 48-49

### 3. He gives the clearest multi-layer timing logic for revolutions

Bonatti allows an annual revolution to be read:

- for the whole year
- by half-year
- by quarter

depending on the structure of the testimony.

Primary anchors:

- pages 35-36

### 4. He strongly supports weather as a real mundane domain

Treatise 10 makes it clear that weather is not marginal in the tradition. The text contains detailed judgment on:

- heavy rains
- winds
- clouds
- mutation of the air
- monthly and seasonal atmospheric conditions

Primary anchors:

- pages 11-12
- pages 238-243

## High-Confidence Doctrine From This Source

- Mundane judgment should be layered, not flat.
- Revolutions can be partitioned into shorter operative windows.
- The extent of a signification depends on region and local geography.
- War, soldiers, enemies, and peace are core parts of mundane doctrine.
- Weather belongs in the corpus, even if it remains research-gated in product.

## Cautions

- Actor language is medieval and must not be mapped to modern politics casually.
- Bonatti is structurally rich but not self-sufficient for modern state examples.
- Weather doctrine is real, but still too under-correlated in the current corpus to expose as a public score family.

## Product Use

Use Bonatti when deciding:

- actor normalization strategy
- revolution timing layers
- regional and locality extent
- whether a doctrine is part of the formal tradition or just later anecdote

Do not use him alone to settle:

- modern national-chart policy
- public institutions like Parliament or cabinet structure
- twentieth-century political examples
