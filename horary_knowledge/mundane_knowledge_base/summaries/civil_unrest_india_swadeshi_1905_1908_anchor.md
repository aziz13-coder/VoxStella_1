# India Swadeshi Unrest 1905-1908 Anchor

## Historical frame

- The Swadeshi Movement was launched in British India on `1905-08-07`.
- It was triggered by the 1905 partition of Bengal and spread through protest, boycott, volunteer networks, and organized agitation.
- For benchmarking, the active unrest window is `1905-08-07` through `1908-12-31`.

## Benchmark use

- domain: `civil_unrest`
- benchmark shape: reform agitation, protest mobilization, boycott pressure, and strike-like public unrest
- use as the first non-British hardening case for the civil-unrest family
