# Epidemic Wave Anchor: U.S. H1N1 Second Wave, 2009-2010

- Source: CDC MMWR, "Influenza Activity — United States, 2009–10 Season"
- URL: https://www.cdc.gov/mmwr/pdf/wk/mm5929.pdf

## Why this anchor is used

- It gives an official modern second-wave benchmark rather than only a generic
  pandemic label.
- It directly supports recurrence logic in the `epidemic_wave_pressure` family.

## Historical note

- CDC describes a second U.S. pandemic H1N1 wave in the 2009-10 season and
  reports an October 2009 peak after the initial June 2009 wave.
