# India COVID-19 Second Wave 2021 Anchor

Status: benchmark anchor

This summary records the external historical anchor used for the Phase 5
`epidemic_wave_pressure` expansion.

## Event Basis

- WHO's 11 May 2021 update said the South-East Asia Region had been rising
  for nine weeks and remained at the highest global case and death levels.
- WHO's regional report tied the India surge to the rapid rise of Delta and
  to urgent control efforts during the second-wave mortality pressure.

Sources:

- [WHO Weekly epidemiological update, 11 May 2021](https://www.who.int/publications/m/item/weekly-epidemiological-update-on-covid-19---11-may-2021)
- [WHO SEARO weekly situation report 25, 2021](https://cdn.who.int/media/docs/default-source/searo/whe/coronavirus19/sear-weekly-reports/searo-weekly-situation-report-25-2021.pdf)

## Benchmark Use

The benchmark treats `2021-04-01` through `2021-05-31` as the India second-wave
surge window and uses it as a non-influenza test for wave recurrence and peak
pressure.
