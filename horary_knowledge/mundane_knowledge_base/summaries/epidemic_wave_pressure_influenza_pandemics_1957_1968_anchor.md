# Epidemic Wave Anchor: Influenza Pandemic Surges, 1957 and 1968

- Source: CDC, "Influenza Milestones"
- URL: https://stacks.cdc.gov/view/cdc/52910/cdc_52910_DS1.pdf

## Why this anchor is used

- It widens epidemic-wave calibration beyond 1918-1919 and COVID-19.
- It provides two later influenza-pandemic surge anchors from one official
  public-health source.

## Historical note

- CDC's timeline records the H2N2 pandemic in 1957 and the H3N2 pandemic in
  1968 as new pandemic-trigger years.
