# Epidemic Wave Anchor: Global Omicron Surge, January 2022

- Source: WHO, "Weekly epidemiological update on COVID-19 - 11 January 2022"
- URL: https://www.who.int/publications/m/item/weekly-epidemiological-update-on-covid-19---11-january-2022

## Why this anchor is used

- It adds a modern global surge case to the epidemic-wave family.
- It prevents the family from remaining overly tied to the 1918 influenza
  timeline plus one India COVID case.

## Historical note

- WHO reported a 55 percent week-over-week increase in global COVID-19 cases
  for 3-9 January 2022, with more than 15 million new weekly cases.
