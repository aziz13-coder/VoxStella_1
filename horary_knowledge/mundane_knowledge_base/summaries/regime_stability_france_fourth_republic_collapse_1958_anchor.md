# France Fourth Republic Collapse 1958 Anchor

## Historical frame

- The Fourth Republic's final constitutional crisis ran from `1958-05-13` through `1958-06-01`.
- The May 13 Algiers rising and the threat of army intervention exposed the regime's inability to maintain parliamentary control.
- The June 1 vote giving de Gaulle full powers marked the de facto end of the Fourth Republic.

## Benchmark use

- domain: `regime_stability`
- benchmark shape: cabinet crisis, constitutional rupture, parliamentary regime failure
- keep distinct from `leadership_transition` because the operative question is collapse of regime structure
