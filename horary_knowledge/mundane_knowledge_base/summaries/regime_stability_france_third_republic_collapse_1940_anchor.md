# France Third Republic Collapse 1940 Anchor

## Historical frame

- The Third Republic ended during the military and political collapse of France in 1940.
- The core institutional-collapse window for benchmarking is `1940-06-14` through `1940-07-10`.
- The regime question is parliamentary and constitutional: collapse of the republican order and replacement by the Vichy state.

## Benchmark use

- domain: `regime_stability`
- benchmark shape: institutional collapse under military defeat
- keep distinct from `leadership_transition`
