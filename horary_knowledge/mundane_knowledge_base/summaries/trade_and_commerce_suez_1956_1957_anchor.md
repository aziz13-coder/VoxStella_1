# Suez Canal Closure 1956-1957 Anchor

Status: benchmark anchor

This summary records the external historical anchor used for the Phase 5
`trade_and_commerce` expansion.

## Event Basis

- Egypt nationalized the Franco-British Suez Canal Company in July 1956.
- During the crisis, Britain's oil supply was damaged by the canal's closure.
- The canal reopened to shipping in April 1957.

Source:

- [National Army Museum, Suez Crisis](https://www.nam.ac.uk/explore/suez-crisis)

## Benchmark Use

The benchmark treats `1956-07-26` through `1957-04-10` as a shipping and
commercial-disruption window, not as a pure war-outbreak event.
