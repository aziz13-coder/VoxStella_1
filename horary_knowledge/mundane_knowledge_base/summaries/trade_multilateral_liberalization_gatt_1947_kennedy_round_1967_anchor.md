# Trade / Commerce Anchor: GATT 1947 and Kennedy Round 1967

- Source: WTO, "Fiftieth Anniversary of the Multilateral Trading System"
- URL: https://www.wto.org/english/thewto_e/minist_e/min96_e/chrono.htm

## Why this anchor is used

- It broadens `trade_and_commerce` beyond embargo and canal-disruption cases.
- It gives multilateral tariff-reduction and rules-based trade cases rather
  than only crisis or restriction cases.

## Historical note

- GATT was signed by 23 nations on 30 October 1947.
- The Kennedy Round Final Act was signed in June 1967 by roughly 50 countries
  accounting for about 75 percent of world trade.
