## Why this anchor is used

- It adds non-energy, non-shipping trade-policy hardening to the commercial
  domain.
- It also gives one negative tariff shock and one positive liberalization turn
  inside the same historical arc.

## Historical note

- Smoot-Hawley was signed on 17 June 1930 and raised already high import
  duties, helping deepen the contraction in international trade.
- The 1934 Reciprocal Trade Agreements Act marked the U.S. turn toward tariff
  reduction and trade liberalization.
