# U.S.-Iran War 2026 Event Anchor

Status: benchmark anchor

This summary records the event anchor used for the first scanner benchmark on the 2026 U.S.-Iran war.

## Event Basis

- AP reported on April 11, 2026 that the war had begun on February 28, 2026 and that the fighting opened with joint U.S.-Israeli strikes on Iran.
- AP also reported on February 28, 2026 that the opening attack killed Iran's supreme leader and marked a new phase of direct U.S. intervention.

Sources:

- [AP, Apr. 11, 2026](https://apnews.com/article/2be904aee3f804892336730279e054b9)
- [AP, Feb. 28, 2026](https://apnews.com/article/c2f11247d8a66e36929266f2c557a54c)

## Benchmark Use

The benchmark treats `2026-02-28` as the war-opening date and uses an exploratory event time of `03:00` in `Asia/Tehran` for scanner stability.

That time is an engineering anchor for scan reproducibility, not a fully verified outbreak minute.
