# Watters Summary

Status: first pass

## Role In The Corpus

Watters is the bridge source between horary/event judgment and full mundane work. She is currently the sharpest source for eclipse activation, war-event charts, retrograde Mars claims, and practical fallback logic when national or event data is incomplete.

## Main Contributions

### 1. She provides the clearest war-chart doctrine

Watters is currently the strongest source for:

- the war chart beginning at the first hostilities
- the first house signifying the aggressor
- the seventh house signifying the defender
- fallback to national or ruler charts when exact war-event timing is unavailable

Primary anchors:

- pages 203-205

### 2. She gives the clearest eclipse-activation doctrine

Watters explicitly treats later contacts to eclipse degrees and angles as release points for:

- war
- riots
- assassination
- death of public men
- political and economic shocks

Primary anchors:

- pages 103-104

### 3. She gives the strongest explicit retrograde Mars doctrine

This is the sharpest single trigger claim in the current corpus. She links retrograde Mars with:

- broken treaties
- unstable rulers
- disgrace or defeat in office
- aggressors losing wars begun under retrograde Mars

Primary anchor:

- page 110

### 4. She strengthens long-cycle thinking through mutation charts

Watters treats Jupiter-Saturn conjunctions and larger mutation cycles as master charts that describe broad eras and national mood shifts. The current benchmark seeds for long-cycle unrest and finance come largely from this part of her work.

Primary anchors:

- pages 55-60

### 5. She justifies fallback hierarchy under imperfect data

Watters is explicit that:

- an exact event chart outranks more general charts when available
- national charts remain useful when event timing is unavailable
- ruler charts can stand in when national charts are weak or disputed

This is essential for any realistic mundane runtime.

Primary anchors:

- pages 55-56
- pages 203-205

## High-Confidence Doctrine From This Source

- Event charts are first-class in mundane work.
- Eclipse activation is a serious trigger family.
- Retrograde Mars deserves benchmark attention.
- Long-cycle mutation context can shape unrest and finance periods.
- Fallback hierarchy is part of the tradition, not a modern patch.

## Cautions

- Several of the strongest claims are concentrated in this one source.
- Some trigger doctrines are operationally attractive but need historical benchmarking before runtime promotion.
- Watters uses outer planets in mundane/event work more freely than a strict traditional model would.

## Product Use

Use Watters first when deciding:

- war-event handling
- fallback chart hierarchy
- eclipse-trigger benchmark design
- retrograde-trigger benchmark design
- whether an event belongs in a trigger layer rather than a framework layer
