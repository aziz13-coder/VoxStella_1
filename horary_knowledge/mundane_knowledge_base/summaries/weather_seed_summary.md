# Weather Seed Summary

Status date: 2026-04-12

## Purpose

This summary anchors the first narrow seeded weather benchmark branch.

It synthesizes the currently usable local source base without claiming runtime readiness.

## Source Base

### Kris Brandt Riske

Raw file:

- `horary_knowledge/weather_earthquake_books_text/Predicting_Weather_Events_with_Astrology_-_Kris_Brandt_Riske.txt`

Key contributions:

- solar ingresses as the seasonal weather framework
- lunar phases as shorter-term triggers
- horizon and meridian lines for locality and target-zone work
- explicit aspect-to-weather mappings
- worked example sets across floods, hurricanes, drought, wind, snow, and thunderstorms / tornadoes

Notable page zones in the converted text:

- contents and case inventory around pages 3-6
- aspect/weather mappings around pages 31-34
- forecasting framework and locality logic around pages 35-41

### Bonatti

Raw file:

- `horary_knowledge/mundane_books_text/Bonatti_on_Mundane_Astrology_Guido_Bonattis_Book_of_Astronomy_Treatise_4_8.1_10_Conjunctions_Revolutions_Weat_fdd3953fa4.txt`

Key contributions:

- treatise on heavy rains
- rains and mutations of the air
- showers, heavy rains, and winds
- Saturn prohibiting rains
- place-specific and month-specific weather judgment

The treatise contents around pages 11-12 make this operational, not just a passing category mention.

### Watters

Raw file:

- `horary_knowledge/Horary Astrology and the Judgment of Events (Barbara H. Watters) (Z-Library).txt`

Key contributions:

- Uranian logic for violent storms, hurricanes, and tornadoes
- support that natural catastrophes belong inside astrology of events

### Green / Raphael / Carter

Raw file:

- `horary_knowledge/mundane_books_text/Mundane_Astrology_The_Astrology_of_Nations_and_States_H._S._Green_Raphael_C.E.O._Carter_z-library.sk_1lib.sk_z-lib.sk.txt`

Key contributions:

- solar ingresses, new moons, and eclipses as a national prediction framework
- category support for natural collective phenomena such as droughts and earthquakes

## Narrow Branch Decision

The current local corpus is strong enough for a narrow seeded benchmark-first weather branch.

It is not yet strong enough for runtime weather modeling.

- floods
- hurricanes
