# Deep Dive: Jean-Baptiste Morin's Astrologia Gallica Book 21
## The Morinus System of Horoscope Interpretation

---

## Historical Context & Significance

**Author:** Jean-Baptiste Morin (Morinus) (1583-1656)  
**Publication:** Paris, 1661 (posthumous)  
**Historical Importance:** Considered the single most important document on astrological interpretation since Ptolemy's Tetrabiblos

Book 21 represents the culmination of Morin's systematic reformation of astrology, written during the 17th century when the scientific revolution was beginning to eclipse the ancient science. Morin, a professor of mathematics at the College de France and physician, spent his career purging astrology of medieval superstitions and establishing it on rational, logical foundations.

---

## Core Revolutionary Concept: Determinations

### The Central Innovation

Morin's primary breakthrough was the concept of **determinations** - a systematic method for understanding how celestial bodies influence specific areas of life. Before Morin, astrologers relied heavily on loose analogies and traditional "significators" that lacked precision and often contradicted experience.

**The Problem with Traditional Astrology:**
- Ancient astrologers (Roman, Greek, Arabian) had inherited truths through tradition but lacked understanding of underlying principles
- They used universal significators (Sun for father, Moon for mother, Mercury for mind) without specific determination
- This led to vague, imprecise predictions that often failed

**Morin's Solution:**
The celestial bodies only act in accordance with their **specific determinations** - their relationship to the houses of the horoscope through:
1. **Location** in houses
2. **Rulership** of houses
3. **Aspects** to house cusps or rulers
4. **Antiscion** (mirror positions)

---

## The Two Types of Determinations

### 1. Active Determinations (Celestial → Sublunary)

**Definition:** How celestial bodies actively determine effects in the sublunary world

**Components:**

#### A. Essential Determinations
- **Primum Caelum (First Heaven):** The universal cause, concurring in all natural effects
- **Zodiacal Signs:** Each determined by its planetary ruler
- **Planetary Natures:** Intrinsic qualities (Saturn = restriction, Jupiter = expansion, Mars = energy/conflict, etc.)

#### B. Accidental Determinations
- **Celestial State:** Position in signs (domicile, exaltation, exile, fall, peregrine)
- **Aspects between planets:** Conjunction, sextile, square, trine, opposition
- **Motion conditions:** Direct, retrograde, speed
- **Relationship to luminaries:** Oriental/occidental to Sun or Moon

#### C. Local Determinations (Most Important)
- **House Position:** The specific house a planet occupies
- **House Rulership:** Which house(s) a planet rules by sign
- **Aspects to House Cusps:** Connections to house beginnings
- **Aspects to House Rulers:** Connections to planets ruling houses

### 2. Passive Determinations (Sublunary → Celestial)

**Definition:** How the sublunary world receives and specifies celestial influence

**Key Principle:** The houses of the horoscope provide the passive determination that makes universal celestial influence specific and particular to an individual.

## The Revolutionary Hierarchy of Significators

### Traditional (Incorrect) Method:
Astrologers would say:
- Sun = Father (always)
- Moon = Mother (always)
- Mercury = Mind (always)
- Jupiter = Wealth (always)

### Morin's Correct Method:

**Primary Significator Hierarchy for Any House:**

1. **First Priority:** Planet actually located in the house
2. **Second Priority:** Ruler of the sign on the house cusp
3. **Third Priority:** Ruler of that ruler (if applicable)
4. **Fourth Priority:** Planets aspecting the house cusp
5. **Fifth Priority:** Planets aspecting the house ruler
6. **Last Priority:** The sign itself in the house

**Critical Insight:** Universal analogies (Sun=father, Jupiter=wealth) only apply when the planet has a **specific determination** to the relevant house through location, rulership, or aspect. Without this determination, the analogy is meaningless.

## Morin's Practical Method: Step-by-Step

### For Any Life Topic (Marriage, Wealth, Parents, Career, etc.):

#### Step 1: Identify the Relevant House
- 1st House: Self, character, physical body
- 2nd House: Money, possessions, resources
- 3rd House: Siblings, short journeys, communications
- 4th House: Parents, home, foundations, endings
- 5th House: Children, pleasures, creativity
- 6th House: Health, servants, daily work
- 7th House: Marriage, partnerships, open enemies
- 8th House: Death, inheritance, transformation
- 9th House: Religion, philosophy, long journeys
- 10th House: Career, honors, public position
- 11th House: Friends, hopes, benefactors
- 12th House: Hidden enemies, imprisonment, illness

#### Step 2: Examine Planets in That House

**Consider Three Factors:**
1. **Nature of the planet** (benefic/malefic, hot/cold, etc.)
2. **Celestial state** (dignified, debilitated, aspected well/poorly)
3. **Analogical relationship** to house meaning

**Key Evaluation Points:**

**Benefics in Good State + Fortunate Houses:**
- Grant abundant, lasting, real benefits
- Example: Jupiter well-placed in 2nd house → genuine wealth

**Benefics in Poor State + Fortunate Houses:**
- Grant little or nothing, or benefits with difficulties
- Results are scanty, unreliable, short-lived

**Malefics in Poor State + Any House:**
- Prevent good or cause evil
- Example: Saturn in fall in 10th → denial of honors

**Malefics in Good State + Fortunate Houses:**
- Can grant benefits but with imperfections, difficulties, or mixed outcomes
- Example: Saturn exalted in 10th → honors but through difficult means

**Planet Closest to Cusp:**
- Has greatest power due to strength of the cusp
- Must be given prime consideration

#### Step 3: Identify the House Ruler

Find which planet rules the sign on the house cusp, then:
- Note which house that ruler is located in
- Combine the meanings of both houses
- This shows the origin or means of the effects

**Example from the text:**
> If the ruler of the 10th house (career) is in the 11th house (friends), honors come through friends and benefactors.

#### Step 4: Evaluate Aspects

**To the house itself:**
- Benefics aspecting = support the house matters
- Malefics aspecting = challenge or obstruct

**To the house ruler:**
- Shows modification and connection to other life areas

#### Step 5: Synthesize Using the Nine-Fold Method

For each planet involved, consider:
1. Its nature (essential qualities)
2. Its celestial state (universal condition)
3. Its terrestrial state (local determinations in the horoscope)

Then combine with the same three factors for other planets involved.

## The Nine Ways of Intrinsic Determination

**Ranked from Strongest to Weakest:**

1. Planet in house, in its own sign, aspecting another planet
2. Planet in house, in its own sign, without aspect to another
3. Planet in house, outside own sign, aspecting its ruler
4. Planet in house, outside own sign, aspecting another planet (not its ruler)
5. Planet in house, outside own sign, without aspect to another
6. Sign in house with aspect from its ruler
7. Aspect from a planet not ruling the house
8. Planet in the opposite house
9. Only a sign in the house, no aspects or antiscion

---

## The Nine Ways of Extrinsic Determination

**When the ruler of a house is located in another house:**

1. Ruler in another house, in its own sign, aspecting another planet
2. Ruler in another house, in its own sign, without aspect
3. Ruler in another house, outside own sign, aspecting its ruler
4. Ruler in another house, outside own sign, aspecting another planet
5. Ruler in another house, outside own sign, without aspect
6. Sign in ruling house with aspect from its ruler
7. Aspect from planet not ruling that house
8. Planet in opposite house
9. Only sign in house, no aspects

**The Fundamental Principle:**
> "The celestial bodies actively determine the sublunary world while the latter provides a determination of the celestial bodies in a passive way only."

## Celestial State: The Universal Condition

### Essential Dignities (Strengthen a planet)

**Domicile (Own Sign):**
- Planet in the sign it rules
- Strongest position by nature
- Example: Sun in Leo, Moon in Cancer

**Exaltation:**
- Planet in sign of its elevation
- Particularly analogous to honors
- Example: Sun in Aries, Jupiter in Cancer

**Triplicity:**
- Planet in sign of its triplicity
- Moderate strength

**Term and Face:**
- Lesser dignities, minor influences

### Essential Debilities (Weaken a planet)

**Exile (Detriment):**
- Planet in sign opposite its domicile
- Significantly weakens nature
- Example: Sun in Aquarius, Saturn in Cancer

**Fall:**
- Planet in sign opposite its exaltation
- Severe weakness
- Example: Sun in Libra, Jupiter in Capricorn

**Peregrine:**
- Planet with no dignity
- Neutral but weak state
- Vulnerable to aspect influence

### Accidental Conditions

**Favorable:**
- Direct motion (especially rapid)
- Oriental to Sun (for outer planets)
- Occidental to Moon (for inner planets)
- Free from malefic aspects
- Receiving benefic aspects

**Unfavorable:**
- Retrograde motion
- Slow or stationary
- Under the beams (too close to Sun)
- Combust (very close to Sun)
- Squared or opposed by malefics
- No support from benefics

**Morin's Key Insight:**
> The celestial state is universal - it affects the entire world the same way while a planet remains in that state. Only through local determinations in individual horoscopes does this universal influence become specific and personal.

---

## Universal vs. Particular Causes

### A Revolutionary Distinction

**Universal Cause:**
- Acts the same way for all individuals
- Concurs with inferior causes
- Example: Sun's role in any human birth

**Particular Cause:**
- Produces specific, individual effects
- Powers that only celestial causes can confer
- Example: Solar character from Sun in 1st house

**Morin's Argument:**
Celestial bodies function as BOTH:
- Universal causes when considered in their general action on the world
- Particular causes when conferring specific qualities that sublunary causes cannot produce

**The Primum Caelum:**
- As a whole: most universal cause in nature
- Divided into signs: particular causes of specific qualities
- Through houses: particular causes of individual effects

## Practical Application: Morin's Own Horoscope Example

**Chart Features:**
- Daytime birth
- Sun, Moon, Mercury, Venus, Saturn in 12th house
- All square Mars (rules Ascendant)
- Moon rules 4th house
- Sun conjunct Jupiter

**Traditional Interpretation (Wrong):**
- Sun in daytime = significator of father
- Moon = significator of mother
- Result should show father's influence

**Morin's Correct Interpretation:**

**Moon's Determination:**
- Ruler of 4th house → significator of parents
- Feminine, in feminine sign (Pisces) → particularly mother
- Separating from Saturn, applying to nothing → dislike by mother
- Historical confirmation: Mother tried to disinherit him

**Sun's Determination:**
- In 12th house with Saturn
- Conjunct Jupiter but in house of hidden enemies
- → Significator of powerful secret enemies (Cardinal Richelieu)
- NOT significator of father (no determination to 4th house)
- Historical confirmation: Father never disliked him; Richelieu was secret enemy

**The Lesson:**
Universal significators (Sun=father, Moon=mother) are "too general" and "remain too general" without specific house determinations.

## Complex Situations: Multiple Planets in a House

### Nine Essential Considerations:

1. **Evaluate each planet separately** according to nature, celestial state, and local determination

2. **Then evaluate planets in combination** with each other

3. **Identify the dominant planet:**
   - One with analogy to house meaning
   - One that rules the others by sign
   - Both analogous AND ruling = maximum power

4. **Note the planet closest to cusp** - this has special importance due to cusp strength

5. **When planets have opposite natures:**
   - Sun (analogous to honors) + Saturn (contrary to honors) in 10th
   - Evaluate which is stronger by sign and state
   - The stronger prevails, but influence of weaker remains
   - Example: Sun in Leo + Saturn in Cancer in 10th = honors come (Sun strong) but with misfortunes (Saturn present)

6. **Two benefics in same house:**
   - Always indicate good (conferring benefits or removing evils)
   - More certain when both in good state

7. **Two malefics in same house:**
   - Always indicate evil (causing harm or preventing good)
   - UNLESS both strong by sign AND in fortunate houses
   - Even then, good is mixed with evil

8. **Application sequence matters:**
   - Benefic → Benefic in fortunate house = stable good
   - Benefic → Malefic in fortunate house = good ends in evil
   - Malefic → Benefic in unfortunate house = evil occurs but native freed from it
   - Malefic → Malefic = most serious evils, no freedom

9. **Consider the ruler of the stellium:**
   - If multiple planets in house, note where their ruler is located
   - The affairs of that ruler's house show the origin of effects

## The Ruler in Another House: The Master Key

### The Fundamental Principle

**When a house ruler is located in a different house, the meanings of both houses are combined.**

**Why This Works:**

1. A planet depends on the sign it occupies (like a partnership)
2. A sign depends essentially on its ruler
3. A house's meanings depend on its sign's ruler as an efficient cause
4. Therefore, the ruler connects the two houses dynamically

**Critical Distinction:**

**It is FALSE to say:**
> "The ruler of house X in house Y means that X affects Y"

**It is TRUE to say:**
> "The ruler of house X in house Y means that X is affected through, by means of, or in the manner of Y"

### Practical Examples:

**Ruler of 2nd (money) in 7th (marriage):**
- Money comes through marriage or partnership
- Not that money affects marriage

**Ruler of 10th (career) in 11th (friends):**
- Career success comes through friends
- Or career matters involve friendship

**Ruler of 4th (parents) in 8th (death):**
- Parent's death is significant theme
- Or inheritance from parents

**Ruler of 7th (marriage) in 12th (imprisonment/illness):**
- Marriage brings restriction or suffering
- Or spouse causes hidden troubles

**Morin's Warning:**
> "This matter of the utmost importance in judicial astrology... has up to now been ignored by other writers."

## Aspects: Their True Function

### Traditional vs. Morin's View

**Traditional (Incomplete):**
Aspects between planets show how they interact based on celestial state alone.

**Morin's Complete Understanding:**
Aspects between planets are only meaningful when both planets have local determinations in the horoscope.

### How Aspects Function:

1. **Celestial State Aspects (Universal):**
   - Affects everyone born while that aspect is in effect
   - Shows the general quality of the planetary combination
   - Example: Mars square Saturn = harsh, restrictive energy for all

2. **Terrestrial Determination Required (Particular):**
   - The aspect only produces specific effects when both planets have house connections
   - Must combine: nature + celestial state + terrestrial state
   - Example: Mars (ruler of 1st) square Saturn (in 10th) = personal energy blocks career

**The Three-Fold Synthesis:**
For any planetary combination, judge:
1. What each planet shows by **nature**
2. What each shows by **celestial state** (universal condition)
3. What each shows by **terrestrial state** (house position/rulership)

Then combine all six factors (3 for each planet) to determine the effect.

## Morin's Theological Framework

### Chapter XVI: Celestial Bodies as God's Instruments

**Morin's Devout Perspective:**
The book ends with a theological argument that celestial bodies are the most perfect natural representation of God's action in creation.

### Eight Ways Celestial Bodies Imitate Divine Action:

1. **Simplicity:** Like God's ineffable will, celestial power is simple and known only by its effects

2. **Omnipotence:** Like God's universal power, celestial bodies concur in all natural effects

3. **Irresistibility:** Nothing in nature can resist God's power; nothing sublunary can resist celestial influence

4. **Instantaneous Action:** As God effects things instantly by will, celestial bodies effect through immediate influence

5. **Hierarchical Causation:** As God concurs with all as absolute first cause, the primum caelum concurs with all as first natural cause

6. **Simultaneous Diverse Effects:** Like God affecting different things simultaneously, celestial bodies affect diverse matters in different houses at once

7. **Universal and Particular Action:** As God acts as both universal and particular cause, so do celestial bodies

8. **Supreme Governance:** As all God effects remains under His rule, all celestial effects remain subject to their influence, including timing of events

**Morin's Conclusion:**
> "The celestial bodies imitate God's way of acting on His creation more perfectly than any other natural cause would be able to do."

## The Method in Practice: Complete Procedure

### For Any Question in a Horoscope:

#### Stage 1: Identify the Question's House(s)
- Primary house(s) related to the topic
- Secondary houses that may be involved

#### Stage 2: Examine Planets in the House

**For each planet:**

A. **Determine its nature:**
   - Essential qualities (hot/cold, dry/moist)
   - Benefic or malefic tendency
   - Speed, visibility, other natural factors

B. **Evaluate celestial state:**
   - Dignity or debility by sign
   - Aspects from other planets
   - Motion, speed, relationship to luminaries
   - Overall universal condition: strong/weak, benefic/malefic

C. **Note analogical relationship:**
   - Does planet's nature support or contradict house meaning?
   - Sun in 10th (analogy to honors) = natural support
   - Saturn in 10th (contrary to honors) = natural opposition

D. **Determine the result:**
   - Nature + celestial state + analogy = the effect
   - Benefic nature + good state + favorable analogy = strong good
   - Malefic nature + poor state + unfavorable analogy = strong evil
   - Mixed combinations = mixed results

#### Stage 3: Identify and Analyze House Ruler

A. **Find the ruler:**
   - Which planet rules the sign on the house cusp?

B. **Locate the ruler:**
   - Which house is the ruler located in?

C. **Combine house meanings:**
   - Original house + ruler's house = connected themes
   - This shows means, manner, or source of effects

D. **Evaluate ruler's condition:**
   - Apply same analysis as planets in house
   - Nature, celestial state, analogies

E. **Check the ruler's ruler (if applicable):**
   - Creates a chain of connections
   - Follow the chain to understand full context

#### Stage 4: Analyze Aspects

A. **To the house cusp:**
   - What planets aspect the cusp itself?
   - Consider benefic/malefic nature and strength

B. **To the house ruler:**
   - What planets aspect the ruler?
   - These modify the ruler's action

C. **Between planets in the house:**
   - How do multiple planets in the house interact?
   - Synthesis of their combined influence

#### Stage 5: Evaluate Antiscion
- Mirror positions across Cancer/Capricorn axis
- Can function like aspects or positions

#### Stage 6: Synthesize the Complete Picture

**The Nine-Way Combination:**

For each involved planet, consider:
1. Nature
2. Celestial state  
3. Terrestrial state

For pairs of planets, create 9 possible combinations:
- Nature A + Nature B
- Nature A + Celestial State B
- Nature A + Terrestrial State B
- Celestial State A + Nature B
- Celestial State A + Celestial State B
- Celestial State A + Terrestrial State B
- Terrestrial State A + Nature B
- Terrestrial State A + Celestial State B
- Terrestrial State A + Terrestrial State B

**Morin's Acknowledgment:**
> "And just here arises the great difficulty in predictive astrology."

#### Stage 7: Make the Judgment

**Consider:**
- Strength ranking of all factors
- Dominant indications vs. secondary influences
- Quality (good/evil), quantity (strong/weak), and stability (lasting/temporary)
- Timing indications (if relevant)

**The Final Rule:**
> "Never make a judgment on marriage, for example, before considering the planets in the seventh, the ruler of the seventh, the ruler of this ruler, the planets aspecting the seventh house cusp or its ruler, and the way these planets are related to each other by rulership over, or aspect to, planets analogous to a male or female spouse; and so on for the other houses and planets."

## Key Distinctions from Traditional Astrology

### What Morin Rejected:

1. **Universal Significators as Primary:**
   - Sun always = father
   - Moon always = mother  
   - Mercury always = mind
   - Jupiter always = wealth
   - Venus always = love/women
   - Mars always = conflict
   - Saturn always = restriction/father

2. **Sign Position as Sole Determinant:**
   - Moon in Aries = aggressive temperament (for everyone!)
   - Mercury in Gemini = clever mind (universally!)

3. **Aspects Alone Determining Events:**
   - Mars square Saturn = difficulty (but specifically what?)

4. **Four Angles as Sufficient:**
   - Only considering angular placement (too general)

5. **Planetary Strength as Everything:**
   - Seeking the "strongest" planet for each topic
   - Ignoring planets that are weak but precisely determined

### What Morin Established:

1. **Specific House Determinations:**
   - Only planets with connections to specific houses signify specific matters
   - Location, rulership, or aspect to house/ruler = determination
   - No determination = no signification (even if "natural significator")

2. **Three-Fold Analysis:**
   - Nature (essential)
   - Celestial state (universal/accidental)
   - Terrestrial state (local/particular)

3. **House Ruler Synthesis:**
   - Combining meanings of connected houses
   - Understanding the means/manner of manifestation

4. **Hierarchical Evaluation:**
   - Clear ranking of significator strength
   - Nine levels of intrinsic determination
   - Nine levels of extrinsic determination

5. **Verification Through Experience:**
   - Constant reference to actual charts (his own, historical figures)
   - Rejection of theories that contradict observed results

## The Historical Impact

### Why Book 21 Matters:

1. **Cleared Up Contradictions:**
   - Resolved why astrologers often disagreed
   - Explained why some rules worked, others didn't

2. **Established Logical Framework:**
   - Based on causal reasoning
   - Eliminated arbitrary rules

3. **Preserved Knowledge:**
   - Saved astrology from total loss to scientific revolution
   - Provided rational foundation for future development

4. **Created Complete System:**
   - Every concept integrated with all others
   - No loose ends or unexplained exceptions

### Why It Was Neglected:

1. **Timing:** Published just as scientific revolution eclipsed astrology
2. **Complexity:** Required complete rethinking of traditional methods
3. **Language:** Written in Latin, not widely translated until recently
4. **Cultural Shift:** Association with Ptolemaic astronomy damaged credibility

### Modern Rediscovery:

The translator (Richard S. Baldwin) notes:
> "The debt owed by astrologers to the work of Morinus has not been realized."

Many modern astrological techniques, unknowingly, are based on principles Morin established.

## Core Secrets of Astrology (According to Morin)

### The Primary Secrets:

1. **Determinations are Everything:**
   > "Herein lie the secrets of astrology" - The celestial bodies only act according to their specific determinations

2. **Universal Alone is Meaningless:**
   - Celestial state applies to whole world
   - Must be made particular through houses
   - Without local determination, even strong planets signify nothing specific

3. **Houses Provide Specificity:**
   - Houses are the passive recipients of celestial influence
   - They determine WHAT is affected
   - They make universal influence particular

4. **Ruler Connections Create Narrative:**
   - How things happen (not just what happens)
   - The means, manner, and source of effects
   - The story behind events

5. **Synthesis Over Simplicity:**
   - Must combine nature + celestial state + terrestrial state
   - Cannot evaluate any factor in isolation
   - The whole is more than sum of parts

6. **Experience Confirms Theory:**
   > "Study and practice until you become perfect"
   - Theory must match observation
   - Frequent reference to actual charts proves principles

### The Meta-Secret:

**Morin's ultimate insight:** The ancients received truth through tradition but lacked understanding of principles. They saw that "planets' locations and rulerships in houses resulted in effects that were striking" but didn't understand why.

The general cause: **Determinations of celestial bodies as described by Morin and "by no one else before now."**

### On Learning Astrology:

> "Study and practice until you become perfect, so that for any two horoscopes you will be able to predict whether the natives can agree or not and why."

### On Difficulty:

> "The prediction of what the effects of these combinations will be is indeed easy for the angels because of their intuition and the luminescence of their intellect, which suffers so little obstruction; but for men the contrary is true and they find it difficult and even impossible without frequent error."

### On Method:

> "The whole knack of these judgments turns on making combinations both possible and appropriate, and interpreting what their effects will be."

### On Precision:

> "Careful attention should be paid to the planets' location by house, or their house-rulerships, and to whether they aspect favorably or unfavorably a planet having an analogy with the meanings of these houses, and what the celestial state and determinations of this other planet may in turn be."

## Summary: The Morinus Revolution

### Before Morin:
- Loose analogies and traditional associations
- Universal significators applied without discrimination
- Contradictory rules without logical foundation
- Predictions often failed, causing loss of credibility

### After Morin:
- Precise system based on determinations
- Clear hierarchy of significator strength
- Logical integration of all factors
- Method that produces verifiable results

### The Essential Method (Ultra-Condensed):

1. **Identify the relevant house(s)** for the question
2. **Find planets in those houses** and evaluate by:
   - Nature
   - Celestial state
   - Analogy to house meaning
3. **Find the house ruler(s)** and note:
   - Which house they occupy
   - Combined meaning of both houses
   - Their own nature, state, and determinations
4. **Check aspects** to houses, rulers, and between planets
5. **Synthesize all factors** using the nine-fold method
6. **Make judgment** based on:
   - Strength ranking
   - Quality (good/evil)
   - Quantity (strong/weak)
   - Stability (lasting/temporary)

### The Foundation:

**Nature + Celestial State + Terrestrial State = Effect**

Applied systematically to every planet, combined through aspects and rulerships, evaluated through houses, synthesized into coherent judgment.

## Final Observation

Morin ends Book 21 with a theological meditation on how celestial bodies perfectly reflect God's action in nature. This is no mere appendix - it reveals Morin's worldview: astrology is not superstition but a rational understanding of divine order in creation.

The celestial bodies are instruments of Providence, and understanding their determinations is understanding how universal divine will manifests in particular earthly effects. This theological framework gave Morin confidence that his logical, systematic approach was discovering real truth, not inventing arbitrary rules.

His work represents the high-water mark of traditional astrology - a complete, rational system established just as the medieval worldview gave way to modernity. Book 21 is both a culmination and a preservation, capturing centuries of astrological wisdom in logical form before it might have been lost forever.
