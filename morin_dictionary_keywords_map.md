## I. THE TWELVE HOUSES: Essential Meanings & Keywords

### House Classification System

**FORTUNATE HOUSES** (Represent Desirable Things):
1st, 2nd, 3rd, 4th, 5th, 6th, 7th, 9th, 10th, 11th

**UNFORTUNATE HOUSES** (Represent Undesirable Things):
8th (death), 12th (imprisonment, illness)
Note: 7th can be unfortunate through litigation and open enemies

**ANGULAR HOUSES** (Greatest Strength):
1st, 4th, 7th, 10th

---

### 1ST HOUSE (Ascendant)
**Primary Keywords:**
- Physical constitution
- Character and temperament
- Disposition
- Mental qualities
- Body and soul condition
- Natural inclinations
- Personal will
- Self-expression
- Essential qualities of the native

**Cusp Name:** Ascendant (eastern horizon)

**Morin's Notes:**
- Most determinative for native's nature
- Combined with ruler shows complete character picture
- Planets here have immediate, powerful effect
- Exaltations of Sun and Moon here = fame-seeking tendency

---

### 2ND HOUSE
**Primary Keywords:**
- Money and wealth
- Financial resources
- Possessions
- Material accumulation
- Inheritance (from parents specifically)
- Personal assets
- Financial stability

**Cusp Name:** Gate of Hades

**Analogous Planets:**
- Jupiter (primary wealth indicator)

**Morin's Notes:**
- Benefics here = real, abundant wealth
- Malefics here deny money or cause waste
- Saturn = parsimony, avarice, conservation
- Mars = squandering, prodigality
- Ruler in 7th = money through marriage
- Ruler in 10th = money through profession

---

### 3RD HOUSE
**Primary Keywords:**
- Brothers and sisters (siblings)
- Relatives
- Short journeys
- Communications
- Neighbors
- Early education
- Local travel
- Written and spoken word

**Cusp Name:** Goddess

**Morin's Notes:**
- Malefics here = death of siblings
- Mars + Saturn in 3rd = sole survivor of many siblings
- Ruler in other houses connects sibling matters to those areas

---

### 4TH HOUSE (IC)
**Primary Keywords:**
- Parents (especially mother by some accounts)
- Home and domestic affairs
- Foundations
- Real estate and land
- Endings of matters
- Inheritances
- Family roots
- Private life
- Estates and properties

**Cusp Name:** Imum Coeli (IC) - Bottom of Heaven

**Analogous Planets:**
- Moon (maternal)
- Sun (paternal, but determination required)
- Saturn (parental authority)
- Jupiter (wealth from parents)

**Morin's Notes:**
- Moon as ruler of 4th = parent significator (especially mother)
- Multiple planets can indicate different parent themes
- Exaltation of ruling planet here can show property acquisition
- Opposition to 10th connects origins to career

---

### 5TH HOUSE
**Primary Keywords:**
- Children (offspring)
- Pleasures and entertainment
- Creativity and artistic expression
- Love affairs (non-marital)
- Recreation
- Gambling and speculation
- Joy and happiness
- Self-expression through creation

**Cusp Name:** Good Fortune

**Morin's Notes:**
- Planets here indicate whether children granted or denied
- Can show children taken away (death)
- Can show fortune or misfortune of children
- Connected to native's creative output

---

### 6TH HOUSE
**Primary Keywords:**
- Servants and employees
- Animals (domestic)
- Daily work and service
- Subordinate position
- Household affairs
- Health (minor illnesses)
- Duties and obligations

**Cusp Name:** Bad Fortune

**Morin's Interpretation:**
- Accidentally connected to illness through opposition to 12th
- Ruler of 1st here = servile occupations
- Stooping to menial tasks
- Love of or concern with servants/animals
- Malefics here threaten losses through servants

**Historical Note:**
Morin corrects Ptolemy - 6th does NOT essentially refer to diseases; this is accidental through opposition to 12th house

---

### 7TH HOUSE (Descendant)
**Primary Keywords:**
- Marriage and spouse
- Partnerships (business and personal)
- Open enemies (known adversaries)
- Litigation and lawsuits
- Contracts and agreements
- The "other" in relationships
- Public relations
- Cooperation or conflict with others

**Cusp Name:** Descendant (western horizon)

**Analogous Planets:**
- Venus (for marriage, harmony)
- Mars (for conflict, litigation)

**Nature:**
- Can be evil through opposition to 1st house
- This opposition creates meaning of "open enemies"
- Otherwise fortunate for marriage/partnership

**Morin's Examples:**
- Venus here = beautiful wife, happy marriage
- Mars here = litigation, conflicts
- Malefics strong here can show death of spouse
- Benefics smooth out conflicts

---

### 8TH HOUSE
**Primary Keywords:**
- Death (manner and timing)
- Inheritance from others (not parents)
- Transformation
- Other people's money
- Legacies
- Violent or fearful death
- Danger to life
- Disgrace in death

**Cusp Name:** Idle Place

**Analogous Planets:**
- Mars (violent death)
- Saturn (fearful, disgraceful death)

**Unfortunate House Classification:**
One of the two essentially evil houses

**Morin's Notes:**
- Mars in Aries in 8th = violent death (Henri d'Effiat)
- Malefics here in poor state = worst possible death conditions
- Benefics here = easy death, prevent violence
- Jupiter + malefics + Sun in 8th = violent but public judicial death
- Opposition to 2nd connects others' money vs. own money

---

### 9TH HOUSE
**Primary Keywords:**
- Religion and philosophy
- Higher learning and education
- Long journeys (foreign travel)
- Legal matters (higher law)
- Wisdom and understanding
- Spiritual matters
- Publishing and teaching
- Foreign affairs
- Higher mind

**Cusp Name:** God/Sun

**Morin's Notes:**
- Connection to religious vocation
- Ruler in other houses shows how/where religious/philosophical interests manifest
- Distinguished from 3rd (local) vs. 9th (distant) travel

---

### 10TH HOUSE (Midheaven/MC)
**Primary Keywords:**
- Career and profession
- Public honor and fame
- Reputation and prestige
- Social position
- Public recognition
- Professional activities
- Achievement and ambition
- Status in society
- Mother (by some traditional accounts)

**Cusp Name:** Midheaven (MC) - Medium Coeli

**Analogous Planets:**
- Sun (primary honor/fame indicator)
- Jupiter (elevation, position)

**Most Important Angular House:**
Strongest position for worldly success

**Morin's Examples:**
- Sun in 10th = public honor, fame
- Saturn in 10th in exile/fall = denial of honors, mean occupation
- Saturn exalted in 10th = honors through difficult means
- Mars exalted in 7th threatened powerful enemies affecting career
- Exaltation most analogous dignity to honors

**Nature by Planet:**
- Sun: Natural agreement with honors (grants preferment)
- Saturn: Contrary to honors by nature (denies preferment)
- Jupiter: Elevation and recognition
- Mars: Honors through military/conflict
- Venus: Honors through arts/diplomacy

---

### 11TH HOUSE
**Primary Keywords:**
- Friends and allies
- Benefactors and patrons
- Hopes and wishes
- Social groups and associations
- Support systems
- Nobles and aristocracy
- Protection from powerful figures
- Royal/princely friends

**Cusp Name:** Good Spirit

**Morin's Examples:**
- Sun in 11th = friends among kings, princes, nobles
- Ruler of 10th in 11th = honors through friends/benefactors
- Mars here = military friends or upsets friendships through quarrels

**Historical Context:**
Morin clarifies this does NOT essentially refer to children (that's 5th house accidental opposition interpretation error)

---

### 12TH HOUSE
**Primary Keywords:**
- Hidden enemies (secret adversaries)
- Imprisonment and confinement
- Illness and disease (serious)
- Exile and isolation
- Self-undoing
- Restrictions
- Suffering and tribulation
- Hospitals, asylums
- Things concealed or secret

**Cusp Name:** Bad Spirit

**Unfortunate House Classification:**
One of the two essentially evil houses

**Analogous Planets:**
- Saturn (imprisonment, chronic illness, secret enemies)
- Mars (acute illness, hidden dangers)

**Morin's Personal Example:**
- Sun conjunct Jupiter in 12th = powerful secret enemy (Cardinal Richelieu)
- Jupiter + Venus in 12th in good state = saved from many illnesses
- Saturn in 12th = severe illnesses, imprisonment, secret enemies
- Benefics in good state here = liberation from evils
- Benefics in poor state here = fail to prevent evils

**Special Notes:**
- Opposition to 6th creates accidental illness meaning for 6th
- Most dangerous house for malefics
- Strong benefics here provide protection and mitigation

## II. THE SEVEN PLANETS: Natures & Keywords

### SUN (☉)
**Essential Nature:**
- Hot and dry
- Masculine
- Diurnal
- The greater light

**Primary Keywords:**
- Vitality and life force
- Father (requires determination)
- Authority figures
- Kings and rulers
- Honor and prestige
- Public recognition
- Fame and glory
- Power and influence
- The ego and will
- Consciousness
- Dignified position
- Solar character/temperament

**Celestial Affinities:**
- Domicile: Leo
- Exaltation: Aries (19°)
- Exile: Aquarius
- Fall: Libra (19°)

**House Affinities:**
- Natural significator of 10th house (honors)
- Strong in 10th, 1st, 11th
- In 12th = powerful hidden enemies

**Action by House (Morin's Examples):**
- 10th: Public honor, fame, preferment
- 11th: Friends among kings and princes
- 12th: Secret powerful enemies (if afflicted)
- 4th: Wealth and position (with other indicators)
- In exile/peregrine/afflicted: Denies what its location promises

**Aspects:**
- Particularly analogous to honors (with Moon)
- Adverse aspect from malefics to Sun = denies recognition
- Sun trine Jupiter = strong support for solar matters

**Special States:**
- Oriental: Not applicable (center)
- Direct/rapid motion: Strengthened
- Partile aspects: Most powerful

**Morin's Note:**
"The Sun, through its very nature, shows preferment when in the tenth since it bears analogy to and is therefore in natural agreement with this house."

---

### MOON (☽)
**Essential Nature:**
- Cold and moist
- Feminine
- Nocturnal
- The lesser light
- Changeable, fluctuating

**Primary Keywords:**
- Mother (requires determination)
- Wife (requires determination)
- Moral character (universal, needs determination for specific)
- Emotions and feelings
- The body and physical condition
- Public and masses
- Daily life and routine
- Fluctuation and change
- Receptivity
- Instincts
- Fertility
- Domestic matters

**Celestial Affinities:**
- Domicile: Cancer
- Exaltation: Taurus (3°)
- Exile: Capricorn
- Fall: Scorpio (3°)

**Special Positional States:**
- Occidental to Sun: Favorable
- Rapid motion: Strengthened
- Slow or void: Weakened
- Separating from/applying to: Shows sequence of events

**House Determinations:**
- As ruler of 4th = parent significator (especially mother)
- Feminine by nature = inclines to mother when in feminine signs
- Separation from Saturn, applying to nothing = parental dislike
- In 7th with determination = wife significator

**Action Principles:**
- Moon's celestial state is universal (same for whole world)
- Must have specific determination to indicate specific matters
- Sign position alone too general to specify moral qualities
- Needs house connection to become meaningful for individual

**Morin's Critical Point:**
"The Moon is indifferent to any specific moral qualities as these are actually determined by its location, rulership, or aspects in the horoscope."

---

### MERCURY (☿)
**Essential Nature:**
- Variable (takes nature of planet it aspects)
- Neutral (can be benefic or malefic depending on state)
- Androgynous
- Cold and dry when alone

**Primary Keywords:**
- Mind and intellect
- Mental qualities and capabilities
- Communication
- Reasoning and logic
- Learning and education
- Speech and writing
- Commerce and trade
- Skills and dexterity
- Adaptability
- Youth
- Messengers
- Quick movement

**Celestial Affinities:**
- Domicile: Gemini, Virgo
- Exaltation: Virgo (15° by some accounts)
- Exile: Sagittarius, Pisces
- Fall: Pisces (15°)

**Special Characteristics:**
- Changeable nature according to aspects
- With benefics = benefic
- With malefics = malefic
- Alone = moderately benefic

**House Affinities:**
- Natural analogy to mental qualities
- In 1st or ruling 1st = indicates mental nature
- Needs specific determination to actually signify mind

**Morin's Key Insight:**
"Mercury is the general significator of the mind according to analogy, but if found to be the particular significator by position or rulership in the first house, the effect on mental qualities will be very strongly pronounced."

**Action by State:**
- In good state in 1st = excellent mental qualities
- Partile sextile to Ascendant = Mercurial character (Morin's chart)
- Without determination to 1st = no reference to mental qualities

- Nocturnal
- Lesser benefic

**Primary Keywords:**
- Love and affection
- Beauty and aesthetics
- Harmony and peace

- Harmony in partnerships

**Celestial Affinities:**
- Domicile: Taurus, Libra

**House Affinities:**

**Morin's Examples:**

### MARS (♂)
**Essential Nature:**
- Hot and dry
- Masculine
- Nocturnal
- Lesser malefic
- Violent, aggressive, hasty

**Primary Keywords:**
- Conflict and strife
- Violence and aggression
- War and military
- Courage and assertion
- Energy and action
- Anger and passion
- Sudden events
- Cuts and wounds
- Fire and heat
- Competition
- Impulse
- Acute illness
- Quarrels and disputes

**Celestial Affinities:**
- Domicile: Aries, Scorpio
- Exaltation: Capricorn (28°)
- Exile: Libra, Taurus
- Fall: Cancer (28°)

**House Affinities:**
- Natural analogy to 7th (conflict, litigation)
- Natural analogy to 8th (violent death)
- In 7th = litigation, conflicts, quarrels
- In 8th = violent death (especially if in Aries or Capricorn)
- In 12th = acute illnesses, hidden dangers

**Malefic Nature:**
- Causes conflict, strife, violence by nature
- Even in good state grants things with difficulties
- Squanders money (opposite of Saturn's conservation)

**By State & Position:**
- In Aries in 8th = violent death (Henri d'Effiat example)
- Exalted in 7th = powerful open enemies
- Square Sun/Moon or ruling 1st/10th = severe challenges
- In good state can bring military honors

**Morin's Examples:**
- Mars ruling 1st (Aries Ascendant), in 12th = constant danger
- Mars + Saturn in 3rd = death of siblings
- Mars in 7th = litigation, conflicts (Venus prevents this)
- Mars in 11th = military friends OR upsets friendships

**Action Keywords:**
- Prodigality (wasteful spending)
- Foolish expenditures
- Useless expenses
- Quarrels and disputes
- Violent manifestation

### JUPITER (♃)
**Essential Nature:**
- Hot and moist
- Masculine
- Diurnal
- Greater benefic
- Expansive, generous

**Primary Keywords:**
- Wealth and abundance
- Expansion and growth
- Wisdom and philosophy
- Religion and higher law
- Good fortune
- Generosity and benevolence
- Justice and ethics
- Honor and dignity
- Protection and preservation
- Optimism
- Higher education
- Legal matters
- Foreign affairs
- Prosperity

**Celestial Affinities:**
- Domicile: Sagittarius, Pisces
- Exaltation: Cancer (15°)
- Exile: Gemini, Virgo
- Fall: Capricorn (15°)

**House Affinities:**
- Primary significator of 2nd (wealth) by analogy
- Natural analogy to 9th (religion, philosophy)
- Strong in 2nd, 10th, 11th, 4th
- Protective in 8th, 12th

**Benefic Action:**
- Grants wealth, especially in 2nd
- Brings elevation and position in 10th
- In unfortunate houses (8th, 12th): prevents or mitigates evil
- In 12th = few diseases, easy cures, liberation from prison
- In 8th = easy death, prevents violence
- Natural analog does not incline to disease, violence, imprisonment

**By State:**
- In good state: Real, abundant, lasting benefits
- In poor state: Can fail to protect (Jupiter in 8th in poor state = violent death examples)
- Even in poor state acts more beneficially than malefics

**Morin's Examples:**
- Jupiter in 10th (Louis Tronson) = public honors
- Jupiter + Saturn in 12th (Morin) = many serious illnesses but recovered
- Jupiter in 2nd = wealth, especially if aspecting ruler
- Jupiter trine Sun = strongly supports honors
- Jupiter in Gemini in 8th with fixed star = death from disease (Richelieu)

**Special Notes:**
- "Through its nature and by analogy does not incline to grave and terrible diseases, prison, or violent death"
- Benefic influence universal but subject to local determinations
- Promotes good, diminishes evil by necessity of its nature

### SATURN (♄)
**Essential Nature:**
- Cold and dry
- Masculine
- Diurnal
- Greater malefic
- Restrictive, limiting, slow

**Primary Keywords:**
- Restriction and limitation
- Time and delay
- Old age and elderly
- Authority and discipline
- Structure and boundaries
- Poverty and lack
- Father (traditional, requires determination)
- Chronic conditions
- Depression and melancholy
- Endurance and persistence
- Responsibility
- Obstacles and hindrances
- Conservation and preservation
- Prudence and caution
- Serious illness
- Imprisonment
- Death (especially fearful, disgraceful)
- Hidden enemies
- Isolation

**Celestial Affinities:**
- Domicile: Capricorn, Aquarius
- Exaltation: Libra (21°)
- Exile: Cancer, Leo
- Fall: Aries (21°)

**House Affinities:**
- Most dangerous in 8th, 12th (unfortunate houses)
- Contrary by nature to 10th house (honors)
- Natural analogy to restriction, imprisonment, chronic illness

**Malefic Action by House:**
- 12th: Severe illnesses, long duration, difficult to cure; imprisonment; secret enemies
- 8th: Fearful, violent, disgraceful death (especially in Leo - Duc de Montmorency)
- 10th: Denies honors by nature (though can grant if exalted/strong)
- 2nd: Denies money, or conservation through parsimony/avarice
- 4th: Parental matters, especially harsh/restrictive parent

**By State:**
- In poor state: Maximum evil effects
- Exalted or in domicile: Can grant benefits but with difficulties
- Even strong, always grants with imperfections or difficult methods

**Morin's Examples:**
- Saturn in Leo in 8th = violent death in disgrace (Duc de Montmorency)
- Saturn in 10th in poor state = denial of honors, mean occupation
- Saturn exalted in 10th = honors through difficult means
- Saturn in 12th = severe chronic illnesses (Morin's experience)
- Saturn + Mars in Capricorn in 2nd = wealth but with avarice (de Chavigny)

**Special Characteristics:**
- Parsimony and avarice in financial matters
- Conservation of resources
- Seriousness and gravitas
- Can provide prudence, advice, authority when well-placed

**Morin's Key Insight:**
"Saturn by nature denies preferment; however, accidentally Saturn in the tenth could bring honors and preferment if it were in its own sign, or in exaltation, oriental to the Sun, moving rapidly in forward motion, and trine the Moon, Jupiter or Venus."

**Action Keywords:**
- Denial and prevention
- Vitiation of planetary power (in exile)
- Weakness and inactivity (in fall)
- Contrary by nature to honors, pleasure, quick action
- Sluggish and lazy effects

## III. THE ZODIACAL SIGNS: Qualities & Keywords

### Essential Qualities System

**BY ELEMENT (Triplicity):**

**FIRE SIGNS (Hot & Dry):**
- Aries, Leo, Sagittarius
- Active, energetic, enthusiastic
- Initiating and assertive
- Same fundamental nature despite planetary differences

**EARTH SIGNS (Cold & Dry):**
- Taurus, Virgo, Capricorn
- Practical, material, stable
- Grounded and concrete

**AIR SIGNS (Hot & Moist):**
- Gemini, Libra, Aquarius
- Intellectual, communicative, social
- Abstract and conceptual

**WATER SIGNS (Cold & Moist):**
- Cancer, Scorpio, Pisces
- Emotional, intuitive, receptive
- Flowing and adaptive
- Cancer = lunar nature
- Scorpio = martial nature
- Pisces = jovial nature

**BY QUALITY (Quadruplicity):**

**CARDINAL (Movable):**
- Aries, Cancer, Libra, Capricorn
- Initiating, acting, beginning
- Associated with angles of chart

**FIXED:**
- Taurus, Leo, Scorpio, Aquarius
- Stable, enduring, persistent
- Resistance to change

**MUTABLE (Common):**
- Gemini, Virgo, Sagittarius, Pisces
- Adaptable, changeable, versatile
- Flexibility and transition

**BY GENDER:**

**MASCULINE (Diurnal):**
- Fire and Air signs
- Aries, Gemini, Leo, Libra, Sagittarius, Aquarius
- Active, projective, yang

**FEMININE (Nocturnal):**
- Earth and Water signs
- Taurus, Cancer, Virgo, Scorpio, Capricorn, Pisces
- Receptive, magnetic, yin

## IV. DIGNITIES & DEBILITIES: Complete System

### Essential Dignities (Strengthen Planet)

**1. DOMICILE (Rulership) - STRONGEST**
**Keywords:** At home, comfortable, full expression of nature

**Traditional Rulerships:**
- Sun: Leo
- Moon: Cancer
- Mercury: Gemini, Virgo
- Venus: Taurus, Libra
- Mars: Aries, Scorpio
- Jupiter: Sagittarius, Pisces
- Saturn: Capricorn, Aquarius

**Effects:**
- Planet acts according to own nature without subordination
- Stability indicated
- Greatest power by essential nature
- "In domicile and ruler of house = easiest evaluation"

**2. EXALTATION - SECOND STRONGEST**
**Keywords:** Elevation, sudden important changes, especially honors

**Exaltation Degrees:**
- Sun: Aries 19°
- Moon: Taurus 3°
- Mercury: Virgo 15°
- Venus: Pisces 27°
- Mars: Capricorn 28°
- Jupiter: Cancer 15°
- Saturn: Libra 21°

**Special Properties:**
- "Among all dignities, most analogous to honors"
- Can influence house affairs even when planet not in that house
- Sudden and important changes
- Elevation and prominence

**3. TRIPLICITY - MODERATE STRENGTH**
**Keywords:** Same element, general conformity, minimal but present

**Triplicity Rulers (Diurnal/Nocturnal system used):**
- Fire: Sun (day), Jupiter (night)
- Earth: Venus (day), Moon (night)
- Air: Saturn (day), Mercury (night)
- Water: Venus (day), Mars (night)

**Morin's Assessment:**
- "Minimal value"
- More valid for temperament/character than events
- Remote cause, not essential dependence
- General conformity in fundamental nature
- Judgment from sign ruler much more reliable

**4. TERM & FACE - MINOR**
Lesser dignities with minimal effect (Morin gives these little attention)

---

### Essential Debilities (Weaken Planet)

**1. EXILE (DETRIMENT) - STRONG DEBILITY**
**Keywords:** Uncomfortable, contrary to nature, vitiated power

**Exile = Opposite Sign of Domicile:**
- Sun: Aquarius
- Moon: Capricorn
- Mercury: Sagittarius, Pisces
- Venus: Aries, Scorpio
- Mars: Taurus, Libra
- Jupiter: Gemini, Virgo
- Saturn: Cancer, Leo

**Effects:**
- "Power is vitiated"
- Signs contrary to planet's nature and quality
- Denies what planet would normally grant
- Severely impairs function
- In unfortunate houses = maximum evil

**Morin's Examples:**
- Sun in Aquarius = adverse state (Morin's own chart)
- Saturn in Leo in 8th = violent death in disgrace
- Saturn in Cancer = parental difficulties

**2. FALL - MODERATE TO STRONG DEBILITY**
**Keywords:** Weakness, inactivity, inability to function properly

**Fall = Opposite Sign of Exaltation:**
- Sun: Libra 19°
- Moon: Scorpio 3°
- Mercury: Pisces 15°
- Venus: Virgo 27°
- Mars: Cancer 28°
- Jupiter: Capricorn 15°
- Saturn: Aries 21°

**Effects:**
- "Made weak and more inactive"
- Contrary to quality and essential nature
- In 10th = denial of honors, mean occupation
- Less severe than exile but still significant

**3. PEREGRINE - NEUTRAL/WEAK STATE**
**Keywords:** Stranger, without dignity, intermediate, vulnerable

**Definition:**
Planet in sign where it has no essential dignity (not in domicile, exaltation, or traditional triplicity/term/face)

**Examples:**
- Sun in Gemini, water or earth signs (except Cancer/Capricorn)
- Any planet wandering without dignity

**Effects:**
- "Not in state of debility" per se
- Neither good nor adverse essentially
- Intermediate between good and bad
- Vulnerable to aspect conditions
- Can accidentally be strong if well-aspected
- "Acts in manner intermediate between good or adverse celestial state"

**Important Distinction:**
- Essentially neutral
- Accidentally can be strengthened or weakened by aspects

---

### Accidental Dignities (Strengthen by Position/Condition)

**1. ANGULAR POSITION**
- 1st, 4th, 7th, 10th houses
- Strongest action, most effective
- Especially when also in domicile/exaltation

**2. DIRECT MOTION**
- Normal forward movement
- Especially rapid direct motion = strengthened

**3. ORIENTAL TO SUN (Outer Planets)**
- Rising before Sun
- Strengthening position

**4. OCCIDENTAL TO MOON (Inner Planets)**
- Setting after Moon
- Favorable position

**5. RECEIVING BENEFIC ASPECTS**
- Trine from Jupiter
- Sextile from Venus
- Support from well-placed planets

**6. FREE FROM MALEFIC ASPECTS**
- Not squared or opposed by Mars/Saturn
- Clean from affliction

---

### Accidental Debilities (Weaken by Position/Condition)

**1. RETROGRADE MOTION**
- Backwards movement
- Weakens and perverts natural action
- Makes uncertain

**2. SLOW OR STATIONARY**
- Weakened by lack of motion
- Sluggish manifestation

**3. UNDER THE BEAMS**
- Within 15° of Sun (some say 17°)
- Obscured, hidden, weakened

**4. COMBUST**
- Within 8° of Sun (exact measurement varies)
- Severely weakened, burnt up
- Morin: "partile conjunction with Sun" strongest effect

**5. RECEIVING MALEFIC ASPECTS**
- Square or opposition from Saturn/Mars
- Affliction and obstruction

**6. CADENT HOUSES**
- 3rd, 6th, 9th, 12th
- Weaker than angular or succedent
- Less effective action (though still important by determination)

## V. ASPECTS: The Five Ptolemaic Aspects

### Core Aspect System

**Morin's Key Principle:**
"Aspects between planets show celestial state (universal) but only produce specific effects when both planets have local determinations (house connections) in the horoscope."

---

### CONJUNCTION (0°) - ☌
**Nature:** Variable (depends on planets involved)
**Orb:** Varies by planets (Sun/Moon larger orbs)
**Keywords:** Union, fusion, blending, intensification

**Effects:**
- Planets act together as single force
- Nature depends on planets involved
- Benefic + benefic = strong benefic
- Malefic + malefic = strong malefic
- Benefic + malefic = mixed, contested
- Partile (exact) conjunction = strongest effect

**Special Types:**
- Partile conjunction: Maximum power, considered strongest aspect
- Sun conjunct Jupiter: Benefic support
- Sun conjunct Saturn: Malefic affliction
- Conjunction of luminaries (Sun-Moon): New Moon, powerful union

**Morin's Examples:**
- Sun partile conjunct Jupiter in 12th = powerful hidden friend/enemy dynamic
- Planets in same house create conjunction effects even if not same degree

---

### SEXTILE (60°) - ⚹
**Nature:** Mildly benefic
**Keywords:** Opportunity, cooperation, ease, mild support

**Effects:**
- Lesser benefic aspect
- Creates opportunities
- Smooth flow between planets
- Support and assistance
- Easier manifestation

**Morin's Use:**
- Mercury partile sextile Ascendant = Mercurial character
- Benefic sextiles support house affairs
- Less powerful than trine

---

### SQUARE (90°) - □
**Nature:** Malefic, challenging
**Keywords:** Conflict, tension, obstacle, challenge, crisis

**Effects:**
- Creates friction and difficulty
- Obstacles to smooth manifestation
- Brings challenges requiring action
- Can stimulate growth through difficulty
- Square to luminaries especially difficult

**Morin's Examples:**
- Mars square Saturn = harsh restrictive energy
- Planets in 12th square ruler of Ascendant = personal difficulties
- Sun squared by malefics = denies preferment
- Square to Sun, ruler of MC = obstacles to career
- "Squared or opposed by malefics" = adverse celestial state

**Special Applications:**
- Square between rulers of houses = conflict in those life areas
- Square to angles = particularly challenging

---

### TRINE (120°) - △
**Nature:** Strongly benefic
**Keywords:** Harmony, ease, flow, natural talent, support

**Effects:**
- Greatest benefic aspect
- Natural harmony and ease
- Talents and gifts
- Strong support and benefit
- Flowing manifestation
- Stable and lasting good

**Morin's Examples:**
- Sun trine Jupiter = strong Jupiterian support to solar matters
- Jupiter trine Moon/Venus = support for benefic outcomes
- Saturn exalted trine benefics = honors with support
- Benefics in trine = most certain good

**Applications:**
- Same element (trine signs share element)
- Natural affinity and understanding
- Support without effort

---

### OPPOSITION (180°) - ☍
**Nature:** Malefic, confrontational
**Keywords:** Polarization, awareness, conflict, separation, culmination

**Effects:**
- Creates awareness through confrontation
- Direct opposition of energies
- Relationship dynamics (us vs. them)
- Can bring crisis or culmination
- Opposition of houses creates reciprocal meanings

**Special Properties:**
- Houses in opposition have related/reciprocal meanings
- 1st-7th: Self vs. other
- 6th-12th: Created accidental illness meaning for 6th
- 2nd-8th: Own money vs. others' money

**Morin's Examples:**
- Planet in domicile has exile in opposite house
- Opposition effects both houses involved
- "Squared or opposed" = standard phrase for affliction
- Opposition to lights = particularly difficult

---

### Aspect Strength Factors

**1. EXACTNESS (ORBS):**
- Partile (exact to degree): Strongest
- Close (within 1-3°): Strong
- Wider orbs: Weaker effect

**2. APPLICATION vs. SEPARATION:**
- Applying: Increasing in strength, future events
- Separating: Decreasing, past events
- "Separating from Saturn, applying to nothing" = no future connection

**3. MUTUAL RECEPTION:**
- Planets in each other's signs
- Strengthens aspect significantly

**4. BOTH PLANETS' STATES:**
- Strong planet to weak: Mixed results
- Both strong: Aspect fully manifests
- Both weak: Aspect less effective

## VI. TECHNICAL TERMS & CONCEPTS

### Determinations (Core Concept)

**ACTIVE DETERMINATION**
- How celestial bodies determine effects in sublunary world
- Celestial → Terrestrial causation

**PASSIVE DETERMINATION**
- How sublunary world (especially houses) determines celestial influence
- Makes universal influence particular

**LOCAL DETERMINATION (Terrestrial State)**
- Connection to specific house by:
  - Location in house
  - Rulership of house
  - Aspect to house cusp
  - Aspect to house ruler
- Makes celestial influence specific to individual

**SPECIFIC DETERMINATION**
Required for universal analogies to become meaningful:
- Sun = father ONLY if determined to 4th house
- Jupiter = wealth ONLY if determined to 2nd house
- Without determination = no signification

---

### States (Conditions)

**CELESTIAL STATE**
- Universal condition applying to whole world
- Determined by:
  - Sign position (dignity/debility)
  - Aspects from other planets
  - Motion and speed
  - Relationship to luminaries
- Remains same for everyone while planet in that state

**TERRESTRIAL STATE**
- Local, particular condition in individual horoscope
- Determined by house connections
- Makes universal influence specific
- "What planet shows through its terrestrial state — that is, through its location in particular house or rulership in particular house"

**ESSENTIAL STATE**
- Basic dignity or debility by sign
- Domicile, exaltation, exile, fall, peregrine

**ACCIDENTAL STATE**
- Temporary conditions: motion, aspects, combustion, etc.
- Can override essential state

---

### Significance Types

**ESSENTIAL SIGNIFICATOR**
By nature/analogy:
- Sun = vitality, authority, father
- Moon = emotions, mother
- Jupiter = wealth
- BUT requires determination to be active

**ACCIDENTAL SIGNIFICATOR**
By house position/rulership:
- Ruler of 2nd = money significator
- Planet in 7th = marriage significator
- More important than essential in Morin's system

**PRIMARY SIGNIFICATOR**
First in hierarchy for any house:
1. Planet in house (strongest)
2. House ruler
3. Ruler of ruler
4. Planets aspecting house/ruler

**UNIVERSAL SIGNIFICATOR**
General meanings (Sun=father) without specific determination
- Too general to be useful alone
- Must be made particular through houses

**PARTICULAR SIGNIFICATOR**
Specific to individual through local determination
- Actually meaningful for prediction
- Created through house connections

---

### Action Modes

**GRANTING**
- Planet bestows the goods of house
- Active giving of benefits
- "Grants the good things of that house"

**DENYING**
- Planet prevents or refuses house benefits
- Active prevention
- "Not only no wealth for native by own efforts, but inheritance dissipated"

**REMOVING**
- Taking away what was granted
- Intermediate between granting and denying
- Children granted then taken away
- Honors achieved then lost

**AFFECTING**
- Modifying what has been granted
- Making fortunate or unfortunate
- Attending with difficulties
- "Through evil means" or "with imperfections"

---

### Quality Assessment Terms

**QUALITY (Good vs. Evil)**
Benefic or malefic nature of effect

**QUANTITY (Strong vs. Weak)**
Strength or intensity of effect:
- Real, abundant, lasting (strong benefic)
- Scanty, spurious, unreliable (weak benefic)
- Severe, grave (strong malefic)
- Moderate, tempered (weak malefic)

**STABILITY (Lasting vs. Temporary)**
Duration of effect:
- Domicile = stability
- Exaltation = sudden changes
- Lasting vs. fleeting results

---

### Relationship Terms

**ANALOGY**
Natural affinity between planet and house meaning:
- Sun to 10th (honors)
- Jupiter to 2nd (wealth)
- Mars to 7th (conflict)
- Venus to 7th (marriage)
- "Bears analogy to and is therefore in natural agreement"

**CONTRARY BY NATURE**
Natural opposition between planet and house:
- Saturn to 10th (honors)
- Saturn to pleasure
- Mars to peace

**COMBINATION**
Mixing of house meanings through ruler connections:
- Ruler of 2nd in 7th = money through marriage
- Must be "possible and appropriate"
- Based on nature and state of planet

**PARTNERSHIP**
Planet and sign act together:
- "Like association or partnership"
- Qualities mixed or combined
- Universal for entire world

---

### Strength Rankings

**CUSP**
- Most effective point of house
- Planet nearest cusp has special power
- "Greatest power over affairs of house"

**ANGULAR > SUCCEDENT > CADENT**
- Angular (1,4,7,10): Strongest
- Succedent (2,5,8,11): Moderate
- Cadent (3,6,9,12): Weakest
- But determination matters more than angularity

**LOCATION > RULERSHIP**
- "Planet's presence in house has greater effect than rulership over that house by planet located elsewhere"
- "Determination by location is immediate"

**DOMICILE > EXALTATION > TRIPLICITY**
Dignity ranking:
- Domicile strongest
- Exaltation second (but special for honors)
- Triplicity minimal

---

### Special Positions

**ANTISCION**
- Mirror positions across Cancer/Capricorn axis
- Can function like aspects or positions
- Mentioned but not extensively developed

**ORIENTAL/OCCIDENTAL**
- Oriental to Sun: Rising before Sun (favorable for outer planets)
- Occidental to Moon: Setting after Moon (favorable for inner planets)

**PARTILE**
- Exact to the degree
- Strongest form of aspect
- "Partile conjunction," "partile sextile"

---

## VII. MORIN'S HIERARCHICAL SYSTEM

### Significator Hierarchy for Any House

**RANK 1 (STRONGEST): Planet in the House**
- Immediate determination
- Greatest power over house affairs
- "Primary significator"

**RANK 2: Ruler of Sign on House Cusp**
- Essential determination
- Sign acts through its ruler's nature
- "After that, the ruler of the house"

**RANK 3: Ruler of the Ruler**
- Secondary chain of signification
- If ruler outside own sign

**RANK 4: Sign Itself in House**
- Through its natural qualities
- "Then the sign in the house"

**RANK 5: Planets Aspecting House Cusp**
- Direct aspects to cusp
- Especially conjunction, square, opposition

**RANK 6: Planets Aspecting House Ruler**
- Indirect modification
- "Last, aspects to the house"

**RANK 7: Planet in Exaltation in House**
- When planet is elsewhere but exaltation sign in house
- Special influence, can show sudden important matters

**RANK 8: Planet in Opposite House**
- Through house opposition
- Weaker but present influence

---

### Nine Ways of Intrinsic Determination
(From Strongest to Weakest)

1. Planet in house, in own sign, aspecting another planet
2. Planet in house, in own sign, without aspect to another
3. Planet in house, outside own sign, aspecting its ruler
4. Planet in house, outside own sign, aspecting another planet (not ruler)
5. Planet in house, outside own sign, without aspect to another
6. Sign in house with aspect from its ruler
7. Aspect from planet not ruling the house
8. Planet in opposite house
9. Only sign in house, no aspects or antiscion to it

---

### Nine Ways of Extrinsic Determination
(When House Ruler in Another House - Same Hierarchy)

1. Ruler in another house, in own sign, aspecting another planet
2. Ruler in another house, in own sign, without aspect
3. Ruler in another house, outside own sign, aspecting its ruler
4. Ruler in another house, outside own sign, aspecting another planet
5. Ruler in another house, outside own sign, without aspect
6. Sign in ruling house with aspect from its ruler
7. Aspect from planet not ruling that house
8. Planet in opposite house
9. Only sign in house, no aspects

---

### Strength Assessment Factors

**When Evaluating Multiple Planets in House:**

1. **Nature** of each planet
2. **Celestial state** of each
3. **Analogical relationship** to house meaning
4. **Rulership** over other planets (by sign)
5. **Proximity to cusp**
6. **Aspects** between the planets
7. **Application sequence** (which planet to which)
8. **Benefic/malefic classification**
9. **Essential and accidental strength**

**The Dominant Planet is:**
- One with analogy to house + ruling others = maximum
- Ruler of others in house
- Analogous to house meaning
- Nearest to cusp
- Strongest by celestial state

## VIII. SPECIAL DOCTRINES & CORRECTIONS

### Morin's Corrections to Traditional Astrology

**CORRECTION 1: Universal Significators**
**Traditional Error:**
"Sun always = father, Moon = mother, Mercury = mind, Jupiter = wealth"

**Morin's Truth:**
"Universal significators are too general without specific determination to houses through location, rulership, or aspect."

---

**CORRECTION 2: Sixth House Meaning**
**Traditional Error (Ptolemy):**
"6th house essentially refers to diseases"

**Morin's Truth:**
"6th house accidentally connected to illness only through opposition to 12th house. Essential meaning is servants, animals, daily work."

---

**CORRECTION 3: Eleventh House Meaning**
**Traditional Error:**
"11th house refers to children"

**Morin's Truth:**
"11th accidentally connected to children only through opposition to 5th. Essential meaning is friends and benefactors."

---

**CORRECTION 4: Four Angles Insufficient**
**Traditional Method:**
Only considering angular placement

**Morin's Truth:**
"Four angles too general - need twelve houses for specific determination."

---

**CORRECTION 5: Planetary Strength as Everything**
**Traditional Error:**
Seeking "strongest" planet for each topic regardless of determination

**Morin's Truth:**
"Planet in house (even if weak) more significant than strong planet without house connection."

---

**CORRECTION 6: Sign Position Alone**
**Traditional Error:**
"Moon in Aries = aggressive for everyone"

**Morin's Truth:**
"Celestial state universal - applies to whole world same way. Only local determination makes it particular."

---

**CORRECTION 7: Always Combining All Rulerships**
**Traditional Error:**
Planet ruling multiple houses always combines all their meanings

**Morin's Truth:**
"Sometimes acts by location, sometimes by one rulership, sometimes another, sometimes combination - depends on context and what's possible."

---

### The Primum Caelum (First Heaven)

**Nature:**
- First cause in nature
- Most universal natural cause
- Concurs in all effects
- Beyond planets and signs

**Function:**
- Determines nature of planets and signs
- Fixed at creation
- Universal for entire world
- Immutable until world's end

**Division:**
- As whole: universal cause
- Divided into signs: particular causes
- Through houses: individual determination

**Morin's Theological View:**
"Among natural causes most similar to God, as befits first natural cause"

---

### Celestial vs. Sublunary

**CELESTIAL BODIES (Active):**
- Actively determine sublunary world
- Pour forth influence
- Universal and particular causes
- Efficient causes of effects

**SUBLUNARY WORLD (Passive):**
- Passively determines celestial influence
- Receives influence
- Provides specific determination through houses
- Does not act back on celestial bodies

**The Interaction:**
"Celestial bodies actively determine sublunary world while latter provides determination of celestial bodies in passive way only."

## IX. PRACTICAL SYNTHESIS RULES

### The Complete Judgment Method

**STEP 1: Identify House**
Determine which house(s) relate to question

**STEP 2: Planets in House**
Evaluate each by:
- Nature (benefic/malefic, hot/cold, etc.)
- Celestial state (dignity, aspects, motion)
- Analogy to house meaning
- Result = Nature + State + Analogy

**STEP 3: House Ruler**
- Find ruler of sign on cusp
- Note which house ruler is in
- Combine meanings of both houses
- Evaluate ruler's nature and state

**STEP 4: Ruler of Ruler**
- If ruler outside own sign
- Follow chain of rulers

**STEP 5: Aspects**
- To house cusp
- To house ruler
- Between planets in house
- From planets outside

**STEP 6: Nine-Fold Synthesis**
For each planet: Nature × State × Determination
For pairs: 9 possible combinations (3×3)

**STEP 7: Make Judgment**
Consider:
- Strength ranking of factors
- Quality (good/evil)
- Quantity (strong/weak)
- Stability (lasting/temporary)
- Possibility given native's station

---

### Benefic/Malefic Action Tables

**BENEFIC IN GOOD STATE + FORTUNATE HOUSE:**
→ Grants good, real, abundant, lasting, without difficulties

**BENEFIC IN POOR STATE + FORTUNATE HOUSE:**
→ Grants nothing OR scanty, spurious, unreliable, with difficulties

**BENEFIC IN INTERMEDIATE STATE + FORTUNATE HOUSE:**
→ Moderate results in quality, quantity, stability

**MALEFIC IN POOR STATE + ANY HOUSE:**
→ Prevents good OR causes evil

**MALEFIC IN GOOD STATE + FORTUNATE HOUSE:**
→ Grants benefits but with imperfections, difficult means, mixed with evil

**MALEFIC IN GOOD STATE + UNFORTUNATE HOUSE:**
→ Rescues from evil OR tempers it (does not prevent)

**MALEFIC IN INTERMEDIATE STATE:**
→ Neither grants nor denies, only prevents good

**BENEFIC IN GOOD STATE + UNFORTUNATE HOUSE:**
→ Prevents or mitigates evil

**BENEFIC IN POOR STATE + UNFORTUNATE HOUSE:**
→ Fails to prevent evil, may cause it

---

### Application Sequence Rules

**BENEFIC → BENEFIC (Fortunate House):**
Good certain and stable

**BENEFIC → MALEFIC (Fortunate House):**
Good ends in evil

**MALEFIC → BENEFIC (Unfortunate House):**
Evil occurs but native freed

**MALEFIC → MALEFIC (Any House):**
Most serious evil, no freedom

**BENEFIC → BENEFIC (Unfortunate House):**
Evil greatly attenuated

---

### Special Combination Rules

**TWO BENEFICS IN SAME HOUSE:**
Always indicate something good
More certain when both strong

**TWO MALEFICS IN SAME HOUSE:**
Always indicate evil
UNLESS both strong by sign AND in fortunate houses
Even then, good mixed with evil

**BENEFIC + MALEFIC IN SAME HOUSE:**
Contested, mixed results
Stronger planet prevails but weaker influences

**PLANET ANALOGOUS + PLANET CONTRARY:**
- Sun + Saturn in 10th
- Evaluate which stronger
- Stronger prevails, weaker modifies

## CONCLUSION

This dictionary represents Morin's complete systematic vocabulary for his revolutionary approach to horoscope interpretation. His innovation was not new planets or houses, but a precise **methodology of determinations** that explained when and how traditional meanings actually apply.

The system's power lies in its logical rigor:
- Nature + Celestial State + Terrestrial State = Effect
- Universal analogies require specific determinations to function
- Houses are the key that makes universal influence particular
- Judgment requires synthesis of multiple factors in hierarchy

Morin transformed astrology from a collection of traditional associations into a logical science with clear rules, verifiable results, and systematic application.
